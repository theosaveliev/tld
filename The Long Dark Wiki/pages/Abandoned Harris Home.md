## Overview

The Abandoned Harris Home can be found by following the road that splits through the Old Island Connector region.

The Abandoned Harris Home consists of three ruined buildings. Outside, a swing set, a bench, a [car](./Car.md), and a mailbox are also present; a [deer](./Deer.md) carcass and a few pieces of [Reclaimed Wood](./Reclaimed%20Wood.md) may lie in the vicinity.

Behind the building is a [Basement (Crumbling Highway)](./Basement%20%28Crumbling%20Highway%29.md) accessible through a bulkhead door, with a few supplies that may be found inside. The basement provides a cold but indoor sheltered place to sleep.
