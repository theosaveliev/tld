> '''Abandoned Mine No. 5''' is a small mine in [[Desolation Point]]. 
> 
> == Overview ==
> '''Abandoned Mine No. 5''' is a vacant coal mine that runs most of the length of Desolation Point.
> 
> It is abundant in [[Coal]], which burns at very high temperatures. Coal can be used in most fires, but it is particularly important for fueling the [[Furnace]], which, along with the [[Heavy Hammer]] allows the player to craft certain key items. A [[Torch]] is always found near the eastern access entrance, regardless of difficulty. It also has a [[Salt Deposit]] located about halfway through.
> 
> === Navigating ===
> The mine is dark but predominately linear. It is highly recommended that anyone venturing into the mine brings with them a light source, such as a [[Flare]] or a fueled [[Storm Lantern]].
> 
> While it is feasible to make it through the cave without a portable light source, it can be almost impossible (particularly during the day), and it is easy to miss important loot that might be scattered about.
