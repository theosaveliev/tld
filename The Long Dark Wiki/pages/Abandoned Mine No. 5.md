## Overview

**Abandoned Mine No. 5** is a vacant coal mine that runs most of the length of Desolation Point.

It is abundant in [Coal](./Coal.md), which burns at very high temperatures. Coal can be used in most fires, but it is particularly important for fueling the [Furnace](./Furnace.md), which, along with the [Heavy Hammer](./Heavy%20Hammer.md) allows the player to craft certain key items. A [Torch](./Torch.md) is always found near the eastern access entrance, regardless of difficulty. It also has a [Salt Deposit](./Salt%20Deposit.md) located about halfway through.

### Navigating

The mine is dark but predominately linear. It is highly recommended that anyone venturing into the mine brings with them a light source, such as a [Flare](./Flare.md) or a fueled [Storm Lantern](./Storm%20Lantern.md).

While it is feasible to make it through the cave without a portable light source, it can be almost impossible (particularly during the day), and it is easy to miss important loot that might be scattered about.
