<h2 id="Uses">Uses</h2>
<p>Acorn Bannocks, at 250 calories and weighing 0.05 kg, are one of the most calorie-dense foods in the game, beating out almost every other food item and, at cooking level 5, beat out the most calorie-dense packaged food, <a href="./Salty%20Crackers.md" class="wikilink" title="Salty Crackers">Salty Crackers</a>. While the in-game description suggests that bannocks are good travel food, it should be noted that they decay at a rate of 20% per day in the player's inventory.</p>
