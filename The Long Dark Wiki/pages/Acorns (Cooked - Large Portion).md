**Acorns (Cooked - Large Portion)** are a natural <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in *The Long Dark*.

## Overview

Acorns (Cooked - Large Portion) are worth 4 times the calories than that of <a href="./Acorns%20%28Cooked%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorns (Cooked)">Acorns (Cooked)</a>, but also weigh 4 times more than regular Acorns (Cooked).

It is much more efficient to turn <a href="./Prepared%20Acorns.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepared Acorns">Prepared Acorns</a> into <a href="./Prepared%20Acorns%20%28Large%20Portion%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepared Acorns (Large Portion)">Prepared Acorns (Large Portion)</a> and then cook the large portion, as it costs 0.25 liters of <a href="./Water%20%28Potable%29.md" class="wikilink" title="water">water</a> to cook one Prepared Acorns into Acorns (Cooked - Large Portion) versus 1.0 liters to cook 4 Prepared Acorns into 4 separate <a href="./Acorns%20%28Cooked%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorns (Cooked)">Acorns (Cooked)</a>.

1 Acorns (Cooked - Large Portion) can be processed into <a href="./Acorn%20Grounds.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorn Grounds">Acorn Grounds</a> using a <a href="./Heavy%20Hammer.md" class="wikilink" title="Heavy Hammer">Heavy Hammer</a>. The action is performable everywhere and takes 10 minutes of in-game time.
