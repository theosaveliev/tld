**Admin Office Key** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode. It is found inside the bathroom of the upper <a href="./Carter%20Hydro%20Dam.md" class="wikilink" title="Carter Hydro Dam">Carter Hydro Dam</a> on a corpse and is used to open the upstairs admin office door so that Mackenzie can recover the <a href="./Aid%20Station%20Medical%20Locker%20Key.md" class="wikilink" title="Aid Station Medical Locker Key">Aid Station Medical Locker Key</a>.
