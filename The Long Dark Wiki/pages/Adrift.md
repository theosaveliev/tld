**Adrift** is a hidden [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[Wintermute](./Wintermute.md)_ [Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things](./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md) of _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

This is an unmissable hidden achievement granted after completing the main story mission [A Cry For Help](./A%20Cry%20For%20Help.md). After exploring derelict Japanese cargo ship, a cutscene will play shortly before Part One of the Episode ends, and the achievement will unlock.
