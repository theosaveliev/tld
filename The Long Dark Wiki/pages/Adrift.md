**Adrift** is a hidden <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>* <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things">Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things</a> of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This is an unmissable hidden achievement granted after completing the main story mission <a href="./A%20Cry%20For%20Help.md" class="wikilink" title="A Cry For Help">A Cry For Help</a>. After exploring derelict Japanese cargo ship, a cutscene will play shortly before Part One of the Episode ends, and the achievement will unlock.
