**Advanced: Guns Guns Guns!** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> inside the rail car at <a href="./Poacher%27s%20Camp.md" class="wikilink" title="Poacher&#39;s Camp">Poacher's Camp</a>. Taking it causes the side quest <a href="./Survival%20School.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival School">Survival School</a> to start and asks the player to cull a single <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf">Wolf</a> in the <a href="./Mystery%20Lake%20%28region%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Mystery Lake">Mystery Lake</a>, <a href="./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md" class="wikilink" title="Forlorn Muskeg">Forlorn Muskeg</a>, <a href="./Broken%20Railroad.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Railroad">Broken Railroad</a> regions.
