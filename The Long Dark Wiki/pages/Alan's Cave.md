> '''Alan's Cave''' is a small cave in [[Mystery Lake (region)|Mystery Lake]]. 
> 
> == Overview ==
> Alan's Cave is not a true cave but it provides some protection against wind chill. It can be found just west of a hunting stand on the eastern side of the region. A frozen river can be found nearby which leads north to [[Carter Hydro Dam]], and south to [[Mystery Lake (location)|Mystery Lake]]. There is also a rabbit area there. 
> 
> In [[Episode 2: Luminance Fugue]], a [[Supply Cache]] is hidden in this cave. The [[Crumpled Note]] marking it on the map can be found on the second level of the dam.
