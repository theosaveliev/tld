# Alice McLeod's Journal 01

> {{IronRoad}}{{Infobox item|name=|image=Alice McLeod's Journal 01 icon.png|release=Iron Road Part 1|update=2.58|story=|type=Collectibles|equipable=No|stackable=No|weight=0.00|actions=Examine|actions_radial=|actions_ground=Take It, Read Text, Leave It|examine=Fragments torn from an old Journal.|key=}}'''Alice McLeod's Journal 01''' is a [[Collectibles|Collectible]] in [[Survival Mode]] as part of the [[Iron Road]] expansion. It is found in [[Anna Island -- Track Section 6]] in the cabin beside the train next to the train key on the table.
