## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Alice McLeod's Journal 01**_

\[Snippets read from a journal damaged by damp and weather\]

(\*\*\*)

Lost my records from the previous months when my digital recorder stopped working. Going to keep notes in this journal until I figure something else out.

(\*\*\*)

...can't believe it took me so long to find the Prototype! It's hinted at in various notes and cylinder recordings I was able to find but the people who hid it, hid it well...

(\*\*\*)

...some exterior damage but it's remarkably intact given how long it's been out here...

(\*\*\*)

...took more than a week just to melt it out of the ice. I never thought so much of my engineering work would involve carrying jerry cans of oil over vast distances...

(\*\*\*)

...managed to get into the interior, finally! What a relief. The cabin is oddly...beautiful?

(\*\*\*)

...the engine appears to be in good shape. After some tinkering I was able to get the firebox going and a couple of cans of oil was enough to get most of the train's systems working again.

I will sleep in the train tonight, and be warm for the first time in months...

(\*\*\*)

...I've checked the false wall at the end of the tunnel and I'm pretty sure I can break through it without damaging the train too much. I hesitate to risk it considering how long it's taken me to get it back into working condition but I need to explore more of these islands and the train is my only way.

(\*\*\*)

...leave my tools and the workshop here for now, but will come back for them when I can. Over the past months, it's come to feel like home.

Tomorrow, this train and I leave this tomb. Who knows what we will discover deeper into the island chain.

(\*\*\*)
