<p><strong>Alice McLeod's Journal 01</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%206.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 6">Anna Island -- Track Section 6</a> in the cabin beside the train next to the train key on the table.</p>
<h2 id="Transcript">Transcript</h2>
<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">
<p>The following text is transcribed from <strong>Alice McLeod's Journal 01</strong></p>
</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>[Snippets read from a journal damaged by damp and weather]</p>
<p>(***)</p>
<p>Lost my records from the previous months when my digital recorder stopped working. Going to keep notes in this journal until I figure something else out.</p>
<p>(***)</p>
<p>...can't believe it took me so long to find the Prototype! It's hinted at in various notes and cylinder recordings I was able to find but the people who hid it, hid it well...</p>
<p>(***)</p>
<p>...some exterior damage but it's remarkably intact given how long it's been out here...</p>
<p>(***)</p>
<p>...took more than a week just to melt it out of the ice. I never thought so much of my engineering work would involve carrying jerry cans of oil over vast distances...</p>
<p>(***)</p>
<p>...managed to get into the interior, finally! What a relief. The cabin is oddly...beautiful?</p>
<p>(***)</p>
<p>...the engine appears to be in good shape. After some tinkering I was able to get the firebox going and a couple of cans of oil was enough to get most of the train's systems working again.</p>
<p>I will sleep in the train tonight, and be warm for the first time in months...</p>
<p>(***)</p>
<p>...I've checked the false wall at the end of the tunnel and I'm pretty sure I can break through it without damaging the train too much. I hesitate to risk it considering how long it's taken me to get it back into working condition but I need to explore more of these islands and the train is my only way.</p>
<p>(***)</p>
<p>...leave my tools and the workshop here for now, but will come back for them when I can. Over the past months, it's come to feel like home.</p>
<p>Tomorrow, this train and I leave this tomb. Who knows what we will discover deeper into the island chain.</p>
<p>(***)</p>
</div>
