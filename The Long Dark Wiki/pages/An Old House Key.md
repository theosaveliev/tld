**An Old House Key** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) found in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) as part of the [Last Horizon](./Last%20Horizon.md) [Tale](./Tales.md). It is found in a [Lock Box](./Lock%20Box.md) buried under the snow near the [Weather Station](./Weather%20Station.md) in [Sundered Pass](./Sundered%20Pass.md) using a powered [Handheld Shortwave Radio](./Handheld%20Shortwave%20Radio.md). It is used to progress the Tale by unlocking a door inside the [Last Lonely House](./Last%20Lonely%20House.md).
