**Armoured** is a positive <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> obtained when wearing a <a href="./Ballistic%20Vest.md" class="wikilink" title="Ballistic Vest">Ballistic Vest</a> in the <a href="./Clothing.md#Accessories" class="wikilink" title="Accessories">Accessories</a> slot. The Affliction remains in effect as long as the Ballistic Vest remains equipped.
