<p><strong>Astrid's Backpack</strong> is a Tool found in the prologue of <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle">Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle</a>. It is placed inside Mackenzie's floatplane in order to progress the story. After the plane crash, the backpack is found next to <a href="./Astrid%27s%20Hardcase.md" class="wikilink" title="Astrid&#39;s Hardcase">Astrid's Hardcase</a>, but serves as an ordinary lootable <a href="./Backpack.md" class="wikilink" title="Backpack">Backpack</a> at this point, containing a <a href="./Granola%20Bar.md" class="wikilink" title="Granola Bar">Granola Bar</a>, <a href="./Fleece%20Mittens.md" class="wikilink" title="Fleece Mittens">Fleece Mittens</a>, and <a href="./Antibiotics.md" class="wikilink" title="Antibiotics">Antibiotics</a>.</p>
