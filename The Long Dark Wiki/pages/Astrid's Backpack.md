**Astrid's Backpack** is a Tool found in the prologue of [Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle](./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md). It is placed inside Mackenzie's floatplane in order to progress the story. After the plane crash, the backpack is found next to [Astrid's Hardcase](./Astrid%27s%20Hardcase.md), but serves as an ordinary lootable [Backpack](./Backpack.md) at this point, containing a [Granola Bar](./Granola%20Bar.md), [Fleece Mittens](./Fleece%20Mittens.md), and [Antibiotics](./Antibiotics.md).
