> '''Astrid's Backpack''' is a Tool found in the prologue of [[Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle]]. It is placed inside Mackenzie's floatplane in order to progress the story. After the plane crash, the backpack is found next to [[Astrid's Hardcase]], but serves as an ordinary lootable [[Backpack]] at this point, containing a [[Granola Bar]], [[Fleece Mittens]], and [[Antibiotics]].
