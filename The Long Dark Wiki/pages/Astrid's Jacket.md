**Astrid's Jacket** is a <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> item worn on the player's outer torso obtained during <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a>.

## Overview

Astrid's jacket can be found on a table in the basement of <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley%20Farmstead.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley Farmstead">Pleasant Valley Farmstead</a> during <a href="./Trauma.md" class="wikilink" title="Trauma">Trauma</a>, the first chapter of <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Crossroads Elegy">Crossroads Elegy</a>. It can not be acquired in Survival Mode, nor at any later stage in the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Story">Story</a>.

The statistics of Astrid's jacket are identical to those of a <a href="./Simple%20Parka.md" class="wikilink" title="Simple Parka">Simple Parka</a>.

There is a small piece of Astrid's scarf tied to her left arm after her encounter with <a href="./Hobbs.md" class="wikilink" title="Hobbs">Hobbs</a>. This piece is all that remains of her scarf after the plane crash.
