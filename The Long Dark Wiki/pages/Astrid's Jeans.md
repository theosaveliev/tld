> '''Astrid's Jeans''' are a [[Clothing]] item worn on the player's legs obtained at the start of [[Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy]].
> 
> ==Overview==
> Astrid's jeans are equipped as default clothing when the player regains consciousness after being saved by [[Molly]] from dying of exposure during [[Trauma]], the first chapter of [[Crossroads Elegy]]. They can not be acquired in Survival Mode, nor at any later stage in the [[Story]].
