**Astrid's Jeans** are a [Clothing](./Clothing.md) item worn on the player's legs obtained at the start of [Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md).

## Overview

Astrid's jeans are equipped as default clothing when the player regains consciousness after being saved by [Molly](./Molly.md) from dying of exposure during [Trauma](./Trauma.md), the first chapter of [Crossroads Elegy](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md). They can not be acquired in Survival Mode, nor at any later stage in the [Story](./Wintermute.md).

The statistics of Astrid's jeans are identical to those of [Jeans](./Jeans.md).
