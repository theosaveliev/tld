<p><strong>Astrid's Jeans</strong> are a <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> item worn on the player's legs obtained at the start of <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Astrid's jeans are equipped as default clothing when the player regains consciousness after being saved by <a href="./Molly.md" class="wikilink" title="Molly">Molly</a> from dying of exposure during <a href="./Trauma.md" class="wikilink" title="Trauma">Trauma</a>, the first chapter of <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Crossroads Elegy">Crossroads Elegy</a>. They can not be acquired in Survival Mode, nor at any later stage in the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Story">Story</a>.</p>
<p>The statistics of Astrid's jeans are identical to those of <a href="./Jeans.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeans">Jeans</a>.</p>
