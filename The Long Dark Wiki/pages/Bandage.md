A **Bandage** is a <a href="./First%20Aid.md" class="wikilink" title="First Aid">First Aid</a> cloth used for stopping bleeding or limb immobilization in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Overview

Bandages are used to stop <a href="./Blood%20Loss.md" class="wikilink" title="Blood Loss">Blood Loss</a>, preventing the player from rapidly losing <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> from bleeding out. They are often found in <a href="./First%20Aid%20Kit.md" class="wikilink" title="First Aid Kits">First Aid Kits</a> and <a href="./Medicine%20Shelf.md" class="wikilink" title="Medicine Shelves">Medicine Shelves</a>. They can also easily be created by harvesting a single <a href="./Cloth.md" class="wikilink" title="Cloth">Cloth</a> and <a href="./Crafting.md" class="wikilink" title="Crafting">Crafting</a> 2 of them anywhere in 15 minutes.

Crafting Bandages while afflicted Blood Loss can be a relatively dangerous process.

- Caution is required to prevent bleeding out while crafting them while at already low Condition. As such, it is advisable to keep a few bandages in one's inventory in case of emergency.
- Blood Loss also attracts predators by giving the player maximum <a href="./Scent.md" class="wikilink" title="Scent">Scent</a>, and can quickly lead to death if the player is attacked while crafting Bandages.
