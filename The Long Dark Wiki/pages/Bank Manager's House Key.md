**Milton Hidden Cache Note** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1">Episode 1</a> of <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>.

It is found inside the bank manager's desk drawer in the <a href="./Milton%20Credit%20Union.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton Credit Union">Milton Credit Union</a>. It unlocks the door to the <a href="./Bank%20Manager%27s%20House.md" class="wikilink" title="Bank Manager&#39;s House">Bank Manager's House</a>.
