The **Bank Manager's House** is a location in <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a> in the <a href="./Mountain%20Town.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountain Town">Mountain Town</a> region. It is labeled as **<a href="./Milton%20House.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton House">Milton House</a>** in Survival Mode.

## Overview

It is a residential building consisting of a kitchen with a <a href="./Pot%20Belly%20Stove.md" class="wikilink" title="Pot Belly Stove">Pot Belly Stove</a>, a bedroom and bathroom. The Bank Manager's House needs to be opened with its own respective key before entering in Story mode. 

The house is located in <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a> a short walk from <a href="./Milton%20Post%20Office.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton Post Office">Milton Post Office</a> in the direction of <a href="./The%20Arch%20%28Mountain%20Town%20location%29.md" class="wikilink" title="The Arch">The Arch</a> and the <a href="./Radio%20Tower.md" class="wikilink" title="Shortwave Tower">Shortwave Tower</a>. From the outside, the house can be identified as a green colored building with stairs up to the front door and a Canadian flag. The interior has a unique floor plan and yellow striped wallpaper. It is also one of the few smaller houses in Milton with a wood-burning stove.

## Episode 1

The house is initially locked but can be opened once <a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will">Will</a> has found the <a href="./Bank%20Manager%27s%20House%20Key.md" class="wikilink" title="Bank Manager&#39;s House Key">Bank Manager's House Key</a> in a desk drawer in <a href="./Milton%20Credit%20Union.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton Credit Union">Milton Credit Union</a>. The code to the bank vault can also be found in the house, making the house a compulsory location for the main questline of <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1">Episode 1</a>.
