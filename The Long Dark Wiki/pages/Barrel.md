# Barrel

> {{Infobox/Item
> |image= Barrel.jpg
> |wintermute=yes}}A '''Barrel''' is an interactable object found near mines. Sometimes they are found intact or half broken. They can both be harvested with a [[Hatchet]] for 2 [[Reclaimed Wood]] and 1 [[Scrap Metal]] after 15 minutes. They can be found near:
> 
> * [[Mining Camp]], [[Gold Mine]] in [[Ash Canyon]]
> * [[Vacant Depot]] in [[Transfer Pass]]
> * [[Cove]], [[Island Cottage]], [[Main Hangar]], [[Justy's Hovel]] in [[Forsaken Airfield]]
> * [[Mirthless Fork]] in [[Keeper's Pass South]]
> * Multiple locations in the [[Zone of Contamination]]
