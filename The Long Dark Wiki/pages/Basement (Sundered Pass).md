**Basement (Sundered Pass)** is an unmarked location in [Sundered Pass](./Sundered%20Pass.md).

## Overview

The Basement is located under one of the destroyed buildings in [Old Settlement](./Old%20Settlement.md), and can only be accessed through the [Last Horizon](./Last%20Horizon.md) Tale.

There is a [Bed](./Bed.md), a [Washer](./Washer.md) and [Dryer](./Dryer.md), food, water, and materials. 4 unique [Cans](./Can.md) can be harvested for a [Recycled Can](./Recycled%20Can.md) each.

2 collectibles can be found near the bed to continue the Last Horizon Tale.

The area above consists of several ruined buildings and one intact two story [cabin](./Cabin%20%28Sundered%20Pass%29.md). The area around the settlement is patrolled by several large packs of [Timberwolves](./Timberwolf.md), making coming and going very difficult.

Despite being underground, the basement does not protect against [Glimmer Fog](./Glimmer%20Fog.md) and [Insomnia Risk](./Insomnia%20Risk.md).
