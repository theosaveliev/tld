# Basement \(Sundered Pass\)

> {{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Basement (Sundered Pass).jpg|region=Sundered Pass|shelter=yes|type=|description=A basement located below [[Old Settlement]]|bed=yes}}'''Basement (Sundered Pass)''' is an unmarked location in [[Sundered Pass]].
> ==Overview==
> The Basement is located under one of the destroyed buildings in [[Old Settlement]], and can only be accessed through the [[Last Horizon|Last Horizon]] Tale.
> 
> There is a [[Bed]], a [[Washer]] and [[Dryer]], food, water, and materials. 4 unique [[Can|Cans]] can be harvested for a [[Recycled Can]] each.
> 
> 2 collectibles can be found near the bed to continue the Last Horizon Tale.
> 
> The area above consists of several ruined buildings and one intact two story [[Cabin (Sundered Pass)|cabin]]. The area around the settlement is patrolled by several large packs of [[Timberwolf|Timberwolves]], making coming and going very difficult.
> 
> Despite being underground, the basement does not protect against [[Glimmer Fog]] and [[Insomnia Risk]].<gallery>
