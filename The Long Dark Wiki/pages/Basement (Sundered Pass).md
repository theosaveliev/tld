<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>The Basement is located under one of the destroyed buildings in <a href="./Old%20Settlement.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Settlement">Old Settlement</a>, and can only be accessed through the <a href="./Last%20Horizon.md" class="wikilink" title="Last Horizon">Last Horizon</a> Tale.</p>
<p>There is a <a href="./Bed.md" class="wikilink" title="Bed">Bed</a>, a <a href="./Washer.md" class="wikilink" title="Washer">Washer</a> and <a href="./Dryer.md" class="wikilink" title="Dryer">Dryer</a>, food, water, and materials. 4 unique <a href="./Can.md" class="wikilink" title="Cans">Cans</a> can be harvested for a <a href="./Recycled%20Can.md" class="wikilink" title="Recycled Can">Recycled Can</a> each.</p>
<p>2 collectibles can be found near the bed to continue the Last Horizon Tale.</p>
<p>The area above consists of several ruined buildings and one intact two story <a href="./Cabin%20%28Sundered%20Pass%29.md" class="wikilink" title="cabin">cabin</a>. The area around the settlement is patrolled by several large packs of <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolves">Timberwolves</a>, making coming and going very difficult.</p>
<p>Despite being underground, the basement does not protect against <a href="./Glimmer%20Fog.md" class="wikilink" title="Glimmer Fog">Glimmer Fog</a> and <a href="./Insomnia%20Risk.md" class="wikilink" title="Insomnia Risk">Insomnia Risk</a>.</p>
