**Basement Key** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 3](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode as a part of the [Trauma](./Trauma.md) mission.

During the course of the mission, Astrid finds the key within the Flower [Vase](./Vase.md) within the [Pleasant Valley Farmstead](./Pleasant%20Valley%20Farmstead.md). The key unlocks the basement that contains medical supplies, a [Revolver](./Revolver.md), [Rifle Ammunition](./Rifle%20Ammunition.md), and an unlocked exit to the house.
