A **Basin** is a single metal pan located on a <a href="./Work%20Bench.md" class="wikilink" title="Work Bench">Work Bench</a> in the <a href="./Quonset%20Garage.md" class="wikilink" title="Quonset Garage">Quonset Garage</a> in the <a href="./Coastal%20Townsite.md" class="wikilink" title="Coastal Townsite">Coastal Townsite</a> in the <a href="./Coastal%20Highway.md" class="wikilink" title="Coastal Highway">Coastal Highway</a> region. Interacting with it will allow a survivor to harvest a variable amount of either <a href="./Water%20%28Unsafe%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Water (Unsafe)">Water (Unsafe)</a> or <a href="./Water%20%28Potable%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Water (Potable)">Water (Potable)</a> (0.20-0.70L). Various buildings use the same metal pan model of a Basin, but the Quonset Garage is the only location with an interactable Basin.

Water inside the Basin will slowly go bad as the days in Survival Mode progress. If the water is not claimed, the initially clean water has a chance to turn into dirty water.

A Basin will not spawn in the Quonset Garage during the <a href="./Whiteout.md" class="wikilink" title="Whiteout">Whiteout</a> Challenge. **Lua execution error in Module:Navbox function navbox**
