# Bear Hide Bed

> {{Infobox item|image=Bear Hide Bed icon.png|update=2.33|weight=10.00|actions=Drop, Place|examine=|story=|release=TFTFT Part 6 Broken Silence}}{{TFTFT}}The '''Bear Hide Bed''' is a [[Decorations]] and [[Bed]] item that can be [[Crafting|Crafted]] as part of the [[Safehouse Customization]] feature.
> ==Overview==
> A lavish wooden bed with a mattress and very warm Black Bear bedsheet, the Bear Hide Bed can be crafted at a [[Furniture Workbench]], but requires [[Woodworking Tools]].
> 
> This bed can be [[Sleep|Slept]] in and gives a [[Warmth]] bonus of +10 °C (+18 °F):
> 
> * This is the warmest bed value in the game, but the Bear Hide Bed requires the highest crafting/material times of any bed option as a tradeoff.
> * It restores ''twice'' the [[Condition]] of a normal bed. On [[Interloper]]/[[Misery]], it provides the greatest Condition recovery of all the bed types in the game.
> 
> Progress for Crafting this item will be stored in the Furniture Workbench's container inventory, rather than the player's inventory. Once crafted, the item can be automatically placed in the Safehouse. Canceling this prompt will force the item back into the Furniture Workbench's container inventory. The bed can only be placed on a wide, clear space on the floor when in Customization Mode.
> 
> Despite having a Backpack carry weight value, the bed cannot be picked up and placed back into the player's inventory once crafted, and so it cannot be carried from one base to another. The only option is to move or destroy it (destroying the bed will yield no resources, so consider the material cost/time investment before creating this item).
> 
> == Harvesting ==
> This item cannot be harvested.
