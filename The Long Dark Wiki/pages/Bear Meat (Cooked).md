**Bear Meat (Cooked)** is a cooked <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in *The Long Dark*.

## Overview

Cooked Bear Meat is obtained by using a lit <a href="./Fire.md" class="wikilink" title="Fire">Fire</a> and improves the <a href="./Cooking.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking">Cooking</a> skill. Cooking increases its condition by 50%, makes it decay 4 times slower than raw meat, and adds a reduced chance of <a href="./Food%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Food Poisoning">Food Poisoning</a> over raw meat.

Using a <a href="./Cooking%20Pot.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Pot">Cooking Pot</a> reduces cooking time by 25%. Cooked food on an active fire for too long will burn it, destroying the item and damaging a Cooking Pot if used.
