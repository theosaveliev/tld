**Bending The Rail** is a hidden <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>* <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things">Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things</a> of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This is an unmissable hidden achievement granted during the main story mission <a href="./To%20SAR%20And%20Back.md" class="wikilink" title="To SAR And Back">To SAR And Back</a>. Retrieve the <a href="./Trauma%20Kit.md" class="wikilink" title="Trauma Kit">Trauma Kit</a> and return to <a href="./Heller.md" class="wikilink" title="Heller">Heller</a>, and the achievement will unlock.
