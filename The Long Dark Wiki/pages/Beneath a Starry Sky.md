**Beneath a Starry Sky** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after surviving for three consecutive nights outside in a single <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> game. It is most easily done on Pilgrim, but will often be acquired through normal gameplay on harder difficulties. It is highly recommended to pack sufficient warm clothing, materials for starting a fire, and food and drink, and survive inside a <a href="./Warm%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Warm Cave">Warm Cave</a>.

Outside refers to being anywhere but inside a building or other structure that has an interior loading screen. Therefore this achievement is obtainable whilst being inside of a train carriage among other structures. However, it is not obtainable in caves.

The best structure to use is a <a href="./Fishing%20Hut.md" class="wikilink" title="fishing hut">fishing hut</a>, as these have protection from the wind, a pot belly stove, and a fishing hole that is an excellent source of food if the player has brought <a href="./Fishing%20Tackle.md" class="wikilink" title="fishing tackle">fishing tackle</a>. In addition, animals will not enter the huts, regardless of whether they have a door or not. Using a fishing hut with a door still counts as being outside.

Another alternative are the railway cars such <a href="./Poacher%27s%20Camp.md" class="wikilink" title="Poacher&#39;s Camp">Poacher's Camp</a>. However, unless the railway car has a fire barrel, the stay may be uncomfortably chilly.
