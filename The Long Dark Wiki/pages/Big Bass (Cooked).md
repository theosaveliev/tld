<p>The <strong>Big Bass</strong> is a legendary <a href="./Smallmouth%20Bass%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Bass">Bass</a> rumored to be located in the <a href="./Pensive%20Pond.md" class="wikilink" title="Pensive Pond">Pensive Pond</a> in <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley">Pleasant Valley</a> and is sought after in the <a href="./Tall%20Tales.md" class="wikilink" title="Tall Tales">Tall Tales</a> side mission of <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3">Episode 3</a> of <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="story">story</a> mode.</p>
<h2 id="Story">Story</h2>
<p>The Big Bass is first learned about by the player after reading the book <em>Local Legends - The Big One</em>. This book can be found in the second bunker of <a href="./Joplin%27s%20Bunker%20Raid.md" class="wikilink" title="Joplin&#39;s Bunker Raid">Joplin's Bunker Raid</a> within <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley">Pleasant Valley</a>.</p>
<p>After reading this book, the player can fish at the <a href="./Pensive%20Pond.md" class="wikilink" title="Pensive Pond">Pensive Pond</a> with <a href="./Fishing%20Tackle.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Tackle">Fishing Tackle</a> and catch the Big Bass. This completes the mission, rewarding the player with a large fish, which may be useful for the <a href="./Coming%20Storm.md" class="wikilink" title="Coming Storm">Coming Storm</a> mission.</p>
