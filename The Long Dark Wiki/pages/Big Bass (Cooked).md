> The '''Big Bass''' is a legendary [[smallmouth bass|Bass]] rumored to be located in the [[Pensive Pond]] in [[Pleasant Valley]] and is sought after in the [[Tall Tales]] side mission of [[Episode 3]] of [[story]] mode. 
> 
> ==Story==
> The Big Bass is first learned about by the player after reading the book ''Local Legends - The Big One''. This book can be found in the second bunker of [[Joplin's Bunker Raid]] within [[Pleasant Valley]]. 
> 
> After reading this book, the player can fish at the [[Pensive Pond]] with [[fishing tackle|Fishing Tackle]] and catch the Big Bass. This completes the mission, rewarding the player with a large fish, which may be useful for the [[Coming Storm]] mission.
