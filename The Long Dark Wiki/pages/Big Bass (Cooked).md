The **Big Bass** is a legendary [Bass](./Smallmouth%20Bass%20%28Raw%29.md) rumored to be located in the [Pensive Pond](./Pensive%20Pond.md) in [Pleasant Valley](./Pleasant%20Valley.md) and is sought after in the [Tall Tales](./Tall%20Tales.md) side mission of [Episode 3](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) of [story](./Wintermute.md) mode.

## Story

The Big Bass is first learned about by the player after reading the book _Local Legends - The Big One_. This book can be found in the second bunker of [Joplin's Bunker Raid](./Joplin%27s%20Bunker%20Raid.md) within [Pleasant Valley](./Pleasant%20Valley.md).

After reading this book, the player can fish at the [Pensive Pond](./Pensive%20Pond.md) with [Fishing Tackle](./Fishing%20Tackle.md) and catch the Big Bass. This completes the mission, rewarding the player with a large fish, which may be useful for the [Coming Storm](./Coming%20Storm.md) mission.
