# Black Black Heart

**Categories:** Achievements, Pages with update history · **Source:** [Fandom](https://thelongdark.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Black_Heart)

**Black Black Heart**

[Black Black Heart icon](../assets/Black_Black_Heart_icon.png)

**Description:** Collect all four bloody pages from the Blackrock Archives.

**Black Black Heart** is a [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in [Episode 4: Fury, then Silence](./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md) of _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

<span id="guide"></span><span id="Guide"></span>

## Guide

This is a side mission achievement achieved after completing the side mission [Dark Star](./Dark%20Star.md). Find all 4 [Archival Report](./Archival%20Report.md) notes and the achievement will unlock.

<span id="tasks"></span><span id="Tasks"></span>

## Tasks

- [Volume A](./Archival%20Report.md#Volume_A) is found in Blackrock Locker #3 (requires a key to open and is an objective of the [Blackrock Lockers](./Blackrock%20Lockers.md) side mission).
- [Volume B](./Archival%20Report.md#Volume_B) is inside the prison transport bus that is near the [work bench](./Work%20Bench.md) on the Blackrock Prison grounds.
- [Volume C](./Archival%20Report.md#Volume_C) is on a desk inside the [Old Substation](./Old%20Substation.md).
- [Volume D](./Archival%20Report.md#Volume_D) is on the [work bench](./Work%20Bench.md) inside the trailer at [Foreman's Clearcut](./Foreman%27s%20Clearcut.md).
