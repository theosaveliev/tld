**Blackrock Locker Key #3** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) note found in [Episode 4](./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story as part of the side mission [Blackrock Lockers](./Blackrock%20Lockers.md). It is found in the [Glove Box](./Glove%20Box.md) of the [Truck](./Truck.md) in the barn near [Jailer's Residence](./Jailer%27s%20Residence.md). It is used to open Locker #3 which contains 5 [Rifle Ammunition](./Rifle%20Ammunition.md), a [Hunting Knife](./Hunting%20Knife.md), an [Emergency Stim](./Emergency%20Stim.md), and a [Archival Report](./Archival%20Report.md).
