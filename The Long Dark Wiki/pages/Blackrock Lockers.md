**Blackrock Locker Keys** is a side mission in [Episode 4](./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md) of [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md).

## Overview

To start the mission, the player must find any of three locker keys scattered around the [Blackrock](./Blackrock.md) region. Each key unlocks a locker in prison guard barracks at the [Blackrock Prison](./Blackrock%20Prison.md). Finding and using all keys will complete the mission.

## Tasks

- Open Blackrock Locker #2
- Open Blackrock Locker #3
- Open Blackrock Locker #8

## Key locations

- Blackrock Locker Key #2 is located in a small forest area north of Blackrock Prison on the [Dead Prison Guard](./Dead%20Prison%20Guard.md) corpse. The locker contains 5 [Rifle Ammunition](./Rifle%20Ammunition.md), [Combat Pants](./Combat%20Pants.md) and [Military Coat](./Military%20Coat.md).
- Blackrock Locker Key #8 is located inside a [Metal Container](./Metal%20Container.md) at the bottom of the staircase leading from [Steam Tunnels](./Steam%20Tunnels.md) back to Blackrock Prison. The locker contains 12 [Revolver Ammunition](./Revolver%20Ammunition.md), [Revolver](./Revolver.md) and [Flare Shell](./Flare%20Shell.md).
- Blackrock Locker Key #3 is located at an un-named farm in the south of the region, a short walk east of [Blocked Bridge](./Blocked%20Bridge.md). The locker contains 5 [Rifle Ammunition](./Rifle%20Ammunition.md), a [Hunting Knife](./Hunting%20Knife.md), an [Emergency Stim](./Emergency%20Stim.md), and the [Archival Report](./Archival%20Report.md).

## Tips

- After the first time, it is impossible to crawl through the infirmary window again, but with a bit of wiggling it is possible to step up the tiny ridge (on the left side, near two "chimney" pipes) which separates you from being able to jump down to the doors of the infirmary. Thus saving you the return trip around the wolves.
