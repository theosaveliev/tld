**Blackrock Memo - Highly Aggressive Wolf** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> note found in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story. It is found in the <a href="./Locker.md" class="wikilink" title="Locker">Locker</a> used to store the player's items to prevent them from being confiscated when returning to the Prison. In the room unlocked by the <a href="./Blackrock%20Gate%20Key.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Gate Key">Blackrock Gate Key</a>, where the first <a href="./Noisemaker.md" class="wikilink" title="Noisemaker">Noisemaker</a> is found. It is part of the side mission <a href="./Blackrock%20Rumours.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Rumours">Blackrock Rumours</a>.

<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">

<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

ATTN: Officers and Staff

To make a tough winter even tougher, we have got some new reports of that unusual wolf we dealt with last year. It's back, apparently. We had hoped its roaming had taken it far afield and away from the grounds, but these sightings, and its aggressive behaviour, are conclusive.

To help us understand if this is an issue with a single animal or part of some larger pattern on the island, we have engaged the regional ecologist to conduct a short study and give us some options.

In the meantime, stay vigilant when outside the grounds or away from your vehicles when traveling.

D. Gordon

Blue Shift Supervisor
