**Blackrock Memo - Suspicious Person** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> note found in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story. It is found on the dashboard of a <a href="./Truck.md" class="wikilink" title="Truck">Truck</a> on the road near the <a href="./Old%20Substation.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Substation">Old Substation</a>. It is part of the side mission <a href="./Blackrock%20Rumours.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Rumours">Blackrock Rumours</a>.
