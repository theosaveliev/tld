## Overview

Blocked Highway is located between [Abandoned Mine No. 3](./No.%203%20Coal%20Mine.md) to the east, and [Katie's Secluded Corner](./Katie%27s%20Secluded%20Corner.md) in the nearby mountain. It serves as a landmark, and can be [Mountain Goated](./Mountain%20Goating.md) via the nearby Abandoned Mine to save time. A [Rope Climbing](./Rope%20Climbing.md) section can be deployed to save time when leaving the region via the mine
