**Blood Loss** is caused by a <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf">Wolf</a>, <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolf">Timberwolf</a>, <a href="./Poisoned%20Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Poisoned Wolf">Poisoned Wolf</a>, <a href="./Cougar.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougar">Cougar</a>, or a <a href="./Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear">Bear</a> <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggle">Struggle</a>, a Timberwolf "bite-and-run" attack, or by falling off of a <a href="./Mountaineering%20Rope.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountaineering Rope">Mountaineering Rope</a>.

Suffering from Blood Loss is a life threatening affliction and will eventually kill the player if left untreated. <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> will be lost at a rate of 30% per hour and blood drops will fall wherever the character walks. Active Blood Loss will always maximize the <a href="./Scent.md" class="wikilink" title="Scent">Scent</a> meter, quickly drawing other predators to your location.

Blood Loss from predator <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggles">Struggles</a> is always paired with an <a href="./Infection%20Risk.md" class="wikilink" title="Infection Risk">Infection Risk</a>, while Blood Loss from falling off a Mountaineering Rope is not. Infection is treated as a separate but equally dangerous affliction.

- Blood Loss is often accompanied by <a href="./Sprained%20Wrist.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Wrist">Sprained Wrist</a>, or <a href="./Sprained%20Wrist.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Wrist">Sprained Wrist</a>. Always make treating Blood Loss your priority if you can to avoid bleeding out.
- Blood Loss can be treated by applying a <a href="./Bandage.md" class="wikilink" title="Bandage">Bandage</a>. As of the "Faithful Cartographer" update, <a href="./Old%20Man%27s%20Beard%20Wound%20Dressing.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Man&#39;s Beard Wound Dressing">Old Man's Beard Wound Dressing</a> will only treat Infection, it can no longer be used as a bandage.
- Blood Loss will remain in effect until death or it is treated.
- It is possible to suffer from multiple instances of Blood Loss as a result of multiple attacks or even a single wolf attack. The effects are cumulative and each instance has to be treated separately.
- Blood Loss will draw predators to the player through the <a href="./Scent.md" class="wikilink" title="Scent">Scent</a> of fresh blood, and gives maximum Scent value. Treating the Blood Loss will remove this odor.
- <a href="./Infection%20Risk.md" class="wikilink" title="Infection Risk">Infection Risk</a> is always paired with Blood Loss, so use Old Man's Beard Wound Dressing or <a href="./Antiseptic.md" class="wikilink" title="Antiseptic">Antiseptic</a> to heal it before it develops into an <a href="./Infection.md" class="wikilink" title="Infection">Infection</a>.
