## Overview

> “**You are bleeding from a wound. If left untreated, you will continue to weaken until you die.**”
>
> — In-game description

Suffering from Blood Loss is a life threatening affliction and will eventually kill the player if left untreated. [Condition](./Condition.md) will be lost at a rate of 30% per hour and blood drops will fall wherever the character walks. Active Blood Loss will always maximize the [Scent](./Scent.md) meter, quickly drawing other predators to your location.

Blood Loss from predator [Struggles](./Struggle.md) is always paired with an [Infection Risk](./Infection%20Risk.md), while Blood Loss from falling off a Mountaineering Rope is not. Infection is treated as a separate but equally dangerous affliction.

- Blood Loss is often accompanied by [Sprained Wrist](./Sprained%20Wrist.md), or [Sprained Wrist](./Sprained%20Wrist.md). Always make treating Blood Loss your priority if you can to avoid bleeding out.
- Blood Loss can be treated by applying a [Bandage](./Bandage.md). As of the "Faithful Cartographer" update, [Old Man's Beard Wound Dressing](./Old%20Man%27s%20Beard%20Wound%20Dressing.md) will only treat Infection, it can no longer be used as a bandage.
- Blood Loss will remain in effect until death or it is treated.
- It is possible to suffer from multiple instances of Blood Loss as a result of multiple attacks or even a single wolf attack. The effects are cumulative and each instance has to be treated separately.
- Blood Loss will draw predators to the player through the [Scent](./Scent.md) of fresh blood, and gives maximum Scent value. Treating the Blood Loss will remove this odor.
- [Infection Risk](./Infection%20Risk.md) is always paired with Blood Loss, so use Old Man's Beard Wound Dressing or [Antiseptic](./Antiseptic.md) to heal it before it develops into an [Infection](./Infection.md).
