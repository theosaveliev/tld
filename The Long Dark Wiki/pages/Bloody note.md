**Bloody note** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectibles">Collectibles</a> found in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> in <a href="./Sundered%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Sundered Pass">Sundered Pass</a>. It is found in the radar station building in <a href="./Weather%20Station.md" class="wikilink" title="Weather Station">Weather Station</a>, in the northwest corner of the room.
