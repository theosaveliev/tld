<p><strong>Boss's Encampment</strong> is a ruined series of buildings high in the mountains near <a href="./Idle%20Camp.md" class="wikilink" title="Idle Camp">Idle Camp</a> in the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>The settlement consists of 3 ruined building and an intact shed. There is no real shelter to be found, but a <a href="./Warm%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Warm Cave">Warm Cave</a>, <a href="./Scaffolder%27s%20Hideout.md" class="wikilink" title="Scaffolder&#39;s Hideout">Scaffolder's Hideout</a>, is a short walk away across a wooden bridge.</p>
<p><a href="./Ptarmigan.md" class="wikilink" title="Ptarmigans">Ptarmigans</a> and <a href="./Rabbit.md" class="wikilink" title="Rabbits">Rabbits</a> spawn nearby in the settlement, with a few containers containing loot to be found in the ruined buildings.</p>
<p>A <a href="./Polaroids.md" class="wikilink" title="vista location">vista location</a> is located at the cliff side overlooking the railroad with a destroyed picnic table. A human skull is found near one of the buildings, which contains a hidden lockbox <a href="./Supply%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Supply Cache">Supply Cache</a> as part of the <a href="./Buried%20Echoes.md" class="wikilink" title="Buried Echoes">Buried Echoes</a> Tale.</p>
<h2 id="Trivia">Trivia</h2>
<p>This location likely was the headquarters for Dr. Rüdiger and/or the <a href="./Langston%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Langston Mine">Langston Mine</a> Foreman while overseeing the construction of the machine in the mine, but was burned down to cover up what took place in the mine after it was evacuated.</p>
