# Boss's Settlement

> '''Boss's Encampment''' is a ruined series of buildings high in the mountains near [[Idle Camp]] in the [[Zone of Contamination]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> The settlement consists of 3 ruined building and an intact shed. There is no real shelter to be found, but a [[Warm Cave]], [[Scaffolder's Hideout]], is a short walk away across a wooden bridge.
> 
> [[Ptarmigan|Ptarmigans]] and [[Rabbit|Rabbits]] spawn nearby in the settlement, with a few containers containing loot to be found in the ruined buildings.
> 
> A [[Polaroids|vista location]] is located at the cliff side overlooking the railroad with a destroyed picnic table. A human skull is found near one of the buildings, which contains a hidden lockbox [[Supply Cache]] as part of the [[Buried Echoes]] Tale.
> 
> == Trivia ==
> This location likely was the headquarters for Dr. Rüdiger and/or the [[Langston Mine]] Foreman while overseeing the construction of the machine in the mine, but was burned down to cover up what took place in the mine after it was evacuated.
