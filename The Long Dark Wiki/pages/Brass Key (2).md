**Brass Key** is a [Collectibles](./Collectibles.md) found in [Episode 5](./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story. It is found inside Dr Larsen's Clinic during the main mission [Perseverance](./Perseverance.md). It is inside the middle desk drawer where [Dr Larsen's Clipboard](./Dr%20Larsen%27s%20Clipboard.md) is found.

It is used to open the [Weapon Locker](./Weapon%20Locker.md) inside the [Constable's Office](./Constable%27s%20Office.md) to obtain a [Revolver](./Revolver.md) and 2 [Revolver Ammunition](./Revolver%20Ammunition.md) cartridges.
