**Brass Key** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectibles">Collectibles</a> found in <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 5">Episode 5</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story. It is found inside Dr Larsen's Clinic during the main mission <a href="./Perseverance.md" class="wikilink" title="Perseverance">Perseverance</a>. It is inside the middle desk drawer where <a href="./Dr%20Larsen%27s%20Clipboard.md" class="wikilink" title="Dr Larsen&#39;s Clipboard">Dr Larsen's Clipboard</a> is found.

It is used to open the <a href="./Weapon%20Locker.md" class="wikilink" title="Weapon Locker">Weapon Locker</a> inside the <a href="./Constable%27s%20Office.md" class="wikilink" title="Constable&#39;s Office">Constable's Office</a> to obtain a <a href="./Revolver.md" class="wikilink" title="Revolver">Revolver</a> and 2 <a href="./Revolver%20Ammunition.md" class="wikilink" title="Revolver Ammunition">Revolver Ammunition</a> cartridges.
