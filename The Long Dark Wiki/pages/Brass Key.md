**Brass Key** is a [Collectibles](./Collectibles.md) found in [Episode 5](./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story. It is found inside [Cooper's Blind](./Cooper%27s%20Blind.md) during the main mission [Perseverance](./Perseverance.md), gifted to [Astrid Greenwood](./Astrid%20Greenwood.md) by [Cooper](./Cooper.md) during their conversation and it is used to open the [Medical Clinic](./Medical%20Clinic.md) in the town.

Despite Astrid promising to return the key to the bartender, she ultimately never does.
