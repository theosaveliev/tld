**Brass Key** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectibles">Collectibles</a> found in <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 5">Episode 5</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story. It is found inside <a href="./Cooper%27s%20Blind.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooper&#39;s Blind">Cooper's Blind</a> during the main mission <a href="./Perseverance.md" class="wikilink" title="Perseverance">Perseverance</a>, gifted to <a href="./Astrid%20Greenwood.md" class="wikilink" title="Astrid Greenwood">Astrid Greenwood</a> by <a href="./Cooper.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooper">Cooper</a> during their conversation and it is used to open the <a href="./Medical%20Clinic.md" class="wikilink" title="Medical Clinic">Medical Clinic</a> in the town.

Despite Astrid promising to return the key to the bartender, she ultimately never does.
