**Broken Body** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

The player must survive a <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> game set to the <a href="./Misery.md" class="wikilink" title="Misery">Misery</a> Experience long enough to acquire the <a href="./Broken%20Body.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Body">Broken Body</a> Affliction (hence the namesake of this achievement).

Using the <a href="./Hibernation.md" class="wikilink" title="Hibernation">Hibernation</a> method can be a way to make time move faster, but the player must ensure that they survive long enough while having to combat the other 4 stages of Misery. The same survival strategies found on the <a href="./Survival%20Mode%20%E2%80%94%20Interloper%20Tips.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode/Interloper Tips">Survival Mode/Interloper Tips</a> can be a good start to learn how to survive on Misery, as Misery is just a harder version of Interloper but by a small margin.

## Stages of Misery

Over time at fixed intervals, the survivor will be <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Afflicted">Afflicted</a> with the 5 Stages of Misery; permanent conditions unique to Misery making survival increasingly challenging.

Stage 1 and 5 are always <a href="./Diminished%20Form.md" class="wikilink" title="Diminished Form">Diminished Form</a> and <a href="./Broken%20Body.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Body">Broken Body</a> respectively, with stages 2-4 randomized between: <a href="./Haunted%20Mind.md" class="wikilink" title="Haunted Mind">Haunted Mind</a>, <a href="./Frigid%20Bones.md" class="wikilink" title="Frigid Bones">Frigid Bones</a>, <a href="./Rheumatic%20Joints.md" class="wikilink" title="Rheumatic Joints">Rheumatic Joints</a>, and <a href="./Sour%20Stomach.md" class="wikilink" title="Sour Stomach">Sour Stomach</a>.

- Stage 1: Hardship
  - 1 day survived: <a href="./Diminished%20Form.md" class="wikilink" title="Diminished Form">Diminished Form</a>
- Stage 2: Sorrow
  - 6 days survived: Random New Misery Affliction
- Stage 3: Despair
  - 16 days survived: Random New Misery Affliction
- Stage 4: Torment
  - 31 days survived: Two Remaining New Misery Afflictions
- Stage 5: Ruin
  - 46 days survived: <a href="./Broken%20Body.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Body">Broken Body</a>
