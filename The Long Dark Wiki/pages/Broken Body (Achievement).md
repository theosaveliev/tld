**Broken Body** is an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

The player must survive a [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) game set to the [Misery](./Misery.md) Experience long enough to acquire the [Broken Body](./Broken%20Body.md) Affliction (hence the namesake of this achievement).

Using the [Hibernation](./Hibernation.md) method can be a way to make time move faster, but the player must ensure that they survive long enough while having to combat the other 4 stages of Misery. The same survival strategies found on the [Survival Mode/Interloper Tips](./Survival%20Mode%20%E2%80%94%20Interloper%20Tips.md) can be a good start to learn how to survive on Misery, as Misery is just a harder version of Interloper but by a small margin.

## Stages of Misery

Over time at fixed intervals, the survivor will be [Afflicted](./Afflictions.md) with the 5 Stages of Misery; permanent conditions unique to Misery making survival increasingly challenging.

Stage 1 and 5 are always [Diminished Form](./Diminished%20Form.md) and [Broken Body](./Broken%20Body.md) respectively, with stages 2-4 randomized between: [Haunted Mind](./Haunted%20Mind.md), [Frigid Bones](./Frigid%20Bones.md), [Rheumatic Joints](./Rheumatic%20Joints.md), and [Sour Stomach](./Sour%20Stomach.md).

- Stage 1: Hardship
  - 1 day survived: [Diminished Form](./Diminished%20Form.md)
- Stage 2: Sorrow
  - 6 days survived: Random New Misery Affliction
- Stage 3: Despair
  - 16 days survived: Random New Misery Affliction
- Stage 4: Torment
  - 31 days survived: Two Remaining New Misery Afflictions
- Stage 5: Ruin
  - 46 days survived: [Broken Body](./Broken%20Body.md)
