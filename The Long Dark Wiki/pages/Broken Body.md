**Broken Body** is an [Affliction](./Afflictions.md) found within _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_. It is one of 6 unique Afflictions that can be encountered only within Misery Mode experience.

## Overview

> “__Your body carries the weight of a dying world.  

 You have lost the ability to recover Condition and are twice as vulnerable to physical damage.__”
>
> — In-game Description

Broken Body is a permanent Affliction unique to [Misery Mode](./Misery.md) which occurs from Stage 5 of Misery (Day 47). It completely disables Condition recovery and doubles all sources of Condition drain.

## Effects

- All forms of [Condition](./Condition.md) recovery are disabled (Passive from met [Needs](./Needs.md), [Sleep](./Sleep.md) and all [Items](./Emergency%20Stim.md))
- +100% Condition damage taken

## Trivia

- All negative condition drains are affected. This includes negative afflictions, falling into [weak ice](./Weak%20Ice.md), fall damage, starvation, contact with [fire hazards](./Burns.md) (electricity and flames), and so on.
- Despite doubling all negative sources that drain Condition, Broken Body does not affect the final damage dealt by post-[struggle](./Struggle.md) attacks from [Bears](./Bear.md), [Moose](./Moose.md), and [Cougars](./Cougar.md). The only exception is [wolves](./Wolf.md).
  - This means that if the player is ever attacked by any of these creatures, having 100% protection will not result in an instant game over.
- Has synergy with the Misery affliction "[Rheumatic Joints"](./Rheumatic%20Joints.md), which causes 4% Condition loss when receiving a [Sprain](./Sprained%20Ankle.md).
- Has synergy with the Misery affliction "[Sour Stomach](./Sour%20Stomach.md)", which disables the benefit from [Cooking Skill](./Cooking.md) level 5 preventing [Food Poisoning](./Food%20Poisoning.md) risk when eating cooked or [artificial food](./Tin%20of%20Sardines.md).
- Has synergy with the Misery affliction "[Frigid Bones](./Frigid%20Bones.md)", which reduces the survivor's [Feels Like](./Feels%20Like.md) temperature and the rate which [Warmth](./Warmth.md) refills.
- Has negative synergy with the Misery affliction "[Haunted Mind](./Haunted%20Mind.md)", which reduces Condition recovery from [Sleep](./Sleep.md) by 50%, while Broken Body disables all forms of Condition recovery completely.
