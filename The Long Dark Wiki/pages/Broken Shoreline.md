# Broken Shoreline

> {{IronRoad}}{{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Broken Shoreline.jpg|imagecaption=|region=Port Remnant|location=Slightly southwest of the Lighthouse|sublocations=|description=A partially sunken ship frozen in the water|type=|bed=|firebox=|forge=|workbench=|shelter=|missions=|characters=|map=Broken Shoreline Location.png}}'''Broken Shoreline''' is a location in [[Port Remnant]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> A sunken sea vessel frozen in the ice, this location has almost nothing of value to it, save for the [[Wax Cylinder 11]] in the snow near the bow of the vessel. The ship cannot be entered. Close by is the [[Lighthouse (Port Remnant)]] and the [[Lighthouse Keeper's Cabin]]. Heading north west will take the player to the hidden [[Sheltered Access]].{{Port Remnant}}
