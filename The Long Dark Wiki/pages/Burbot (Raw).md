**Burbot (Raw)** is a raw <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in *The Long Dark*. Burbot have a minimum raw weight of 3.9 kg and a maximum raw weight of 6.0 kg, making them the largest fish in-game, aside from the <a href="./Big%20Bass%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Big Bass">Big Bass</a> in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>. They are also the most calorie-dense fish, at 325 kcal/kg.

Burbot can be harvested to obtain <a href="./Fishing%20Bait.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Bait">Fishing Bait</a>, yielding 3 units per kilogram harvested. When cooked, they produce a <a href="./Burbot%20%28Cooked%29.md" class="wikilink" title="cooked Burbot">cooked Burbot</a> and an above-average yield of 0.03 L of lamp oil per kg of fish, making them relatively efficient at fuel production.
