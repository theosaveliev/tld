 **Burdened** is a unique [Affliction](./Afflictions.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_ only encountered in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) when consuming certain [Cooking](./Cooking.md) Recipes. It was added in the Part 3 update to the [Tales from the Far Territory](./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md).

## Overview

> “**Your pack feels heavier because of something you ate.**”

Burdened is acquired from consuming certain recipes that effects a survivor's [Encumbrance](./Encumbrance.md) by temporarily lowering their maximum total carry weight by 3kg for 1 hour. This can be an especially dangerous Affliction to acquire when a survivor is already under the negative effects of low carrying capacity gained from low [Fatigue](./Fatigue.md).

The following foods, when consumed, grant the Burdened Affliction as a side effect:

- ![Prepper's Pie icon](../assets/Prepper%27s_Pie_icon.png) [Prepper's Pie](./Prepper%27s%20Pie.md)
- ![Stalker's Pie icon](../assets/Stalker%27s_Pie_icon.png) [Stalker's Pie](./Stalker%27s%20Pie.md)

The effect of this Affliction does not stack, and each application will not override the previous Affliction. Partially eating the previously mentioned foods only adds a partial amount of Burdened Recovery Time.
