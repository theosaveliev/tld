# Burdened

> {{Infobox/Affliction|image=Burdened icon.png|category=[[Afflictions]]|caused by=Consume [[Food And Drink]]|+/- effect=Negative|type (Benefit/Injury/Disease)=Injury|benefit duration=|release=21 June 2023|update=2.17|affliction duration=Until Recovery Time passes|treatment=|recoverytime=1 Hour}}{{TFTFT}}
> '''Burdened''' is a unique [[Afflictions|Affliction]] in ''[[The Long Dark]]'' only encountered in [[Survival Mode]] when consuming certain [[Cooking]] Recipes. It was added in the Part 3 update to the [[Tales from the Far Territory]].
> ==Overview==
> {{Quote2|Your pack feels heavier because of something you ate.}}
> Burdened is acquired from consuming certain recipes that effects a survivor's [[Encumbrance]] by temporarily lowering their maximum total carry weight by 3kg for 1 hour. This can be an especially dangerous Affliction to acquire when a survivor is already under the negative effects of low carrying capacity gained from low [[Fatigue]].
> 
> The following foods, when consumed, grant the Burdened Affliction as a side effect:
> 
> * {{Iconlink|1=Prepper's Pie}}
> * {{Iconlink|1=Stalker's Pie}}
> The effect of this Affliction does not stack, and each application will not override the previous Affliction. Partially eating the previously mentioned foods only adds a partial amount of Burdened Recovery Time.
> 
> == Trivia ==
> A symbol similar for the Burdened Affliction icon appears in [[Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy]] whenever [[Astrid Greenwood]] is carrying a [[Crash Survivor]].
