<td><p>|} <strong>Burdened</strong> is a unique <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> in <em><a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a></em> only encountered in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> when consuming certain <a href="./Cooking.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking">Cooking</a> Recipes. It was added in the Part 3 update to the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>

<td><p><span style="font-style:italic">Your pack feels heavier because of something you ate.</span></p></td>
<td style="vertical-align: bottom; font-size: 3em; color: #5d5980; padding-left: 10px"><p>”</p></td>
</tr>
</tbody>

<p>Burdened is acquired from consuming certain recipes that effects a survivor's <a href="./Encumbrance.md" class="wikilink" title="Encumbrance">Encumbrance</a> by temporarily lowering their maximum total carry weight by 3kg for 1 hour. This can be an especially dangerous Affliction to acquire when a survivor is already under the negative effects of low carrying capacity gained from low <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a>.</p>
<p>The following foods, when consumed, grant the Burdened Affliction as a side effect:</p>
<ul>
<li><img src="../assets/Prepper%27s_Pie_icon.png" loading="lazy" width="29" height="29" alt="Prepper&#39;s_Pie_icon.png" /> <a href="./Prepper%27s%20Pie.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper&#39;s Pie">Prepper's Pie</a></li>
<li><img src="../assets/Stalker%27s_Pie_icon.png" loading="lazy" width="29" height="29" alt="Stalker&#39;s_Pie_icon.png" /> <a href="./Stalker%27s%20Pie.md" class="wikilink" title="Stalker&#39;s Pie">Stalker's Pie</a></li>
</ul>
<p>The effect of this Affliction does not stack, and each application will not override the previous Affliction. Partially eating the previously mentioned foods only adds a partial amount of Burdened Recovery Time.</p>
