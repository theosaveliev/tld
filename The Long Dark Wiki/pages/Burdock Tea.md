**Burdock Tea** is a medicinal <a href="./First%20Aid.md" class="wikilink" title="First Aid">First Aid</a> drink brewed from <a href="./Prepared%20Burdock.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepared Burdock">Prepared Burdock</a> Roots.

It was added in the free update portion of the Part 3: Frontier Cooking for the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> expansion.

## Overview

It is a natural antibiotic drink, and like <a href="./Reishi%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Reishi Tea">Reishi Tea</a>, helps a player suffering from the following <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Afflictions">Afflictions</a>:

- <a href="./Food%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Food Poisoning">Food Poisoning</a> (1 dose and 10 hours Sleep, or 12-24 hours if untreated)
- <a href="./Infection.md" class="wikilink" title="Infection">Infection</a> (1 dose and 8 hours Sleep)
- <a href="./Dysentery.md" class="wikilink" title="Dysentery">Dysentery</a> (1 dose and 10 hours Sleep)
- <a href="./Intestinal%20Parasites.md" class="wikilink" title="Intestinal Parasites">Intestinal Parasites</a> (1 dose and 10 days Sleep on Stalker (20 days on Interloper))

Compared to Reishi Tea, Burdock Tea takes more effort to find materials for and then cook into tea, with <a href="./Burdock.md" class="wikilink" title="Burdock">Burdock</a> saplings being less plentiful to find compared to <a href="./Reishi%20Mushroom.md" class="wikilink" title="Reishi Mushrooms">Reishi Mushrooms</a>.
