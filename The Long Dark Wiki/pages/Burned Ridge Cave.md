> |map=Burned Ridge Cave Location.png}}'''Burned Ridge Cave''' is one of several caves dotted throughout [[Pleasant Valley]].
> == Overview ==
> Burned Ridge Cave lies just south-west of [[Skeeter's Ridge]] on the western most side of the region. It is not far from the plane [[Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)|crash site]], and lies just off a path down the mountain. This is a prime location to rest at when rescuing a survivor of a crash.  The cave is deep enough for cover from the wind, but also allows you to build a fire. There is also a makeshift bed inside the cave, which is useful if the player is not carrying a [[bedroll]].
> 
> [[Rose Hip|Rose hips]], and maybe a frozen [[Deer Carcass]], can be found nearby.
