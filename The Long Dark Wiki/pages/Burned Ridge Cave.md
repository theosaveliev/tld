<td><p>Provides some shelter|} <strong>Burned Ridge Cave</strong> is one of several caves dotted throughout <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley">Pleasant Valley</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Burned Ridge Cave lies just south-west of <a href="./Skeeter%27s%20Ridge.md" class="wikilink" title="Skeeter&#39;s Ridge">Skeeter's Ridge</a> on the western most side of the region. It is not far from the plane <a href="./Crash%20Site%20%28Pleasant%20Valley%29.md" class="wikilink" title="crash site">crash site</a>, and lies just off a path down the mountain. This is a prime location to rest at when rescuing a survivor of a crash. The cave is deep enough for cover from the wind, but also allows you to build a fire. There is also a makeshift bed inside the cave, which is useful if the player is not carrying a <a href="./Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="bedroll">bedroll</a>.</p>
<p><a href="./Rose%20Hip.md" class="wikilink" title="Rose hips">Rose hips</a>, and maybe a frozen <a href="./Deer%20Carcass.md" class="wikilink" title="Deer Carcass">Deer Carcass</a>, can be found nearby.</p>
