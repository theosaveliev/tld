<p><strong>Bushcraft Bow Blueprint Card</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> found in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a>. It is acquired from a trade from the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a> and unlocks the ability to craft a <a href="./Bushcraft%20Bow.md" class="wikilink" title="Bushcraft Bow">Bushcraft Bow</a> at a <a href="./Work%20Bench.md" class="wikilink" title="Work Bench">Work Bench</a>. This item only appears in the Trader's inventory after fully completing <a href="./Sutherland%27s%20Tale.md" class="wikilink" title="Sutherland&#39;s Tale">Sutherland's Tale</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Trade">Trade</h2>
<p>This items sometimes appears as a potential trade, and costs:</p>
<ul>
<li>10.00 kg of any raw animal or raw fish meat</li>
<li>5 <a href="./Cured%20Maple%20Sapling.md" class="wikilink" title="Cured Maple Sapling">Cured Maple Sapling</a></li>
<li>2 any cured hides</li>
</ul>
<p>At Trust level 6 the costs are reduced to:</p>
<ul>
<li>5.00 kg of any raw animal or raw fish meat</li>
<li>2 <a href="./Cured%20Maple%20Sapling.md" class="wikilink" title="Cured Maple Sapling">Cured Maple Sapling</a></li>
<li>2 any cured hides</li>
</ul>
