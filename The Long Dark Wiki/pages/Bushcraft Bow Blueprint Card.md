> '''Bushcraft Bow Blueprint Card''' is a [[Collectible]] found in [[Survival Mode]]. It is acquired from a trade from the [[Trader]] and unlocks the ability to craft a [[Bushcraft Bow]] at a [[Work Bench]]. This item only appears in the Trader's inventory after fully completing [[Sutherland's Tale]].
> ==Trade==
> This items sometimes appears as a potential trade, and costs:
> *10.00 kg of any raw animal or raw fish meat
> *5 [[Cured Maple Sapling]]
> *2 any cured hides
> At Trust level 6 the costs are reduced to:
> 
> * 5.00 kg of any raw animal or raw fish meat
> * 2 [[Cured Maple Sapling]]
> * 2 any cured hides
