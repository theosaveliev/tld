## Overview

Caffeine Pills are used to help eliminate [Fatigue](./Fatigue.md), and are only acquired from the [Trader](./Trader.md).

- Each box contains 5 Pills.
- Each pill gives 5 Calories (25 Calories a pack).
- Once consumed, immediately gives +25% [Fatigue](./Fatigue.md) and provides [Fatigue Reduced](./Fatigue%20Reduced.md) for 4 hours.
- As a consequence, these pills also give a [Headache](./Headache.md) for 3 hours once consumed.

The screen will pulse around the edges as though the player has consumed an [Emergency Stim](./Emergency%20Stim.md) (the pulsing is not nearly as fast however). There will also be a blue outline around the edges as though the player consumed a [Go! Energy Drink](./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md).

Each pill weighs 0.02 kg, for 0.10kg a pack.

1 box of 5 pills costs:

- 9 any harvestable plant
- 3.00 L [Water (Potable)](./Water%20%28Potable%29.md)
- 3 [Crow Feather](./Crow%20Feather.md)

## Location

Caffeine Pills are acquired by trading with the [Trader](./Trader.md), and are only unlocked after reaching 400 [Trust](./Trader.md#Trust).
