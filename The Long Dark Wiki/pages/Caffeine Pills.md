<p><strong>Caffeine Pills</strong> are a <a href="./First%20Aid.md" class="wikilink" title="First Aid">First Aid</a> item in <em><a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a></em>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Caffeine Pills are used to help eliminate <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a>, and are only acquired from the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li>Each box contains 5 Pills.</li>
<li>Each pill gives 5 Calories (25 Calories a pack).</li>
<li>Once consumed, immediately gives +25% <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a> and provides <a href="./Fatigue%20Reduced.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue Reduced">Fatigue Reduced</a> for 4 hours.</li>
<li>As a consequence, these pills also give a <a href="./Headache.md" class="wikilink" title="Headache">Headache</a> for 3 hours once consumed.</li>
</ul>
<p>The screen will pulse around the edges as though the player has consumed an <a href="./Emergency%20Stim.md" class="wikilink" title="Emergency Stim">Emergency Stim</a> (the pulsing is not nearly as fast however). There will also be a blue outline around the edges as though the player consumed a <a href="./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Go! Energy Drink">Go! Energy Drink</a>.</p>
<p>Each pill weighs 0.02 kg, for 0.10kg a pack.</p>
<p>1 box of 5 pills costs:</p>
<ul>
<li>9 any harvestable plant</li>
<li>3.00 L <a href="./Water%20%28Potable%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Water (Potable)">Water (Potable)</a></li>
<li>3 <a href="./Crow%20Feather.md" class="wikilink" title="Crow Feather">Crow Feather</a></li>
</ul>
<h2 id="Location">Location</h2>
<p>Caffeine Pills are acquired by trading with the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a>, and are only unlocked after reaching 400 <a href="./Trader.md#Trust" class="wikilink" title="Trust">Trust</a>.</p>
