# Caffeine Pills

> {{TFTFT}}{{Infobox item
> |name=|image=Caffeine Pills icon.png|release=2 December 2024|update=2.33|story=No|type=First Aid|equipable=No|stackable=Yes|calories=5|hydration=|vitaminc=|weight=0.10|decay-inside=|decay-outside=|actions=|actions_radial=|actions_ground=|examine=Pure Caffeine. The tablets help resist the accumulation and onset of Fatique.|key=GEAR_BottleCaffeine|renewable=Yes ([[Trader]] only)}}'''Caffeine Pills''' are a [[First Aid]] item in ''[[The Long Dark]]''.
> 
> ==Overview ==
> Caffeine Pills are used to help eliminate [[Fatigue]], and are only acquired from the [[Trader]].
> 
> *Each box contains 5 Pills.
> *Each pill gives 5 Calories (25 Calories a pack).
> *Once consumed, immediately gives +25% [[Fatigue]] and provides [[Fatigue Reduced]] for 4 hours.
> *As a consequence, these pills also give a [[Headache]] for 3 hours once consumed.
> 
> The screen will pulse around the edges as though the player has consumed an [[Emergency Stim]] (the pulsing is not nearly as fast however). There will also be a blue outline around the edges as though the player consumed a [[Go! Energy Drink]].
> 
> Each pill weighs 0.02 kg, for 0.10kg a pack.
> 
> 1 box of 5 pills costs:
> 
> * 9 any harvestable plant
> * 3.00 L [[Water (Potable)]]
> * 3 [[Crow Feather]]
> 
> ==Location==
> Caffeine Pills are acquired by trading with the [[Trader]], and are only unlocked after reaching 400 [[Trader#Trust|Trust]].
