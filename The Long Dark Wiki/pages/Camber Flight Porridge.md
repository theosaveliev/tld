> |renewable=No ([[Rose Hip]])}}'''Camber Flight Porridge''' is a food item in ''The Long Dark'', it is learned from a unique [[Recipe Cards|Recipe Card]] and is made with a [[Cooking Pot]].
> ==Overview==
> Camber Flight Porridge is a relatively simple and efficient unique recipe with good calorie density and a positive (if small) [[Condition]] restoring effect, comparable to [[Lily's Pancakes]], but uses [[Running River Oats]] instead of [[Old Mill Flour]]. [[Rose Hip|Rose Hips]] can be found abundantly on [[Great Bear Island]], [[Maple Syrup]] rarer but used in small amounts, making [[Pinnacle Peaches]] the main limiting ingredient. Acquiring the Recipe Card for Camber Flight Porridge requires traversing the long and cold Far Range Branch Line, Transfer Pass, and the cold, windy and exposed plains of Forsaken Airfield.
> 
> It's Recipe Card can be found inside the kitchen of the [[Main Hangar]] in [[Forsaken Airfield]].
