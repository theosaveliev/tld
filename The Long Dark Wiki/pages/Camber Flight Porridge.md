<p><strong>Camber Flight Porridge</strong> is a food item in <em>The Long Dark</em>, it is learned from a unique <a href="./Recipe%20Cards.md" class="wikilink" title="Recipe Card">Recipe Card</a> and is made with a <a href="./Cooking%20Pot.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Pot">Cooking Pot</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Camber Flight Porridge is a relatively simple and efficient unique recipe with good calorie density and a positive (if small) <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> restoring effect, comparable to <a href="./Lily%27s%20Pancakes.md" class="wikilink" title="Lily&#39;s Pancakes">Lily's Pancakes</a>, but uses <a href="./Running%20River%20Oats.md" class="wikilink" title="Running River Oats">Running River Oats</a> instead of <a href="./Old%20Mill%20Flour.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Mill Flour">Old Mill Flour</a>. <a href="./Rose%20Hip.md" class="wikilink" title="Rose Hips">Rose Hips</a> can be found abundantly on <a href="./Great%20Bear%20Island.md" class="wikilink" title="Great Bear Island">Great Bear Island</a>, <a href="./Maple%20Syrup.md" class="wikilink" title="Maple Syrup">Maple Syrup</a> rarer but used in small amounts, making <a href="./Pinnacle%20Peaches.md" class="wikilink" title="Pinnacle Peaches">Pinnacle Peaches</a> the main limiting ingredient. Acquiring the Recipe Card for Camber Flight Porridge requires traversing the long and cold Far Range Branch Line, Transfer Pass, and the cold, windy and exposed plains of Forsaken Airfield.</p>
<p>It's Recipe Card can be found inside the kitchen of the <a href="./Main%20Hangar.md" class="wikilink" title="Main Hangar">Main Hangar</a> in <a href="./Forsaken%20Airfield.md" class="wikilink" title="Forsaken Airfield">Forsaken Airfield</a>.</p>
