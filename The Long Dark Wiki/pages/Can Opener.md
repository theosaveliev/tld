A **Can Opener** is a tool primarily used for opening food cans.

## Overview

Can openers allows players to open <a href="./Cooking.md#Canned" class="wikilink" title="canned foods">canned foods</a> without losing between 10% and 30% of the food as spillage via smashing open. While either a <a href="./Hunting%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Knife">Knife</a> or <a href="./Hatchet.md" class="wikilink" title="Hatchet">Hatchet</a> will also allow the player to open a can without spillage, a Can Opener is only used to open cans. Using a Can Opener allows the player to preserve those tools' condition for other uses. Using a Can Opener also allows the canned food to be eaten and yield a <a href="./Recycled%20Can.md" class="wikilink" title="Recycled Can">Recycled Can</a> after consumption, whereas smashing the can open will not.

A Can Opener will degrade by **2%** each time it is used. The Can Opener will not degrade in any other circumstances.

A Can Opener can be harvested for 1 unit of <a href="./Scrap%20Metal.md" class="wikilink" title="Scrap Metal">Scrap Metal</a>.

It can also be repaired 1 unit of Scrap Metal. Repairing restores 25% condition and takes 45 minutes.
