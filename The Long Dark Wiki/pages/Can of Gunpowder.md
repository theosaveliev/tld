**Can of Gunpowder** is an item in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Overview

Gunpowder can be crafted at the <a href="./Ammunition%20Work%20Bench.md" class="wikilink" title="Ammunition Work Bench">Ammunition Work Bench</a> from <a href="./Dusting%20Sulfur.md" class="wikilink" title="Dusting Sulfur">Dusting Sulfur</a>, <a href="./Stump%20Remover.md" class="wikilink" title="Stump Remover">Stump Remover</a>, and two <a href="./Charcoal.md" class="wikilink" title="Charcoal">Charcoal</a>. It may also be acquired by harvesting Revolver or Rifle Ammunition (with higher chance of receiving them at higher Gunsmithing skill).

Gunpowder is used to craft <a href="./Revolver%20Ammunition.md" class="wikilink" title="Revolver Ammunition">Revolver Ammunition</a>, <a href="./Rifle%20Ammunition.md" class="wikilink" title="Rifle Ammunition">Rifle Ammunition</a>, or <a href="./Noisemaker.md" class="wikilink" title="Noisemakers">Noisemakers</a>; it can also be used as an accelerant for starting <a href="./Campfire.md" class="wikilink" title="campfires">campfires</a>. One full can is enough to craft 50 ammunition (regardless of type), 5 noisemakers, or to accelerate 5 fires.

## Products

## Locations

Cans of Gunpowder can spawn rarely in high loot quality locations of <a href="./Great%20Bear%20Island.md" class="wikilink" title="Great Bear Island">Great Bear Island</a>, such as <a href="./Cinder%20Hills%20Coal%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Cinder Hills Coal Mine">Cinder Hills Coal Mine</a>, <a href="./Maintenance%20Yard.md" class="wikilink" title="Maintenance Yard">Maintenance Yard</a>, or <a href="./Skeeter%27s%20Ridge.md" class="wikilink" title="Skeeter&#39;s Ridge">Skeeter's Ridge</a> basement. They can also be found in <a href="./Prepper%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper Caches">Prepper Caches</a> of any type and inside of <a href="./Safe.md" class="wikilink" title="Safes">Safes</a>.

### Spawnpoints

A large amount of pre-placed Gunpowder cans are located within <a href="./Blackrock%20Prison.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Prison">Blackrock Prison</a>, namely 2 can spawn on the catwalks/roofs leading from guard barracks to the infirmary, 1 is almost always located on the workbench and 1 is inside Cellblock C inside the guardroom near the cells. A large amount of <a href="./Dusting%20Sulfur.md" class="wikilink" title="Dusting Sulfur">Dusting Sulfur</a> and <a href="./Stump%20Remover.md" class="wikilink" title="Stump Remover">Stump Remover</a> are also scattered throughout the prison meaning even more gunpowder can be crafted.

At least 1 Gunpowder can will be located inside <a href="./Last%20Prospect.md" class="wikilink" title="Last Prospect">Last Prospect</a> mine, usually located at level 2 on the bottom of the collapsed wooden stairs in the main room.

## Trivia

- <a href="wikipedia:gunpowder" class="wikilink" title="Gunpowder">Gunpowder</a> (black powder) consists of a mixture of <a href="wikipedia:sulfur" class="wikilink" title="sulfur">sulfur</a> (S), <a href="wikipedia:charcoal" class="wikilink" title="charcoal">charcoal</a> (C), and <a href="wikipedia:potassium_nitrate" class="wikilink" title="potassium nitrate">potassium nitrate</a> (<a href="wikipedia:saltpeter" class="wikilink" title="saltpeter">saltpeter</a>, KNO<sub>3</sub>). The sulfur and charcoal act as fuels while the saltpeter is an <a href="wikipedia:oxidizer" class="wikilink" title="oxidizer">oxidizer</a>.
- The in-game recipe actually creates black powder (considering ingredients), which in real life would be almost useless to reload any modern rounds due to much slower combustion and therefore lesser kinetic energy of bullets. Using ammunition crafted in that way could result in jamming of the barrel. Black powder also leaves a lot more residue in the firearm, causing the need to clean it more often.
- In previous versions, a glitch was present where reloading save files or exiting/entering buildings would refill dropped cans of gunpowder to their maximum of 0.5kg.
- The Errant Pilgrim update introduced <a href="./Gunsmithing.md" class="wikilink" title="Gunsmithing">Gunsmithing</a>.
