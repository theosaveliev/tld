<td><p>|}<strong>Canned Corn</strong> is a packaged <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> which recovers hydration by 5% and provides 295 calories.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Consuming Canned Corn restores a small amount of calories and hydration, with relatively low energy density making it heavy for the calories it provides. It may also be warmed by a <a href="./Fire.md" class="wikilink" title="Fire">Fire</a> to grant the benefit <a href="./Warming%20Up.md" class="wikilink" title="Warming Up">Warming Up</a>. Canned Corn and <a href="./Canned%20Ham.md" class="wikilink" title="Canned Ham">Canned Ham</a> can only be found after entering the Far Territory, though Bunkers which contain them can be found in <a href="./Region.md" class="wikilink" title="Lower Great Bear">Lower Great Bear</a> with the <a href="./Handheld%20Shortwave%20Radio.md" class="wikilink" title="Handheld Shortwave">Handheld Shortwave</a>:</p>
<ul>
<li>The 4 <a href="./Signal%20Void.md" class="wikilink" title="Signal Void">Signal Void</a> bunkers
<ul>
<li><a href="./Bunker%20Alpha.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Alpha">Bunker Alpha</a> (2 cans)</li>
<li><a href="./Bunker%20Beta.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Beta">Bunker Beta</a> (1-3 cans)</li>
<li><a href="./Bunker%20Gamma.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Gamma">Bunker Gamma</a> (2 cans)</li>
<li><a href="./Bunker%20Omega.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Omega">Bunker Omega</a> (5 cans)</li>
</ul></li>
<li>The top floor of the <a href="./Concentrator.md" class="wikilink" title="Concentrator">Concentrator</a> of the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a> (3 cans)
<ul>
<li>Level 2 of the <a href="./Langston%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Langston Mine">Langston Mine</a> in the storage area</li>
<li>Level 3: On a Corpse in a gas filled section of the mine leading to the open pit</li>
</ul></li>
<li>Occasionally in <a href="./Supply%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Supply Cache">Supply Caches</a> (except on <a href="./Interloper.md" class="wikilink" title="Interloper">Interloper</a>)</li>
<li>Inside the "food" themed <a href="./Prepper%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper Cache">Prepper Cache</a> (except on Interloper)</li>
<li>Traded from the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a></li>
</ul>
<p>Like other canned foods, Canned Corn may be opened with a <a href="./Can%20Opener.md" class="wikilink" title="Can Opener">Can Opener</a> or <a href="./Tools.md" class="wikilink" title="bladed tool">bladed tool</a>, or smashed open (with no tool) but spilling some of its contents. If opened with a tool, a <a href="./Recycled%20Can.md" class="wikilink" title="Recycled Can">Recycled Can</a> is received after consuming the contents.</p>
