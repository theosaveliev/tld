> 
> ==Overview ==
> Consuming Canned Corn restores a small amount of calories and hydration, with relatively low energy density making it heavy for the calories it provides. It may also be warmed by a [[Fire]] to grant the benefit [[Warming Up]]. Canned Corn and [[Canned Ham]] can only be found after entering the Far Territory, though Bunkers which contain them can be found in [[Region|Lower Great Bear]] with the [[Handheld Shortwave Radio|Handheld Shortwave]]:
> *The 4 [[Signal Void]] bunkers
> **[[Bunker Alpha]] (2 cans)
> **[[Bunker Beta]] (1-3 cans)
> **[[Bunker Gamma]] (2 cans)
> **[[Bunker Omega]] (5 cans)
> *The top floor of the [[Concentrator]] of the [[Zone of Contamination]] (3 cans)
> **Level 2 of the [[Langston Mine]] in the storage area
> **Level 3: On a Corpse in a gas filled section of the mine leading to the open pit
> *Occasionally in [[Supply Cache]]s (except on [[Interloper]])
> *Inside the "food" themed [[Prepper Cache]] (except on Interloper)
> *Traded from the [[Trader]]
> Like other canned foods, Canned Corn may be opened with a [[Can Opener]] or [[Tools|bladed tool]], or smashed open (with no tool) but spilling some of its contents. If opened with a tool, a [[Recycled Can]] is received after consuming the contents.
