**Canyon** is a location in [Anna Island -- Track Section 4](./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%204.md).

## Overview

A small track of train track runs through a depression in the earth. There is not much of note about this location, save for the nearby [Railyard (Track Section 4)](./Railyard%20%28Track%20Section%204%29.md) to the east that leads to [Anna Island -- Track Section 6](./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%206.md) or further east leading to [Anna Island -- Track Section 5](./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%205.md).

Heading further west will cross through the [Pond (Track Section 4)](./Pond%20%28Track%20Section%204%29.md) and a [Repair Depot](./Repair%20Depot.md) at the western Railyard.
