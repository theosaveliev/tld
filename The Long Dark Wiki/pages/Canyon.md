<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>A small track of train track runs through a depression in the earth. There is not much of note about this location, save for the nearby <a href="./Railyard%20%28Track%20Section%204%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Railyard (Track Section 4)">Railyard (Track Section 4)</a> to the east that leads to <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%206.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 6">Anna Island -- Track Section 6</a> or further east leading to <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%205.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 5">Anna Island -- Track Section 5</a>.</p>
<p>Heading further west will cross through the <a href="./Pond%20%28Track%20Section%204%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Pond (Track Section 4)">Pond (Track Section 4)</a> and a <a href="./Repair%20Depot.md" class="wikilink" title="Repair Depot">Repair Depot</a> at the western Railyard.</p>
