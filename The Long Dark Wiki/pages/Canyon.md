# Canyon

> {{IronRoad}}{{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Canyon.jpg|imagecaption=|region=Anna Island -- Track Section 4|location=The middle of the region, closer to the eastern Railway|sublocations=|description=|type=|bed=|firebox=|forge=|workbench=|shelter=|missions=|characters=|map=}}'''Canyon''' is a location in [[Anna Island -- Track Section 4]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> A small track of train track runs through a depression in the earth. There is not much of note about this location, save for the nearby [[Railyard (Track Section 4)]] to the east that leads to [[Anna Island -- Track Section 6]] or further east leading to [[Anna Island -- Track Section 5]].
> 
> Heading further west will cross through the [[Pond (Track Section 4)]] and a [[Repair Depot]] at the western Railyard.
