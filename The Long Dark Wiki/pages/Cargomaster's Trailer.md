<p><strong>Cargomaster's Trailer</strong> is a small trailer just near the "Y" section of the runway of the airfield, north of the <a href="./Control%20Tower.md" class="wikilink" title="Control Tower">Control Tower</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Not much can be found in the surroundings, except for a large pile of pallets, which can be broken down to yield a large amount of <a href="./Reclaimed%20Wood.md" class="wikilink" title="Reclaimed Wood">Reclaimed Wood</a> if the player is carrying a hatchet. However if one walks down the runway towards the lake on the right there is a shed that may have a heavy hammer. There may also be a backpack inside one of the metal tubes near the trailer.</p>
<p>Inside the trailer there are 3 bunk beds, a few shelves, a desk, and a set of lockers. The lockers have a <a href="./Paper.md" class="wikilink" title="note someone left behind">note someone left behind</a> underneath them. A Revolver may spawn on the desk inside the trailer.</p>
