> '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a small trailer just near the "Y" section of the runway of the airfield, north of the [[Control Tower]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> Not much can be found in the surroundings, except for a large pile of pallets, which can be broken down to yield a large amount of [[Reclaimed Wood]] if the player is carrying a hatchet. However if one walks down the runway towards the lake on the right there is a shed that may have a heavy hammer. There may also be a backpack inside one of the metal tubes near the trailer.
> 
> Inside the trailer there are 3 bunk beds, a few shelves, a desk, and a set of lockers. The lockers have a [[Paper|note someone left behind]] underneath them. A Revolver may spawn on the desk inside the trailer.
