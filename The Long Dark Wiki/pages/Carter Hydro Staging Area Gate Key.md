**Carter Hydro Staging Area Gate Key** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode. It is found on a desk of the trailers near <a href="./Carter%20Hydro%20Dam.md" class="wikilink" title="Carter Hydro Dam">Carter Hydro Dam</a>, next to the <a href="./Breyerhouse%20Crew%20Winter%20Warning.md" class="wikilink" title="Breyerhouse Crew Winter Warning">Breyerhouse Crew Winter Warning</a> note. It is needed to unlock the gate to enter the Dam.
