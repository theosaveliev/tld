## Overview

It is a bridge on the main mountain road leading north to [Placid Notch](./Placid%20Notch.md) or south to [Lotte's Rest](./Lotte%27s%20Rest.md), with a nearby frozen waterfall just under the bridge. The frozen waterfall under the bridge is actually [Route One Waterfall](./Route%20One%20Waterfall.md), and can be seen from far away even in the valley below, acting as a great landmark for the region.

Entering the river upstream past a large boulder and turning left will take the player down a [Rock Climb](./Rock%20Climb.md) and into a cave system. A crossroads is found shortly upon descending the cave:

- The right path leads to a crossroads leading to either [Prayerful Window](./Prayerful%20Window.md) to the north east of the bridge or [Idle Hideout](./Idle%20Hideout.md) at the northeastern most section of the lower valley.
- The forward path leads to an exit into the middle section of the valley, slightly to the north just below Cascading Gap.
