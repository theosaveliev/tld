<td><p>Has no shelter|} <strong>Cascading Gap</strong> is a location in <a href="./Sundered%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Sundered Pass">Sundered Pass</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>It is a bridge on the main mountain road leading north to <a href="./Placid%20Notch.md" class="wikilink" title="Placid Notch">Placid Notch</a> or south to <a href="./Lotte%27s%20Rest.md" class="wikilink" title="Lotte&#39;s Rest">Lotte's Rest</a>, with a nearby frozen waterfall just under the bridge. The frozen waterfall under the bridge is actually <a href="./Route%20One%20Waterfall.md" class="wikilink" title="Route One Waterfall">Route One Waterfall</a>, and can be seen from far away even in the valley below, acting as a great landmark for the region.</p>
<p>Entering the river upstream past a large boulder and turning left will take the player down a <a href="./Rock%20Climb.md" class="wikilink" title="Rock Climb">Rock Climb</a> and into a cave system. A crossroads is found shortly upon descending the cave:</p>
<ul>
<li>The right path leads to a crossroads leading to either <a href="./Prayerful%20Window.md" class="wikilink" title="Prayerful Window">Prayerful Window</a> to the north east of the bridge or <a href="./Idle%20Hideout.md" class="wikilink" title="Idle Hideout">Idle Hideout</a> at the northeastern most section of the lower valley.</li>
<li>The forward path leads to an exit into the middle section of the valley, slightly to the north just below Cascading Gap.</li>
</ul>
