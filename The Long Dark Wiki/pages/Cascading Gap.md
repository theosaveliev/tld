# Cascading Gap

> {{Infobox/Location
> |title =
> |image =Cascading Gap.jpg
> |region = Sundered Pass
> |shelter = no
> |map=Cascading Gap Location.png}}'''Cascading Gap''' is a location in [[Sundered Pass]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> It is a bridge on the main mountain road leading north to [[Placid Notch]] or south to [[Lotte's Rest]], with a nearby frozen waterfall just under the bridge. The frozen waterfall under the bridge is actually [[Route One Waterfall]], and can be seen from far away even in the valley below, acting as a great landmark for the region.
> 
> Entering the river upstream past a large boulder and turning left will take the player down a [[Rock Climb]] and into a cave system. A crossroads is found shortly upon descending the cave:
> 
> * The right path leads to a crossroads leading to either [[Prayerful Window]] to the north east of the bridge or [[Idle Hideout]] at the northeastern most section of the lower valley.
> * The forward path leads to an exit into the middle section of the valley, slightly to the north just below  Cascading Gap.
