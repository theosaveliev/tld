> '''Cave''' is a named [[Warm Cave]] in [[Mountain Town]]. It is located just behind the [[Radio Tower]].
> 
> ==Overview==
> Not much can be found inside aside from large amounts of [[Coal]] and a [[Frozen Corpse]], but the cave may make a decent place to sleep if travelling to [[Mackenzie's Crashed Plane]]. The interior is a constant temperature and is warm enough to keep the player character from freezing, if they have proper warm clothing.
