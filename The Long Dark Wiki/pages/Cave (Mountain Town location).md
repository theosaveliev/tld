<p><strong>Cave</strong> is a named <a href="./Warm%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Warm Cave">Warm Cave</a> in <a href="./Mountain%20Town.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountain Town">Mountain Town</a>. It is located just behind the <a href="./Radio%20Tower.md" class="wikilink" title="Radio Tower">Radio Tower</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Not much can be found inside aside from large amounts of <a href="./Coal.md" class="wikilink" title="Coal">Coal</a> and a <a href="./Frozen%20Corpse.md" class="wikilink" title="Frozen Corpse">Frozen Corpse</a>, but the cave may make a decent place to sleep if travelling to <a href="./Mackenzie%27s%20Crashed%20Plane.md" class="wikilink" title="Mackenzie&#39;s Crashed Plane">Mackenzie's Crashed Plane</a>. The interior is a constant temperature and is warm enough to keep the player character from freezing, if they have proper warm clothing.</p>
