> 
> '''Cave''' is a location in [[Timberwolf Mountain]], north of [[Engine (East)]]. 
> 
> == Overview ==
> Cave is not a cave in the usual nomenclature; rather it is a quite open area shaded by a low height rock ceiling. A backpack and burned out campfire can be found in the center, with a [[Survive the Outdoors!]] book almost always spawning in the backpack.
> 
> To the west is a dead end that leads to the [[Deer Clearing]] ([[Mountaineering Rope]] must first be deployed).
> 
> To the east is an open field that leads to a [[Prepper Cache]].
> 
> To the south is [[Engine (East)]] and a cave system the leads to [[Engine (West)]].{{Timberwolf Mountain}}
