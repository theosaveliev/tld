**Centurion** is an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after surviving for one hundred whole days in a single [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) game. It is most easily done on Pilgrim.

## Game history

This achievement was introduced on 1 April 2015 in [version 0.212](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Changelog.md#0.212).
