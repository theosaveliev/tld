**Chocolate Bars** were temporarily available during the [4 Days of Night Events](./4%20Days%20of%20Night%20Event.md) that occurred from 2016 - 2020. They are considered [Cut/Discontinued Content](./Cut%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20Content.md), since they cannot be encountered anymore.

These bars in the Halloween Event "[4 Days of Night](./4%20Days%20of%20Night%20Event.md)" (4DoN) provided 2500 calories per unit, the maximum calorie store amount. They are far more plentiful than average loot rate in this game mode, and are often found in bowls in front of houses (along with [Granola Bar](./Granola%20Bar.md) and [Energy Bar](./Energy%20Bar.md)) as Trick or Treat offerings. This 2500 calorie variant is only found in this game mode.
