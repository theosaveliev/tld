<td><p>Has an interior|} The <strong>Co-op</strong> is a location found in <a href="./Perseverance%20Mills.md" class="wikilink" title="Perseverance Mills">Perseverance Mills</a>, and serves as one of the few explorable locations of the town.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>A store, post office, and local co-op for the unfortunate, it is located in the middle of town close to the Medical Clinic. The stores suffers from a similar fate as the <a href="./Orca%20Gas%20Station.md" class="wikilink" title="Orca Gas Station">Orca Gas Station</a> in <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a>, with its gas pumps having run dry, its ATMs being out of cash, and regular supply shipments being rare.</p>
<p>The outside has a single gas pump. The inside has a post office to the back left, a freezer to the back right, and a register to the right. Several items can be found inside still on the shelves, such as food, first aid, clothing, and other items. A second entrance to the building can be found in the back next to the bathroom.</p>
<p>Several items not found anywhere else on Great Bear Island are found inside the store, such as unique company brand cardboard cutouts for fishing lures and various foods and medicines.</p>
