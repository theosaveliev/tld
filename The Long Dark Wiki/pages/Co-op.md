# Co\-op

> {{Infobox/Location|image=No picture.png|region=Perseverance Mills|location=|description=The local co-op of Perseverance Mills.|type=Commercial|firebox=|bed=|shelter=yes|mapfile=|mapx=|mapy=|mappin=|mapcaption=}}The '''Co-op''' is a location found in [[Perseverance Mills]], and serves as one of the few explorable locations of the town.
> 
> == Overview ==
> A store, post office, and local co-op for the unfortunate, it is located in the middle of town close to the Medical Clinic. The stores suffers from a similar fate as the [[Orca Gas Station]] in [[Milton]], with its gas pumps having run dry, its ATMs being out of cash, and regular supply shipments being rare.
> 
> The outside has a single gas pump. The inside has a post office to the back left, a freezer to the back right, and a register to the right. Several items can be found inside still on the shelves, such as food, first aid, clothing, and other items. A second entrance to the building can be found in the back next to the bathroom.
> 
> Several items not found anywhere else on Great Bear Island are found inside the store, such as unique company brand cardboard cutouts for fishing lures and various foods and medicines.
>

> 
> === [[Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things]] ===
> [[Astrid Greenwood]] arrives at the Co-op while searching for clues as to the town's disappearing populace, and briefly explores the store.
