# Coast Mountain Road

> 
> '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is an ruined road avalanche in [[Bleak Inlet]], located near the center of the map.
> 
> == Overview ==
> While the road at one point offered a connection to the upper mountain area that leads to [[Echo One Radio Tower]], it has long been buried in rubble by an earthquake. The only way to navigate around it is via [[Rope Climbing]] near [[Pensive Lookout]]. The rope climb must first be set up, which requires the player navigate to [[The Ravine]] and travel down the [[Ravine Basin]] to reach the upper area, then travel to Pensive Lookout to deploy the rope.
> 
> [[Deer]] and [[Timberwolf|timberwolves]] both frequent the area, and the entrance to the [[Secret Mountain Path]] is located a short walk to the southwest.
