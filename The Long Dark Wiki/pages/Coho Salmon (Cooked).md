**Coho Salmon (Cooked)** is a cooked <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in *The Long Dark*.

## Overview

At 0% condition food disappears if inside of a container, but not outside of containers or in the player's inventory.

Using salmon on a lit <a href="./Fire.md" class="wikilink" title="fire">fire</a> yields Cooked Salmon. Cooked fish increases its condition by 50%, decays 4 times slower than raw meat, and has a reduced chance of <a href="./Food%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Food Poisoning">Food Poisoning</a> over raw meat, while decreasing its weight by 35%. Cooked salmon's energy density is 455 calories-per-kilogram. Cooking fish also yields a small amount of <a href="./Lantern%20Fuel.md" class="wikilink" title="Lantern Fuel">Lantern Fuel</a>.

Cooking Salmon improves the <a href="./Skills.md#Cooking" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Skill">Cooking Skill</a> and requires a lit fire and 10 minutes. Using a <a href="./Cooking%20Pot.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Pot">Cooking Pot</a> reduces cooking time by 25%. Cooked food on an active fire for too long will burn (twice its 'Ready' cooking time), destroying the item and damaging a Cooking Pot if used.

## Trivia

- Cooked Coho Salmons are currently one of the only food items in the game that can yield more than 2500 kcal and therefore satiate a starving player completely (the other being <a href="./Burbot%20%28Cooked%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Burbot">Burbot</a>). But it requires player's Cooking Skill and <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a> Skill to be at Level 5.
