> '''Commuter's Lament''' is a location in the south-easternmost corner of [[Coastal Highway]]. Its defining feature is the decaying roadway partly buried by rockfall debris.
> 
> == Overview ==
> Commuter's Lament leads to [[Crumbling Highway]].
> 
> A [[Car]], [[Green Birch Sapling]], and [[Rose Hip]] bushes can be found in the area. The Car sometimes has a [[Hunting Rifle]] inside. 
> 
> Excluding the Car, there are no indoor shelters in this location. The nearest indoor location in the region can be found in the [[Waterfront Cottages]].
