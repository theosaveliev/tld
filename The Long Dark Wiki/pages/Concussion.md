**Concussion** is a unique <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>* only encountered in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> during the mission <a href="./Fallen%20Star.md" class="wikilink" title="Fallen Star">Fallen Star</a> in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a>. It shares the same icon as the <a href="./Headache.md" class="wikilink" title="Headache">Headache</a> Affliction.

## Overview

Concussion affects the <a href="./Crash%20Survivor.md" class="wikilink" title="Crash Survivors">Crash Survivors</a> Franklin and Bennet in the <a href="./Community%20Hall.md" class="wikilink" title="Community Hall">Community Hall</a>.

## Treatment

Despite being a negative Affliction, Astrid diagnoses each survivor and concludes that Franklin and Bennet do not need any further medical help. She possibly assumes that the Affliction will heal on its own without needing any First Aid.
