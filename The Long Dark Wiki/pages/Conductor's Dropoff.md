<p><strong>Conductor's Dropoff</strong> is a location west of the <a href="./Vacant%20Depot.md" class="wikilink" title="Vacant Depot">Vacant Depot</a> in the <a href="./Transfer%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Transfer Pass">Transfer Pass</a> region. It can be found by climbing up a hill near some frozen ponds and is easily identifiable by the dirty grey snow, but the location offers nothing but a scenic vista of the eastern unplayable section of the map. Despite the location name, there are no corpses or loot dropped off at the top or bottom of the cliff. The location has no notable loot, save for some <a href="./Stone.md" class="wikilink" title="Stones">Stones</a> and a <a href="./Ptarmigan.md" class="wikilink" title="Ptarmigan">Ptarmigan</a> Nest that spawns nearby.</p>
