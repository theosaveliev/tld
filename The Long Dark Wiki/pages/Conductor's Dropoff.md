|  | Has no shelter\|} / **Conductor's Dropoff** is a location west of the [Vacant Depot](./Vacant%20Depot.md) in the [Transfer Pass](./Transfer%20Pass.md) region. It can be found by climbing up a hill near some frozen ponds and is easily identifiable by the dirty grey snow, but the location offers nothing but a scenic vista of the eastern unplayable section of the map. Despite the location name, there are no corpses or loot dropped off at the top or bottom of the cliff.
