The **Constable's Office** is a location found in [Perseverance Mills](./Perseverance%20Mills.md), and serves as one of the few explorable locations of the town. It belongs to Constable Pete, the sole law enforcement officer of the town.

## Overview

2 jail cells, a bathroom, and a bed are found inside.

a small kitchen can be found with a 6 slot [Stove](./Stove.md).
