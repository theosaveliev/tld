The **Contraband Storage** Locker is a unique <a href="./Locker.md" class="wikilink" title="Locker">Locker</a> found at the gate entrance to <a href="./Blackrock%20Prison.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Prison">Blackrock Prison</a> during <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence</a>.

## Overview

It serves as a quick way to store any items found by the player while exploring the outskirts of the region during the episode, such as food, clothing, and weapons, in order to prevent them from being permanently taken by the <a href="./Convicts.md" class="wikilink" title="Convicts">Convicts</a> when returning to the Prison to progress the narrative.

It is different from a normal Locker, containing 10.00 kg more storage capacity than regular Lockers, and even appears in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a>.**Lua execution error in Module:Navbox function navbox**
