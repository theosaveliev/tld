\| style="vertical-align:bottom; font-size:3em; color:#5d5980; padding-left:10px" \|” \|}**Convict Caches** is a side mission in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a>.

## Overview

To start the mission, the player must find a note at <a href="./Cook%27s%20Farm.md" class="wikilink" title="Cook&#39;s Farm">Cook's Farm</a> in <a href="./Blackrock%20Mountain.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Mountain">Blackrock Mountain</a> region. This will add 4 points of interest on the survivors map.

## Tasks

- Find All the Convict´s hidden caches
  - Convict cache 1
  - Convict cache 2
  - Convict cache 3
  - Convict cache 4

## Cache locations/routes

- Cache 1 is located behind a stone in the "roots" of a fallen tree where the cliff face and the mountain meet above the <a href="./Old%20Substation.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Substation">Old Substation</a>. Getting there is very easy, start at the <a href="./Old%20Substation.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Substation">Old Substation</a> and go towards the Mountain then along the bottom of it to the left. In survival a <a href="./Prepper%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper cache">Prepper cache</a> may spawn in this location instead.

<!-- -->

- Cache 2 is located inside the mountain pass which leads to fools corner, in survival a rope to the creek would be there, the entrance to this pass is just after the No road cave (<a href="./Blackrock%20Rumours.md" class="wikilink" title="aggressive wolf cave">aggressive wolf cave</a>) and is actually visible on the in-game map. The cache itself is hidden behind a jagged rock on the right side of the pass.

<!-- -->

- Cache 3 is located at the very top of the mountain above <a href="./Foreman%27s%20Clearcut.md" class="wikilink" title="Foreman&#39;s Clearcut">Foreman's Clearcut</a>. To reach it by follow the road, beware of a pack of timberwolves patrols here, and then take the first left steep path up the mountain. Keep more to the right of this path and at the very top on a big flat rock outcropping (more in the middle of the mountain than the edge), the cache is hidden behind one of the trees.

<!-- -->

- Cache 4 is located at the foot of the second electrical power pole that is on the right side of the road (when going from <a href="./Blackrock%20Prison.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Prison">Blackrock Prison</a>), it´s the one visible but unreachable from the road. One way of reaching it is by going through the "tunnel/pass" and then backtracking a long way around it´s outside face, beware of a pack of timberwolves near the farm. The other way is to climb up to the previous Power pole, then climbing down a mountaineering rope (survivor must not be over-encumbered) and taking the first left up a steep path. After reaching the pole the cache should be easily visible on the right.
