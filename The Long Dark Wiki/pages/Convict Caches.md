## Overview

To start the mission, the player must find a note at [Cook's Farm](./Cook%27s%20Farm.md) in [Blackrock Mountain](./Blackrock%20Mountain.md) region. This will add 4 points of interest on the survivors map.

## Tasks

- Find All the Convict´s hidden caches
  - Convict cache 1
  - Convict cache 2
  - Convict cache 3
  - Convict cache 4

## Cache locations/routes

- Cache 1 is located behind a stone in the "roots" of a fallen tree where the cliff face and the mountain meet above the [Old Substation](./Old%20Substation.md). Getting there is very easy, start at the [Old Substation](./Old%20Substation.md) and go towards the Mountain then along the bottom of it to the left. In survival a [Prepper cache](./Prepper%20Cache.md) may spawn in this location instead.

- Cache 2 is located inside the mountain pass which leads to fools corner, in survival a rope to the creek would be there, the entrance to this pass is just after the No road cave ([aggressive wolf cave](./Blackrock%20Rumours.md)) and is actually visible on the in-game map. The cache itself is hidden behind a jagged rock on the right side of the pass.

- Cache 3 is located at the very top of the mountain above [Foreman's Clearcut](./Foreman%27s%20Clearcut.md). To reach it by follow the road, beware of a pack of timberwolves patrols here, and then take the first left steep path up the mountain. Keep more to the right of this path and at the very top on a big flat rock outcropping (more in the middle of the mountain than the edge), the cache is hidden behind one of the trees.

- Cache 4 is located at the foot of the second electrical power pole that is on the right side of the road (when going from [Blackrock Prison](./Blackrock%20Prison.md)), it´s the one visible but unreachable from the road. One way of reaching it is by going through the "tunnel/pass" and then backtracking a long way around it´s outside face, beware of a pack of timberwolves near the farm. The other way is to climb up to the previous Power pole, then climbing down a mountaineering rope (survivor must not be over-encumbered) and taking the first left up a steep path. After reaching the pole the cache should be easily visible on the right.

## Rewards

- Cache 1 contains:
  - 5x [Revolver Ammunition](./Revolver%20Ammunition.md)
  - 5x [Rifle Ammunition](./Rifle%20Ammunition.md)
  - 2x [Flare Shell](./Flare%20Shell.md)
  - Worn [Balaclava](./Balaclava.md)

- Cache 2 contains:
  - 10x [Revolver Ammunition](./Revolver%20Ammunition.md)
  - 2x [Accelerant](./Accelerant.md)
  - [Energy Bar](./Energy%20Bar.md)
  - ~23% condition firestarter

- Cache 3 contains:
  - 5x [Rifle Ammunition](./Rifle%20Ammunition.md)
  - 2x [Flare Shell](./Flare%20Shell.md)
  - 1x [Emergency Stim](./Emergency%20Stim.md)
  - 1x [Maple Syrup](./Maple%20Syrup.md)

- Cache 4 contains:
  - ~78% condition [Revolver](./Revolver.md)
  - 10x [Revolver Ammunition](./Revolver%20Ammunition.md)
  - Ragged [Climbing Socks](./Climbing%20Socks.md)
