**Cooked Potato** is a cooked <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Food And Drink">Food And Drink</a> item in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Overview

**Cooked Potato** is obtained by placing a <a href="./Potato.md" class="wikilink" title="Potato">Potato</a> upon a cooking slot of a lit <a href="./Fire.md" class="wikilink" title="fire">fire</a> for 60 minutes, and increases its condition by 50%. Collecting a finished one improves the <a href="./Cooking.md" class="wikilink" title="cooking skill">cooking skill</a>.

Using a <a href="./Cooking%20Pot.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Pot">Cooking Pot</a> or <a href="./Cooking%20Skillet.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Skillet">Cooking Skillet</a> reduces cooking time by 25% and increases burn time by 50%.

Cooked food left on an active fire for too long will burn, after another 80% of "Ready" time (for the potato after 40 minutes), destroying the item and damaging a Cooking Pot/Skillet if used.
