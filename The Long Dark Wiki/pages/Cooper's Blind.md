Has an interior **Cooper's Blind** is a location found in [Perseverance Mills](./Perseverance%20Mills.md). The bar is owned and run by [Cooper](./Cooper.md), and serves as one of the few explorable locations of the town.

<span id="overview"></span><span id="Overview"></span>

## Overview

The bar contains an outside patio on the ground and first floor. There are 2 entrances to the bar, in front of the building and the upper back side. A 3rd entrance leads to the bar/kitchen area itself, but cannot be entered.

The first floor has many seating booths, a bath room, a fireplace with a large wooden bear carving, a piano, and the bar. An ATM and 2 dart boards can also be found near the front entrance. A set of stairs leads to the upper area overlooking the main floor, and leads to an office and a storage room.

The bar is notable for serving Cooper's Whiskey and several beer bottles.

### [Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things](./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md)

[Astrid Greenwood](./Astrid%20Greenwood.md) arrives at the bar while searching for the town's local doctor, Dr. Larsen. She meets Cooper and several of the town's residents inside, currently waiting out a quarantine placed by Dr. Larsen. Cooper updates Astrid on the town's current problems before giving her a key to the nearby Medical Clinic.

Astrid eventually returns to the bar after finding out the truth regarding the several missing people, and warns everyone that they need to evacuate. before they can, [Mathis](./Mathis.md) and his Convicts show up and hold everyone hostage in the bar, eventually threatening to burn it down. The residents of the town eventually flee the town, but the bar remains as one of the few buildings not burned down in the town.
