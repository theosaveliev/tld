> == Overview ==
> Cougar Claws are only acquired after killing a very rare but dangerous [[Cougar]]. Each [[Cougar Carcass]] contains a  single Claw (note that it does not appear as a harvesting option, it is automatically obtained after harvesting the [[Fresh Cougar Hide]]). The Claw currently has one use; being used as a material to be crafted into a Cougar Claw Knife.
