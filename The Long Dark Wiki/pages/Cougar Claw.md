<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Cougar Claws are only acquired after killing a very rare but dangerous <a href="./Cougar.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougar">Cougar</a>. Each <a href="./Cougar%20Carcass.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougar Carcass">Cougar Carcass</a> contains a single Claw (note that it does not appear as a harvesting option, it is automatically obtained after harvesting the <a href="./Fresh%20Cougar%20Hide.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Cougar Hide">Fresh Cougar Hide</a>). The Claw currently has one use; being used as a material to be crafted into a Cougar Claw Knife.</p>
