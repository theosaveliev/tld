<p><strong>Cougar Meat (Raw)</strong> is a raw <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in <em>The Long Dark</em>. It can be obtained by killing the <a href="./Cougar.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougar">Cougar</a>, though this requires the player to be well prepared, as the Cougar is a fierce and dangerous animal to hunt.</p>
