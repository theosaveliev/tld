<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>A <strong>Cougar Quarter</strong> is a section harvested from a <a href="./Cougar.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougar">Cougar</a>. You can obtain Cougar quarters by <em>quartering</em> a <a href="./Cougar%20Carcass.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougar Carcass">Cougar Carcass</a>. To quarter an animal, you have to go into the harvest menu, then select the quartering action. For a Cougar, this will always take 2 hours. You can only quarter a Cougar if its less than 50% frozen.</p>
<p>After quartering, the carcass will despawn, leaving behind 2 quarters, as well as its <a href="./Fresh%20Gut.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Gut">Fresh Gut</a> and <a href="./Fresh%20Cougar%20Hide.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Cougar Hide">Fresh Cougar Hide</a>. It will also leave behind the Cougar's legs and blood, but this cannot be picked up, and it will soon despawn.</p>
<p>Unlike directly harvesting a fresh carcass' meat, you will still need to harvest a quarter for its meat. 50% of the quarters total weight will be turned into usable <a href="./Cougar%20Meat%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougar Meat (Raw)">Cougar Meat (Raw)</a>. Any remaining raw meat left inside the bag will be lost if the Quarter's Condition reaches 0%, with bags spoiling within 1 day of harvesting.</p>
