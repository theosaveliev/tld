## Overview

A **Cougar Quarter** is a section harvested from a [Cougar](./Cougar.md). You can obtain Cougar quarters by _quartering_ a [Cougar Carcass](./Cougar%20Carcass.md). To quarter an animal, you have to go into the harvest menu, then select the quartering action. For a Cougar, this will always take 2 hours. You can only quarter a Cougar if its less than 50% frozen.

After quartering, the carcass will despawn, leaving behind 2 quarters, as well as its [Fresh Gut](./Fresh%20Gut.md) and [Fresh Cougar Hide](./Fresh%20Cougar%20Hide.md). It will also leave behind the Cougar's legs and blood, but this cannot be picked up, and it will soon despawn.

Unlike directly harvesting a fresh carcass' meat, you will still need to harvest a quarter for its meat. 50% of the quarters total weight will be turned into usable [Cougar Meat (Raw)](./Cougar%20Meat%20%28Raw%29.md). Any remaining raw meat left inside the bag will be lost if the Quarter's Condition reaches 0%, with bags spoiling within 1 day of harvesting.
