# Craggy Passage

> {{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Craggy Passage.jpg|region=Zone of Contamination|map=Craggy Passage Location.png}}'''Craggy Passage''' is a location in the [[Zone of Contamination]] near [[Enduring Waterfall]]. It is directly south west of the map, and is nothing but a small canyon. Very little loot is here, and there is no shelter.
