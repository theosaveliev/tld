**Craggy Passage** is a location in the [Zone of Contamination](./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md) near [Enduring Waterfall](./Enduring%20Waterfall.md). It is directly south west of the map, and is nothing but a small canyon. Very little loot is here, and there is no shelter.
