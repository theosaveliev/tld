**Crumpled Note** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode. It is found on a metal bench when first entering the <a href="./Maintenance%20Yard.md" class="wikilink" title="Maintenance Yard">Maintenance Yard</a> office area. Picking it up unlocks the <a href="./Lake%20Gunshots.md" class="wikilink" title="Lake Gunshots">Lake Gunshots</a> side mission.
