**Crumpled Note** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 2](./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode. It is found on a desk in the offices near the [Medical Locker](./Medical%20Locker.md) in upper [Carter Hydro Dam](./Carter%20Hydro%20Dam.md). It unlocks a cache for the [Mystery Lake Supply Caches](./Mystery%20Lake%20Supply%20Caches.md) side mission.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Crumpled Note**_

The penmanship on this note suggests the author was in a hurry.

"I left it in the cave. Head back out from the dam. Follow the river. Look for a cave near a clearing with a hunter's blind."
