A **Cup of Acorn Coffee** is a brewed drink in The Long Dark, made from <a href="./Acorn%20Grounds.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorn Grounds">Acorn Grounds</a>, which in turn are made out of <a href="./Acorns%20%28Cooked%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooked Acorns">Cooked Acorns</a>.

## Overview

It can be thought of as a weaker version of the <a href="./Tin%20of%20Coffee.md" class="wikilink" title="commercial coffee">commercial coffee</a> found in the game. Drinking a <a href="./Cup%20of%20Acorn%20Coffee.md" class="wikilink" title="Cup of Acorn Coffee">Cup of Acorn Coffee</a> will help to reduce fatigue usage. "<a href="./Fatigue%20Reduced.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue Reduced">Fatigue Reduced</a>" lasts 30 minutes and while in effect, the player's fatigue is immediately restored by 5% and fatigue loss rate is reduced by 25%. Sprinting will also use 25% less energy. Fatigue Reduced does not affect the fatigue loss rate when climbing ropes.

Acorn Coffee is a non-renewable resource.
