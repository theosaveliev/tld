**Cured Gut** is an animal-based <a href="./Materials.md" class="wikilink" title="Material">Material</a> that is acquired after air-drying <a href="./Fresh%20Gut.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Gut">Fresh Gut</a> indoors for 5 days. They can be used to craft or repair hide/pelt based <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> and several <a href="./Tools.md" class="wikilink" title="Tools">Tools</a>.

## Cured Gut

Cured Gut is an item required for crafting or repairing many items, such as <a href="./Line.md" class="wikilink" title="line">line</a>, <a href="./Snare.md" class="wikilink" title="snare">snares</a>, and animal skin based Clothing. Unlike Fresh Gut, Cured Gut do not contribute to a player's overall <a href="./Scent.md" class="wikilink" title="Scent">Scent</a>. Cured Gut can occasionally be found inside containers.
