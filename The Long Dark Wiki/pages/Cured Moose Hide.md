**Cured Moose Hide** is the cured hide of a [Fresh Moose Hide](./Fresh%20Moose%20Hide.md). It can be used as a [Material](./Materials.md) for crafting or repairing [Moose-Hide Cloak](./Moose-Hide%20Cloak.md) and crafting [Moose-Hide Satchel](./Moose-Hide%20Satchel.md).

## Overview

Cured Moose Hide is leaving [Fresh Moose Hide](./Fresh%20Moose%20Hide.md) indoors for 10 days (in-game). Once cured, the hide will take on a darker complexion, and can then be used for crafting.

Fresh Moose Hides are a very rare resource to obtain, since they require first finding a very rare encounter with a [Moose](./Moose.md) and it's hunting it with a [weapon](./Tools.md). Each Moose Carcass will always have one hide, along with [meat](./Moose%20Meat%20%28Raw%29.md) and [Cured Gut](./Cured%20Gut.md).
