**Cured Moose Hide** is the cured hide of a <a href="./Fresh%20Moose%20Hide.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Moose Hide">Fresh Moose Hide</a>. It can be used as a <a href="./Materials.md" class="wikilink" title="Material">Material</a> for crafting or repairing <a href="./Moose-Hide%20Cloak.md" class="wikilink" title="Moose-Hide Cloak">Moose-Hide Cloak</a> and crafting <a href="./Moose-Hide%20Satchel.md" class="wikilink" title="Moose-Hide Satchel">Moose-Hide Satchel</a>.

## Overview

Cured Moose Hide is leaving <a href="./Fresh%20Moose%20Hide.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Moose Hide">Fresh Moose Hide</a> indoors for 10 days (in-game). Once cured, the hide will take on a darker complexion, and can then be used for crafting.

Fresh Moose Hides are a very rare resource to obtain, since they require first finding a very rare encounter with a <a href="./Moose.md" class="wikilink" title="Moose">Moose</a> and it's hunting it with a <a href="./Tools.md" class="wikilink" title="weapon">weapon</a>. Each Moose Carcass will always have one hide, along with <a href="./Moose%20Meat%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="meat">meat</a> and <a href="./Cured%20Gut.md" class="wikilink" title="Cured Gut">Cured Gut</a>.
