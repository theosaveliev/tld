**Cured Rabbit Pelt** is the cured hide of a [Fresh Rabbit Pelt](./Fresh%20Rabbit%20Pelt.md). It can be used as a [Material](./Materials.md) for crafting or repairing [Rabbitskin Mitts](./Rabbitskin%20Mitts.md) and [Rabbitskin Hats](./Rabbitskin%20Hat.md).

## Overview

Fresh Rabbit Pelt are acquired by harvesting a [Rabbit Carcass](./Rabbit%20Carcass.md), they can then be cured by leaving them indoors for 3 days (in-game). Cured Rabbit Pelts are used for crafting or repairing [Rabbitskin Hats](./Rabbitskin%20Hat.md) and [Rabbitskin Mitts](./Rabbitskin%20Mitts.md). Once cured, the hide will take on a darker complexion, and can then be used for crafting.

Rabbit Pelts are acquired by [Trapping](./Trapping.md), stunning rabbits with a [Stone](./Stone.md), or shooting a rabbit. Each Rabbit Carcass will have one pelt, along with [meat](./Rabbit%20%28Raw%29.md) and [Fresh Gut](./Fresh%20Gut.md).

Cured Rabbit Pelts are widely used for [trading](./Trader.md), as they are the fastest hide to cure and can easily be obtained in large quantities. They can be used to purchase [Rifle Ammunition](./Rifle%20Ammunition.md), [Revolver Ammunition](./Revolver%20Ammunition.md), [flare shells](./Flare%20Shell.md), an [Aviator Cap](./Aviator%20Cap.md), [Thin Wool Sweater](./Thin%20Wool%20Sweater.md), [Wool Shirt](./Wool%20Shirt.md), [Thick Wool Sweater](./Thick%20Wool%20Sweater.md), [Light Shell](./Light%20Shell.md), [Mackinaw Jacket](./Mackinaw%20Jacket.md), [Mukluks](./Mukluks.md), [Manufactured Arrow](./Manufactured%20Arrow.md), the [Bushcraft Bow Blueprint Card](./Bushcraft%20Bow%20Blueprint%20Card.md), as well as [three](./T-Shirt%20%28CM%29.md) [different](./T-Shirt%20%28GBI%29.md) [T-Shirts](./T-Shirt%20%28Snappy%29.md) and [three](./Patterned%20Wool%20Socks%20%28Dots%29.md) [different](./Patterned%20Wool%20Socks%20%28Moose%29.md) [wool socks](./Patterned%20Wool%20Socks%20%28Plaid%29.md).
