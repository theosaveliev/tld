**Cutty's Cave** is one of the many small caves located in <a href="./Blackrock.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock">Blackrock</a>.

## Overview

This cave is best accessed by going down the road from the prison, then taking a left, followed by a right near two pickup trucks. As with most caves, this one provides a warmth bonus and can be used to cure hides. Its most notable feature is the dead prison guard outside, which can be looted to yield the <a href="./Blackrock%20Infirmary%20Medication.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Infirmary Medication">Blackrock Infirmary Medication</a> needed to treat <a href="./Franklin.md" class="wikilink" title="Franklin">Franklin</a> in the first mission of Episode 4. The cave also has a leaf bed, which provides a small warmth bonus. Be careful when visiting, for a pack of <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="wolves">wolves</a> prowls the area.
