# Damaged Track

> {{IronRoad}}{{Infobox/Item|image=Damaged Tracks.jpg|wintermute=no|harvest=}}'''Damaged Track''' are stationary interactable objects in [[The Long Dark|''The Long Dark'']].
> 
> == Overview ==
> '''Damaged Tracks''' are sections of train tracks that are highly damaged on [[Cauldron Island]]. They appear as warped, rusted, and chipped train track, with damaged cross ties. Driving over them can damage the [[Prototype Train|Prototype Train's]] Wheels.
> 
> * Full reverse or full acceleration at speed 1 will cause an intense amount of screeching and train bobbling, resulting in condition damage to the wheels. The louder the screeching of metal becomes, the more damage is being done to the Wheels.
> * Faster train movement will cause exponentially increased levels of damage to the wheels, up to 28% condition loss or more on one section of damaged track.
> * The track section can be repaired if you have a [[Heavy Hammer]], and do not require Scrap Metal. Each repair section takes 2 hours and repairs the tracks completely.
> ** Alternatively, you can avoid repairing the tracks and damaging the train by alternating between speed settings 1 and Neutral to slowly move the train. This will prevent any damage to the Wheels when passing through this hazard.
> ** Sometimes a parallel track can be found that prevents contact with these rails to begin with. Spending some time to use them can help preserve your train's condition in the long run.
>
