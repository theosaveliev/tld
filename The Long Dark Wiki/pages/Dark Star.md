**Dark Star** is a side mission in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a>.

## Overview

To start the mission, the player must find any of four <a href="./Archival%20Report.md" class="wikilink" title="Archival Report">Archival Report</a> objects scattered across the <a href="./Blackrock.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock">Blackrock</a> region. Finding all four will complete the mission.

## Tasks

- Search for bloodstained pages inside and outside the Blackrock grounds.

## Key locations

- <a href="./Archival%20Report.md#Volume_A" class="wikilink" title="Volume A">Volume A</a> is found in Blackrock Locker \#3 (requires a key to open and is an objective of the <a href="./Blackrock%20Lockers.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Lockers">Blackrock Lockers</a> side mission).
- <a href="./Archival%20Report.md#Volume_B" class="wikilink" title="Volume B">Volume B</a> is inside the prison transport bus that is near the <a href="./Work%20Bench.md" class="wikilink" title="work bench">work bench</a> on the Blackrock Prison grounds.
- <a href="./Archival%20Report.md#Volume_C" class="wikilink" title="Volume C">Volume C</a> is on a desk inside the <a href="./Old%20Substation.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Substation">Old Substation</a>.
- <a href="./Archival%20Report.md#Volume_D" class="wikilink" title="Volume D">Volume D</a> is on the <a href="./Work%20Bench.md" class="wikilink" title="work bench">work bench</a> inside the trailer at <a href="./Foreman%27s%20Clearcut.md" class="wikilink" title="Foreman&#39;s Clearcut">Foreman's Clearcut</a>.
