> '''Dave's Quiet Clearing''' is location in [[Mystery Lake (region)|Mystery Lake]]. 
> 
> == Overview ==
> Dave's Quiet Clearing is a shallow cave which provides very little shelter. A [[Frozen Corpse]] or a [[backpack]] can be found inside and searched for what little [[:Category:Items|loot]] it may contain. The clearing connects to the [[Camp Office]] through the [[Lake Trail]] and to [[Mystery Lake (location)|Mystery Lake]] through the [[Mystery Lake (Western Access)|Western Access]]. 
> 
> The clearing can be found south of the Camp Office and west of Mystery Lake. A [[Prepper Cache]] can be found on the hill to the east of the cave.
