A **Dead Prison Guard** is a uniquely named [Frozen Corpse](./Frozen%20Corpse.md) found in [Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md). He belonged to the [Crashed Prison Transport Bus](./Crashed%20Prison%20Transport%20Bus.md) after the [First Flare](./First%20Flare.md) caused some of the inmates to scatter into [Pleasant Valley](./Pleasant%20Valley.md). He holds the [Blackrock Penitentiary Memo](./Blackrock%20Penitentiary%20Memo.md), but otherwise functions as a normal Frozen Corpse and has the same storage capacity as any other corpse.

A Dead Prison Guard is found in [Episode 4: Fury, then Silence](./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md) as part of the [Blackrock Blues](./Blackrock%20Blues.md) mission next to a [First Aid Kit](./First%20Aid%20Kit.md) containing the [Blackrock Infirmary Medication](./Blackrock%20Infirmary%20Medication.md).

Another Dead Prisoner Guard can be found as part of the side mission [Blackrock Rumours](./Blackrock%20Rumours.md) - Missing Employee as a dead man named Murphy. He was panning for gold, and can be found in a ravine with the [Lock Box Key](./Lock%20Box%20Key.md) and [Notebook Page](./Notebook%20Page.md) items. His lockbox key opens a lockbox containing a [Gold Nugget](./Gold%20Nugget.md).
