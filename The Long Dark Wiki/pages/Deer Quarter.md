## Overview

**Deer Quarter**s are sections harvested from a <a href="./Deer.md" class="wikilink" title="Deer">Deer</a> carcass. Deer quarters can be obtained by going into the harvest menu after killing a deer. While in the harvest screen, switch to the right side of the screen putting you in the quartering screen. Unlike harvesting the meat directly, you cannot quarter a deer with your bare hands, even if the carcass is not frozen. You cannot quarter an animal if it is 50% frozen or more.

Unlike the misleading term "quarter", a deer carcass will have 4-5 quarters, and having five makes it technically a fifth, not a quarter.

When a deer is quartered, you also get the <a href="./Fresh%20Gut.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Gut">Fresh Gut</a> and <a href="./Fresh%20Deer%20Hide.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Deer Hide">Fresh Deer Hide</a>. Quartering a deer will always take 1 in-game hour, making it more time efficient. However, you still need to harvest the quarters for usable <a href="./Venison%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Venison (Raw)">Venison (Raw)</a>. Any remaining raw meat left inside the bag will be lost if the Quarter's Condition reaches 0%, with bags spoiling within 1 day of harvesting.

Also, only 50% of a quarters weight will be useable meat, more than likely as a drawback for the quick harvesting time.
