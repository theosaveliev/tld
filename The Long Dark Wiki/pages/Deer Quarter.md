## Overview

**Deer Quarter**s are sections harvested from a [Deer](./Deer.md) carcass. Deer quarters can be obtained by going into the harvest menu after killing a deer. While in the harvest screen, switch to the right side of the screen putting you in the quartering screen. Unlike harvesting the meat directly, you cannot quarter a deer with your bare hands, even if the carcass is not frozen. You cannot quarter an animal if it is 50% frozen or more.

Unlike the misleading term "quarter", a deer carcass will have 4-5 quarters, and having five makes it technically a fifth, not a quarter.

When a deer is quartered, you also get the [Fresh Gut](./Fresh%20Gut.md) and [Fresh Deer Hide](./Fresh%20Deer%20Hide.md). Quartering a deer will always take 1 in-game hour, making it more time efficient. However, you still need to harvest the quarters for usable [Venison (Raw)](./Venison%20%28Raw%29.md). Any remaining raw meat left inside the bag will be lost if the Quarter's Condition reaches 0%, with bags spoiling within 1 day of harvesting.

Also, only 50% of a quarters weight will be useable meat, more than likely as a drawback for the quick harvesting time.
