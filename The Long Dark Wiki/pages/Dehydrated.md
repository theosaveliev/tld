**Dehydrated** is a unique <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>* only encountered in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> during the missions <a href="./Fallen%20Star.md" class="wikilink" title="Fallen Star">Fallen Star</a> and <a href="./Coming%20Storm.md" class="wikilink" title="Coming Storm">Coming Storm</a> in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a>.

## Overview

Dehydrated can affect the <a href="./Crash%20Survivor.md" class="wikilink" title="Crash Survivors">Crash Survivors</a> Gwen, Ada, Logan, and Wade.

- If their hydration levels hit 0, they will take <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> damage until they die, thus failing the game and sending the player back to a previous checkpoint.

## Treatment

Simply giving the necessary amount of potable water to a carried survivor will cure the Affliction.
