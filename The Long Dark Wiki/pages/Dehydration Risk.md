**Dehydration Risk** is a unique <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>* only encountered in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> during the missions <a href="./Fallen%20Star.md" class="wikilink" title="Fallen Star">Fallen Star</a> and <a href="./Coming%20Storm.md" class="wikilink" title="Coming Storm">Coming Storm</a> in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a>.

## Overview

Dehydration Risk can affect the <a href="./Crash%20Survivor.md" class="wikilink" title="Crash Survivors">Crash Survivors</a> Eric, Gwen, Ada, Logan, and Wade.

- Eric is found originally in the <a href="./Community%20Hall.md" class="wikilink" title="Community Hall">Community Hall</a> and has Dehydration Risk when diagnosed. He needs 0.50 L of <a href="./Water%20%28Potable%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Water (Potable)">Water (Potable)</a> to cure it as part of the Fallen Star mission.
- Gwen, Ada, Logan, and Wade can be carried to the Community and will eventually develop Dehydration Risk during your travels.

## Treatment

Aside from Eric needing 0.50 L of water, Simply giving the necessary amount of potable water to a carried survivor will prevent the Risk from developing into an Affliction.
