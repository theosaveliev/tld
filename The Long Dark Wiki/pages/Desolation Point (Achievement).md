**Description:** Craft a full set of improvised Tools.

**Desolation Point** is an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

To get this achievement you have to use a [Forge](./Forge.md) to create both an [Improvised Knife](./Improvised%20Knife.md) and an [Improvised Hatchet](./Improvised%20Hatchet.md). You need at least 8 pieces of [Scrap Metal](./Scrap%20Metal.md) and 2 pieces of [Cloth](./Cloth.md) as well as enough [Coal](./Coal.md) (minimum of 4 pieces) to bring a Forge to a temperature of at least 150° Celsius (302° Fahrenheit). You also have to find a [Heavy Hammer](./Heavy%20Hammer.md) to craft the items. Crafting a [Cougar Claw Knife](./Cougar%20Claw%20Knife.md) or [Arrowhead](./Arrowhead.md) is not necessary for this achievement.

### Forge locations

Forges are quite rare in the world: only four exist on [Great Bear Island](./Great%20Bear%20Island.md):

- [The Riken](./The%20Riken.md) in [Desolation Point](./Desolation%20Point.md)
- [Old Spence Family Homestead](./Old%20Spence%20Family%20Homestead.md) in [Forlorn Muskeg](./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md)
- [Maintenance Shed](./Maintenance%20Yard.md) in [Broken Railroad](./Broken%20Railroad.md)
- [Main Hangar](./Main%20Hangar.md) in [Forsaken Airfield](./Forsaken%20Airfield.md) (requires [Tales from the Far Territory](./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md) DLC)
