**Desolation Point** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

To get this achievement you have to use a <a href="./Forge.md" class="wikilink" title="Forge">Forge</a> to create both an <a href="./Improvised%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Knife">Improvised Knife</a> and an <a href="./Improvised%20Hatchet.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Hatchet">Improvised Hatchet</a>. You need at least 8 pieces of <a href="./Scrap%20Metal.md" class="wikilink" title="Scrap Metal">Scrap Metal</a> and 2 pieces of <a href="./Cloth.md" class="wikilink" title="Cloth">Cloth</a> as well as enough <a href="./Coal.md" class="wikilink" title="Coal">Coal</a> (minimum of 4 pieces) to bring a Forge to a temperature of at least 150° Celsius (302° Fahrenheit). You also have to find a <a href="./Heavy%20Hammer.md" class="wikilink" title="Heavy Hammer">Heavy Hammer</a> to craft the items. Crafting a <a href="./Cougar%20Claw%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougar Claw Knife">Cougar Claw Knife</a> or <a href="./Arrowhead.md" class="wikilink" title="Arrowhead">Arrowhead</a> is not necessary for this achievement.

### Forge locations

Forges are quite rare in the world: only four exist on <a href="./Great%20Bear%20Island.md" class="wikilink" title="Great Bear Island">Great Bear Island</a>:

- <a href="./The%20Riken.md" class="wikilink" title="The Riken">The Riken</a> in <a href="./Desolation%20Point.md" class="wikilink" title="Desolation Point">Desolation Point</a>
- <a href="./Old%20Spence%20Family%20Homestead.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Spence Family Homestead">Old Spence Family Homestead</a> in <a href="./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md" class="wikilink" title="Forlorn Muskeg">Forlorn Muskeg</a>
- <a href="./Maintenance%20Yard.md" class="wikilink" title="Maintenance Shed">Maintenance Shed</a> in <a href="./Broken%20Railroad.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Railroad">Broken Railroad</a>
- <a href="./Main%20Hangar.md" class="wikilink" title="Main Hangar">Main Hangar</a> in <a href="./Forsaken%20Airfield.md" class="wikilink" title="Forsaken Airfield">Forsaken Airfield</a> (requires <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> DLC)
