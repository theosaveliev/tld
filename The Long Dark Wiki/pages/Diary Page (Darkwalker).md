**Diary Page** is a series of <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectibles">Collectibles</a> found only in the <a href="./Challenge.md" class="wikilink" title="Challenge">Challenge</a> <a href="./Escape%20the%20Darkwalker.md" class="wikilink" title="Escape the Darkwalker">Escape the Darkwalker</a>.

There are 10 notes found across the Challenge in different locations, always found near a Green <a href="./Campfire.md" class="wikilink" title="Campfire">Campfire</a> in set regions. All must be collected in order to banish the Darkwalker. They are different from the starting note for the Challenge, <a href="./On%20the%20Nature%20of%20Darkwalkers.md" class="wikilink" title="On the Nature of Darkwalkers">On the Nature of Darkwalkers</a>.
