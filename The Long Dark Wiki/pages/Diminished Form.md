Diminished Form is a permanent Affliction unique to <a href="./Misery.md" class="wikilink" title="Misery Mode">Misery Mode</a> which occurs from Stage 1 Misery (Day 2). It permanently reduces the maximum carry weight of the survivor and grants a <a href="./Stamina.md" class="wikilink" title="Mobility Penalty">Mobility Penalty</a> to sprinting. Additionally it reduces the chance per-attack during a <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggle">Struggle</a> that the hostile wildlife will flee.

Note: Diminished Form does 'not' affect Climbing Stamina, if starting a climb at 100% of ***current*** Stamina, similar to other Stamina penalties like wearing <a href="./Bearskin%20Coat.md" class="wikilink" title="Bearskin Coat">Bearskin Coat</a>.

- -5kg <a href="./Encumbrance.md" class="wikilink" title="Carry Weight">Carry Weight</a>

- Reduced chance of hostile wildlife fleeing during struggle (<a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf">Wolf</a>, <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolf">Timberwolf</a> and <a href="./Poisoned%20Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Poisoned Wolf">Poisoned Wolf</a>)
