**Diocese Report** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 3](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode as a part of the [Church Artifact](./Church%20Artifact%20%28mission%29.md) side mission.

During the course of the mission, [Astrid Greenwood](./Astrid%20Greenwood.md) finds the note _Diocese Report_ in the [Thomson's Crossing](./Thomson%27s%20Crossing.md) church.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Diocese Report**_

From: Diocese of the Northern Coast

To: Fr. Thomas, Thomson’s Crossing Parish

Re: Break-in and Missing Property

We were saddened, all of us, to hear about the recent theft.

I've spoken with a constable here, and his initial phone call inquiries suggest something to do with an old homestead near a place called Skeeter's Ridge. Do you know it?

Has he not been in contact? He promised me he would look into it further, but it would mean travel to Great Bear, and well, you know what that means these days.

Do keep in touch,

Bishop Stevens
