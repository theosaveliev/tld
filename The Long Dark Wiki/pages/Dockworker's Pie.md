<p><strong>Dockworker's Pie</strong> is a food item in <em>The Long Dark</em>, it is learned from a unique <a href="./Recipe%20Cards.md" class="wikilink" title="Recipe Card">Recipe Card</a> and is made with a <a href="./Cooking%20Skillet.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Skillet">Cooking Skillet</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Dockworker's Pie is a relatively simple recipe requiring mostly common ingredients and featuring a powerful Condition restoring effect, similar to <a href="./Prepper%27s%20Pie.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper&#39;s Pie">Prepper's Pie</a>. Compared with Prepper's Pie, Dockworker's Pie requires more artificial ingredients, with the most limited being <a href="./Potato.md" class="wikilink" title="Potatoes">Potatoes</a>. <a href="./Coho%20Salmon%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Coho Salmon">Coho Salmon</a> and <a href="./Rockfish%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Rockfish">Rockfish</a> are both saltwater fish that can be acquired by <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a> in any Coastal Region (e.g., Bleak Inlet), with Coho being 'uncommon' and Rockfish being 'rare' (note: rare fish can be increased in frequency with <a href="./Fishing%20Lure%20%28disambiguation%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Lures">Lures</a>, at the cost of taking longer before a bite).</p>
<p>The Recipe Card for Dockworker's Pie is found inside of the <a href="./Last%20Resort%20Cannery.md" class="wikilink" title="Last Resort Cannery">Last Resort Cannery</a> <a href="./Workshop.md" class="wikilink" title="Workshop">Workshop</a> in <a href="./Bleak%20Inlet.md" class="wikilink" title="Bleak Inlet">Bleak Inlet</a>, which is sealed by an electronically locked door (requiring the key code found on a note in <a href="./Echo%20One%20Radio%20Tower.md" class="wikilink" title="Echo One Radio Tower">Echo One Radio Tower</a>, and an active <a href="./Aurora.md" class="wikilink" title="Aurora">Aurora</a> at night to power the door). The Last Resort Cannery can be difficult to access: being very cold, windy and infested with a pack of <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolves">Timberwolves</a>.</p>
<p>Dockworker's Pie produces a <a href="./Scent.md" class="wikilink" title="Scent">Scent</a> that attracts <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="predators">predators</a>. It may be dropped as a <a href="./Scent.md#Baiting%2FDecoys" class="wikilink" title="decoy">decoy</a>.</p>
