> == Overview ==
> Dockworker's Pie is a relatively simple recipe requiring mostly common ingredients and featuring a powerful Condition restoring effect, similar to [[Prepper's Pie]]. Compared with Prepper's Pie, Dockworker's Pie requires more artificial ingredients, with the most limited being [[Potato|Potatoes]]. [[Coho Salmon (Raw)|Coho Salmon]] and [[Rockfish (Raw)|Rockfish]] are both saltwater fish that can be acquired by [[Ice Fishing]] in any Coastal Region (e.g., Bleak Inlet), with Coho being 'uncommon' and Rockfish being 'rare' (note: rare fish can be increased in frequency with [[Fishing Lure (disambiguation)|Lures]], at the cost of taking longer before a bite).
> 
> The Recipe Card for Dockworker's Pie is found inside of the [[Last Resort Cannery]] [[Workshop]] in [[Bleak Inlet]], which is sealed by an electronically locked door (requiring the key code found on a note in [[Echo One Radio Tower]], and an active [[Aurora]] at night to power the door). The Last Resort Cannery can be difficult to access: being very cold, windy and infested with a pack of [[Timberwolf|Timberwolves]].
> 
> Dockworker's Pie produces a [[Scent]] that attracts [[Wolf|predators]]. It may be dropped as a [[Scent#Baiting/Decoys|decoy]].
>
