# Document Stash 1

> |name=|image=Document Stash 1 icon.png|release=2 December 2024|update=2.33|story=No|type=Tools|equipable=No|stackable=No|weight=0.00|decay-inside=|decay-outside=|examine=An old lockbox.|key=}}'''Document Stash 1''' is an item found during [[Sutherland's Tale]].
> ==Overview==
> It is found in a [[Loose Bricks Stash]] in the Stockpile [[Prepper Cache]] and contains [[Wyatt's Lost Papers 1]] and [[Bunker Schematic Fragment 1]]. It must be placed in the [[Trade Drop Box]] as the 1st Special Request to Sutherland to advance his questline.
> 
> It cannot be dropped or placed in a container, so it cannot be lost.
