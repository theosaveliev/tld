**Document Stash 1** is an item found during [Sutherland's Tale](./Sutherland%27s%20Tale.md).

## Overview

It is found in a [Loose Bricks Stash](./Loose%20Bricks%20Stash.md) in the Stockpile [Prepper Cache](./Prepper%20Cache.md) and contains [Wyatt's Lost Papers 1](./Wyatt%27s%20Lost%20Papers%201.md) and [Bunker Schematic Fragment 1](./Bunker%20Schematic%20Fragment%201.md). It must be placed in the [Trade Drop Box](./Trade%20Drop%20Box.md) as the 1st Special Request to Sutherland to advance his questline.

It cannot be dropped or placed in a container, so it cannot be lost.
