# Document Stash 2

> {{TFTFT}}{{Infobox item
> |name=|image=Document Stash 2 icon.png|release=2 December 2024|update=2.33|story=No|type=Tools|equipable=No|stackable=No|weight=0.00|decay-inside=|decay-outside=|examine=An old lockbox.|key=}}'''Document Stash 2''' is an item found during [[Sutherland's Tale]].
> ==Overview==
> It is found in a [[Loose Bricks Stash]] in the Hunter [[Prepper Cache]] and contains [[Wyatt's Lost Papers 2]] and [[Bunker Schematic Fragment 2]]. It must be placed in the [[Trade Drop Box]] as the 2nd Special Request to Sutherland to advance his questline.
> 
> It cannot be dropped or placed in a container, so it cannot be lost.
> 
> ''There's still a Known Issue with Sutherland's Tale where there is a chance the Loose Brick Stash in the Hunter  Prepper Cache does not spawn. If this is the case the Document Stash 2 can be found at the Special Request 4 location (Joplin's Prepper Cache).''
