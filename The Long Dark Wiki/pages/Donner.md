## Overview

**Donner** is an unseen character that is the driving motivational force behind the actions of <a href="./Mathis.md" class="wikilink" title="Mathis">Mathis</a> and the <a href="./Convicts.md" class="wikilink" title="Convicts">Convicts</a> in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence</a>, eventually making a brief appearance in <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things">Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things</a>.

## Appearance

In Episode 5, he wears glasses.

First mentioned by <a href="./Vachon.md" class="wikilink" title="Vachon">Vachon</a> and <a href="./Heller.md" class="wikilink" title="Heller">Heller</a>, Donner is a prisoner locked in the solitary isolation wing of the <a href="./Blackrock%20Prison.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Penitentiary Prison">Blackrock Penitentiary Prison</a> located on <a href="./Great%20Bear%20Island.md" class="wikilink" title="Great Bear Island">Great Bear Island</a>. Evil to the core, Donner is described as being a chip off the old block and then some compared to his father, <a href="./Mathis.md" class="wikilink" title="Mathis">Mathis</a>. He is currently serving a life sentence in Blackrock, as there is no "going back to regular society" for a man such as Donner.

Mathis cares deeply for his son and is willing to do anything to free him, going so far to beat the prison Warden <a href="./Franklin.md" class="wikilink" title="Franklin">Franklin</a> to death to acquire information on how to open Solitary and forcing <a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will Mackenzie">Will Mackenzie</a> to risk his life in the hostile wilderness in order to acquire old mining detonators to blast open the wing of Donner's cell. Simply hearing Franklin utter Donner's name was also enough to cause Mathis' mood to instantly sour as he begin savagely beating Franklin.

Due to the combined actions of Will Mackenzie and <a href="./Jace.md" class="wikilink" title="Jace">Jace</a>, an explosion destroyed Blackrock Prison and unintentionally freed Donner. He is last mentioned by Heller at the <a href="./Power%20Plant.md" class="wikilink" title="Power Plant">Power Plant</a> to be critically wounded but alive, and needs immediate medical attention.

Donner finally makes an appearance in Episode 5, being heavily wounded and suffering from internal bleeding from the Prison explosion. His father travels to <a href="./Perseverance%20Mills.md" class="wikilink" title="Perseverance Mills">Perseverance Mills</a> with him and forces <a href="./Astrid%20Greenwood.md" class="wikilink" title="Astrid Greenwood">Astrid Greenwood</a> to find a way to heal Donner by threatening to kill <a href="./Jane%20Yang.md" class="wikilink" title="Jane Yang">Jane Yang</a>, with the doctor being forced to head to a distant SAR Station to find a <a href="./Trauma%20Kit.md" class="wikilink" title="Trauma Kit">Trauma Kit</a> for its advanced First Aid uses.

She eventually retrieves it and heals Donner's external wounds with the kit to the best of her abilities. Astrid tells Mathis that unless she gets better equipment, she won't know how bad the internal damage is to Donner's body. Just before Mathis is about to kill Astrid, Donner then speaks to his father about how he saw the <a href="./Aurora.md" class="wikilink" title="lights and black water in his dreams">lights and black water in his dreams</a>, before eventually perishing from his wounds.
