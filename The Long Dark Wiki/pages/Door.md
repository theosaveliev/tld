A **Door** is a piece of a clothing <a href="./Cabinet.md" class="wikilink" title="Cabinet">Cabinet</a> that can be broken down into <a href="./Reclaimed%20Wood.md" class="wikilink" title="Reclaimed Wood">Reclaimed Wood</a>. They are very rarely found in the game world. One can be found in the cabin at the <a href="./Cannery%20Worker%20Residences.md" class="wikilink" title="Cannery Worker Residences">Cannery Worker Residences</a> in <a href="./Bleak%20Inlet.md" class="wikilink" title="Bleak Inlet">Bleak Inlet</a>.

This item cannot be interacted with currently. An option to remove it does not appear, even in safehouse customization mode.
