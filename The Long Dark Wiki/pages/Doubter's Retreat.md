# Doubter's Retreat

> {{Infobox/Location|image=Doubter's Retreat.jpg|region=Perseverance Wilds|location=|description=A secluded cabin with a pier, boat shack, and fishing hut near the edge of the the sea|type=residential|firebox=|bed=yes|shelter=yes|mapfile=|mapx=|mapy=|mappin=|mapcaption=}}
> 
> '''Doubter's Retreat''' is a location in [[Perseverance Wilds]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> Located to the southwestern most section of the map, it is a lone cabin located near the sea. A [[Timberwolf]] pack is often found near the area.
> 
> The cabin consists of 1 room, with cabinets and a bed, and several animal themed trophies along the walls. A trap door leads to a room under the cabin that stores a boat on the water.
> 
> A unique [[Fishing Hut]] can be found on the ice, this one designed to be on land all year round.
> 
> == Wintermute ==
> [[Astrid Greenwood|Astrid]] heads to this Cabin in search of one of the three missing persons from [[Perseverance Mills]].
