<p><strong>Doubter's Retreat</strong> is a location in <a href="./Perseverance%20Wilds.md" class="wikilink" title="Perseverance Wilds">Perseverance Wilds</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Located to the southwestern most section of the map, it is a lone cabin located near the sea. A <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolf">Timberwolf</a> pack is often found near the area.</p>
<p>The cabin consists of 1 room, with cabinets and a bed, and several animal themed trophies along the walls. A trap door leads to a room under the cabin that stores a boat on the water.</p>
<p>A unique <a href="./Fishing%20Hut.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Hut">Fishing Hut</a> can be found on the ice, this one designed to be on land all year round.</p>
<h2 id="Wintermute">Wintermute</h2>
