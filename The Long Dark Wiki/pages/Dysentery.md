**Dysentery** is a lethal disease that can be contracted by drinking <a href="./Water%20%28Unsafe%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Water (Unsafe)">Water (Unsafe)</a>. Drinking Unsafe Water does not immediately guarantee Dysentary due to RNG calculation chance, but generally speaking; the more Unsafe Water you drink, the greater your chance of contracting the disease.

While in effect, <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> will not be restored during <a href="./Sleep.md" class="wikilink" title="Sleep">Sleep</a>, coupled with approximately 4% Condition loss per hour. The <a href="./Thirst.md" class="wikilink" title="Thirst">Thirst</a> and <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a> bars will also rapidly empty, further compounding the loss of Condition once those <a href="./Needs.md" class="wikilink" title="Needs">Needs</a> empty.

- Treatment includes consuming 2 <a href="./Antibiotics.md" class="wikilink" title="Antibiotics">Antibiotics</a> or a cup of <a href="./Reishi%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Reishi Tea">Reishi Tea</a>/<a href="./Burdock%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Burdock Tea">Burdock Tea</a>, followed by cumulatively drinking 2.00 L <a href="./Water%20%28Potable%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Water (Potable)">Water (Potable)</a>, then followed by 10 cumulative hours of <a href="./Sleep.md" class="wikilink" title="Sleep">Sleep</a>.
- The treatment process cannot be done out of order: medicine and water must first be fully consumed before the 10 hours of rest will begin to count towards the healing process. (Make sure medicine and water have 2 check marks next to them before sleeping).
- After consuming the required medicine, the player will stop losing Condition, but thirst will still deplete rapidly until the sleeping recovery time is completed.
