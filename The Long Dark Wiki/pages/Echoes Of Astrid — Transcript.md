## The big old house shows the first signs of habitation since you crashed. Investigate.

### 3rd Person Cutscene

*Mackenzie walks up to the front door, as wolves from deeper in the town approach his location. He steps inside as a third person cutscene plays*

Mackenzie: "Hello? Anybody here?

*Mackenzie steps inside and peaks his head to his right, just as a bullet wizzes by and hits the wall behind him*

Mackenzie: "Holy shi...wait...hold your fire!"

*An old woman in a chair is pointing a rifle at Mackenzie*

<a href="./Grey%20Mother.md" class="wikilink" title="Grey Mother">Grey Mother</a>: "Come closer, stranger."

Mackenzie: "Ok ok...just take it easy."

*Mackenzie slowly walks towards the blindfolded woman, who never points her aim away from the pilot*

Grey Mother: "Why are you here?"

Mackenzie: "I saw the smoke from your chimney."

Grey Mother: "Mainlander?"

Mackenzie: "Uhh...What?"

*The old woman smirks and chuckles*

Grey Mother: "Thought so. Mainlander. Outsider. I don't recognize your voice and you haven't left like the other cowards so...must be a Mainlander. Someone who doesn't know any better."

Mackenzie: "My plane...I crashed in the mountains nearby."

Grey Mother: "A likely story."

Mackenzie: "Listen...could you maybe point that gun someplace safe?"

Grey Mother: "You think because my eyes are covered, I can't see you? Make the wrong move, say the wrong thing...you'll see how good a shot I am."

Mackenzie: "I don't doubt it."

*The woman slowly lowers her rifle, aiming it away from Mackenzie and placing it in her lap. Her finger never leaving the trigger*

Grey Mother: "The others, are all gone. If you're here, things must be worse than they thought."

### Conversation

*The cutscene ends as the camera switches between Mackenzie and the Old woman, as the sound of the wind makes the old house creek*

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "What is this place? This town?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "This is Milton. A small town in the mountains. You're far from home."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "But...where are we on Great Bear? My plane came down in bad weather, and..."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "You're wherever you're supposed to be, Outsider. The Maker in his great wisdom has put you here with me. But what is his purpose? What is yours?"

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I'm looking for someone. She crashed with me, but I think she walked out...would have been when the lights first appeared."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "I'm not interested in your story, Outsider. I'm cold. Prove your worth to me and I'll think about telling you what I know. My fire will be out soon. Fetch me some firewood, and then maybe we'll talk more."

*A dialogue compass appears offering more choices to speak with the woman.*

- WHO ARE YOU?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "My name's Mackenzie. And you are..."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "The liars call me Grey Mother."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "So...I should..."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "You should call me Grey Mother also."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Right. So...if everyone left, why are you still here?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "They're afraid of me. And even if I could leave, I wouldn't. I was born here. I'll die here. This is the way it has to be."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "The way it has to be?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "You wouldn't understand loyalty, Mainlander."

- OTHERS?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "You said everyone is gone, what happened here?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Five days since the lights in the sky. Five days of hell. First they thought they'd be safe and then they realized - - they weren't ready for the end. Then they fled. None of them will make it."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "How do you know? I mean...you can't..."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "See? No, I can't. But I know what I hear. Sooner or later all their words, all their lies, find their way up to me."

- FUEL?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "So...fuel. Where can I find some?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Surely a big strapping man like you can find some firewood in a forest."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Right."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Take the axe in the woodshed out back, if you must. I know some of the townsfolks cut wood on the edge of town."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I'll be back."

- HOUSE?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "This is a big house. You live here by yourself?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "None of your business, Outsider. Just stay out of my private things and don't try to steal from me. Or you'll never find out what I know about your...friend."

- FINISHED

*Once the player selects this option, the final conversation plays*

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Come back when you've sorted out my firewood situation, if you come back at all."

*Another tutorial message pops up*

Many people you meet will be suspicious of you. But most of them have needs they may not be able to fulfill without your help. Helping them will earn you Trust, which will in turn unlock useful Knowledge, Locations, Items, and more. Check the character's Trust entry in your Journal to learn more about them, their needs, and how they can help you.</span>
