**Elevator Maintenance Note** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode. It is found in the admin office next to the <a href="./Aid%20Station%20Medical%20Locker%20Key.md" class="wikilink" title="Aid Station Medical Locker Key">Aid Station Medical Locker Key</a> in upper <a href="./Carter%20Hydro%20Dam.md" class="wikilink" title="Carter Hydro Dam">Carter Hydro Dam</a>.
