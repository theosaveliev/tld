**Emergency Kit Note** was a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle](./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode in [Paradise Meadows Farm](./Paradise%20Meadows%20Farm.md). It is considered [Cut/Discontinued Content](./Cut%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20Content.md), as it did not survive the transition from Predux Episode 1 to Redux Episode 1, as the Lockbox and [Lock Box Key (Predux)](./Lock%20Box%20Key%20%28Predux%29.md) needed to open it was removed from the game and replaced with a different Lockbox that [Hobbs](./Hobbs.md) finds.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Emergency Kit Note**_

This Emergency Kit is distributed, free of charge, by the Great Bear Earthquake Memorial Society (GBEMS). Our non-profit work helps guarantee full preparedness for any future emergencies on Great Bear.

Our Motto: WE REMEMBER.
