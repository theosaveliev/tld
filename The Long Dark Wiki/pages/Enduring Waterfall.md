<p><strong>Enduring Waterfall</strong> is a location to the southern section of the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>. <a href="./Deer.md" class="wikilink" title="Deer">Deer</a>, <a href="./Rabbit.md" class="wikilink" title="Rabbits">Rabbits</a> and <a href="./Cat%20Tail.md" class="wikilink" title="Cat Tails">Cat Tails</a> are found near the river. Following the river south will lead you to the transition to <a href="./Sundered%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Sundered Pass">Sundered Pass</a> and <a href="./Forsaken%20Airfield.md" class="wikilink" title="Forsaken Airfield">Forsaken Airfield</a>, which can be used for shelter when hunting in the area.</p>
<p>To the east is a hill that leads to <a href="./Dubious%20Overlook.md" class="wikilink" title="Dubious Overlook">Dubious Overlook</a> and <a href="./Smoker%27s%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Smoker&#39;s Cave">Smoker's Cave</a>. On the flipside, a tree bridge and be used to gain access to a cave behind the falls that leads to the upper western section of the map, close to <a href="./Craggy%20Passage.md" class="wikilink" title="Craggy Passage">Craggy Passage</a>.</p>
<p>The <a href="./The%20Security%20Chief%27s%20Remains.md" class="wikilink" title="The Security Chief&#39;s Remains">The Security Chief's Remains</a> must be buried here as part of the <a href="./Buried%20Echoes.md" class="wikilink" title="Buried Echoes">Buried Echoes</a> <a href="./Tales.md" class="wikilink" title="Tale">Tale</a>.</p>
