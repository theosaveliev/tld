**Enduring Waterfall** is a location to the southern section of the [Zone of Contamination](./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md). [Deer](./Deer.md), [Rabbits](./Rabbit.md) and [Cat Tails](./Cat%20Tail.md) are found near the river. Following the river south will lead you to the transition to [Sundered Pass](./Sundered%20Pass.md) and [Forsaken Airfield](./Forsaken%20Airfield.md), which can be used for shelter when hunting in the area.

To the east is a hill that leads to [Dubious Overlook](./Dubious%20Overlook.md) and [Smoker's Cave](./Smoker%27s%20Cave.md). On the flipside, a tree bridge and be used to gain access to a cave behind the falls that leads to the upper western section of the map, close to [Craggy Passage](./Craggy%20Passage.md).

The [The Security Chief's Remains](./The%20Security%20Chief%27s%20Remains.md) must be buried here as part of the [Buried Echoes](./Buried%20Echoes.md) [Tale](./Tales.md).
