# Enduring Waterfall

> {{Infobox/Location
> |title=
> |image=Enduring Waterfall.jpg
> |region=Zone of Contamination
> |map=Enduring Waterfall Location.png}}
> '''Enduring Waterfall''' is a location to the southern section of the [[Zone of Contamination]]. [[Deer]], [[Rabbit|Rabbits]] and [[Cat Tail|Cat Tails]] are found near the river. Following the river south will lead you to the transition to [[Sundered Pass]] and [[Forsaken Airfield]], which can be used for shelter when hunting in the area.
> 
> To the east is a hill that leads to [[Dubious Overlook]] and [[Smoker's Cave]]. On the flipside, a tree bridge and be used to gain access to a cave behind the falls that leads to the upper western section of the map, close to [[Craggy Passage]].
> 
> The [[The Security Chief's Remains]] must be buried here as part of the [[Buried Echoes]] [[Tales|Tale]].
