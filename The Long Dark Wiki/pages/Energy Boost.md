**Energy Boost** is a positive <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> gained by drinking <a href="./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Go! Energy Drink">Go! Energy Drink</a> or injecting yourself with an <a href="./Emergency%20Stim.md" class="wikilink" title="Emergency Stim">Emergency Stim</a>.

## Overview

Using either item will give the Energy boost benefit for several minutes. The two items have different effects.

### Emergency Stim

- <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a> and <a href="./Stamina.md" class="wikilink" title="Stamina">Stamina</a> are fully replenished (100%) and do not decrease for duration.
- If the energy boost effect is applied, the character will not lose <a href="./Stamina.md" class="wikilink" title="Stamina">Stamina</a> and will lose very little <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a>, allowing him to run away from predators, for example, or <a href="./Rope%20Climbing.md" class="wikilink" title="Rope Climbing">Rope Climbing</a> easily.
- Benefit lasts for 60 minutes or 75 minutes with the <a href="./Feats.md" class="wikilink" title="Feat">Feat</a> Straight to the Heart (time increased by 25%).

### Go! Energy Drink

- <a href="./Stamina.md" class="wikilink" title="Stamina">Stamina</a> is replenished (60%).
- <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a> is replenished (50%). At the same time, the "<a href="./Fatigue%20Reduced.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue Reduced">Fatigue Reduced</a>" effect restores 10% fatigue.
- <a href="./Stamina.md" class="wikilink" title="Stamina">Stamina</a> recovers faster due to replenished <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a>.
- Benefit lasts for 30 minutes or 37.5 minutes with the <a href="./Feats.md" class="wikilink" title="Feat">Feat</a> Straight to the Heart (time increased by 25%).

## Negative effects

After either the Energy drink or the Emergency Stim is consumed and the primary effects wore off, the player will experience some negative afflictions: <span class="thumb"><img src="../assets/Emergency_Stim_icon.png" loading="lazy" width="70" height="70" /></span>

- <a href="./Emergency%20Stim.md" class="wikilink" title="Emergency Stim">Emergency Stim</a> will empty your <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a> after use, meaning you either need to find a method of sleeping or use another drink or stim.

Energy Drink</a> on the other hand has its own negative affliction called <a href="./Headache.md" class="wikilink" title="Headache">Headache</a> which causes the player to have shots of blurry vision similar to <a href="./Pain.md" class="wikilink" title="Pain">Pain</a>.
