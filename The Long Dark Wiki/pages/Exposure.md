**Exposure** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is easiest to unlock when playing on <a href="./Pilgrim.md" class="wikilink" title="Pilgrim">Pilgrim</a>. The player must take 1 photo in each main <a href="./Region.md" class="wikilink" title="Region">Region</a> of the game in The Far Territory and Lower Great Bear. Taking a photo in <a href="./Region.md#Transition_Zones" class="wikilink" title="Transition Zones">Transition Zones</a> or indoors does not count towards unlocking this achievement.

Since these are 16 regions to cover, targeting this could be combined with achieving the <a href="./Shutterbug.md" class="wikilink" title="Shutterbug">Shutterbug</a>, which is to shoot 30 photos, 10 with each of the 3 film types. By changing the film type for every photo and shooting 2 photo per region, the player achieves both achievements combined.
