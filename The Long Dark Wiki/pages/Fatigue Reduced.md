**Fatigue Reduced** is a positive <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> gained by consuming certain <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Food And Drink">Food And Drink</a> or <a href="./First%20Aid.md" class="wikilink" title="First Aid">First Aid</a> items.

## Overview

Upon consumption of specific items, the player's <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a> is immediately restored by a set percentage and the player gains the *Fatigue Reduced* affliction. This buff lasts for a set period of time (dependant on the item consumed), lowering the overall rate of Fatigue loss and reducing the amount of Fatigue used while sprinting. *Fatigue Reduced* does not affect the rate of Fatigue loss when <a href="./Rope%20Climbing.md" class="wikilink" title="Rope Climbing">Rope Climbing</a>.
