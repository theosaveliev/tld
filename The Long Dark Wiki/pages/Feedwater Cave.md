# Feedwater Cave

> {{Infobox/Location

> '''Feedwater Cave''' is the only cave found in [[Transfer Pass]], and is located up the hill to the west of the [[Distempered Flatcars]] and south of the [[Loitering Marsh]]. A few [[Rabbit|Rabbits]] and [[Oak tree|Oak Trees]] can be located on the hill leading up to the cave, with 2 [[Rose Hip]] bushes outside the cave entrance. A piece of [[Cloth]] with 3 Rose Hip Buds will always spawn inside the cave to the right of the entrance (sometimes a corpse and other supplies will be near it). There is no bedding, requiring the player to bring a [[Bedroll]].
