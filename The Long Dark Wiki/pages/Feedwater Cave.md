<p><strong>Feedwater Cave</strong> is the only cave found in <a href="./Transfer%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Transfer Pass">Transfer Pass</a>, and is located up the hill to the west of the <a href="./Distempered%20Flatcars.md" class="wikilink" title="Distempered Flatcars">Distempered Flatcars</a> and south of the <a href="./Loitering%20Marsh.md" class="wikilink" title="Loitering Marsh">Loitering Marsh</a>. A few <a href="./Rabbit.md" class="wikilink" title="Rabbits">Rabbits</a> and <a href="./Oak%20Tree.md" class="wikilink" title="Oak Trees">Oak Trees</a> can be located on the hill leading up to the cave, with 2 <a href="./Rose%20Hip.md" class="wikilink" title="Rose Hip">Rose Hip</a> bushes outside the cave entrance. A piece of <a href="./Cloth.md" class="wikilink" title="Cloth">Cloth</a> with 3 Rose Hip Buds will always spawn inside the cave to the right of the entrance (sometimes a corpse and other supplies will be near it). There is no bedding, requiring the player to bring a <a href="./Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Bedroll">Bedroll</a>.</p>
