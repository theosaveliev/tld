**Feedwater Cave** is the only cave found in [Transfer Pass](./Transfer%20Pass.md), and is located up the hill to the west of the [Distempered Flatcars](./Distempered%20Flatcars.md) and south of the [Loitering Marsh](./Loitering%20Marsh.md). A few [Rabbits](./Rabbit.md) and [Oak Trees](./Oak%20Tree.md) can be located on the hill leading up to the cave, with 2 [Rose Hip](./Rose%20Hip.md) bushes outside the cave entrance. A piece of [Cloth](./Cloth.md) with 3 Rose Hip Buds will always spawn inside the cave to the right of the entrance (sometimes a corpse and other supplies will be near it). There is no bedding, requiring the player to bring a [Bedroll](./Bedroll.md).
