**Field Dressing Your Kill, Vol. I** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1">Episode 1</a> and <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode, and starts the <a href="./The%20Basics%20of%20Survival.md" class="wikilink" title="The Basics of Survival">The Basics of Survival</a> side mission. Completing the associated task rewards the blueprints for the <a href="./Rabbitskin%20Hat.md" class="wikilink" title="Rabbitskin Hat">Rabbitskin Hat</a> and <a href="./Rabbitskin%20Mitts.md" class="wikilink" title="Rabbitskin Mitts">Rabbitskin Mitts</a>.

#### Episode 1:

During the course of the story, the survivor can find the book *Field Dressing Your Kill, Vol. I* on a <a href="./Work%20Bench.md" class="wikilink" title="work bench">work bench</a> in the outside shed by <a href="./Paradise%20Meadows%20Farm.md" class="wikilink" title="Paradise Meadows Farm">Paradise Meadows Farm</a> in <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a> and are tasked to place a <a href="./Snare.md" class="wikilink" title="snare">snare</a> and catch at least one <a href="./Rabbit.md" class="wikilink" title="rabbit">rabbit</a>.

#### Episode 2:

If not collected in Episode 1, the book *Field Dressing Your Kill, Vol. I* can instead be found on a shelf on the first floor of the <a href="./Hunting%20Lodge.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Lodge">Hunting Lodge</a> in the <a href="./Broken%20Railroad.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Railroad">Broken Railroad</a> region. If the survivor has already achieved the goals of placing a <a href="./Snare.md" class="wikilink" title="snare">snare</a> and catching a <a href="./Rabbit.md" class="wikilink" title="rabbit">rabbit</a>, the mission may auto-complete at this time.
