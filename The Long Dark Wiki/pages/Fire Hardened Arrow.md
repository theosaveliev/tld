**Fire Hardened Arrows** are craftable ammuntion items in *The Long Dark*. They released in the free update of Part 2: Signal Void for the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> expansion.

## Overview

A **Fire Hardened Arrow** is a craftable ammunition for the <a href="./Survival%20Bow.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Bow">Survival Bow</a> made from a <a href="./Campfire.md" class="wikilink" title="Campfire">Campfire</a>. It's made from (5) sticks and (5) crow feathers, and provides experience for the <a href="./Archery.md" class="wikilink" title="Archery">Archery</a> skill when landed on a living target.

Fire Hardened Arrows are effective at hunting small game, such as, <a href="./Rabbit.md" class="wikilink" title="Rabbit">Rabbit</a> and <a href="./Ptarmigan.md" class="wikilink" title="Ptarmigan">Ptarmigan</a> which they kill in one hit, and are easier to craft than <a href="./Simple%20Arrow.md" class="wikilink" title="Simple Arrows">Simple Arrows</a>. However, Fire Hardened Arrows do not cause damage to large game, such as <a href="./Deer.md" class="wikilink" title="Deer">Deer</a>, <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolves">Wolves</a>, <a href="./Poisoned%20Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Poisoned Wolves">Poisoned Wolves</a>, <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolves">Timberwolves</a>, <a href="./Cougar.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougars">Cougars</a>, <a href="./Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear">Bear</a>, <a href="./Moose.md" class="wikilink" title="Moose">Moose</a>, and will break upon impact when hitting these larger targets.

Fire Hardened Arrows won't scare a charging Wolf, but direct hits on a Wolf made while undetected can cause it to flee (80% chance).

## Wear

The total durability of fire hardened arrows is variable and depends on a material table to determine it's condition loss; with the hardness of the material and the angle at which the arrow impacts the target determining the amount of degradation received. After a hit takes the arrow's condition below 0% it will break and turn into a <a href="./Broken%20Fire%20Hardened%20Arrow.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Fire Hardened Arrow">Broken Fire Hardened Arrow</a> then fall to the ground where it may be retrieved and harvested for 1 <a href="./Crow%20Feather.md" class="wikilink" title="Crow Feather">Crow Feather</a>.

Fire hardened arrows are unique in that they break instantly upon impact with all <a href="./Wildlife.md" class="wikilink" title="animals">animals</a> except rabbit and ptarmigan.

### Arrow condition loss on Impact (Increased by perpendicular hits)

| Material       | Snow | Flesh | Wood  | Stone/Metal |
|----------------|------|-------|-------|-------------|
| Condition Loss | 7.5% | 47.5% | 59.5% | 100%\*      |

\*Full condition fire hardened arrows may survive hard impacts.
