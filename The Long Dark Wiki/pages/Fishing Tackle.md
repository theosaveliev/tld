A **Fishing Tackle** is a <a href="./Tools.md" class="wikilink" title="Tool">Tool</a> that is required to catch fish while <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a>. It can also be used to repair clothing as part of the <a href="./Mending.md" class="wikilink" title="Mending">Mending</a> survival skill or craft items at the <a href="./Work%20Bench.md" class="wikilink" title="Work Bench">Work Bench</a>, in the same manner as a <a href="./Sewing%20Kit.md" class="wikilink" title="Sewing Kit">Sewing Kit</a>.

## Overview

Time penalty is added when using Fishing Tackle to craft or repair items, due to its less than efficient design as a sewing needle. While Ice Fishing, a <a href="./Fishing%20Lure%20%28disambiguation%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Lure">Fishing Lure</a> or <a href="./Fishing%20Bait.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Bait">Fishing Bait</a> can be added to the tackle to increase the odds of landing a fish. It has a chance to snap while fishing, breaking the item and losing Bait or Lures attached to it.

A Fishing Tackle cannot be repaired or harvested. It can be used as an ingredient in the creation of a <a href="./Fishing%20Tip-Up.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Tip-Up">Fishing Tip-Up</a>.
