<td><p>Has no shelter|} <strong>Flagman's Gap</strong> is a location in the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>.</p>
<p>This location consists of the road connecting the <a href="./Langston%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Langston Mine">Langston Mine</a> and <a href="./Transfer%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Transfer Pass">Transfer Pass</a> regions and two hills on both the right and left side. The vicinity of the road ahead is littered with contamination (which can give the player the <a href="./Chemical%20Poisoning%20Risk.md" class="wikilink" title="Chemical Poisoning Affliction">Chemical Poisoning Affliction</a>) by an overturned chemical freight wagon in one way and by spilled barrels in the other. (The pollution from the wagon can be avoided by a small footpath through the hills).</p>
