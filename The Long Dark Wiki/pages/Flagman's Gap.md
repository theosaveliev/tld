**Flagman's Gap** is a location in the [Zone of Contamination](./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md).

This location consists of the road connecting the [Langston Mine](./Langston%20Mine.md) and [Transfer Pass](./Transfer%20Pass.md) regions and two hills on both the right and left side. The vicinity of the road ahead is littered with contamination (which can give the player the [Chemical Poisoning Affliction](./Chemical%20Poisoning%20Risk.md)) by an overturned chemical freight wagon in one way and by spilled barrels in the other. (The pollution from the wagon can be avoided by a small footpath through the hills).
