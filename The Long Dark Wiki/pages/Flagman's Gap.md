# Flagman's Gap

> {{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Flagman's Gap.jpg|region=Zone of Contamination|location=West of [[Boss's Settlement]], south from [[Idle Camp]]|description=A small road passing through a gap between large boulders|map=Flagman's Gap Location.png}}'''Flagman's Gap''' is a location in the [[Zone of Contamination]].
> 
> This location consists of the road connecting the [[Langston Mine]] and [[Transfer Pass]] regions and two hills on both the right and left side. The vicinity of the road ahead is littered with contamination (which can give the player the [[Chemical Poisoning Risk|Chemical Poisoning Affliction]]) by an overturned chemical freight wagon in one way and by spilled barrels in the other. (The pollution from the wagon can be avoided by a small footpath through the hills).
