## Overview

Just a landmark with a rope leading in the canyon downside. The climbing is quite long but there is a ledge to stay and breathe for a while.

Due to its location near the broken [Mule Bridge](./Mule%20Bridge.md) (in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md)), this rope is useful for those who followed the main road to get to [Last Prospect](./Last%20Prospect.md) by allowing them to go into the ravine, down more a bit, and then re-ascending on the other side to gain access to the mines.
