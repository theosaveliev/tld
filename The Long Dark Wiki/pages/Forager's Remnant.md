> |map=Forager's Remnant Location.png}}'''Forger's Remnant''' is a location in [[Blackrock]] up the road into the nearby woods near the [[Power Plant]].
> 
> The area consists of a burned down, ruined house, a [[Work Bench]], and a stripped [[Truck]]. Some [[Coal]], [[Reclaimed Wood]], and a few other items can be found, as well as a [[Wood Stove]] in the ruined building. There is no shelter in this area, and [[Timberwolf]] packs tend to patrol the road and woods.
