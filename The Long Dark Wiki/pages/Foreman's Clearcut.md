**Foreman's Clearcut** is an area situated between [Cook's Farm](./Cook%27s%20Farm.md), which is close to the west, and [Bricklayer's Retreat](./Bricklayer%27s%20Retreat.md) to the east. The primary feature is a trailer that provides shelter. In [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md), it also contains a [workbench](./Work%20Bench.md) and some bed sheets as a place to [sleep](./Sleep.md). Unlike Cook's Farm, there is no fire barrel here.

The road outside the trailer leads to [Mule Bridge](./Mule%20Bridge.md) in the east, and to [Narrow Bridge](./Narrow%20Bridge.md) as well as [Blackrock Prison](./Blackrock%20Prison.md) in the west. The road makes generous curves around the hills in this area, giving foot travelers the option to take off-road shortcuts.

A path to [Timberwolf Mountain](./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md) begins just to the south of Foreman's Clearcut. The area in-between is often roamed by [timberwolves](./Timberwolf.md).
