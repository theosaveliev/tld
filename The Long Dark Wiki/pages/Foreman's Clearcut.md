> '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is an area situated between [[Cook's Farm]], which is close to the west, and [[Bricklayer's Retreat]] to the east. The primary feature is a trailer that provides shelter. In [[Wintermute]], it also contains a [[workbench]] and some bed sheets as a place to [[sleep]]. Unlike Cook's Farm, there is no fire barrel here.
> 
> The road outside the trailer leads to [[Mule Bridge]] in the east, and to [[Narrow Bridge]] as well as [[Blackrock Prison]] in the west. The road makes generous curves around the hills in this area, giving foot travelers the option to take off-road shortcuts.
> 
> A path to [[Timberwolf Mountain]] begins just to the south of Foreman's Clearcut. The area in-between is often roamed by [[timberwolves]].
