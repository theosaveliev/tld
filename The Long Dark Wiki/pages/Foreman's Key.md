The **Foreman's Key** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> found as part of the <a href="./Buried%20Echoes.md" class="wikilink" title="Buried Echoes">Buried Echoes</a> Tale in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a>. It is found in the mountains near <a href="./Old%20Corral.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Corral">Old Corral</a>, buried under the tail section of a crashed <a href="./Helicopter.md" class="wikilink" title="Helicopter">Helicopter</a> inside a <a href="./Supply%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Supply Cache">Supply Cache</a> (along with <a href="./Security%20Chief%27s%20Log%2031.md" class="wikilink" title="Security Chief&#39;s Log 31">Security Chief's Log 31</a>) that can be uncovered by an active <a href="./Handheld%20Shortwave%20Radio.md" class="wikilink" title="Handheld Shortwave Radio">Handheld Shortwave Radio</a>. To obtain the key, you first must first start the Tale by finding the hidden cache at the Boss's Settlement.

It is needed to open 2 doors:

- One in the <a href="./Headframe%20Building.md" class="wikilink" title="Headframe Building">Headframe Building</a> leading to the small office container to get to the <a href="./Bolt%20Cutters.md" class="wikilink" title="Bolt Cutters">Bolt Cutters</a>.
- The other inside the Langston Mine in one of the buildings at the bottom of the elevator shaft on Level 3.
