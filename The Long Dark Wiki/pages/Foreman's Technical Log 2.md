## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Foreman's Technical Log 2**_

[lazy handwriting, with some passages scratched out...]

Rüdiger’s here somewhere – the Chief’s logbook said as much. I’ll keep looking. There’s a settlement nearby I need to check out.

I’m gonna try and keep a record of all this, as long as I’m able. Photos, whatever I can. These notes. If I make it back I’ll need something to show for how this all went to shit.

_They\_ will want to know.

But I keep losing things, leaving them behind…can’t hold it together.
