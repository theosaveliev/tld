> '''Forest Cave''' is a small unmarked [[Warm Cave]] located in [[Timberwolf Mountain]] close to the [[Wing]]. 
> 
> == Overview ==
> Forest Cave, like all caves, provides some protection from the cold and from the wind. Some supplies and a backpack can be found inside the cave. 
> 
> A large [[Cedar Limb]], several [[Reishi Mushroom|reishi mushrooms]], and [[Rose Hip|rose hip]] bushes can be found in the area surrounding the cave.
> 
> Can be especially useful as an indoor shelter during/after the [[Carcass Harvesting|butchering]] of a successfully hunted [[Moose]] at the close by [[Wing]] spawn. It might even be recommended to lure him towards the cave before executing a successful killshot.
