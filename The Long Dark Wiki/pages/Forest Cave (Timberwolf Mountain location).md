**Forest Cave** is a small unmarked [Warm Cave](./Warm%20Cave.md) located in [Timberwolf Mountain](./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md) close to the [Wing](./Wing.md).

## Overview

Forest Cave, like all caves, provides some protection from the cold and from the wind. Some supplies and a backpack can be found inside the cave.

A large [Cedar Limb](./Cedar%20Limb.md), several [reishi mushrooms](./Reishi%20Mushroom.md), and [rose hip](./Rose%20Hip.md) bushes can be found in the area surrounding the cave.

Can be especially useful as an indoor shelter during/after the [butchering](./Carcass%20Harvesting.md) of a successfully hunted [Moose](./Moose.md) at the close by [Wing](./Wing.md) spawn. It might even be recommended to lure him towards the cave before executing a successful killshot.
