**Forest Talker Dam Note** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode. It is found on the corpse of a <a href="./Forest%20Talkers.md" class="wikilink" title="Forest Talker">Forest Talker</a> in the bathroom of the upper <a href="./Carter%20Hydro%20Dam.md" class="wikilink" title="Carter Hydro Dam">Carter Hydro Dam</a>.
