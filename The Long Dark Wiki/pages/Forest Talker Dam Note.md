**Forest Talker Dam Note** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 2](./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode. It is found on the corpse of a [Forest Talker](./Forest%20Talkers.md) in the bathroom of the upper [Carter Hydro Dam](./Carter%20Hydro%20Dam.md).

## Transcript

*The following text is transcribed from **Forest Talker Dam Note***

**Phil,

Jackson's seen some wolves around the area, and last week Emily said she thought she saw a bear skulking around the camp. I'm not convinced it isn't those Breyerhouse thugs trying to scare us off, but just in case, keep this flare gun handy. It's the closest thing we have to a firearm.

John.**
