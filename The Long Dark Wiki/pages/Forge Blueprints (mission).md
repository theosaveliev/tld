**Forge Blueprints (mission)** was a side mission of the Predux <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story and the last mission in the Predux storyline. It has since been replaced after the V1.47 Wintermute Redux version of the game, but is still playable to those that download versions V1.15, V1.30, or V1.37 of Steam version of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>* by using the <a href="./Time%20Capsule.md" class="wikilink" title="Time Capsule">Time Capsule</a>.

This mission was cut from the game, with <a href="./Forge.md" class="wikilink" title="Forge">Forge</a> blueprints being available from the start, and the Forge being used to craft a new <a href="./Spear%20Head.md" class="wikilink" title="Spear Head">Spear Head</a> for the <a href="./Bear%20Spear.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear Spear">Bear Spear</a>.

## Tasks

- Go to the Forge in Forlorn Muskeg.
- Locate the blueprints near the Forge.

## Walkthrough

### Trust

You will first need to raise <a href="./Jeremiah.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s">Jeremiah's</a> Trust to 75 and select the Survival Tools dialogue option. Jeremiah will comment that Mackenzie will need better tools to survive alone out on the Island, and will redirect him to an old <a href="./Forge.md" class="wikilink" title="Forge">Forge</a> near <a href="./Old%20Spence%20Family%20Homestead.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Spence Family Homestead">Old Spence Family Homestead</a>.

Travel to <a href="./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md" class="wikilink" title="Forlorn Muskeg">Forlorn Muskeg</a> and travel to the Homestead. When you arrive, loot inside a <a href="./Metal%20Container.md" class="wikilink" title="Metal Container">Metal Container</a> behind the Forge. Inside you will find a document <a href="./Forge%20Blueprints.md" class="wikilink" title="Forge Blueprints">Forge Blueprints</a>, a <a href="./Heavy%20Hammer.md" class="wikilink" title="Heavy Hammer">Heavy Hammer</a>, and 1 piece of <a href="./Coal.md" class="wikilink" title="Coal">Coal</a>. Plenty of Coal is nearby to raise the Forge's temperature to 150°C. Then craft either the <a href="./Improvised%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Knife">Improvised Knife</a> or <a href="./Improvised%20Hatchet.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Hatchet">Improvised Hatchet</a> and the mission will complete.

## Differences from Redux Episode 2

Several plot points and locations have been replaced/altered since the Redux re-release of Episode 2:

### Plot Points and Gameplay Changes

- This entire side mission was cut from the game, but using the <a href="./Forge.md" class="wikilink" title="Forge">Forge</a> was required to craft a new <a href="./Spear%20Head.md" class="wikilink" title="Spear Head">Spear Head</a> for the <a href="./Bear%20Spear.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear Spear">Bear Spear</a> in Redux.
  - The <a href="./Improvised%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Knife">Improvised Knife</a> and <a href="./Improvised%20Hatchet.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Hatchet">Improvised Hatchet</a> blueprints are automatically unlocked before using the Forge to fix the Bear Spear.
  - <a href="./Hunting%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Knives">Hunting Knives</a> and <a href="./Hatchet.md" class="wikilink" title="Hatchets">Hatchets</a> are also far more plentiful throughout Episode 1 and 2 in the Redux, with Hunting Knives being entirely absent in both Predux Episodes (until acquiring <a href="./Jeremiah%27s%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s Knife">Jeremiah's Knife</a>).
