**Forsaken Shore** is the 3rd [Modded Region](./The%20Long%20Development.md) added to the game, as part of The Long Development's unofficial region mods. The region connects from [Rocky Thoroughfare](./Rocky%20Thoroughfare.md).

## Overview

The region features two areas: an area near the coast, and an inland half with significant elevation differences. There is a [Chemical Hazard](./Chemical%20Hazard.md) present around one of islands just off the shore.

## Locations

There are a number of accessible locations in this region, including:

- Bisected Path
- Cliffside Cabin
- Coastal Community
- Derelict Oil Platform
- Despondent Crag (formerly a region, but merged into Forsaken Shore)
- Dom's Cave
- Fishing Lodge
- Kai's Hideout
- Loggers Residence
- Lower Pipeline
- Smoldered Respite
- Travelers Pass
- Upper Pipeline
- Upper Shelf

The region also has a beached fishing trawler, using the same model as The Harbinger near the [Last Resort Cannery](./Last%20Resort%20Cannery.md), containing a [Cargo Container](./Cargo%20Container.md) on board.
