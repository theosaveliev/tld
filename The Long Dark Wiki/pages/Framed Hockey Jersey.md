The **Framed Hockey Jersey** is a [Decorations](./Decorations.md) item that can be [Crafted](./Crafting.md) as part of the [Safehouse Customization](./Safehouse%20Customization.md) feature.

## Overview

A makeshift case displaying a Hockey Jersey, a Framed Hockey Jersey can be crafted at a [Furniture Workbench](./Furniture%20Workbench.md), but requires [Woodworking Tools](./Woodworking%20Tools.md) and 1 [Hockey Jersey](./Hockey%20Jersey.md).

Once crafted, the item is automatically placed into the player's inventory. It can only be placed on walls surfaces when in Customization Mode. It can later be picked up and placed back into the player's inventory or destroyed (yielding no resources).
