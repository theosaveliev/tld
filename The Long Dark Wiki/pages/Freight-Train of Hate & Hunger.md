**Freight-Train of Hate & Hunger** is a <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

### Redux EP 2

This is an unmissable story achievement achieved after completing the mission <a href="./What%20One%20Man%20Can%20Do.md" class="wikilink" title="What One Man Can Do">What One Man Can Do</a>. Simply avoid the <a href="./Old%20Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Bear">Old Bear</a> inside the <a href="./Old%20Bear%27s%20Den.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Bear&#39;s Den">Old Bear's Den</a> and find the <a href="./Bear%20Spear.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear Spear">Bear Spear</a>, then win the 3 Bear <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggles">Struggles</a> and kill the bear. The achievement is earned after the cutscene ends.

### Predux EP 2

Simply track and kill the Old Bear as part of <a href="./Jeremiah%27s%20Folly.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s Folly">Jeremiah's Folly</a> and the achievement will unlock.

## Trivia

The achievement name is from a quote from <a href="./Jeremiah.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah">Jeremiah</a> during <a href="./Survival%20School%20%28Predux%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival School (Predux)">Survival School (Predux)</a>:

"He'll come for you when you're not expecting it. But you won't miss it. It won't be subtle. It'll sound like a ton of muscle and bone bearing down on you like a freight train of hate and hunger. You stand your ground. Do not flinch. You raise your rifle. You sight him. And when you see between his eyes - - you squeeze that trigger."
