**Freight-Train of Hate &amp; Hunger**

[Freight Train of Hate &amp; Hunger icon](../assets/Freight-Train_of_Hate_%26_Hunger_icon.png)

**Description:** Kill the Old Bear.

**Freight-Train of Hate &amp; Hunger** is a [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in [Episode 2: Luminance Fugue](./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md) of _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

### Redux EP 2

This is an unmissable story achievement achieved after completing the mission [What One Man Can Do](./What%20One%20Man%20Can%20Do.md). Simply avoid the [Old Bear](./Old%20Bear.md) inside the [Old Bear's Den](./Old%20Bear%27s%20Den.md) and find the [Bear Spear](./Bear%20Spear.md), then win the 3 Bear [Struggles](./Struggle.md) and kill the bear. The achievement is earned after the cutscene ends.

### Predux EP 2

Simply track and kill the Old Bear as part of [Jeremiah's Folly](./Jeremiah%27s%20Folly.md) and the achievement will unlock.

## Trivia

The achievement name is from a quote from [Jeremiah](./Jeremiah.md) during [Survival School (Predux)](./Survival%20School%20%28Predux%29.md):

"He'll come for you when you're not expecting it. But you won't miss it. It won't be subtle. It'll sound like a ton of muscle and bone bearing down on you like a freight train of hate and hunger. You stand your ground. Do not flinch. You raise your rifle. You sight him. And when you see between his eyes - - you squeeze that trigger."
