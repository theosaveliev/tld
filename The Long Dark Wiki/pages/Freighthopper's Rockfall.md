|  | Has no shelter\|} / ## Overview / **Freighthopper's Rockfall** is a wooden rockfall shelter that protects a small section of the [Trans-Island Railway Line](./Trans-Island%20Railway%20Line.md). It is found in the [Far Range Branch Line](./Far%20Range%20Branch%20Line.md) region, and is located between the train tunnel leading to [Transfer Pass](./Transfer%20Pass.md) and [Remnant Cave](./Remnant%20Cave.md). The structure still remains standing despite being abandoned for decades, and a single Coal car has been left on the track under the wooden structure. / Despite its name, the rockfall shelter is designed to offer more shelter and protection for trains and their cars and not able-bodied survivors walking on foot. As such, the area provides very little protection from the elements.
