# Freighthopper's Rockfall

> {{Infobox/Location|title=Freighthopper's Rockfall|region=Far Range Branch Line|description=Location near the end of Far Range Branch Line|image=Freighthopper's Rockfall Title.jpg|map=Freighthopper's Rockfall Location.png}}
> 
> == Overview ==
> '''Freighthopper's Rockfall''' is a wooden rockfall shelter that protects a small section of the [[Trans-Island Railway Line]]. It is found in the [[Far Range Branch Line]] region, and is located between the train tunnel leading to [[Transfer Pass]] and [[Remnant Cave]]. The structure still remains standing despite being abandoned for decades, and a single Coal car has been left on the track under the wooden structure.
> 
> Despite its name, the rockfall shelter is designed to offer more shelter and protection for trains and their cars and not able-bodied survivors walking on foot. As such, the area provides very little protection from the elements. There is no loot in the area, aside from a few rabbits that may wander nearby.
> 
> The area does not appear as a location on the map, even when surveyed with [[Charcoal]].
