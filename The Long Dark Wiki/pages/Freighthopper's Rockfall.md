<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p><strong>Freighthopper's Rockfall</strong> is a wooden rockfall shelter that protects a small section of the <a href="./Trans-Island%20Railway%20Line.md" class="wikilink" title="Trans-Island Railway Line">Trans-Island Railway Line</a>. It is found in the <a href="./Far%20Range%20Branch%20Line.md" class="wikilink" title="Far Range Branch Line">Far Range Branch Line</a> region, and is located between the train tunnel leading to <a href="./Transfer%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Transfer Pass">Transfer Pass</a> and <a href="./Remnant%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Remnant Cave">Remnant Cave</a>. The structure still remains standing despite being abandoned for decades, and a single Coal car has been left on the track under the wooden structure.</p>
<p>Despite its name, the rockfall shelter is designed to offer more shelter and protection for trains and their cars and not able-bodied survivors walking on foot. As such, the area provides very little protection from the elements. There is no loot in the area, aside from a few rabbits that may wander nearby.</p>
<p>The area does not appear as a location on the map, even when surveyed with <a href="./Charcoal.md" class="wikilink" title="Charcoal">Charcoal</a>.</p>
