**Frigid Bones** is an [Affliction](./Afflictions.md) found within *[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)*. It is one of 6 unique Afflictions that can be encountered only within Misery Mode experience.

## Overview

> “**You are chilled to the core.  
   
 Your Feels Like temperature is permanently reduced, and it takes you longer to warm up.**”
>
> — In-game Description

Frigid Bones is a permanent Affliction unique to [Misery Mode](./Misery.md) which occurs from Stages 2-4 of Misery. It reduces the survivor's Feels Like temperature and increases the time required to fill the Warmth meter.

## Effects

- -5°C [Feels Like](./Feels%20Like.md) temperature penalty (Outdoors and Indoors)
- [Warmth](./Warmth.md) meter recovers 50% slower
