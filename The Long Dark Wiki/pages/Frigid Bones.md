Frigid Bones is a permanent Affliction unique to <a href="./Misery.md" class="wikilink" title="Misery Mode">Misery Mode</a> which occurs from Stages 2-4 of Misery. It reduces the survivor's Feels Like temperature and increases the time required to fill the Warmth meter.

## Effects

- -5°C <a href="./Feels%20Like.md" class="wikilink" title="Feels Like">Feels Like</a> temperature penalty (Outdoors and Indoors)
- <a href="./Warmth.md" class="wikilink" title="Warmth">Warmth</a> meter recovers 50% slower
