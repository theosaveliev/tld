**Frostbite** is a physical Injury acquired from having bare skin exposed to the cold for too long and not managing [Frostbite Risk](./Frostbite%20Risk.md), and permanently reduces overall [Condition](./Condition.md) by 10%.

## Process

Frostbite Risk will only appear when 1 of 5 extremities of bare skin is exposed directly to the cold, or by wearing [Clothing](./Clothing.md) that is currently Frozen on a particular body part. Frostbite Risk will continue to increase until said body part is either covered up with unfrozen Clothing, or it is sufficiently warmed at a fire until the Risk subsides. Should a body part continue to remain exposed to the freezing cold, Frostbite will develop, and can potentially begin Frostbite Risk on that limb again.

## Affliction

> “**You have sustained frostbite. Your general condition is permanently reduced.**”
>
> — In-game Description

Each instance of acquired Frostbite reduces your maximum [Condition](./Condition.md) level by 10%. Frostbite is currently the only affliction to have no cure (Unless the player [Cheats Death](./Cheat%20Death.md)). Players should take care to pay attention to their [Frostbite Risk](./Frostbite%20Risk.md), so that Frostbite can be prevented.

- A player can only receive Frostbite for 5 body parts (Head, Torso, Hands, Legs, Feet), however you can get 5 Frostbite Afflictions for every body part, resulting in a death at 10 instances of Frostbite (or 11 temporarily with the extra 15% acquired from [Well Fed](./Well%20Fed.md) and [Nourished](./Nourished.md), until Nourished wears off).
- Theoretically, if one's Condition were high enough, it would be possible to acquire 25 instances of Frostbite, totaling for a maximum of -250% Condition loss.
- The Condition bar loss from Frostbite stacks with [Burns](./Burns.md) or [Chemical Poisoning](./Chemical%20Poisoning.md).
