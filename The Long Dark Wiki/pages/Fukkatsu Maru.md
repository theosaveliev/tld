The **Fukkatsu Maru** is an abandoned Japanese cargo ship that became trapped in ice after drifting into <a href="./Desperation%20Bay.md" class="wikilink" title="Desperation Bay">Desperation Bay</a>. It is explored during the mission <a href="./A%20Cry%20For%20Help.md" class="wikilink" title="A Cry For Help">A Cry For Help</a>.

## Overview

It has an exterior deck that can be accessed by using on of the dropped anchors as <a href="./Rope%20Climbing.md" class="wikilink" title="Rope Climbing">Rope Climbing</a> spot.

The interior consists of the Fukkatsu Maru Cargo Hold, whilst the upper decks include the Crew Quarters, Mess Hall, Bathrooms, an Infirmary, the Bridge, and the Captain's Quarters.

## Trivia

The name "Fukkatsu Maru" is Japanese and likely written as ふっかつ丸 or possibly 復活丸 (post-World War II Japanese vessels tend to use hiragana rather than kanji in their main names). "Fukkatsu" (復活 or ふっかつ) means "revival", while "maru" (丸), usually meaning "circle", is a common suffix attached to the names of merchant or civilian ships. Taken literally, the name could be read as "circle of revival".
