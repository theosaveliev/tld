**Fuse Box** is a unique storage container only found in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a> as part of the <a href="./Aftermath.md" class="wikilink" title="Aftermath">Aftermath</a> mission. There are 3 of them in the lower level of the <a href="./Cinder%20Hills%20Coal%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Cinder Hills Coal Mine">Cinder Hills Coal Mine</a>, and are part of a puzzle needed to power the elevator to escape. The damaged ones contain a <a href="./Burned%20Out%20Fuse.md" class="wikilink" title="Burned Out Fuse">Burned Out Fuse</a> and need a new <a href="./Replacement%20Fuse.md" class="wikilink" title="Replacement Fuse">Replacement Fuse</a>. Replacing the fuse will turn the power back on to the elevator, but will also electrify any nearby water if the <a href="./Aurora.md" class="wikilink" title="Aurora">Aurora</a> is active. **Lua execution error in Module:Navbox function navbox**
