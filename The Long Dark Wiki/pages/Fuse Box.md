**Fuse Box** is a unique storage container only found in [Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) as part of the [Aftermath](./Aftermath.md) mission. There are 3 of them in the lower level of the [Cinder Hills Coal Mine](./Cinder%20Hills%20Coal%20Mine.md), and are part of a puzzle needed to power the elevator to escape. The damaged ones contain a [Burned Out Fuse](./Burned%20Out%20Fuse.md) and need a new [Replacement Fuse](./Replacement%20Fuse.md).
