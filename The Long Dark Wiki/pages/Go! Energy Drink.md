**Go! Energy Drink** is a <a href="./First%20Aid.md" class="wikilink" title="First Aid">First Aid</a> item in *The Long Dark*.

## Overview

Consuming Go! Energy Drink restores hydration and a small amount of calories. It also provides the benefits <a href="./Energy%20Boost.md" class="wikilink" title="Energy Boost">Energy Boost</a> for 30 minutes, and <a href="./Fatigue%20Reduced.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue Reduced">Fatigue Reduced</a> for 45 min.

When the duration of Energy Boost ends, it causes the <a href="./Headache.md" class="wikilink" title="Headache">Headache</a> <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> for 20 minutes.

Upon consumption, Fatigue is increased by 60% (to a maximum of 100%), but once the benefits expire 50% Fatigue will be lost immediately. This makes consuming Go! while above 40% Fatigue a waste, and above 50% a net loss in Fatigue. Care should be taken to the remaining duration of Go! Energy Drink's benefit, since if it expires during demanding tasks (such as <a href="./Rope%20Climbing.md" class="wikilink" title="Rope Climbing">Rope Climbing</a>), Fatigue may drop to 0% resulting in a dangerous situation.

Go! Energy Drink does not spawn on Interloper difficulty.
