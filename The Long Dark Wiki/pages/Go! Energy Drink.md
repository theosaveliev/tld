**Go! Energy Drink** is a [First Aid](./First%20Aid.md) item in *The Long Dark*.

## Overview

Consuming Go! Energy Drink restores hydration and a small amount of calories. It also provides the benefits [Energy Boost](./Energy%20Boost.md) for 30 minutes, and [Fatigue Reduced](./Fatigue%20Reduced.md) for 45 min.

When the duration of Energy Boost ends, it causes the [Headache](./Headache.md) [Affliction](./Afflictions.md) for 20 minutes.

Upon consumption, Fatigue is increased by 60% (to a maximum of 100%), but once the benefits expire 50% Fatigue will be lost immediately. This makes consuming Go! while above 40% Fatigue a waste, and above 50% a net loss in Fatigue. Care should be taken to the remaining duration of Go! Energy Drink's benefit, since if it expires during demanding tasks (such as [Rope Climbing](./Rope%20Climbing.md)), Fatigue may drop to 0% resulting in a dangerous situation.

Go! Energy Drink does not spawn on Interloper difficulty.
