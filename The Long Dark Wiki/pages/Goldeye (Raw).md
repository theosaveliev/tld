**Goldeye (Raw)** is a raw [food](./Food%20And%20Drink.md) item in _The Long Dark_. Goldeye have a minimum raw weight of 0.5 kg and a maximum raw weight of 2.1 kg.

Goldeye can be harvested to obtain [Fishing Bait](./Fishing%20Bait.md), yielding 3 units per kilogram harvested.
