**Goldeye (Raw)** is a raw <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in *The Long Dark*. Goldeye have a minimum raw weight of 0.5 kg and a maximum raw weight of 2.1 kg.

Goldeye can be harvested to obtain <a href="./Fishing%20Bait.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Bait">Fishing Bait</a>, yielding 3 units per kilogram harvested.
