# Goods and Services

**Categories:** Achievements · **Source:** [Fandom](https://thelongdark.fandom.com/wiki/Goods_and_Services)

**Goods and Services**

![Goods and Services icon](../assets/Goods_and_Services_icon.png)

**Description:** Achieve the highest Trust level with the Trader.

**Goods and Services** is an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

This achievement is unlocked when the player reaches maximum Trust (500) with the [Trader](./Trader.md). The achievement is easiest to unlock when playing on [Pilgrim](./Pilgrim.md).

Note: The player does not need to complete [Sutherland's Tale](./Sutherland%27s%20Tale.md) in order to fully raise Trust with the trader.
