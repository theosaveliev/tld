**Goods and Services** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is unlocked when the player reaches maximum Trust (500) with the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a>. The achievement is easiest to unlock when playing on <a href="./Pilgrim.md" class="wikilink" title="Pilgrim">Pilgrim</a>.

Note: The player does not need to complete <a href="./Sutherland%27s%20Tale.md" class="wikilink" title="Sutherland&#39;s Tale">Sutherland's Tale</a> in order to fully raise Trust with the trader.
