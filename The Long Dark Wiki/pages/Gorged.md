# Gorged

> {{Infobox/Affliction|image=Gorged icon.png|category=[[Afflictions]]|caused by=Consume [[Food And Drink]]|+/- effect=Negative|type (Benefit/Injury/Disease)=Injury|benefit duration=|release=21 June 2023|update=2.17|affliction duration=Until Recovery Time passes|recoverytime=1 Hour}}{{TFTFT}}'''Gorged''' is a unique [[Afflictions|Affliction]] in ''[[The Long Dark]]'' only encountered in [[Survival Mode]] when consuming certain [[Cooking]] Recipes. It was added in the Part 3 update to the [[Tales from the Far Territory]].
> ==Overview==
> {{Quote2|You feel weighed down after eating a meal.}}
> Gorged is acquired from consuming certain recipes that affect a survivor by temporarily decreasing their maximum [[Stamina]] by 15% for 1 hour.
> 
> The following foods, when consumed, grant the Gorged Affliction as a side effect:
> 
> * {{Iconlink|Breyerhouse Pie}}
> *{{Iconlink|Dockworker's Pie}}
> The effect of this Affliction does not stack, and each new application will not override the previous Affliction. Partially eating the previously mentioned foods only adds a partial amount of Gorged Recovery Time.
