<td><p>|}<strong>Gorged</strong> is a unique <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> in <em><a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a></em> only encountered in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> when consuming certain <a href="./Cooking.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking">Cooking</a> Recipes. <h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<table>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td style="vertical-align: top; font-size: 3em; color: #5d5980; padding-right: 10px"><p>“</p></td>
<td><p><span style="font-style:italic">You feel weighed down after eating a meal.</span></p></td>
<td style="vertical-align: bottom; font-size: 3em; color: #5d5980; padding-left: 10px"><p>”</p></td>
</tr>
</tbody>

<p>Gorged is acquired from consuming certain recipes that affect a survivor by temporarily decreasing their maximum <a href="./Stamina.md" class="wikilink" title="Stamina">Stamina</a> by 15% for 1 hour.</p>
<p>The following foods, when consumed, grant the Gorged Affliction as a side effect:</p>
<ul>
<li><img src="../assets/Breyerhouse_Pie_icon.png" loading="lazy" width="29" height="29" alt="Breyerhouse_Pie_icon.png" /> <a href="./Breyerhouse%20Pie.md" class="wikilink" title="Breyerhouse Pie">Breyerhouse Pie</a></li>
<li><img src="../assets/Dockworker%27s_Pie_icon.png" loading="lazy" width="29" height="29" alt="Dockworker&#39;s_Pie_icon.png" /> <a href="./Dockworker%27s%20Pie.md" class="wikilink" title="Dockworker&#39;s Pie">Dockworker's Pie</a></li>
</ul>
<p>The effect of this Affliction does not stack, and each new application will not override the previous Affliction. Partially eating the previously mentioned foods only adds a partial amount of Gorged Recovery Time.</p>
