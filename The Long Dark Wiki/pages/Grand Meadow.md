**Grand Meadow** is a small plain close to the [Power Plant](./Power%20Plant.md). It is located next to the road towards [Narrow Bridge](./Narrow%20Bridge.md), by the turn-off for [Forager's Remnant](./Forager%27s%20Remnant.md). The road is blocked by a landslide at [Bear's Bend](./Bear%27s%20Bend.md) further south.

Packs of [timberwolves](./Timberwolf.md) often roam this area. An abandoned car provides some refuge.
