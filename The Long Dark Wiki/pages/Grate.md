Wintermute

Yes A **Grate** is a ventilation cover found inside [Prepper Cache](./Prepper%20Cache.md) bunkers. It is often located near a corner of the room and can be opened to reveal concealed loot. Grates often conceal [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) notes in many [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) Episodes and can also hide loot such as:

- [Revolver](./Revolver.md)
- [Revolver Ammunition](./Revolver%20Ammunition.md)
- [Rifle Ammunition](./Rifle%20Ammunition.md)
- [Hunting Knife](./Hunting%20Knife.md)
- [Emergency Stim](./Emergency%20Stim.md)
