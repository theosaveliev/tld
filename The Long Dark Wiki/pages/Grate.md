# Grate

> {{Infobox/Item
> |image= Grate.jpg
> |wintermute=yes}}A '''Grate''' is a ventilation cover found inside [[Prepper Cache]] bunkers. It is often located near a corner of the room and can be opened to reveal concealed loot. Grates often conceal [[Collectibles|Collectible]] notes in many [[Wintermute]] Episodes and can also hide loot such as:
> 
> * [[Revolver]]
> * [[Revolver Ammunition]]
> * [[Rifle Ammunition]]
> * [[Hunting Knife]]
> * [[Emergency Stim]]
