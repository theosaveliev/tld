# Great Bear Highway

**Great Bear Highway** (also known as **Highway 15**) is a highway that intersects along the coast line of [Great Bear Island](./Great%20Bear%20Island.md).

## Overview

According to the [Town of Milton](./Town%20of%20Milton.md) leaflet, the Highway was created during the mid-1980's, as Great Bear began to further industrialize and expand, thus requiring the creation of the Great Bear Highway. As an unfortunate consequence, the Town of [Milton](./Milton.md) would no longer serve as a gateway into the southern section of the Island, being bypassed entirely by the coastal highway.

In the game, the highway crosses through four regions. From west to east, these are:

- [Bleak Inlet](./Bleak%20Inlet.md)
- [Coastal Highway](./Coastal%20Highway.md)
- [Crumbling Highway](./Crumbling%20Highway.md) (sometimes called "Old Island Connector")
- [Desolation Point](./Desolation%20Point.md)
