# Great Bear Highway

<div>

**Great Bear Highway** (also known as **Highway 15**) is a highway that intersects along the coast line of <a href="./Great%20Bear%20Island.md" class="wikilink" title="Great Bear Island">Great Bear Island</a>.

## Overview

According to the <a href="./Town%20of%20Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Town of Milton">Town of Milton</a> leaflet, the Highway was created during the mid-1980's, as Great Bear began to further industrialize and expand, thus requiring the creation of the Great Bear Highway. As an unfortunate consequence, the Town of <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a> would no longer serve as a gateway into the southern section of the Island, being bypassed entirely by the coastal highway.

In the game, the highway crosses through four regions. From west to east, these are:

- <a href="./Bleak%20Inlet.md" class="wikilink" title="Bleak Inlet">Bleak Inlet</a>
- <a href="./Coastal%20Highway.md" class="wikilink" title="Coastal Highway">Coastal Highway</a>
- <a href="./Crumbling%20Highway.md" class="wikilink" title="Crumbling Highway">Crumbling Highway</a> (sometimes called "Old Island Connector")
- <a href="./Desolation%20Point.md" class="wikilink" title="Desolation Point">Desolation Point</a>

The towns of <a href="./Perseverance%20Mills.md" class="wikilink" title="Perseverance Mills">Perseverance Mills</a> and Port Mary could be linked to the rest of the island by the highway as well, but this is unconfirmed.**Lua execution error in Module:Navbox function navbox**
