**Great Bear Wolf Behaviour Study** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> found in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story as part of the <a href="./Blackrock%20Rumours.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Rumours">Blackrock Rumours</a> side mission. It is found in a cave of a <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf">Wolf</a> on a dead researcher corpse.
