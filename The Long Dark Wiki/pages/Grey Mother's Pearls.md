**Grey Mother's Pearls** are a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item given to the <a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will Mackenzie">Will Mackenzie</a> in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1">Episode 1</a> of <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> to complete the <a href="./One%20Last%20Thing.md" class="wikilink" title="One Last Thing">One Last Thing</a> side mission.

During the course of the mission, Mackenzie delivers the pearls to the <a href="./Graveyard.md" class="wikilink" title="gravesite">gravesite</a> of <a href="./Lilith%20Barker.md" class="wikilink" title="Lilith Barker">Lilith Barker</a> across from <a href="./St.%20Christopher%27s%20Church.md" class="wikilink" title="St. Christopher&#39;s Church">St. Christopher's Church</a> outside <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a>.

## Trivia

<a href="./Grey%20Mother.md" class="wikilink" title="Grey Mother">Grey Mother</a> reveals that these pearls were her mother's and that she gave them to Lily Barker on her 16th birthday, the same year that she died.
