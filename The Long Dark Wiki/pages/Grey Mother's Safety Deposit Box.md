**Grey Mother's Safety Deposit Box** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1">Episode 1</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode during the <a href="./The%20Grey%20Mother.md" class="wikilink" title="The Grey Mother">The Grey Mother</a> mission.

During the course of the mission, <a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will Mackenzie">Will Mackenzie</a> retrieves Grey Mother's Safety Deposit Box from the <a href="./Milton%20Credit%20Union.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton Credit Union">Milton Credit Union</a> in <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a>, and returns it to <a href="./Grey%20Mother.md" class="wikilink" title="Grey Mother">Grey Mother</a> at <a href="./Milton%20House%20%28Grey%20Mother%27s%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton House (Grey Mother&#39;s)">Milton House (Grey Mother's)</a>.
