<p><strong>Grip's Cave</strong> is a small cave located in <a href="./Forsaken%20Airfield.md" class="wikilink" title="Forsaken Airfield">Forsaken Airfield</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>The cave is located directly north of <a href="./Junker%27s%20Paddock.md" class="wikilink" title="Junker&#39;s Paddock">Junker's Paddock</a>, but on the other side of the mountain behind <a href="./Stony%20Passage.md" class="wikilink" title="Stony Passage">Stony Passage</a>. It is best reached by taking the path south of <a href="./Sudden%20Washout.md" class="wikilink" title="Sudden Washout">Sudden Washout</a>. It is an isolated cave, requiring the player to turn back the way they came once they are finished exploring the area.</p>
<p>The cave doesn't have a bed or campfire, but spawns a frozen corpse alongside a plastic container, a <a href="./Wolf%20Carcass.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf Carcass">Wolf Carcass</a>, and a <a href="./Survival%20Bow.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Bow">Survival Bow</a> with several arrows, which may or may not be broken.</p>
<p>The cave is a source of <a href="./Coal.md" class="wikilink" title="Coal">Coal</a>.</p>
