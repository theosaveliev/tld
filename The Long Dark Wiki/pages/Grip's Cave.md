## Overview

The cave is located directly north of [Junker's Paddock](./Junker%27s%20Paddock.md), but on the other side of the mountain behind [Stony Passage](./Stony%20Passage.md). It is best reached by taking the path south of [Sudden Washout](./Sudden%20Washout.md). It is an isolated cave, requiring the player to turn back the way they came once they are finished exploring the area.

The cave doesn't have a bed or campfire, but spawns a frozen corpse alongside a plastic container, a [Wolf Carcass](./Wolf%20Carcass.md), and a [Survival Bow](./Survival%20Bow.md) with several arrows, which may or may not be broken.

The cave is a source of [Coal](./Coal.md).
