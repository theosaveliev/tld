> '''Grip's Cave''' is a small cave located in [[Forsaken Airfield]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> The cave is located directly north of [[Junker's Paddock]], but on the other side of the mountain behind [[Stony Passage]]. It is best reached by taking the path south of [[Sudden Washout]]. It is an isolated cave, requiring the player to turn back the way they came once they are finished exploring the area.
> 
> The cave doesn't have a bed or campfire, but spawns a frozen corpse alongside a plastic container, a [[Wolf Carcass]], and a [[Survival Bow]] with several arrows, which may or may not be broken.
> 
> The cave is a source of [[Coal]].
