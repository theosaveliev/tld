A **Hand Cart** is an interactable object that can be harvested with a [Hacksaw](./Hacksaw.md) and yields 4 [Scrap Metal](./Scrap%20Metal.md) after 1 hour 30 minutes. They can mainly be found near industrial areas.

| Object | Scrap Metal | Time | Calories | Condition (of Hacksaw) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hand Cart | 4 | 1 hour 30 minutes | 270 | -5% |
