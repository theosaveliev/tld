**Hank's Journal - Part Two** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode during the <a href="./Hank%27s%20Hatch.md" class="wikilink" title="Hank&#39;s Hatch">Hank's Hatch</a> side mission.

## Transcript

<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">

The following text is transcribed from **Hank's Journal - Part Two**

</div>

<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

Things have not turned out the way I hoped. I guess that's why they say "hope is not a plan". I was on my way to drop some supplies off in the bunker, but wolves hunted me the whole way here and I couldn't escape them. One managed to take a chunk out of my leg just before I could get in here and slam the hatch door. It feels like it's on fire -- I think it's infected. And now I hear something like scratching on the hatch door. I'm afraid to open up. Angela -- I pray to god it isn't you or your mother. But it can't be -- it's just too soon. I'm just going to hold out down here for a bit, and will check when it's daylight again. Just have to find a way to keep track of the time down here...

</div>
