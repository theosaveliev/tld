**Hank's Lock Box Key** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode during the <a href="./Hank%27s%20Hatch.md" class="wikilink" title="Hank&#39;s Hatch">Hank's Hatch</a> side mission. It is found on the dead body near the <a href="./Unnamed%20Pond.md" class="wikilink" title="Unnamed Pond">Unnamed Pond</a> and starts the side mission.
