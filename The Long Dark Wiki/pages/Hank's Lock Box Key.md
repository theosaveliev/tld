**Hank's Lock Box Key** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 2](./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode during the [Hank's Hatch](./Hank%27s%20Hatch.md) side mission. It is found on the dead body near the [Unnamed Pond](./Unnamed%20Pond.md) and starts the side mission.
