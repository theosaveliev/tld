**Happy Harvester** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after harvesting twenty-five <a href="./Cat%20Tail.md" class="wikilink" title="cat tail plants">cat tail plants</a>, <a href="./Rose%20Hip.md" class="wikilink" title="rose hip">rose hip</a> bushes, <a href="./Reishi%20Mushroom.md" class="wikilink" title="reishi mushrooms">reishi mushrooms</a> and <a href="./Old%20Man%27s%20Beard%20Lichen.md" class="wikilink" title="old man&#39;s beard lichens">old man's beard lichens</a> in a single game.

Note: That is twenty-five harvest actions of each type. Rose hip bushes currently yield exactly eight rose hips per plant, so a total of twenty-five harvests would yield 200 rose hips total. Mushrooms and lichens count individually since they are individual actions, even though they may grow on the same stump or tree. The game has no counter anywhere of how far along this achievement the player is.

See the individual pages for where to look for these plants.[^1]
