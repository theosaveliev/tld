Haunted Mind is a permanent Affliction unique to <a href="./Misery.md" class="wikilink" title="Misery Mode">Misery Mode</a> which occurs from Stages 2-4 of Misery. It reduces the survivor's Condition recovery while Sleeping and limits the maximum number of hours they can sleep consecutively.

## Effects

- Maximum consecutive <a href="./Sleep.md" class="wikilink" title="Sleep">Sleep</a> hours capped at 7 (normally 12)
- -50% <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> recovered by Sleep
