**Headache** is an Affliction that is inflicted on the survivor after consuming certain <a href="./First%20Aid.md" class="wikilink" title="First Aid">First Aid</a> or <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Food And Drink">Food And Drink</a> items.

## Overview

While the Affliction is active, the entire screen will occasionally distort similar to the <a href="./Pain.md" class="wikilink" title="Pain">Pain</a> Affliction, but with a more intense distortion. The screen will zoom in and out slightly to simulate an intense headache until the affliction wears off. The player also cannot use <a href="./Research%20Book.md" class="wikilink" title="Research Books">Research Books</a> until the Affliction passes. Unlike the Pain Affliction, this visual effect can't be treated with <a href="./Painkillers.md" class="wikilink" title="Painkillers">Painkillers</a> or <a href="./Rose%20Hip%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Rose Hip Tea">Rose Hip Tea</a>.

Headaches have no long-term effects on the player, only serving as a simple visual distraction and a slight annoyance. If in a safe spot, the player can simply use the "Pass Time" option or <a href="./Sleep.md" class="wikilink" title="Sleep">Sleep</a> to make the Affliction pass quickly.

Drinking a <a href="./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Go! Energy Drink">Go! Energy Drink</a> or eating <a href="./Caffeine%20Pills.md" class="wikilink" title="Caffeine Pills">Caffeine Pills</a> will produce a Headache. When either are consumed, the edges of the player's screen will distort and blur, producing a chromatic aberration effect.

- When the <a href="./Energy%20Boost.md" class="wikilink" title="Energy Boost">Energy Boost</a> effect of a consumed Go! Energy Drink has expired, a Headache Affliction begins. As a side effect, all <a href="./Stamina.md" class="wikilink" title="Stamina">Stamina</a> is instantly lost when a Headache begins.
- Caffeine Pills instantly give a 3 hour long Headache once consumed.
- A slight blue filter appears over the screen when a Headache begins after consuming First Aid items.
