<span id="overview"></span><span id="Overview"></span>

## Overview

While the Affliction is active, the entire screen will occasionally distort similar to the [Pain](./Pain.md) Affliction, but with a more intense distortion. The screen will zoom in and out slightly to simulate an intense headache until the affliction wears off. The player also cannot use [Research Books](./Research%20Book.md) until the Affliction passes. Unlike the Pain Affliction, this visual effect can't be treated with [Painkillers](./Painkillers.md) or [Rose Hip Tea](./Rose%20Hip%20Tea.md).

Headaches have no long-term effects on the player, only serving as a simple visual distraction and a slight annoyance. If in a safe spot, the player can simply use the "Pass Time" option or [Sleep](./Sleep.md) to make the Affliction pass quickly.

## First Aid Headaches

> “**You are suffering from a head-ache. It's not debilitating, but the occasional shot of pain and blurred vision can be distracting.**”
>
> — Go! Energy Drink description

> **Head-ache from taking Caffeine Pills.**
>
>
> — Caffeine Pills description

Drinking a [Go! Energy Drink](./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md) or eating [Caffeine Pills](./Caffeine%20Pills.md) will produce a Headache. When either are consumed, the edges of the player's screen will distort and blur, producing a chromatic aberration effect.

- When the [Energy Boost](./Energy%20Boost.md) effect of a consumed Go! Energy Drink has expired, a Headache Affliction begins. As a side effect, all [Stamina](./Stamina.md) is instantly lost when a Headache begins.
- Caffeine Pills instantly give a 3 hour long Headache once consumed.
- A slight blue filter appears over the screen when a Headache begins after consuming First Aid items.

| [Go! Energy Drink icon](../assets/Go!_Energy_Drink_icon.png) | [Go! Energy Drink](./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md) | 20 minutes |
| [Caffeine Pills icon](../assets/Caffeine_Pills_icon.png) | [Caffeine Pills](./Caffeine%20Pills.md) | 3 hours |

## Food and Drink Headaches

> “**Head-ache from eating a sweet dish.**”
>
> — Food Recipes description

Certain Recipes will also produce a Headache. Partially consuming Recipes will not always produce a Headache however, but fully consuming the item will guarantee one. Unlike First Aid items, Recipes do not produce a blue tint on the screen when a Headache begins.
