**Highway Robbery** is a side mission started in [Episode 1](./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md) of the [story](./Wintermute.md). In the mission, the survivor investigates an abandoned truck on [Spruce Falls Bridge](./Spruce%20Falls%20Bridge.md) after finding a [note](./Highway%20Robbery.md) describing a rich Mainlander abandoning a truck on the bridge. The mission can only be completed in [Episode 1](./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md), prior to the survivor [leaving Milton](./Paradise%20Lost.md).

## Official description

> “**You found a note describing the possible location of emergency supplies near a bridge outside of Milton.**”

## Overview

To start the mission, the survivor has to find one of the corresponding note at the [Orca Gas Station](./Orca%20Gas%20Station.md) outside [Milton](./Milton.md):

- [Highway Robbery](./Highway%20Robbery.md)

Once the [note](./Highway%20Robbery.md) is found, the survivor can start their investigation

## Locating emergency supplies

Search [Orca Gas Station](./Orca%20Gas%20Station.md) and locate the note. It is located by a [Bedroll](./Bedroll.md) in the back of the station. Once the survivor has found and read the note, they can proceed to [Spruce Falls Bridge](./Spruce%20Falls%20Bridge.md).

The easiest method to get to this location is to start at [Milton House (Grey Mother's)](./Milton%20House%20%28Grey%20Mother%27s%29.md), near the [Grey Mother](./Grey%20Mother.md), and follow the road to the right towards [St. Christopher's Church](./St.%20Christopher%27s%20Church.md). Continue past the church, staying on the road, until Spruce Falls Bridge is encountered.

The first pickup truck has a trunk located in its bed. Search the trunk, taking the supplies within, and the mission will be completed.
