**Highway Robbery** is a side mission started in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1">Episode 1</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="story">story</a>. In the mission, the survivor investigates an abandoned truck on <a href="./Spruce%20Falls%20Bridge.md" class="wikilink" title="Spruce Falls Bridge">Spruce Falls Bridge</a> after finding a <a href="./Highway%20Robbery.md" class="wikilink" title="note">note</a> describing a rich Mainlander abandoning a truck on the bridge. The mission can only be completed in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1">Episode 1</a>, prior to the survivor <a href="./Paradise%20Lost.md" class="wikilink" title="leaving Milton">leaving Milton</a>.

## Overview

To start the mission, the survivor has to find one of the corresponding note at the <a href="./Orca%20Gas%20Station.md" class="wikilink" title="Orca Gas Station">Orca Gas Station</a> outside <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a>:

- <a href="./Highway%20Robbery.md" class="wikilink" title="Highway Robbery">Highway Robbery</a>

Once the <a href="./Highway%20Robbery.md" class="wikilink" title="note">note</a> is found, the survivor can start their investigation

## Locating emergency supplies

Search <a href="./Orca%20Gas%20Station.md" class="wikilink" title="Orca Gas Station">Orca Gas Station</a> and locate the note. It is located by a <a href="./Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Bedroll">Bedroll</a> in the back of the station. Once the survivor has found and read the note, they can proceed to <a href="./Spruce%20Falls%20Bridge.md" class="wikilink" title="Spruce Falls Bridge">Spruce Falls Bridge</a>.

The easiest method to get to this location is to start at <a href="./Milton%20House%20%28Grey%20Mother%27s%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton House (Grey Mother&#39;s)">Milton House (Grey Mother's)</a>, near the <a href="./Grey%20Mother.md" class="wikilink" title="Grey Mother">Grey Mother</a>, and follow the road to the right towards <a href="./St.%20Christopher%27s%20Church.md" class="wikilink" title="St. Christopher&#39;s Church">St. Christopher's Church</a>. Continue past the church, staying on the road, until Spruce Falls Bridge is encountered.

The first pickup truck has a trunk located in its bed. Search the trunk, taking the supplies within, and the mission will be completed.
