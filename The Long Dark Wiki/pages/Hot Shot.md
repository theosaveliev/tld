**Hot Shot** is an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

This achievement is easiest to unlock when playing on [Pilgrim](./Pilgrim.md), although it will often be obtained through normal gameplay on higher difficulties.

The player needs must kill 25 [Rabbits](./Rabbit.md) with a [Fire Hardened Arrow](./Fire%20Hardened%20Arrow.md) in a single game in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md). Craft a [Survival Bow](./Survival%20Bow.md) and multiple arrows and search for where Rabbits congregate. Continue hunting Rabbits until the achievement unlocks.
