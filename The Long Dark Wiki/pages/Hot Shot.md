**Hot Shot** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is easiest to unlock when playing on <a href="./Pilgrim.md" class="wikilink" title="Pilgrim">Pilgrim</a>, although it will often be obtained through normal gameplay on higher difficulties.

The player needs must kill 25 <a href="./Rabbit.md" class="wikilink" title="Rabbits">Rabbits</a> with a <a href="./Fire%20Hardened%20Arrow.md" class="wikilink" title="Fire Hardened Arrow">Fire Hardened Arrow</a> in a single game in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a>. Craft a <a href="./Survival%20Bow.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Bow">Survival Bow</a> and multiple arrows and search for where Rabbits congregate. Continue hunting Rabbits until the achievement unlocks.
