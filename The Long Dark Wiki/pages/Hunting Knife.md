The **Hunting Knife** is a tool in *The Long Dark* for butchering, crafting and self defense.

## Overview

The Hunting Knife has a number of uses: self-defense, harvesting gut, hide and meat from <a href="./Wildlife.md" class="wikilink" title="animal">animal</a> carcasses, as well as <a href="./Cloth.md" class="wikilink" title="cloth">cloth</a> and <a href="./Tinder%20Plug.md" class="wikilink" title="Tinder Plugs">Tinder Plugs</a>. It's used as a tool for <a href="./Crafting.md" class="wikilink" title="Crafting">Crafting</a> varied equipment. It may also serve as a makeshift <a href="./Can%20Opener.md" class="wikilink" title="Can Opener">Can Opener</a> to avoid spillage, or to break through iced-over fishing holes for <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a>.

Knives are repairable with a <a href="./Whetstone.md" class="wikilink" title="Whetsone">Whetsone</a> or at the <a href="./Milling%20Machine.md" class="wikilink" title="Milling Machine">Milling Machine</a>.

Uses of Hunting Knife:

- Self Defense during a <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf Struggle">Wolf Struggle</a>.
- May be used to <a href="./Carcass%20Harvesting.md" class="wikilink" title="harvest frozen carcasses">harvest frozen carcasses</a> (Knives have faster unfrozen meat, gut and hide harvesting speeds. Hatchets are only faster at harvesting frozen meat)
- Crafting <a href="./Survival%20Bow.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Bows">Survival Bows</a>, <a href="./Simple%20Arrow.md" class="wikilink" title="Simple Arrows">Simple Arrows</a> and <a href="./Arrow%20Shaft.md" class="wikilink" title="Arrow Shafts">Arrow Shafts</a>.
- Crafting animal hide clothing/tools: <a href="./Bear%20Skin%20Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear Skin Bedroll">Bear Skin Bedroll</a>, <a href="./Bearskin%20Coat.md" class="wikilink" title="Bearskin Coat">Bearskin Coat</a>, <a href="./Deerskin%20Boots.md" class="wikilink" title="Deerskin Boots">Deerskin Boots</a>, <a href="./Deerskin%20Pants.md" class="wikilink" title="Deerskin Pants">Deerskin Pants</a>, <a href="./Moose-Hide%20Cloak.md" class="wikilink" title="Moose-Hide Cloak">Moose-Hide Cloak</a>, <a href="./Travois.md" class="wikilink" title="Travois">Travois</a> and <a href="./Wolfskin%20Coat.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolfskin Coat">Wolfskin Coat</a>.
- Open or maintain holes in the ice for <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a> (Bladed tools break ice faster, but take more condition loss when doing so).
- Harvesting textiles (<a href="./Curtain.md" class="wikilink" title="Curtains">Curtains</a>, <a href="./Old%20Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Bedrolls">Old Bedrolls</a>, <a href="./Pillow.md" class="wikilink" title="Pillows">Pillows</a> or <a href="./Towel.md" class="wikilink" title="Towels">Towels</a>) for <a href="./Cloth.md" class="wikilink" title="Cloth">Cloth</a>.
- Breaking down <a href="./Cardboard%20Box.md" class="wikilink" title="Cardboard Boxes">Cardboard Boxes</a> for <a href="./Tinder%20Plug.md" class="wikilink" title="Tinder">Tinder</a>
- Open <a href="./Cooking.md#Canned" class="wikilink" title="canned foods">canned foods</a> without spillage

When carried and selected for self-defense at the start of a Wolf Struggle, Hunting Knives deal medium damage with a low chance per-hit to cause the wolf to flee, and a high chance to inflict puncture wounds, a fast form of <a href="./Blood%20Loss.md" class="wikilink" title="Blood Loss">Blood Loss</a> (which is lethal to the Wolf over time). Hunting Knives are as effective at filling the Struggle Meter as <a href="./Prybar.md" class="wikilink" title="Prybar">Prybar</a>, and slightly more effective than <a href="./Improvised%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Knife">Improvised Knife</a> at ending struggles in this way, especially on difficulty modes other than <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Interloper">Interloper</a>. Hunting Knives take low damage from being used in a struggle. For more information on Struggles, see: <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Wildlife Struggles">Wildlife Struggles</a>.

## Obtaining

Hunting Knives may be found in all regions, usually near frozen corpses or structures. They may also be found inside structures atop/beneath furniture or in drawers.

In Interloper mode hunting knives will never spawn; requiring forging to create an <a href="./Improvised%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Knife">Improvised Knife</a>.

## Repair

The hunting knife can be repaired using a <a href="./Whetstone.md" class="wikilink" title="whetstone">whetstone</a> and the <a href="./Sharpening.md" class="wikilink" title="Sharpening">Sharpening</a> skill, restoring 3% condition to the hunting knife and for 5% condition damage to the whetstone.

The <a href="./Milling%20Machine.md" class="wikilink" title="Milling Machine">Milling Machine</a> in <a href="./Bleak%20Inlet.md" class="wikilink" title="Bleak Inlet">Bleak Inlet</a> or <a href="./Blackrock.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock">Blackrock</a> may repair a non-ruined hunting knife to 100% condition for one <a href="./Scrap%20Metal.md" class="wikilink" title="Scrap Metal">Scrap Metal</a>, during an active <a href="./Aurora.md" class="wikilink" title="Aurora">Aurora</a>. N.B. The knife has a breaking threshold at 15% condition, usage below this number risks ruining it for good.
