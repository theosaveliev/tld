# Hushed River

> {{Infobox/Location
> |region = Hushed River Valley

> |type = outdoors
> |image = HushedRiverValleyFromPensiveVista.png|map=Hushed River Location.png}}'''Hushed River''' is a long and winding frozen river that is the namesake of the [[Hushed River Valley]] region in ''[[The Long Dark]].'' It flows through opposite ends of the map.
> 
> ==Overview==
> Hushed River is noted for its many waterfalls that include [[Reclusive Falls]], [[Offset Falls]], and [[Twin Sisters Falls]]. It also has access points to some ice caves and many waterfall locations. The river contains an abundance of cattails and [[Coal]], but [[wolf|wolves]] are also common on the western half of the river, so it's a good idea to be careful when exploring. To get into the eastern portion of the valley, it is necessary to [[Rope Climbing|descend using a rope]], either from [[Pensive Vista]] or from [[Many Falls Vista]].
> 
> ==Shelters==
> There are a number of caves along the river which can serve as shelter. One of the entrances to the [[Ice Cave]] may be found here, but going through it will soak the player, limiting its usefulness as a base. Another cave can be found slightly to the east. Once past Twin Sisters Falls, the player will run into [[Valley Cave]], a popular safe house when exploring the eastern part of the valley. A small cave is present at the [[Landslide (Hushed River Valley)|Landslide]], but this cave doesn't offer any warmth benefit.
