## Overview

> “**Your body temperature is very low. If it remains low, you'll get Hypothermia.**”
>
> — In-game description

If the player stays out in the freezing cold for too long, and their [Clothing](./Clothing.md) is not sufficiently warm enough to keep their [Feels Like](./Feels%20Like.md) temperature above 0°, their [Warmth](./Warmth.md) [Need](./Needs.md) will drop. Once Warmth hits zero and reaches "Freezing", the Hypothermia Risk will appear. Should a player continue to freeze in this state, they will gain the [Hypothermia](./Hypothermia.md) affliction after 2 hours.

## Tips

- The treatment for Hypothermia and Hypothermia Risk is keeping Warmth above freezing until the affliction heals. Should the player start freezing while recovering from Hypothermia, the recovery time will reset.
- The time to recover from Hypothermia is based on the experience mode: 6 hours for Pilgrim, 12 for Voyageur, 18 for Stalker and 24 for Interloper. Remaining recovery time is shown on the Status menu.
- [Hot](./Warming%20Up.md) food or drinks and [campfires](./Campfire.md) can help stay above freezing, even when outdoors.
- Freezing, even without Hypothermia, is by far the deadliest Need (Freezing: 20% Condition loss per hour (40% with Hypothermia), Thirst: 2% per hour, Hunger/Fatigue 1% per hour).
