**Hypothermia** is an <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> in *The Long Dark*, caused by exposure to the cold.

If the player stays out in the freezing cold for too long, and their <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> is not sufficiently warm enough to keep their <a href="./Feels%20Like.md" class="wikilink" title="Feels Like">Feels Like</a> temperature above 0°, their <a href="./Warmth.md" class="wikilink" title="Warmth">Warmth</a> <a href="./Needs.md" class="wikilink" title="Needs">Needs</a> will drop. Once Warmth hits zero and reaches "Freezing", the <a href="./Hypothermia%20Risk.md" class="wikilink" title="Hypothermia Risk">Hypothermia Risk</a> will appear. Should a player continue to freeze in this state, they may begin to suffer from Hypothermia.

- If Hypothermia Risk reaches 100%, the player will suffer from Hypothermia.
- Hypothermia is acquired instantly if a player falls through <a href="./Weak%20Ice.md" class="wikilink" title="Weak Ice">Weak Ice</a>.

## Affliction

Once manifested Hypothermia doubles the <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> loss from freezing, from 20% to 40% loss per hour, and causes <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a> to drain at double the normal rate. The time to recover from Hypothermia is based on the experience mode: 6 hours for Pilgrim, 12 for Voyageur, 18 for Stalker and 24 for Interloper. Remaining recovery time is shown on the Status menu.

## Tips

- The treatment for Hypothermia and Hypothermia Risk is keeping Warmth above freezing until the affliction heals. Should the player start freezing while recovering from Hypothermia, the recovery time will reset.
- <a href="./Warming%20Up.md" class="wikilink" title="Hot">Hot</a> food or drinks and <a href="./Campfire.md" class="wikilink" title="campfires">campfires</a> can help stay above freezing, even when outdoors.
- Freezing, even without Hypothermia, is by far the deadliest <a href="./Needs.md" class="wikilink" title="Need">Need</a> (Freezing: 20% Condition loss per hour (40% with Hypothermia), Thirst: 2% per hour, Hunger/Fatigue 1% per hour).
