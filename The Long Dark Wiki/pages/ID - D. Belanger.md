**ID - D. Belanger** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 3](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode as a part of the [Fallen Star](./Fallen%20Star.md) mission.

During the course of the mission, Astrid finds the ID on a corpse at the [Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)](./Crash%20Site%20%28Pleasant%20Valley%29.md).

## Location

The ID is located on a [Frozen Corpse](./Frozen%20Corpse.md) found after immediately falling from the main body of the plane and turning to the right, near the left side of the plane, next to the roaring fire. The ID is later returned to Dimitri at the [Community Hall](./Community%20Hall.md), with Astrid giving her sympathies.
