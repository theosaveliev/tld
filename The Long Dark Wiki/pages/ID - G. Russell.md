**ID - G. Russell** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 3](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode as a part of the [Fallen Star](./Fallen%20Star.md) mission.

During the course of the mission, Astrid finds the ID after finding [Gwen](./Characters.md#Gwen) at the [Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)](./Crash%20Site%20%28Pleasant%20Valley%29.md). It is automatically added to the player's inventory before after the cutscene with her ends.

The ID disappears when Gwen is returned to the [Community Hall](./Community%20Hall.md) and placed on a bed.
