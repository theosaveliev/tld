**ID - T. Chan** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 3](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode as a part of the [Fallen Star](./Fallen%20Star.md) mission.

During the course of the mission, Astrid finds the ID on a corpse at the [Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)](./Crash%20Site%20%28Pleasant%20Valley%29.md).

## Location

The ID is located on a [Frozen Corpse](./Frozen%20Corpse.md) found directly across from the middle plane section in the valley, hidden behind some debris. The ID is later returned to Sadie at the [Community Hall](./Community%20Hall.md), with Astrid giving her sympathies.
