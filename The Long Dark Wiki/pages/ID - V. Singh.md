**ID - V. Singh** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3">Episode 3</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode as a part of the <a href="./Fallen%20Star.md" class="wikilink" title="Fallen Star">Fallen Star</a> mission.

During the course of the mission, Astrid finds the ID on a corpse at the <a href="./Crash%20Site%20%28Pleasant%20Valley%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)">Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)</a>.

## Location

<div>

The ID is located on a <a href="./Frozen%20Corpse.md" class="wikilink" title="Frozen Corpse">Frozen Corpse</a> found near the top of the hill, slightly before encountering Gwen. This is also the corpse that holds the <a href="./Passenger%20Manifest.md" class="wikilink" title="Passenger Manifest">Passenger Manifest</a>. The ID is later returned to Sam at the <a href="./Community%20Hall.md" class="wikilink" title="Community Hall">Community Hall</a>, with Astrid giving her sympathies.
