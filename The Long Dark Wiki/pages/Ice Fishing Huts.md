> |map=Ice Fishing Huts Location.png}}'''Ice Fishing Huts''' is a location on [[Mystery Lake (location)]] in the [[Mystery Lake (region)|Mystery Lake region]] in ''[[The Long Dark]]''.
> ==Overview==
> There are 4 [[Fishing Hut|Fishing Huts]] on the lake, with 2 east of the little middle island being the location that triggers the '''Ice Fishing Huts''' location. The remaining 2 huts are closer to [[Lone Lake Cabin]].
> 
> Mystery Lake is a good location for collecting supplies due to the abundance of cabin and huts around the lake.
> 
> A [[Camp Office]] is located at the north-western tip of the lake between the lake and the rail road tracks, this building has an abundance of supplies and is a contender for one of the best base locations in the region.
> 
> There are two collections of [[Lake Cabins]] on the south and south-eastern end of the lake, usually one cabin out of each collection is burnt down, but there is still a large amount of supplies to be found in each.
> 
> Further up past the triple lake cabins a [[Lone Lake Cabin]] quietly sits abandoned.
> 
> There is the occasional [[Wolf]] (usually two or 4) as well as a [[Bear]] on the lake which makes exploring slightly risky sometimes. You can harvest the cattails from lake edge as a food and tinder source. Several fishing huts (each with a stove, cupboard and fishing hole) are scattered over the frozen lake and a small strip of land sits in the middle of the lake. Over all Mystery Lake is a great location for supplies. A [[Hunting Rifle]] may spawn near a corpse behind Lone Lake Cabin.{{Mystery Lake}}{{Lake}}
