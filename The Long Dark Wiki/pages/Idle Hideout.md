**Idle Hideout** is a location in [Sundered Pass](./Sundered%20Pass.md).

## Overview

It is located directly below the destroyed bridge leading to the [Weather Station](./Weather%20Station.md), deep below the treeline in the north eastern most section of the valley. The cave entrance often has a campfire and some supplies outside (possibly a [Cooking Skillet](./Cooking%20Skillet.md)), with the cave itself leading to an indoor cave system.

The cave system acts as a shortcut leading out of the lower valley to 3 different areas:

- At the first crossroads, choosing to go left will result in the player eventually reaching a [Rope Climb](./Rope%20Climbing.md) and ending at [Prayerful Window](./Prayerful%20Window.md), close to the [Polaroid](./Polaroids.md) Vista overlooking the [Old Settlement](./Old%20Settlement.md).

If the player instead goes straight at the first crossroads, they will eventually reach an area with smooth boulders at a T intersection leading to 2 different exits.

- The left path leading up leads to the upper mountain road, up a [Rock Climb](./Rock%20Climb.md) onto a frozen river leading to the [Cascading Gap](./Cascading%20Gap.md) bridge.
- The right path leading down exits further up the road of Cascading Gap, but below the cliff into the middle section of the valley via a slope.
