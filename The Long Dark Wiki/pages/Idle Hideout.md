# Idle Hideout

> {{Infobox/Location
> |title=
> |image=Idle Hideout.jpg
> |region=Sundered Pass
> |shelter=no
> |description=A cave entrance located far below the destroyed bridge leading to the [[Weather Station]]|map=Idle Hideout Location.png}}'''Idle Hideout''' is a location in [[Sundered Pass]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> It is located directly below the destroyed bridge leading to the [[Weather Station]], deep below the treeline in the north eastern most section of the valley. The cave entrance often has a campfire and some supplies outside (possibly a [[Cooking Skillet]]), with the cave itself leading to an indoor cave system.
> 
> The cave system acts as a shortcut leading out of the lower valley to 3 different areas:
> 
> * At the first crossroads, choosing to go left will result in the player eventually reaching a [[Rope Climbing|Rope Climb]] and ending at [[Prayerful Window]], close to the [[Polaroids|Polaroid]] Vista overlooking the [[Old Settlement]].
> 
> If the player instead goes straight at the first crossroads, they will eventually reach an area with smooth boulders at a T intersection leading to 2 different exits.
> 
> * The left path leading up leads to the upper mountain road, up a [[Rock Climb]] onto a frozen river leading to the [[Cascading Gap]] bridge.
> * The right path leading down exits further up the road of Cascading Gap, but below the cliff into the middle section of the valley via a slope.
