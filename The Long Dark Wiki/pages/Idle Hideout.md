<td><p>Has no shelter|} <strong>Idle Hideout</strong> is a location in <a href="./Sundered%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Sundered Pass">Sundered Pass</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>It is located directly below the destroyed bridge leading to the <a href="./Weather%20Station.md" class="wikilink" title="Weather Station">Weather Station</a>, deep below the treeline in the north eastern most section of the valley. The cave entrance often has a campfire and some supplies outside (possibly a <a href="./Cooking%20Skillet.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Skillet">Cooking Skillet</a>), with the cave itself leading to an indoor cave system.</p>
<p>The cave system acts as a shortcut leading out of the lower valley to 3 different areas:</p>
<ul>
<li>At the first crossroads, choosing to go left will result in the player eventually reaching a <a href="./Rope%20Climbing.md" class="wikilink" title="Rope Climb">Rope Climb</a> and ending at <a href="./Prayerful%20Window.md" class="wikilink" title="Prayerful Window">Prayerful Window</a>, close to the <a href="./Polaroids.md" class="wikilink" title="Polaroid">Polaroid</a> Vista overlooking the <a href="./Old%20Settlement.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Settlement">Old Settlement</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p>If the player instead goes straight at the first crossroads, they will eventually reach an area with smooth boulders at a T intersection leading to 2 different exits.</p>
<ul>
<li>The left path leading up leads to the upper mountain road, up a <a href="./Rock%20Climb.md" class="wikilink" title="Rock Climb">Rock Climb</a> onto a frozen river leading to the <a href="./Cascading%20Gap.md" class="wikilink" title="Cascading Gap">Cascading Gap</a> bridge.</li>
<li>The right path leading down exits further up the road of Cascading Gap, but below the cliff into the middle section of the valley via a slope.</li>
</ul>
