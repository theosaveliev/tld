<p><strong>Improvised Down Bedroll</strong> is a Tool released for the Part 3: Frontier Comforts update for those who purchase the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> expansion.</p>
<p>The Down Bedroll weighs 0.10 kg more than a regular <a href="./Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Bedroll">Bedroll</a> and is harder to repair, requiring <a href="./Ptarmigan%20Down.md" class="wikilink" title="Ptarmigan Down">Ptarmigan Down</a> in addition to <a href="./Cloth.md" class="wikilink" title="Cloth">Cloth</a>, but is also significantly warmer, making it a good alternative to the regular Bedroll.</p>
<h2 id="Crafting">Crafting</h2>
<p>The Improvised Down Bedroll takes an ordinary <a href="./Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Bedroll">Bedroll</a> and stuffs it full of Ptarmigan Down feathers, resulting in a base warmth increase of the Bedroll by 3°C at 100% (for a total of 8°C). The weight of the Bedroll also increases, from 1 kg to 1.1 kg.</p>
<p>Can be crafted anywhere by hand. Takes 3/6 hours to craft with <a href="./Sewing%20Kit.md" class="wikilink" title="Sewing Kit">Sewing Kit</a>/<a href="./Fishing%20Tackle.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Tackle">Fishing Tackle</a> respectively.</p>
<p>Note that a completely ruined <a href="./Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Bedroll">Bedroll</a> (0%) can still be crafted into an Improvised Down Bedroll at 100%. (doesn't seem to be current anymore for 2.39 version of the game - needs further verification)</p>

<h2 id="Tips">Tips</h2>
<ul>
<li>The Improvised Down Bedroll provides the same Condition gain when sleeping as the Bedroll, providing for example, +27 Condition from a 10 hour sleep on Interloper difficulty. Compared to +32 Condition from beds (may vary) or the <a href="./Bear%20Skin%20Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear Skin Bedroll">Bear Skin Bedroll</a> over the same sleep period.<br />
</li>
</ul>
