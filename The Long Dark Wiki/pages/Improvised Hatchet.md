The **Improvised Hatchet** is a tool in *The Long Dark*, primarily for woodcutting and self defense.

## Overview

Functioning similarly to the hatchet, improvised hatchets may be used to harvest [sticks](./Stick.md) from branches, [Cedar Firewood](./Cedar%20Firewood.md) and [Fir Firewood](./Fir%20Firewood.md) from tree limbs, [Reclaimed Wood](./Reclaimed%20Wood.md) from furniture or to break ice for [Ice Fishing](./Ice%20Fishing.md).

The improvised hatchet is slightly heavier (0.25 kg) and 1/3 slower to use than the [Hatchet](./Hatchet.md). Its condition is reduced by 3% per use. Hatchets are a finite resource, so once all have been ruined, improvised hatchets must be crafted to complete some tasks. The improvised hatchet may be used as a lighter substitute to the heavy hammer to break down furniture or branches.

Improvised Hatchets are repairable with [Whetstone](./Whetstone.md) or at the [Milling Machine](./Milling%20Machine.md).

Uses of Improvised Hatchet:

- Self Defense during a [Wolf Struggle](./Struggle.md).
- [Harvest frozen carcasses](./Carcass%20Harvesting.md) ([Knives](./Hunting%20Knife.md) have faster unfrozen meat, gut and hide harvesting speeds. Hatchets are only faster at harvesting frozen meat).
- Harvest downed tree limbs (for [Fir Firewood](./Fir%20Firewood.md) or [Cedar Firewood](./Cedar%20Firewood.md)).
- Open or maintain holes in the ice for [Ice Fishing](./Ice%20Fishing.md) (bladed tools break ice faster, but take more condition loss).
- Breaking down Furniture and Crates (for [Reclaimed Wood](./Reclaimed%20Wood.md) or [Cloth](./Cloth.md)).
- Crafting [Arrow Shafts](./Arrow%20Shaft.md).
- Breaking [Scrub Brushes](./Scrub%20Brush.md).
- For the [Whiteout](./Whiteout.md) [challenge's](./Challenge.md) objective, an improvised hatchet may substitute the requirement of a hatchet.

When carried and selected for self-defense at the start of a Wolf Struggle, Improvised Hatchets deal high damage with a low chance per-hit to cause the wolf to flee, as well as a moderate chance to inflict [Blood Loss](./Blood%20Loss.md) on the Wolf (which is lethal over time). Improvised Hatchets take low damage from being used in a struggle, and are inferior to Hatchets when used in this way. For more information on Struggles, see: [Wildlife Struggles](./Struggle.md).

Improvised Hatchets may be rarely found in some regions, usually near frozen corpses or structures; such as in [Hushed River Valley](./Hushed%20River%20Valley.md) or [Milton Basin](./Milton%20Basin.md) in [Mountain Town](./Mountain%20Town.md) near [frozen corpses](./Frozen%20Corpse.md). One may also spawn atop the workbench in the [Gold Mine](./Gold%20Mine.md) of [Ash Canyon](./Ash%20Canyon.md).

Forging improvised hatchets is only possible at a [furnace](./Furnace.md) with a [Heavy Hammer](./Heavy%20Hammer.md); [coal](./Coal.md) is required to a heat furnace hot enough for forging along with the necessary crafting materials: [Scrap Metal](./Scrap%20Metal.md) and [cloth](./Cloth.md).

In Interloper mode hatchets and improvised hatchets will never spawn; requiring forging to create.

## Repair

Improvised Hatchets may be repaired using a [Whetstone](./Whetstone.md) and the [Sharpening](./Sharpening.md) skill, restoring 3% condition to the hatchet and causing 5% condition damage to the whetstone.

The [Milling Machine](./Milling%20Machine.md) in [Bleak Inlet](./Bleak%20Inlet.md) or [Blackrock](./Blackrock.md) may repair a non-ruined improvised hatchet to 100% condition for one scrap metal, during an active [Aurora](./Aurora.md). Repairing Improvised Hatchet or Improvised Knife with the Milling Machine is more efficient with Scrap Metal than [forging](./Forge.md) new ones.
