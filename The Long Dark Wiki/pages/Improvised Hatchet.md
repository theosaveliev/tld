The **Improvised Hatchet** is a tool in *The Long Dark*, primarily for woodcutting and self defense.

## Overview

Functioning similarly to the hatchet, improvised hatchets may be used to harvest <a href="./Stick.md" class="wikilink" title="stick">sticks</a> from branches, <a href="./Cedar%20Firewood.md" class="wikilink" title="Cedar Firewood">Cedar Firewood</a> and <a href="./Fir%20Firewood.md" class="wikilink" title="Fir Firewood">Fir Firewood</a> from tree limbs, <a href="./Reclaimed%20Wood.md" class="wikilink" title="Reclaimed Wood">Reclaimed Wood</a> from furniture or to break ice for <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a>.

The improvised hatchet is slightly heavier (0.25 kg) and 1/3 slower to use than the <a href="./Hatchet.md" class="wikilink" title="Hatchet">Hatchet</a>. Its condition is reduced by 3% per use. Hatchets are a finite resource, so once all have been ruined, improvised hatchets must be crafted to complete some tasks. The improvised hatchet may be used as a lighter substitute to the heavy hammer to break down furniture or branches.

Improvised Hatchets are repairable with <a href="./Whetstone.md" class="wikilink" title="Whetstone">Whetstone</a> or at the <a href="./Milling%20Machine.md" class="wikilink" title="Milling Machine">Milling Machine</a>.

Uses of Improvised Hatchet:

- Self Defense during a <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf Struggle">Wolf Struggle</a>.
- <a href="./Carcass%20Harvesting.md" class="wikilink" title="Harvest frozen carcasses">Harvest frozen carcasses</a> (<a href="./Hunting%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Knives">Knives</a> have faster unfrozen meat, gut and hide harvesting speeds. Hatchets are only faster at harvesting frozen meat).
- Harvest downed tree limbs (for <a href="./Fir%20Firewood.md" class="wikilink" title="Fir Firewood">Fir Firewood</a> or <a href="./Cedar%20Firewood.md" class="wikilink" title="Cedar Firewood">Cedar Firewood</a>).
- Open or maintain holes in the ice for <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a> (bladed tools break ice faster, but take more condition loss).
- Breaking down Furniture and Crates (for <a href="./Reclaimed%20Wood.md" class="wikilink" title="Reclaimed Wood">Reclaimed Wood</a> or <a href="./Cloth.md" class="wikilink" title="Cloth">Cloth</a>).
- Crafting <a href="./Arrow%20Shaft.md" class="wikilink" title="Arrow Shafts">Arrow Shafts</a>.
- Breaking <a href="./Scrub%20Brush.md" class="wikilink" title="Scrub Brushes">Scrub Brushes</a>.
- For the <a href="./Whiteout.md" class="wikilink" title="Whiteout">Whiteout</a> <a href="./Challenge.md" class="wikilink" title="challenge&#39;s">challenge's</a> objective, an improvised hatchet may substitute the requirement of a hatchet.

When carried and selected for self-defense at the start of a Wolf Struggle, Improvised Hatchets deal high damage with a low chance per-hit to cause the wolf to flee, as well as a moderate chance to inflict <a href="./Blood%20Loss.md" class="wikilink" title="Blood Loss">Blood Loss</a> on the Wolf (which is lethal over time). Improvised Hatchets take low damage from being used in a struggle, and are inferior to Hatchets when used in this way. For more information on Struggles, see: <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Wildlife Struggles">Wildlife Struggles</a>.

Improvised Hatchets may be rarely found in some regions, usually near frozen corpses or structures; such as in <a href="./Hushed%20River%20Valley.md" class="wikilink" title="Hushed River Valley">Hushed River Valley</a> or <a href="./Milton%20Basin.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton Basin">Milton Basin</a> in <a href="./Mountain%20Town.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountain Town">Mountain Town</a> near <a href="./Frozen%20Corpse.md" class="wikilink" title="frozen corpses">frozen corpses</a>. One may also spawn atop the workbench in the <a href="./Gold%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Gold Mine">Gold Mine</a> of <a href="./Ash%20Canyon.md" class="wikilink" title="Ash Canyon">Ash Canyon</a>.

Forging improvised hatchets is only possible at a <a href="./Furnace.md" class="wikilink" title="furnace">furnace</a> with a <a href="./Heavy%20Hammer.md" class="wikilink" title="Heavy Hammer">Heavy Hammer</a>; <a href="./Coal.md" class="wikilink" title="coal">coal</a> is required to a heat furnace hot enough for forging along with the necessary crafting materials: <a href="./Scrap%20Metal.md" class="wikilink" title="Scrap Metal">Scrap Metal</a> and <a href="./Cloth.md" class="wikilink" title="cloth">cloth</a>.

In Interloper mode hatchets and improvised hatchets will never spawn; requiring forging to create.

## Repair

Improvised Hatchets may be repaired using a <a href="./Whetstone.md" class="wikilink" title="Whetstone">Whetstone</a> and the <a href="./Sharpening.md" class="wikilink" title="Sharpening">Sharpening</a> skill, restoring 3% condition to the hatchet and causing 5% condition damage to the whetstone.

The <a href="./Milling%20Machine.md" class="wikilink" title="Milling Machine">Milling Machine</a> in <a href="./Bleak%20Inlet.md" class="wikilink" title="Bleak Inlet">Bleak Inlet</a> or <a href="./Blackrock.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock">Blackrock</a> may repair a non-ruined improvised hatchet to 100% condition for one scrap metal, during an active <a href="./Aurora.md" class="wikilink" title="Aurora">Aurora</a>. Repairing Improvised Hatchet or Improvised Knife with the Milling Machine is more efficient with Scrap Metal than <a href="./Forge.md" class="wikilink" title="forging">forging</a> new ones.
