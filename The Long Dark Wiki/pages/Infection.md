An **Infection** is a life threating <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> acquired from a <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf">Wolf</a>, <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolf">Timberwolf</a>, <a href="./Poisoned%20Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Poisoned Wolf">Poisoned Wolf</a>, <a href="./Cougar.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougar">Cougar</a>, or <a href="./Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear">Bear</a> <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggle">Struggle</a>.

Infection has a chance to occur when an <a href="./Infection%20Risk.md" class="wikilink" title="Infection Risk">Infection Risk</a> is not disinfected. Once the player has the affliction, they will lose around 5% condition every hour and fatigue will increase.

Infection is treated by consuming either 2 x <a href="./Antibiotics.md" class="wikilink" title="Antibiotics">Antibiotics</a> or 1 x cup <a href="./Reishi%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Reishi Tea">Reishi Tea</a>/<a href="./Burdock%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Burdock Tea">Burdock Tea</a>, followed by 8 hours of cumulative sleep. If left untreated, you will die.

It is very important to note that one can have multiple Infections at once, even from the same <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggle">Struggle</a>.
