## Affliction

> “**You have an infection that could kill you if left untreated.**”
>
> — In-game description

Infection has a chance to occur when an [Infection Risk](./Infection%20Risk.md) is not disinfected. Once the player has the affliction, they will lose around 5% condition every hour and fatigue will increase.

Infection is treated by consuming either 2 x [Antibiotics](./Antibiotics.md) or 1 x cup [Reishi Tea](./Reishi%20Tea.md)/[Burdock Tea](./Burdock%20Tea.md), followed by 8 hours of cumulative sleep. If left untreated, you will die.

It is very important to note that one can have multiple Infections at once, even from the same [Struggle](./Struggle.md).
