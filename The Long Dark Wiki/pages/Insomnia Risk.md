<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p><strong>Insomnia Risk</strong> is an <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> added to the Part 1 Update of the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> addon, and is contracted from spending too much time in <a href="./Glimmer%20Fog.md" class="wikilink" title="Glimmer Fog">Glimmer Fog</a> found within certain <a href="./Region.md" class="wikilink" title="Regions">Regions</a> within the Far Territory Frontier. At present, it can only be contracted in the <a href="./Forsaken%20Airfield.md" class="wikilink" title="Forsaken Airfield">Forsaken Airfield</a>, <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>, and <a href="./Sundered%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Sundered Pass">Sundered Pass</a> Regions.</p>
<h2 id="Risk">Risk</h2>
<dl>
<dt></dt>
<dd>
"<em>You risk Insomnia by spending time inside the Glimmer Fog</em>"
</dd>
<dd>
— In-game description
</dd>
</dl>
<p>The Affliction Risk for Insomnia can be mitigated by escaping Glimmer Fog when it arrives, either by finding an underground shelter or entering <a href="./Transfer%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Transfer Pass">Transfer Pass</a> (pursuing the latter will instantly remove any Insomnia Risk buildup).</p>
<p>There are 6 known underground locations that allow a survivor to escape the effects of Glimmer Fog:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="./Cave%20%28Transition%20Zone%29.md#Forsaken_Airfield_to_Zone_of_Contamination" class="wikilink" title="Transition Cave">Transition Cave</a> between Forsaken Airfield, Zone of Contamination, and Sundered Pass</li>
</ul>
<p><a href="./Forsaken%20Airfield.md" class="wikilink" title="Forsaken Airfield">Forsaken Airfield</a></p>
<ul>
<li><a href="./Hangar%20Basement.md" class="wikilink" title="Hangar Basement">Hangar Basement</a></li>
<li><a href="./Bunker%20Omega.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Omega">Bunker Omega</a> (must be unlocked)</li>
</ul>
<p><a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a></p>
<ul>
<li><a href="./Langston%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Langston Mine">Langston Mine</a></li>
</ul>
<p><a href="./Sundered%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Sundered Pass">Sundered Pass</a></p>
<ul>
<li>The cave system connecting <a href="./Prayerful%20Window.md" class="wikilink" title="Prayerful Window">Prayerful Window</a>, <a href="./Idle%20Hideout.md" class="wikilink" title="Idle Hideout">Idle Hideout</a>, and <a href="./Cascading%20Gap.md" class="wikilink" title="Cascading Gap">Cascading Gap</a></li>
<li><a href="./Abandoned%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Abandoned Mine">Abandoned Mine</a></li>
</ul>
<p>The following areas <strong>DO NOT</strong> protect against Insomnia Risk:</p>
<ul>
<li>The Main Hangar stairwell outside the Hangar Basement</li>
<li>Any outdoor caves</li>
<li>Anywhere inside the <a href="./Concentrator.md" class="wikilink" title="Concentrator">Concentrator</a>, <a href="./Conveyor%20Room.md" class="wikilink" title="Conveyor Room">Conveyor Room</a>, or even the very bottom elevator shaft of the <a href="./Headframe%20Building.md" class="wikilink" title="Headframe Building">Headframe Building</a></li>
<li><a href="./Basement%20%28Sundered%20Pass%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Basement (Sundered Pass)">Basement (Sundered Pass)</a> (despite being underground)</li>
<li><a href="./Cabin%20%28Sundered%20Pass%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Cabin (Sundered Pass)">Cabin (Sundered Pass)</a> (even the bottom floor)</li>
</ul>
<p>The game currently lacks any real countermeasures in the form of foods or gear to effectively combat or hide from Glimmer Fog, so the player will almost certainly contract Insomnia if they are not already near an underground shelter when the fog arrives. It can also be hard to tell the difference between a Glimmer Fog or Aurora event during the early hours of the day while indoors for some locations, due to the inability to simply glance outside at the weather.</p>
<p>Failure to escape Glimmer Fog will result in <a href="./Insomnia.md" class="wikilink" title="Insomnia">Insomnia</a>.</p>
