![Interloper\_icon.png](../assets/Interloper_icon.png){| style="margin:auto" | style="vertical-align:top; font-size:3em; color:#5d5980; padding-right:10px" |“ | **You are not part of Mother Nature's plan, and you will bear the full force of her wrath. The true test of Human VS Nature.** | style="vertical-align:bottom; font-size:3em; color:#5d5980; padding-left:10px" |” |} **Interloper** is the 4th [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) Experience in *[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)*. It is the standard "expert" difficulty setting, being a major departure from the previous 3 Experiences by introducing multiple handicaps to make the game even harder than normal.

## Overview

Interloper doesn't just raise the difficulty of [Stalker](./Stalker.md) to the next level, but also reduces that overall resource and tool availability in the game world, with several tools and clothing outright never spawning. As such, players are faced with an extremely cold world with limited loot, abundant hostile wildlife, and a smaller loot pool present compared to the other Experiences. Most high quality tools and clothing will never appear in game, making it a necessity to craft and forge animal hide clothing and improvised tools for survival. [Foraging](./Foraging.md) and [Hunting](./Hunting.md) become the main methods to acquire food, as most homes have very little, if any food in them.

Interloper's learning curve is a sharp one, forcing players to relearn how to survive by using more advanced game mechanics, such as [Daisy Chaining](./Daisy%20Chaining.md), [Hibernation](./Hibernation.md), [Mountain Goating](./Mountain%20Goating.md), and [Beachcombing](./Beachcombing.md).

*A guide for players new to Interloper can be found [here](./Survival%20Mode%20%E2%80%94%20Interloper%20Tips.md).*

## Interloper Settings

- [Great Bear Island](./Great%20Bear%20Island.md) has few items to be found (only ~20% of containers will contain any items).
  - Critical items have limitations on their spawn frequency (more details can be found in [this Google Docs spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1sFqnIM9BPeI3ZitcDRekWeWEWfJL7BLyqyirBT3Y53E/edit?gid=0#gid=0))
    - [Cardboard Matches](./Cardboard%20Matches.md) never spawn. [Wood Matches](./Wood%20Matches.md) and other fire starters are rare.
    - Most tools never spawn: [Hunting Rifles](./Hunting%20Rifle.md), [Revolvers](./Revolver.md), [Hatchets](./Hatchet.md) and [Hunting Knives](./Hunting%20Knife.md) (and their Improvised Versions), [Bows](./Survival%20Bow.md), [Simple Arrows](./Simple%20Arrow.md), [Flashlights](./Flashlight.md), and [Moose-Hide Satchel](./Moose-Hide%20Satchel.md).
    - The [Spelunker's Lantern](./Spelunker%27s%20Lantern.md) is the only unique item variant which will spawn.
    - [Improvised Hatchets](./Improvised%20Hatchet.md) and [Improvised Knives](./Improvised%20Knife.md) must be [Forged](./Forge.md) to craft Bows or Arrows. Arrowheads and Broken Arrows may be found rarely, but not Simple Arrows or Bows.
    - High-Tier Clothing does not spawn. Many High-Tier Foodstuffs do not spawn (see full list in next section).
  - Half of [Cargo Containers](./Cargo%20Container.md) do not spawn on [Timberwolf Mountain](./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md), and the remaining ones have downgraded loot (e.g. [Tomato Soup](./Tomato%20Soup.md) instead of [Condensed Milk](./Condensed%20Milk.md)).
  - Certain [Mementos](./Mementos.md) containing high level loot do not spawn.
  - Most of the [Coastal Houses](./Coastal%20House.md) and Cabins in [Coastal Highway](./Coastal%20Highway.md) are destroyed, providing no shelter and minimal loot.
    - [Abandoned Lookout](./Abandoned%20Lookout.md): The 4 Cabins below the Lookout will only have 2 intact Cabins (formerly 3).
    - [Bear Creek Campground](./Bear%20Creek%20Campground.md): The Cabin on the hill just south of the Campground (and north west of Fishing Camp) will be destroyed.
    - [Coastal Townsite](./Coastal%20Townsite.md): Of the 11 houses in the town, only 2 will be standing (the one on the hill outside of town will also be destroyed). The [Quonset Garage](./Quonset%20Garage.md) will always be intact.
    - [Fishing Camp](./Fishing%20Camp.md): The Cabin with the [Work Bench](./Work%20Bench.md) and [Buffer Memory](./Buffer%20Memories.md) laptop is usually destroyed (the same Buffer Memory laptop will appear inside the [Rabbit Grove](./Rabbit%20Grove.md) Cabin instead).
    - [Log Sort](./Log%20Sort.md): The house near the shed and trailer will always be ruined (the trailer can still be used).
    - [Waterfront Cottages](./Waterfront%20Cottages.md): The 3 houses here ***always*** spawn as ruined houses.
- [Needs](./Needs.md) erode very quickly.
- Starting equipment is a [Recycled Can](./Recycled%20Can.md) and an incomplete set of low-quality clothing (head and hands will always be exposed).
- Game will start outdoors. Chance to spawn at night.
  - Player will randomly start in a small list of predetermined starting regions (cannot spawn in "Beginner" regions).
  - Higher chance to start in Advanced (most dangerous) regions.
  - The list of possible spawns are: [Ash Canyon](./Ash%20Canyon.md), [Blackrock](./Blackrock.md), [Desolation Point](./Desolation%20Point.md), [Forlorn Muskeg](./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md), [Hushed River Valley](./Hushed%20River%20Valley.md), [Pleasant Valley](./Pleasant%20Valley.md), and [Timberwolf Mountain](./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md).
- [Rabbits](./Rabbit.md), [Ptarmigan](./Ptarmigan.md), and [Deer](./Deer.md) are rarer. [Fish](./Ice%20Fishing.md) have up to half the catch-rate (twice as long of wait time at Day 50 survived), and [Wolf](./Wolf.md)/[Timberwolf](./Timberwolf.md)/[Poisoned Wolf](./Poisoned%20Wolf.md) are extremely deadly (but spawn less frequently than Stalker mode, on par with Voyageur rates/detection).
- Wildlife becomes rarer and the environment colder over time (at Day 10, 25, 50, 100 and 200).
- Meat from predators carries [Intestinal Parasites](./Intestinal%20Parasites.md) risk, with a more difficult treatment than Stalker mode.
- [Fires](./Fire.md) do not automatically prevent [Freezing](./Hypothermia.md) ("Feels Like" Temperature must be raised above 0° C).
- No automatic waking from [sleep](./Sleep.md) when Freezing.
- [Hypothermia](./Hypothermia.md) and [Frostbite](./Frostbite.md) progresses faster and heal slower.
- Clothing and Food [Decay](./Decay.md) condition quickly.
- Rest is a Resource: the survivor cannot sleep above high [Fatigue](./Fatigue.md) (95% or greater) levels, except to treat [Afflictions](./Afflictions.md).
- [Prepper Caches](./Prepper%20Cache.md) will always spawn as the "Abandoned" type.
- Loot acquired from the 3 [Tales](./Tales.md) will be virtually nonexistent, but they can be completed.
- [Supply Cache](./Supply%20Cache.md) loot is of much poorer quality.
- The [Trader's](./Trader.md) loot is of lower quality.
- [Scurvy](./Scurvy.md) is present (at the same rate as Stalker mode).
- [Cabin Fever](./Cabin%20Fever.md) is present and progresses faster than Stalker mode.
- The option to enable the [Trader](./Trader.md), [Cougar](./Cougar.md), and [Scurvy](./Scurvy.md) on or off is present.
- Allows 2 active [Feats](./Feats.md).

## Starting spawn location

The player cannot select which region they will spawn in. Instead, the game will spawn them in one of the following Lower Great Bear regions at random:

- [Ash Canyon](./Ash%20Canyon.md)
- [Blackrock](./Blackrock.md)
- [Desolation Point](./Desolation%20Point.md)
- [Forlorn Muskeg](./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md)
- [Hushed River Valley](./Hushed%20River%20Valley.md)
- [Pleasant Valley](./Pleasant%20Valley.md)
- [Timberwolf Mountain](./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md)
