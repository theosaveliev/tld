<p><strong>Jailer's Residence</strong> is a location in <a href="./Blackrock.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock">Blackrock</a>, located not far from <a href="./Blocked%20Bridge.md" class="wikilink" title="Blocked Bridge">Blocked Bridge</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>The Jailer's Residence is completely burned to the ground, with some <a href="./Reclaimed%20Wood.md" class="wikilink" title="Reclaimed Wood">Reclaimed Wood</a> found in the surroundings of the ruins as well as a <a href="./Metal%20Container.md" class="wikilink" title="Metal Container">Metal Container</a>.</p>
<p>A nearby barn houses a car and a <a href="./Fire%20Barrel.md" class="wikilink" title="Fire Barrel">Fire Barrel</a>, providing some shelter for the area. <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolves">Timberwolves</a> patrol the field near the Residence, and can be a problem if the player is spotted.</p>
