> ==Overview==
> The Jailer's Residence is completely burned to the ground, with some [[Reclaimed Wood]] found in the surroundings of the ruins as well as a [[Metal Container]].
> 
> A nearby barn houses a car and a [[Fire Barrel]], providing some shelter for the area. [[Timberwolf|Timberwolves]] patrol the field near the Residence, and can be a problem if the player is spotted.
