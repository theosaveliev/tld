**Jeremiah's Broken Rifle** was a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode as part of the mission \[<https://thelongdark.fandom.com/wiki/You%20Fix%20My%20Rifle>? You Fix My Rifle?\]. It is considered <a href="./Cut%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20Content.md" class="wikilink" title="Cut/Discontinued Content">Cut/Discontinued Content</a>, as it did not survive the transition from Predux Episode 2 to Redux Episode 2, as Jeremiah's Broken Rifle was instead replaced with an ordinary <a href="./Hunting%20Rifle.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Rifle">Hunting Rifle</a> at the start of the mission <a href="./The%20Wounded%20Trapper.md" class="wikilink" title="The Wounded Trapper">The Wounded Trapper</a>.

## Overview

It was obtained at the start of the mission You Fix My Rifle?. Mackenzie would then need to travel and use the <a href="./Hand-Powered%20Milling%20Tool.md" class="wikilink" title="Hand-Powered Milling Tool">Hand-Powered Milling Tool</a> in the <a href="./Maintenance%20Yard.md" class="wikilink" title="Maintenance Yard">Maintenance Yard</a> Shed to fix Jeremiah's Broken Rifle into <a href="./Jeremiah%27s%20Repaired%20Rifle.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s Repaired Rifle">Jeremiah's Repaired Rifle</a>.

It has no weight, despite being a Hunting Rifle.
