**Jeremiah's Knife** was a tool in pre-redux [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) from [Episode 2](./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md). it was removed in the 2018 Redux release of Episode 2.

<span id="overview"></span><span id="Overview"></span>

## Overview

Jeremiah's knife functions identically to a [Hunting Knife](./Hunting%20Knife.md): a self-defense tool, harvesting [animal](./Wildlife.md) carcasses, [Cloth](./Cloth.md) from curtains/pillows, tinder from cardboard, breaking ice for [Ice Fishing](./Ice%20Fishing.md), and crafting at a [Work Bench](./Work%20Bench.md).

It was given to [Will Mackenzie](./Will%20Mackenzie.md) after graduating from Jeremiah's [Survival School (Predux)](./Survival%20School%20%28Predux%29.md), and is used by Mackenzie to cut his hand to make the scent from his blood attract the [Old Bear](./Old%20Bear.md) to his position, in order for him to kill it.

It was removed during the Redux of Wintermute: Episodes 1 and 2 and is now unobtainable, but is still present in the Wintermute game files.

<span id="repair"></span><span id="Repair"></span>

## Repair

Jeremiah's knife can be repaired with a [Whetstone](./Whetstone.md) and the [Sharpening](./Sharpening.md) skill, restoring 3% condition to the knife.

<span id="harvest"></span><span id="Harvest"></span>

## Harvest

It can be harvested for 1 [Scrap Metal](./Scrap%20Metal.md).

<span id="combat"></span><span id="Combat"></span>

## Combat

Jeremiah's Knife cannot be equipped, but may be selected to defend one's self at the start of a [Wolf](./Wolf.md) [Struggle](./Struggle.md), causing it some Condition damage.
