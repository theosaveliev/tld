**Jeremiah's Knife** was a tool in pre-redux <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> from <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a>. it was removed in the 2018 Redux release of Episode 2.

## Overview

Jeremiah's knife functions identically to a <a href="./Hunting%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Knife">Hunting Knife</a>: a self-defense tool, harvesting <a href="./Wildlife.md" class="wikilink" title="animal">animal</a> carcasses, <a href="./Cloth.md" class="wikilink" title="Cloth">Cloth</a> from curtains/pillows, tinder from cardboard, breaking ice for <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a>, and crafting at a <a href="./Work%20Bench.md" class="wikilink" title="Work Bench">Work Bench</a>.

It was given to <a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will Mackenzie">Will Mackenzie</a> after graduating from Jeremiah's <a href="./Survival%20School%20%28Predux%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival School (Predux)">Survival School (Predux)</a>, and is used by Mackenzie to cut his hand to make the scent from his blood attract the <a href="./Old%20Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Bear">Old Bear</a> to his position, in order for him to kill it.

It was removed during the Redux of Wintermute: Episodes 1 and 2 and is now unobtainable, but is still present in the Wintermute game files.

## Repair

Jeremiah's knife can be repaired with a <a href="./Whetstone.md" class="wikilink" title="Whetstone">Whetstone</a> and the <a href="./Sharpening.md" class="wikilink" title="Sharpening">Sharpening</a> skill, restoring 3% condition to the knife.

## Harvest

It can be harvested for 1 <a href="./Scrap%20Metal.md" class="wikilink" title="Scrap Metal">Scrap Metal</a>.

## Combat

Jeremiah's Knife cannot be equipped, but may be selected to defend one's self at the start of a <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf">Wolf</a> <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggle">Struggle</a>, causing it some Condition damage.

## Trivia

- On December 3rd, 2023, Jeremiah's Knife can be seen at 5:00 in the Part 4: Buried Echoes update for the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> (as seen in the video below). The knife, however, was not released in the update. This is the first time the knife has officially been seen since its removal in 2018.
- Jeremiah's Knife was once again spotted in the May 13th, 2024 [official developer update](https://www.thelongdark.com/news/dev-diary-may-2024/), as part of the Misery Mode concept art. [^1]
