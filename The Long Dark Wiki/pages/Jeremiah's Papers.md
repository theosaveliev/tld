**Jeremiah's Papers** was a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode. It was found on a desk inside of <a href="./Jeremiah%27s%20Hideout.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s Hideout">Jeremiah's Hideout</a>. It is considered <a href="./Cut%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20Content.md" class="wikilink" title="Cut/Discontinued Content">Cut/Discontinued Content</a>, as it did not survive the transition from Predux Episode 2 to Redux Episode 2, as the side mission Jeremiah's Hideout was instead replaced with the side mission <a href="./Aurora%20Hatch.md" class="wikilink" title="Aurora Hatch">Aurora Hatch</a>.

## Overview

Jeremiah's Hideout was unlocked by reaching 375 points of Trust with Jeremiah, unlocking the dialogue option "Old Correspondence." He would then ask <a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will Mackenzie">Will Mackenzie</a> to burn some of his old letters, then give the coordinates to his old hideout and an access code. The Hideout was a <a href="./Prepper%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper Cache">Prepper Cache</a> with 2 red lights on top of it, and could only be opened during an <a href="./Aurora.md" class="wikilink" title="Aurora">Aurora</a>. Once inside, live wires blocked passage to the other side of the bunker, forcing Will to sleep until the Aurora ended and trapping him inside. Jeremiah's Papers would be on the other side.

The papers could later be burned, as per Jeremiah's request.

## Trivia

- Jeremiah's Papers cannot be examined.
- Jeremiah's Papers are the only Collectible that can be used as <a href="./Fire%20Starting.md" class="wikilink" title="Fire Starting">Fire Starting</a> fuel, and have:
  - A Burn Time of 6 minutes
  - Heat increase of +2°C
  - and a bonus chance of +35%
