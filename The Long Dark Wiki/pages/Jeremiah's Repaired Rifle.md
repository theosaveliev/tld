**Jeremiah's Repaired Rifle** was a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 2: Luminance Fugue](./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode as part of the mission [You Fix My Rifle?](./You%20Fix%20My%20Rifle%3F.md). It is considered [Cut/Discontinued Content](./Cut%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20Content.md), as it did not survive the transition from Predux Episode 2 to Redux Episode 2, as [Jeremiah's Broken Rifle](./Jeremiah%27s%20Broken%20Rifle.md) was instead replaced with an ordinary [Hunting Rifle](./Hunting%20Rifle.md) at the start of the mission [The Wounded Trapper](./The%20Wounded%20Trapper.md).

## Overview

It was obtained after finding the [Hand-Powered Milling Tool](./Hand-Powered%20Milling%20Tool.md) in the [Maintenance Yard](./Maintenance%20Yard.md) Shed and fixing Jeremiah's Broken Rifle, producing Jeremiah's Repaired Rifle. It would later be returned to [Jeremiah](./Jeremiah.md).

Even after being repaired, it cannot be fired, and the player would have to find their own rifle, the [Hunting Lodge Rifle](./Hunting%20Lodge%20Rifle.md) in the nearby [Hunting Lodge](./Hunting%20Lodge.md).
