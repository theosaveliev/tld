**Jeremiah's Repaired Rifle** was a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode as part of the mission You Fix My Rifle?. It is considered <a href="./Cut%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20Content.md" class="wikilink" title="Cut/Discontinued Content">Cut/Discontinued Content</a>, as it did not survive the transition from Predux Episode 2 to Redux Episode 2, as <a href="./Jeremiah%27s%20Broken%20Rifle.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s Broken Rifle">Jeremiah's Broken Rifle</a> was instead replaced with an ordinary <a href="./Hunting%20Rifle.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Rifle">Hunting Rifle</a> at the start of the mission <a href="./The%20Wounded%20Trapper.md" class="wikilink" title="The Wounded Trapper">The Wounded Trapper</a>.

## Overview

It was obtained after finding the <a href="./Hand-Powered%20Milling%20Tool.md" class="wikilink" title="Hand-Powered Milling Tool">Hand-Powered Milling Tool</a> in the <a href="./Maintenance%20Yard.md" class="wikilink" title="Maintenance Yard">Maintenance Yard</a> Shed and fixing Jeremiah's Broken Rifle, producing Jeremiah's Repaired Rifle. It would later be returned to <a href="./Jeremiah.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah">Jeremiah</a>.

Even after being repaired, it cannot be fired, and the player would have to find their own rifle, the <a href="./Hunting%20Lodge%20Rifle.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Lodge Rifle">Hunting Lodge Rifle</a> in the nearby <a href="./Hunting%20Lodge.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Lodge">Hunting Lodge</a>.
