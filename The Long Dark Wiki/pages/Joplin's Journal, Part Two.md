**Joplin's Journal, Part Two** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 3](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode as a part of the [Joplin's Bunker Raid](./Joplin%27s%20Bunker%20Raid.md) side mission.

During the course of the mission, [Astrid Greenwood](./Astrid%20Greenwood.md) finds the note _Joplin's Journal, Part Two_ from a bunker near [Three Strikes Farmstead](./Three%20Strikes%20Farmstead.md).

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Joplin's Journal, Part Two**_

All these quakes are a sign. Old world’s comin down around me. Got to be on the move, can't stay still.

Got to get away, got to get up higher. Someplace in the open sky. Past the timberline. Way up.
