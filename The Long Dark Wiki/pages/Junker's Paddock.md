> '''{{PAGENAME}}''' is a small trailer along the road connecting [[Transfer Pass]] to the [[Field 31]].
> 
> Inside the trailer, there are some [[locker]]s, mid-level loot, a [[bed]], and a [[Work Bench]].
> 
> A few [[truck]]s and [[rabbit]]s are situated nearby. A [[Polaroids|polaroid]] may be found in one of the vehicles. Some [[coal]] can be collected just outside the trailer near a scrapped truck, which also contains a collectible [[Notes|note]]. The coal respawns over time.
> 
> Continuing northwest, a [[Deer Carcass]] can be found, as well as the southern entrance to [[Stony Passage]].
