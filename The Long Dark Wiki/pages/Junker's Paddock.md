**Junker's Paddock** is a small trailer along the road connecting [Transfer Pass](./Transfer%20Pass.md) to the [Field 31](./Field%2031.md).

Inside the trailer, there are some [lockers](./Locker.md), mid-level loot, a [bed](./Bed.md), and a [Work Bench](./Work%20Bench.md).

A few [trucks](./Truck.md) and [rabbits](./Rabbit.md) are situated nearby. A [polaroid](./Polaroids.md) may be found in one of the vehicles. Some [coal](./Coal.md) can be collected just outside the trailer near a scrapped truck, which also contains a collectible [note](./Notes.md). The coal respawns over time.

Continuing northwest, a [Deer Carcass](./Deer%20Carcass.md) can be found, as well as the southern entrance to [Stony Passage](./Stony%20Passage.md).
