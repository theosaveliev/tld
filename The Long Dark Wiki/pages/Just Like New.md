## Guide

This is an unmissable story achievement achieved during the mission [The Power Plant](./The%20Power%20Plant.md).

Navigate the [Steam Tunnels](./Steam%20Tunnels.md) and "fix" the locking mechanism machine tied to Solitary with a [Heavy Hammer](./Heavy%20Hammer.md), and the achievement will unlock.
