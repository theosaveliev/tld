**Just Like New** is a <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, then Silence</a> of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This is an unmissable story achievement achieved during the mission <a href="./The%20Power%20Plant.md" class="wikilink" title="The Power Plant">The Power Plant</a>.

Navigate the <a href="./Steam%20Tunnels.md" class="wikilink" title="Steam Tunnels">Steam Tunnels</a> and "fix" the locking mechanism machine tied to Solitary with a <a href="./Heavy%20Hammer.md" class="wikilink" title="Heavy Hammer">Heavy Hammer</a>, and the achievement will unlock.
