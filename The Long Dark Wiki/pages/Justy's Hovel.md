<p><strong>Justy's Hovel</strong> is an indoor location in <a href="./Forsaken%20Airfield.md" class="wikilink" title="Forsaken Airfield">Forsaken Airfield</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Justy's Hovel is a small cabin located on a plateau on the south-east corner of the map.</p>
<p>The cabin itself has decent loot: it spawns mid tier clothing, food and some tools. It also has a fireplace. A <a href="./Cowichan%20Sweater.md" class="wikilink" title="Cowichan Sweater">Cowichan Sweater</a> might be found on the bed.</p>
<p>The plateau on which the hovel stand has decent resources. It has two Rabbit coves, plenty of firewood, and deer also spawn. A wolf may patrol the area. It can be reached by climbing the rope to the west or by walking up a path to the east.</p>
<p>The cabin is a great place to wait for an <a href="./Aurora.md" class="wikilink" title="Aurora">Aurora</a> or <a href="./Glimmer%20Fog.md" class="wikilink" title="Glimmer Fog">Glimmer Fog</a> to power the <a href="./Handheld%20Shortwave%20Radio.md" class="wikilink" title="Handheld Shortwave Radio">Handheld Shortwave Radio</a> to uncover <a href="./Bunker%20Omega.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Omega">Bunker Omega</a>.</p>
