## Overview

Justy's Hovel is a small cabin located on a plateau on the south-east corner of the map.

The cabin itself has decent loot: it spawns mid tier clothing, food and some tools. It also has a fireplace. A [Cowichan Sweater](./Cowichan%20Sweater.md) might be found on the bed.

The plateau on which the hovel stand has decent resources. It has two Rabbit coves, plenty of firewood, and deer also spawn. A wolf may patrol the area. It can be reached by climbing the rope to the west or by walking up a path to the east.

The cabin is a great place to wait for an [Aurora](./Aurora.md) or [Glimmer Fog](./Glimmer%20Fog.md) to power the [Handheld Shortwave Radio](./Handheld%20Shortwave%20Radio.md) to uncover [Bunker Omega](./Bunker%20Omega.md).
