> '''Justy's Hovel''' is an indoor location in [[Forsaken Airfield]]. 
> 
> == Overview ==
> Justy's Hovel is a small cabin located on a plateau on the south-east corner of the map.
> 
> The cabin itself has decent loot: it spawns mid tier clothing, food and some tools. It also has a fireplace. A [[Cowichan Sweater]] might be found on the bed. 
> 
> The plateau on which the hovel stand has decent resources. It has two Rabbit coves, plenty of firewood, and deer also spawn. A wolf may patrol the area. It can be reached by climbing the rope to the west or by walking up a path to the east.
> 
> The cabin is a great place to wait for an [[Aurora]] or [[Glimmer Fog]] to power the [[Handheld Shortwave Radio]] to uncover [[Bunker Omega]].
