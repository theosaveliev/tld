**Ketchup Chip** is a packaged <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark"><em>The Long Dark</em></a>.

## Overview

Consuming Ketchup Chips provides up to 300 calories of <a href="./Hunger.md" class="wikilink" title="energy">energy</a>, at the cost of minor <a href="./Thirst.md" class="wikilink" title="dehydration">dehydration</a>. They are relatively common and weigh a mere 0.10 kg, making its energy density a high 3,000 calories-per-kilogram.

Ketchup Chips do not cause <a href="./Food%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Food Poisoning">Food Poisoning</a> above 20% condition and <a href="./Decay.md" class="wikilink" title="decay">decay</a> more slowly when stored outdoors, like other non-canned foods.

## Trivia

- Ketchup Chips were first introduced for the limited time "<a href="./Winter%27s%20Embrace.md" class="wikilink" title="Winter&#39;s Embrace">Winter's Embrace</a>" event (June 29th - July 31st, 2020), along with <a href="./Maple%20Syrup.md" class="wikilink" title="Maple Syrup">Maple Syrup</a>.
- During the event, eating 25 Ketchup Chips and 25 Maple Syrup, awarded the "Canadian Feast Badge".
- The Ketchup Chips is packaged as a product called "*Chips"*, with a picture of a ketchup bottle branded "K" in front of an image of potato chips.
  - The ketchup bottle looks superficially like the glass bottles used by Heinz brand of ketchup, which for years was made in Leamington, Ontario.
- Ketchup Chips are readily available in Canada, but almost unheard of in most other countries.
