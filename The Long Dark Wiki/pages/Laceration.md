A **Laceration** may be inflicted during a <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggle">Struggle</a>. Lacerations instantly damage condition but do not require any treatment to remove, and because of this it never appears directly on the afflictions menu.

## Cause

The player can receive a Laceration during a <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggle">Struggle</a> with a <a href="./Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear">Bear</a>, <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolf">Timberwolf</a>, <a href="./Poisoned%20Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Poisoned Wolf">Poisoned Wolf</a>, or <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf">Wolf</a>.

## Effect

-10% to -15% instant condition loss. Cannot be treated and disappears immediately after being inflicted.
