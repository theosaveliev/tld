**Lake Whitefish (Raw)** is a raw <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in *The Long Dark*.

## Overview

Lake Whitefish are small freshwater fish found commonly in lakes and ponds. They can be caught by <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a> using <a href="./Fishing%20Tackle.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Tackle">Fishing Tackle</a>. Lake Whitefish can be between 1.5 and 2.3 kg raw.

Cooking Whitefish takes 20+ minutes and is be obtained by using a lit <a href="./Fire.md" class="wikilink" title="fire">fire</a>. Cooking increases its condition by 50%, decays 4 times slower than raw meat, and has a reduces chance of <a href="./Food%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Food Poisoning">Food Poisoning</a> over raw meat, but decreasing it's weight by 35%. Cooked whitefish's energy density is 385 calories-per-kilogram. Cooking fish also yields a small amount of <a href="./Lantern%20Fuel.md" class="wikilink" title="Lantern Fuel">Lantern Fuel</a>, although they provide the lowest yield (tied with the <a href="./Goldeye%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Goldeye">Goldeye</a>) of 0.015L/kg.

Cooking whitefish improves the <a href="./Skills.md#Cooking" class="wikilink" title="cooking skill">cooking skill</a>.
