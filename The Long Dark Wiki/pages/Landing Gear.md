**Landing Gear** is a part of a wrecked plane that crashed on the summit of [Timberwolf Mountain](./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md). It is found near a canyon leading to [Pleasant Valley](./Pleasant%20Valley.md).

## Overview

[Rose Hips](./Rose%20Hip.md) can be found growing by the landing gear. Debris from the crashed plane can be found scattered nearby, semi-buried in the snow on the way to [Mountaineer's Hut](./Mountaineer%27s%20Hut.md).

## Trivia

There is another Landing Gear on the [Summit](./Summit.md), though this is not considered a location. A 3rd set of landing gear is never found on or around the mountain (though it may be under the Tail Section). Both sets of Landing Gear may have been from the Wings.
