<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Lanterns are stationary objects which can be lit (taking ~1 in-game minute) to provide light to the surrounding area but no additional <a href="./Warmth.md" class="wikilink" title="Warmth">Warmth</a>. They do not require a <a href="./Fire%20Starting.md" class="wikilink" title="starter">starter</a> to ignite or consume <a href="./Lantern%20Fuel.md" class="wikilink" title="fuel">fuel</a> to burn, and cannot be looted or moved. Interacting with a lit Lantern grants the option to extinguish it. Lighting lanterns can be useful for navigation, conserving fuel and determining where the survivor has traveled.</p>
<p>Lanterns are found at indoors at:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> Episode 4 <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Fury, Then Silence">Fury, Then Silence</a> at the <a href="./Steam%20Tunnels.md" class="wikilink" title="Steam Tunnels">Steam Tunnels</a> in <a href="./Blackrock.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock">Blackrock</a>.</li>
<li><a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival">Survival</a> Mode inside the <a href="./Langston%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Langston Mine">Langston Mine</a> in <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>.</li>
</ul>
