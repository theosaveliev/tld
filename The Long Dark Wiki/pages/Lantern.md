> |wintermute=yes}}'''Lanterns''' are stationary light-producing objects in ''The Long Dark.'' For the hand-held light source, see [[Storm Lantern]].
> ==Overview==
> Lanterns are stationary objects which can be lit (taking ~1 in-game minute) to provide light to the surrounding area but no additional [[Warmth]]. They do not require a [[Fire Starting|starter]] to ignite or consume [[Lantern Fuel|fuel]] to burn, and cannot be looted or moved. Interacting with a lit Lantern grants the option to extinguish it. Lighting lanterns can be useful for navigation, conserving fuel and determining where the survivor has traveled.
>
