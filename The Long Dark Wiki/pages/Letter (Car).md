**Letter** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) note found in [Episode 4](./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story. It is found in a [Car](./Car.md) on the road near [Blocked Bridge](./Blocked%20Bridge.md). It most likely belongs to [Jace](./Jace.md).

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Letter**_

**\[heading on the letter is smudged and unreadable\]

We are pleased to inform you that your paper, "Black Hole Collisions and a New Architecture for Dark Energy Cosmology" has been accepted for evaluation and potential publication in Canadian Physics Review.

As the publication process moves forward, we will touch with you should any issues arise.

Warm regards,

B. Tonkov

Editor-in-Chief**
