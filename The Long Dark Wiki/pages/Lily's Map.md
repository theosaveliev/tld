**Lily's Map** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="collectible">collectible</a> item obtained during <a href="./Paradise%20Lost.md" class="wikilink" title="Chapter Four - Paradise Lost">Chapter Four - Paradise Lost</a>.

## Overview

Lily's map is found in the locked blue <a href="./Trunk.md" class="wikilink" title="Trunk">Trunk</a> upstairs in Lily's room in <a href="./Milton%20House%20%28Grey%20Mother%27s%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton House (Grey Mother&#39;s)">Milton House (Grey Mother's)</a>. The trunk can only be only be opened with <a href="./Lily%27s%20Trunk%20Key.md" class="wikilink" title="Lily&#39;s Trunk Key">Lily's Trunk Key</a>, which is received as a reward from the <a href="./Grey%20Mother.md" class="wikilink" title="Grey Mother">Grey Mother</a> after completing <a href="./The%20Grey%20Mother.md" class="wikilink" title="Chapter Three - The Grey Mother">Chapter Three - The Grey Mother</a>.

The map outlines the route that the player can take to leave <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a> to reach <a href="./Mystery%20Lake%20%28region%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Mystery Lake (region)">Mystery Lake (region)</a>.
