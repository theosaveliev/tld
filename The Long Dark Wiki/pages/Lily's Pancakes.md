> '''Lily's Pancakes''' is a food item in ''The Long Dark.'' It is learned from a unique [[Recipe Cards|Recipe Card]] and is made with a [[Cooking Skillet]].
> ==Overview ==
> Lily's Pancakes are a relatively simple and efficient unique recipe with good calorie density and a positive (if small) [[Condition]] restoring effect, comparable to [[Camber Flight Porridge]], but it uses [[Acorn Grounds]] instead of [[Running River Oats]]. [[Acorns]] can be found abundantly on [[Great Bear Island]], but require a [[Heavy Hammer]] to process. [[Cooking Oil]] and [[Maple Syrup]] are rarer (outside of acquiring them from the [[Trader]]), but are used in small amounts, making [[Pinnacle Peaches]] the main limiting ingredient for Lily's Pancakes. 
> 
> Acquiring the recipe card for Lily's Pancakes requires multiple rope climbs from [[Forlorn Muskeg]] or [[Mystery Lake (region)|Mystery Lake]] and braving the [[wolf]]-infested town of Milton. The card can be found in the [[Milton House (Grey Mother's)]] in [[Mountain Town]].

> ==Trivia==
> *"Lily's Pancakes" is a reference to the in-game character [[Lilith Barker]], daughter of [[Grey Mother]] and [[Martin Barker]] who lived in the [[Milton House (Grey Mother's)]] (where the recipe card is found). The description of Lily's Pancakes references climbing, Lily's favorite pastime and the cause of her death prior to the events of ''Wintermute''.
