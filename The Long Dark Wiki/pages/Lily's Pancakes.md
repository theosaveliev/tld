<p><strong>Lily's Pancakes</strong> is a food item in <em>The Long Dark.</em> It is learned from a unique <a href="./Recipe%20Cards.md" class="wikilink" title="Recipe Card">Recipe Card</a> and is made with a <a href="./Cooking%20Skillet.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Skillet">Cooking Skillet</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Lily's Pancakes are a relatively simple and efficient unique recipe with good calorie density and a positive (if small) <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> restoring effect, comparable to <a href="./Camber%20Flight%20Porridge.md" class="wikilink" title="Camber Flight Porridge">Camber Flight Porridge</a>, but it uses <a href="./Acorn%20Grounds.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorn Grounds">Acorn Grounds</a> instead of <a href="./Running%20River%20Oats.md" class="wikilink" title="Running River Oats">Running River Oats</a>. <a href="./Acorns.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorns">Acorns</a> can be found abundantly on <a href="./Great%20Bear%20Island.md" class="wikilink" title="Great Bear Island">Great Bear Island</a>, but require a <a href="./Heavy%20Hammer.md" class="wikilink" title="Heavy Hammer">Heavy Hammer</a> to process. <a href="./Cooking%20Oil.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Oil">Cooking Oil</a> and <a href="./Maple%20Syrup.md" class="wikilink" title="Maple Syrup">Maple Syrup</a> are rarer (outside of acquiring them from the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a>), but are used in small amounts, making <a href="./Pinnacle%20Peaches.md" class="wikilink" title="Pinnacle Peaches">Pinnacle Peaches</a> the main limiting ingredient for Lily's Pancakes.</p>
<p>Acquiring the recipe card for Lily's Pancakes requires multiple rope climbs from <a href="./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md" class="wikilink" title="Forlorn Muskeg">Forlorn Muskeg</a> or <a href="./Mystery%20Lake%20%28region%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Mystery Lake">Mystery Lake</a> and braving the <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="wolf">wolf</a>-infested town of Milton. The card can be found in the <a href="./Milton%20House%20%28Grey%20Mother%27s%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton House (Grey Mother&#39;s)">Milton House (Grey Mother's)</a> in <a href="./Mountain%20Town.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountain Town">Mountain Town</a>.</p>
<p>Locations:</p>
<ul>
<li>Kitchen, on the table.</li>
<li>Living Room, behind a framed photograph on a cabinet.</li>
<li>Second floor storage closet, on the lower shelves.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="Trivia">Trivia</h2>
<ul>
<li>"Lily's Pancakes" is a reference to the in-game character <a href="./Lilith%20Barker.md" class="wikilink" title="Lilith Barker">Lilith Barker</a>, daughter of <a href="./Grey%20Mother.md" class="wikilink" title="Grey Mother">Grey Mother</a> and <a href="./Martin%20Barker.md" class="wikilink" title="Martin Barker">Martin Barker</a> who lived in the <a href="./Milton%20House%20%28Grey%20Mother%27s%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton House (Grey Mother&#39;s)">Milton House (Grey Mother's)</a> (where the recipe card is found). The description of Lily's Pancakes references climbing, Lily's favorite pastime and the cause of her death prior to the events of <em>Wintermute</em>.</li>
</ul>
