**Lily's Pancakes** is a food item in _The Long Dark._ It is learned from a unique [Recipe Card](./Recipe%20Cards.md) and is made with a [Cooking Skillet](./Cooking%20Skillet.md).

## Overview

Lily's Pancakes are a relatively simple and efficient unique recipe with good calorie density and a positive (if small) [Condition](./Condition.md) restoring effect, comparable to [Camber Flight Porridge](./Camber%20Flight%20Porridge.md), but it uses [Acorn Grounds](./Acorn%20Grounds.md) instead of [Running River Oats](./Running%20River%20Oats.md). [Acorns](./Acorns.md) can be found abundantly on [Great Bear Island](./Great%20Bear%20Island.md), but require a [Heavy Hammer](./Heavy%20Hammer.md) to process. [Cooking Oil](./Cooking%20Oil.md) and [Maple Syrup](./Maple%20Syrup.md) are rarer (outside of acquiring them from the [Trader](./Trader.md)), but are used in small amounts, making [Pinnacle Peaches](./Pinnacle%20Peaches.md) the main limiting ingredient for Lily's Pancakes.

Acquiring the recipe card for Lily's Pancakes requires multiple rope climbs from [Forlorn Muskeg](./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md) or [Mystery Lake](./Mystery%20Lake%20%28region%29.md) and braving the [wolf](./Wolf.md)-infested town of Milton. The card can be found in the [Milton House (Grey Mother's)](./Milton%20House%20%28Grey%20Mother%27s%29.md) in [Mountain Town](./Mountain%20Town.md).

Locations:

- Kitchen, on the table.
- Living Room, behind a framed photograph on a cabinet.
- Second floor storage closet, on the lower shelves.

## Effects

- +10% [Condition](./Condition.md) (instant)
- [Nourished](./Nourished.md) +10% Maximum Condition (for 3 hours)
- [Headache](./Headache.md) (3 hours)

## Trivia

- "Lily's Pancakes" is a reference to the in-game character [Lilith Barker](./Lilith%20Barker.md), daughter of [Grey Mother](./Grey%20Mother.md) and [Martin Barker](./Martin%20Barker.md) who lived in the [Milton House (Grey Mother's)](./Milton%20House%20%28Grey%20Mother%27s%29.md) (where the recipe card is found). The description of Lily's Pancakes references climbing, Lily's favorite pastime and the cause of her death prior to the events of _Wintermute_.
