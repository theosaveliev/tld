**Living Off the Land** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after surviving for twenty-five consecutive days by only consuming calories obtained from animals or plants harvested by the player in a single <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> game. It is most easily done on Pilgrim.

Unlike <a href="./Silent%20Hunter.md" class="wikilink" title="Silent Hunter">Silent Hunter</a> or <a href="./Pacifist.md" class="wikilink" title="Pacifist">Pacifist</a>, Living Off the Land can be obtained at any point during a single Survival mode play through. Note however that it is consecutive not cumulative. Eating the wrong food or drink will reset the twenty-five day counter.

Eating or drinking processed foods or drinks such as <a href="./Peanut%20Butter.md" class="wikilink" title="Peanut Butter">Peanut Butter</a> or <a href="./Orange%20Soda.md" class="wikilink" title="Orange Soda">Orange Soda</a> will reset the counter. Eating raw or cooked meat **found inside buildings or otherwise** will reset the counter,[^1] which includes any assortment of fish or plants that spawn via <a href="./Beachcombing.md" class="wikilink" title="beachcombing">beachcombing</a>.

Drinking <a href="./Cup%20of%20Herbal%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Cup of Herbal Tea">Cup of Herbal Tea</a> or <a href="./Cup%20of%20Coffee.md" class="wikilink" title="Cup of Coffee">Cup of Coffee</a> will also reset the counter.

In addition, consuming <a href="./Antibiotics.md" class="wikilink" title="Antibiotics">Antibiotics</a> or <a href="./Painkillers.md" class="wikilink" title="Painkillers">Painkillers</a> will also reset the counter progress.
