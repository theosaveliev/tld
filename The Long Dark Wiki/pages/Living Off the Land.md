**Living Off the Land** is an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after surviving for twenty-five consecutive days by only consuming calories obtained from animals or plants harvested by the player in a single [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) game. It is most easily done on Pilgrim.

Unlike [Silent Hunter](./Silent%20Hunter.md) or [Pacifist](./Pacifist.md), Living Off the Land can be obtained at any point during a single Survival mode play through. Note however that it is consecutive not cumulative. Eating the wrong food or drink will reset the twenty-five day counter.

Eating or drinking processed foods or drinks such as [Peanut Butter](./Peanut%20Butter.md) or [Orange Soda](./Orange%20Soda.md) will reset the counter. Eating raw or cooked meat **found inside buildings or otherwise** will reset the counter,[¹](#fn1) which includes any assortment of fish or plants that spawn via [beachcombing](./Beachcombing.md).

Drinking [Cup of Herbal Tea](./Cup%20of%20Herbal%20Tea.md) or [Cup of Coffee](./Cup%20of%20Coffee.md) will also reset the counter.

In addition, consuming [Antibiotics](./Antibiotics.md) or [Painkillers](./Painkillers.md) will also reset the counter progress.
