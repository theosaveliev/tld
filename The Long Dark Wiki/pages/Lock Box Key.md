**Lock Box Key** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) found in [Episode 4](./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story as part of the [Blackrock Rumours](./Blackrock%20Rumours.md) side mission. It is found on the [Dead Prison Guard](./Dead%20Prison%20Guard.md) Murphy in the ravine near [Jailer's Residence](./Jailer%27s%20Residence.md) and unlocks the Lockbox in the Blackrock Officers Quarters.
