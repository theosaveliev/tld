**Lock Box Key** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> found in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story as part of the <a href="./Blackrock%20Rumours.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Rumours">Blackrock Rumours</a> side mission. It is found on the <a href="./Dead%20Prison%20Guard.md" class="wikilink" title="Dead Prison Guard">Dead Prison Guard</a> Murphy in the ravine near <a href="./Jailer%27s%20Residence.md" class="wikilink" title="Jailer&#39;s Residence">Jailer's Residence</a> and unlocks the Lockbox in the Blackrock Officers Quarters.
