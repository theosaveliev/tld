## Overview

The location is directly adjacent to the railroad, and is hard to miss in even the worst of weather. It can be used to gain bearings during a blizzard. Unfortunately, the lake is not deep enough to create Free Form Ice Fishing holes, but [Oak Trees](./Oak%20Tree.md), [Ptarmigan](./Ptarmigan.md), and [Cat Tails](./Cat%20Tail.md) can be found nearby for food.
