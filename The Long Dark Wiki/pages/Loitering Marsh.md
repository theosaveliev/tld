<p><strong>Loitering Marsh</strong> is a location in <a href="./Transfer%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Transfer Pass">Transfer Pass</a>, located due south of <a href="./Vacant%20Depot.md" class="wikilink" title="Vacant Depot">Vacant Depot</a> and underneath the <a href="./Broad%20Aerie.md" class="wikilink" title="Broad Aerie">Broad Aerie</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>The location is directly adjacent to the railroad, and is hard to miss in even the worst of weather. It can be used to gain bearings during a blizzard. Unfortunately, the lake is not deep enough to create Free Form Ice Fishing holes, but <a href="./Oak%20Tree.md" class="wikilink" title="Oak Trees">Oak Trees</a>, <a href="./Ptarmigan.md" class="wikilink" title="Ptarmigan">Ptarmigan</a>, and <a href="./Cat%20Tail.md" class="wikilink" title="Cat Tails">Cat Tails</a> can be found nearby for food.</p>
