> '''Loitering Marsh''' is a location in [[Transfer Pass]], located due south of [[Vacant Depot]] and underneath the [[Broad Aerie]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> The location is directly adjacent to the railroad, and is hard to miss in even the worst of weather. It can be used to gain bearings during a blizzard. Unfortunately, the lake is not deep enough to create Free Form Ice Fishing holes, but [[Oak Tree|Oak Trees]], [[Ptarmigan]], and [[Cat Tail|Cat Tails]] can be found nearby for food.
