<p>The <strong>Lone Lake Cabin</strong> is the unofficial name given to a single cabin in <a href="./Mystery%20Lake%20%28region%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Mystery Lake">Mystery Lake</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Lone Lake Cabin is located along the eastern shore of <a href="./Mystery%20Lake%20%28location%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Mystery Lake">Mystery Lake</a>.</p>
<p>Some supplies can be found inside the cabin, which also provides good protection from the elements. The cabin has a bunk bed. Occasionally, a hunting rifle and a Frozen Corpse can be found behind the cabin.</p>
<p>A possible <a href="./Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="bear">bear</a> and a cave may spawn north of the cabin. For players wishing to hunt the bear, the cabin can make a good place to cure the bear's guts and hide.</p>
<h2 id="Notes">Notes</h2>
<ul>
<li>In Episode 2 - Luminance Fugue, this cabin needs a key to be unlocked.</li>
