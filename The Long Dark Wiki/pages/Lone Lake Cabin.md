# Lone Lake Cabin

> }}{{Unofficial}}The '''Lone Lake Cabin''' is the unofficial name given to a single cabin in [[Mystery Lake (region)|Mystery Lake]]. 
> 
> == Overview ==
> Lone Lake Cabin is located along the eastern shore of [[Mystery Lake (location)|Mystery Lake]].  
> 
> Some supplies can be found inside the cabin, which also provides good protection from the elements. The cabin has a bunk bed. Occasionally, a hunting rifle and a Frozen Corpse can be found behind the cabin. 
> 
> A possible [[bear]] and a cave may spawn north of the cabin. For players wishing to hunt the bear, the cabin can make a good place to cure the bear's guts and hide.
> 
> == Notes ==
> * In Episode 2 - Luminance Fugue, this cabin needs a key to be unlocked.
