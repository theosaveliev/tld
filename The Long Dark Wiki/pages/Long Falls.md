## Overview

Long Falls is located directly north of Broad Falls just before [a cave with an interior](./Cave%20%28Ash%20Canyon%20location%29.md) that transitions to higher elevations. A wolf (two on Stalker) may patrol the region near the base of the falls. The pond here is deep enough for [Ice Fishing](./Ice%20Fishing.md) with Free Form holes.
