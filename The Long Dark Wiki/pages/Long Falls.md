> ''Not to be confused with [[Long Shot Falls]].''

> '''Long Falls''' is a large waterfall located in the northern part of [[Ash Canyon]]. It's one of three named waterfalls in the region, the other two being [[Broad Falls]] and [[Narrow Falls Cave|Narrow Falls]].
> ==Overview==
> Long Falls is located directly north of Broad Falls just before [[Cave (Ash Canyon location)|a cave with an interior]] that transitions to higher elevations. A wolf (two on Stalker) may patrol the region near the base of the falls. The pond here is deep enough for [[Ice Fishing]] with Free Form holes.
