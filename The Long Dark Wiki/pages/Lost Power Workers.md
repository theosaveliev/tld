**Lost Power Workers** is a side mission in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a>.

## Overview

To start the mission, the player must find the <a href="./Old%20Substation%20Work%20Memo.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Substation Work Memo">Old Substation Work Memo</a> on a bench right inside the <a href="./Old%20Substation.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Substation">Old Substation</a>.

## Tasks

- Find the missing power company workers.
  - Locate worker 1
  - Locate worker 2

## Worker Locations

- Worker 1
  - Worker 1 is at the bottom of the ravine near Blackrock prison, to reach him climb down the two rosehip roots/rocks near the bridge leading to <a href="./Cook%27s%20Farm.md" class="wikilink" title="Cook&#39;s Farm">Cook's Farm</a> and then continue upstream. It is impossible to reach this place in story mode by going downstream from the mine. His <a href="./Backpack.md" class="wikilink" title="Backpack">Backpack</a> contains <a href="./Power%20Company%20ID.md" class="wikilink" title="Power Company ID">Power Company ID</a> identifying the worker as Samual Franks, Journeyman Electrician and his corpse holds <a href="./Lost%20Worker%27s%20Note.md" class="wikilink" title="Lost Worker&#39;s Note">Lost Worker's Note</a>.

<!-- -->

- Worker 2
  - Worker 2 is located in the trailer up the road from the <a href="./Power%20Plant.md" class="wikilink" title="Power Plant">Power Plant</a> right where the map points. To get to the trailer climb up the rosebush/roots and then before crossing the ravine over the second tree, make a sharp 180° turn and on the left side you should be able to climb up a steep path and then cross 2 fallen trees, then walk through the (top) rock arch and continue left. The corpse inside is dead via heavy bleeding from her arm and her corpse contains a Power Company ID identifying her as Linda Burnley, Journeyman Electrician.
  - Due to the <a href="./Revolver.md" class="wikilink" title="Revolver">Revolver</a> she carries with 3 cartridges inside, a bloody bandage, a spare bandage nearby, and the large amount of blood on the ground, it may be presumed that she was attacked by wildlife or shot herself after the <a href="./Convicts.md" class="wikilink" title="Convicts">Convicts</a> overtook the Prison. She may have also have been shot by Samual for unknown reasons (likely memory issues).
