**Lost Worker's Note** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> found in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story as part of the <a href="./Lost%20Power%20Workers.md" class="wikilink" title="Lost Power Workers">Lost Power Workers</a> side mission. It is found on the corpse of worker 2 in the ravine.

## Transcript

<div>

<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">

The following text is transcribed from **Lost Worker's Note**

</div>

<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

\[a page of a journal, torn out\]

Keep writing. Writing helps give me focus, and focus keeps me still.

I can't remember why I'm here, what I do, or who this woman is. I think we work together?? I know her, I think. She watches me scribble this stuff down, but she smiles. Does she know? I try and watch her work on the machines when I wake up. And copy her movements when she goes to bed. She tells me things but they fade unless I write them down. Like the wind picks them up and takes them far away.

I keep seeing a river in my head, rushing water to the edge of something. Into a deep foggy place.

I just can't remember anymore...

</div>
