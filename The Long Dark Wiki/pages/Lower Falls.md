## Overview

Lower Falls serves no more than a landmark located on the eastern side of the region near [Pensive Pond](./Pensive%20Pond.md) and is south of [Heartbreak Bridge](./Heartbreak%20Bridge.md). A Bear cave can be found to the west near the cliff face, along with a [Rope Climbing](./Rope%20Climbing.md)section that leads to [Signal Hill](./Signal%20Hill.md). A few [Cat Tail](./Cat%20Tail.md) plants and some Deer may be found nearby.
