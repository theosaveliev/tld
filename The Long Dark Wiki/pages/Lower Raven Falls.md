# Lower Raven Falls

> {{Infobox/Location|image = SS-BI-LowerRavenFall.jpg
> |description = A former picnic site |region = Bleak Inlet|type = outdoors |map=Lower Raven Falls Location.png}}
> 
> '''Lower Raven Falls''' is one of the many waterfalls found in [[Bleak Inlet]]. 
> 
> ==Overview==
> It is located a short walk to the north-west of the [[Cabin]]. There are plenty of [[Cat Tail|Cat tails]] on the river, and [[Deer]] may spawn there too, making it a convenient hunting ground for players living in the nearby cabin. Hunting here is made safer by there being fewer [[Timberwolf|timberwolves]] than in most other parts of the map. 
> 
> A few pieces of food may spawn near the picnic table, and the table can be broken down into [[Reclaimed Wood]] if required. A [[Green Maple Sapling]] can also spawn near the picnic table.
