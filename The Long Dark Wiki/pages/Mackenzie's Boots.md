**Mackenzie's Boots** are a [Clothing](./Clothing.md) item worn on the player's outer feet and are exclusive to [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md).

## Overview

Mackenzie's Boots are equipped as default clothing when the player awakens from the plane crash during [Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle](./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md). They can not be acquired in Survival Mode.

The statistics of Mackenzie's Boots are identical to those of [Trail Boots](./Trail%20Boots.md).

## Episode 1

Mackenzie's Boots are already acquired when starting the mission [Memories](./Memories.md) in [Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle](./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md), already being worn by [Will Mackenzie](./Will%20Mackenzie.md).

The description of the boots changes during the mission [The Crash](./The%20Crash.md), but they otherwise remain the same visually and mechanically.

They can be carried into Episode 2 if the player holds onto it by the end of Episode 1.

## Episode 2

If the player starts [Episode 2: Luminance Fugue](./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md) out of order, Mackenzie starts the Episode wearing his Boots.

## Episode 4

Mackenzie's Boots are still worn when playing [Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence](./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md), being some of the clothing left on Mackenzie to keep him warm after he was stripped of most of his gear and transported by [Mathis](./Mathis.md).
