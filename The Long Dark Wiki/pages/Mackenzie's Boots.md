**Mackenzie's Boots** are a <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> item worn on the player's outer feet and are exclusive to <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>.

## Overview

Mackenzie's Boots are equipped as default clothing when the player awakens from the plane crash during <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle">Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle</a>. They can not be acquired in Survival Mode.

The statistics of Mackenzie's Boots are identical to those of <a href="./Trail%20Boots.md" class="wikilink" title="Trail Boots">Trail Boots</a>.

## Episode 1

Mackenzie's Boots are already acquired when starting the mission <a href="./Memories.md" class="wikilink" title="Memories">Memories</a> in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle">Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle</a>, already being worn by <a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will Mackenzie">Will Mackenzie</a>.

The description of the boots changes during the mission <a href="./The%20Crash.md" class="wikilink" title="The Crash">The Crash</a>, but they otherwise remain the same visually and mechanically.

They can be carried into Episode 2 if the player holds onto it by the end of Episode 1.

## Episode 2

If the player starts <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> out of order, Mackenzie starts the Episode wearing his Boots.

## Episode 4

Mackenzie's Boots are still worn when playing <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence</a>, being some of the clothing left on Mackenzie to keep him warm after he was stripped of most of his gear and transported by <a href="./Mathis.md" class="wikilink" title="Mathis">Mathis</a>.
