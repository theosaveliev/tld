<p><strong>Mackenzie's Pants</strong> are a <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> item worn on the player's inner legs obtained exclusively in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Mackenzie's Pants are equipped as default clothing when the player awakens from the plane crash during the first chapter of <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Do Not Go Gentle">Do Not Go Gentle</a>.</p>
<p>The statistics of Mackenzie's Pants are identical to those of <a href="./Jeans.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeans">Jeans</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Episode_1">Episode 1</h2>
<p>Mackenzie's Pants are already acquired when starting the mission <a href="./Memories.md" class="wikilink" title="Memories">Memories</a> in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle">Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle</a>, already being worn by <a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will Mackenzie">Will Mackenzie</a>.</p>
<p>The description and appearance of the Pants changes during the mission <a href="./The%20Crash.md" class="wikilink" title="The Crash">The Crash</a>, with a small bloody bandage appearing on the left leg. They otherwise remain the same mechanically despite the appearance change.</p>
<p>They can be carried into Episode 2 if the player holds onto it by the end of Episode 1.</p>
<h2 id="Episode_2">Episode 2</h2>
<p>If the player starts <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> out of order, Mackenzie starts the Episode wearing his Pants.</p>
<h2 id="Episode_4">Episode 4</h2>
<p>Mackenzie's Pants are still worn when playing <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence</a>, being some of the clothing left on Mackenzie to keep him warm after he was stripped of most of his gear and transported by <a href="./Mathis.md" class="wikilink" title="Mathis">Mathis</a>.</p>
