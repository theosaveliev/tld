> '''Mackenzie's Pants''' are a [[Clothing]] item worn on the player's inner legs obtained exclusively in [[Wintermute]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> Mackenzie's Pants are equipped as default clothing when the player awakens from the plane crash during the first chapter of [[Do Not Go Gentle]].
> 
> The statistics of Mackenzie's Pants are identical to those of [[Jeans]].
> ==Episode 1==
> Mackenzie's Pants are already acquired when starting the mission [[Memories]] in [[Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle]], already being worn by [[Will Mackenzie]].
> 
> The description and appearance of the Pants changes during the mission [[The Crash]], with a small bloody bandage appearing on the left leg. They otherwise remain the same mechanically despite the appearance change.
> 
> They can be carried into Episode 2 if the player holds onto it by the end of Episode 1.
> == Episode 2==
> If the player starts [[Episode 2: Luminance Fugue]] out of order, Mackenzie starts the Episode wearing his Pants.
> ==Episode 4 ==
> Mackenzie's Pants are still worn when playing [[Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence]], being some of the clothing left on Mackenzie to keep him warm after he was stripped of most of his gear and transported by [[Mathis]].<gallery>
