> '''Mackenzie's Parka''' is a [[Clothing]] item worn on the player's outer torso obtained exclusively in [[Wintermute]].
> 
> The statistics of Mackenzie's Parka are identical to those of a [[Simple Parka]].
> 
> == Episode 1 ==
> Mackenzie's Parka can be found on a table in the [[Jackrabbit Hangar]] during [[Memories]] in [[Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle]], but cannot be worn. Picking it up near the end of the mission ends the playable section of the Hangar.
> 
> After resting in the cave for the night during the mission [[The Crash]], it is found near the [[Rock Climb]] in the ravine below [[Mackenzie's Crashed Plane]] as the player climbs out.
> 
> It can be carried into Episode 2 if the player holds onto it by the end of Episode 1.
> 
> ==Episode 2==
> If the player starts [[Episode 2: Luminance Fugue]] out of order, Mackenzie starts the Episode wearing his Parka.
> 
> ==Episode 4==
> Mackenzie's Parka is missing when playing [[Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence]], being discarded by [[Mathis]] (along with [[Mackenzie's Toque]]) after Mackenzie is stripped of his gear and transported.
