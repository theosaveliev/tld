<p><strong>Mackenzie's Parka</strong> is a <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> item worn on the player's outer torso obtained exclusively in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>.</p>
<p>The statistics of Mackenzie's Parka are identical to those of a <a href="./Simple%20Parka.md" class="wikilink" title="Simple Parka">Simple Parka</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Episode_1">Episode 1</h2>
<p>Mackenzie's Parka can be found on a table in the <a href="./Jackrabbit%20Hangar.md" class="wikilink" title="Jackrabbit Hangar">Jackrabbit Hangar</a> during <a href="./Memories.md" class="wikilink" title="Memories">Memories</a> in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle">Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle</a>, but cannot be worn. Picking it up near the end of the mission ends the playable section of the Hangar.</p>
<p>After resting in the cave for the night during the mission <a href="./The%20Crash.md" class="wikilink" title="The Crash">The Crash</a>, it is found near the <a href="./Rock%20Climb.md" class="wikilink" title="Rock Climb">Rock Climb</a> in the ravine below <a href="./Mackenzie%27s%20Crashed%20Plane.md" class="wikilink" title="Mackenzie&#39;s Crashed Plane">Mackenzie's Crashed Plane</a> as the player climbs out.</p>
<p>It can be carried into Episode 2 if the player holds onto it by the end of Episode 1.</p>
<h2 id="Episode_2">Episode 2</h2>
<p>If the player starts <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> out of order, Mackenzie starts the Episode wearing his Parka.</p>
<h2 id="Episode_4">Episode 4</h2>
<p>Mackenzie's Parka is missing when playing <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence</a>, being discarded by <a href="./Mathis.md" class="wikilink" title="Mathis">Mathis</a> (along with <a href="./Mackenzie%27s%20Toque.md" class="wikilink" title="Mackenzie&#39;s Toque">Mackenzie's Toque</a>) after Mackenzie is stripped of his gear and transported.</p>
