**Mackenzie's Toque**is a <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> item worn on the player's head obtained exclusively in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>.

## Overview

Mackenzie's Toque is equipped as default clothing when the player awakens from the plane crash during <a href="./Memories.md" class="wikilink" title="Memories">Memories</a>, the first chapter of <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle">Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle</a>.

The statistics of Mackenzie's Toque are in between those of a <a href="./Wool%20Toque.md" class="wikilink" title="Wool Toque">Wool Toque</a> and the <a href="./Cotton%20Toque.md" class="wikilink" title="Cotton Toque">Cotton Toque</a>.

## Episode 1

Mackenzie's Toque is not found when starting the mission <a href="./Memories.md" class="wikilink" title="Memories">Memories</a> in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle">Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle</a>.

The Toque instead first appears automatically equipped on the player during the mission <a href="./The%20Crash.md" class="wikilink" title="The Crash">The Crash</a>.

It can be carried into Episode 2 if the player holds onto it by the end of Episode 1.

## Episode 2

If the player starts <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> out of order, Mackenzie starts the Episode wearing his Toque.

## Episode 4

Mackenzie's Toque is missing when playing <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence</a>, being discarded by <a href="./Mathis.md" class="wikilink" title="Mathis">Mathis</a> (along with <a href="./Mackenzie%27s%20Parka.md" class="wikilink" title="Mackenzie&#39;s Parka">Mackenzie's Parka</a>) after Mackenzie is stripped of his gear and transported.
