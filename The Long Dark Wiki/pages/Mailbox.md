# Mailbox

> {{Infobox/Item
> |image= Mailbox.jpg
> |wintermute=yes|harvest=}}A '''Mailbox''' is a stationary object often found near many houses across the Island. They have a single cover that can be opened. Most of the time they are empty, but occasionally a single [[Rifle Ammunition]], [[Newsprint Roll]], or [[Chocolate Bar]] can be found inside.
