**Major Bruising** is an <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> that may be inflicted or caused during a <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggle">Struggle</a> or from falls.

Major Bruising damages <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> by 10%, and makes any future losses of condition slightly more damaging than it normally would. Bruising cannot be treated, and will disappear after the fall or struggle is resolved.
