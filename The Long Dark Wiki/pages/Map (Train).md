# Map \(Train\)

> {{IronRoad}}{{Infobox item|name=Map|image=Map (Train) icon.png|release=Iron Road Part 1|update=2.58|story=|type=Collectibles|equipable=No|stackable=No|weight=0.00|actions=Examine|actions_radial=|actions_ground=Take It, Read Text, Leave It|examine=A hand-drawn map. Faded writing reads 'Anna Island Track Section Five'.|key=}}'''Map''' is a [[Collectibles|Collectible]] in [[Survival Mode]] as part of the [[Iron Road]] expansion. It is found in [[Port Remnant]] at the [[Whale Bone Shrine]] near the [[Desecrated Church]]. The map leads to the nearby [[Anna Island -- Track Section 5]], and shows 2 waterfalls and a pathway into the mountains. Traveling close to the end of Track Section 5 and taking the path near the 2 waterfalls will eventually take the player to a hidden mountain path.
