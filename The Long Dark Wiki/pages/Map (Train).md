<p><strong>Map</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Port%20Remnant.md" class="wikilink" title="Port Remnant">Port Remnant</a> at the <a href="./Whale%20Bone%20Shrine.md" class="wikilink" title="Whale Bone Shrine">Whale Bone Shrine</a> near the <a href="./Desecrated%20Church.md" class="wikilink" title="Desecrated Church">Desecrated Church</a>. The map leads to the nearby <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%205.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 5">Anna Island -- Track Section 5</a>, and shows 2 waterfalls and a pathway into the mountains. Traveling close to the end of Track Section 5 and taking the path near the 2 waterfalls will eventually take the player to a hidden mountain path.</p>
