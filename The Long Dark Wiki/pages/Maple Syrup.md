**Maple Syrup** is a packaged <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark"><em>The Long Dark</em></a>.

## Overview

Consuming Maple Syrup provides up to 850 calories of <a href="./Hunger.md" class="wikilink" title="energy">energy</a>, and does not cause <a href="./Thirst.md" class="wikilink" title="dehydration">dehydration</a>. They are rarely found in pantries and other containers, but more commonly in fixed spawns at named locations.

Maple Syrup are the rarest food item, with only 5-7 found in an entire world, roughly half the spawn chance of the next rarest food item: <a href="./Military-Grade%20MRE.md" class="wikilink" title="Military-Grade MRE">Military-Grade MRE</a>. Maple Syrup decays relatively rapidly for an artificial item, resulting in potentially even fewer than 5-7 present if some become Ruined (0% Condition) inside of a container and are destroyed.

Maple Syrup can never cause <a href="./Food%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Food Poisoning">Food Poisoning</a> above 20% condition, and <a href="./Decay.md" class="wikilink" title="decays">decays</a> more slowly when outdoors.

## Trivia

- Maple Syrup was first introduced for the limited time "<a href="./Winter%27s%20Embrace.md" class="wikilink" title="Winter&#39;s Embrace">Winter's Embrace</a>" event (June 29th - July 31st, 2020), along with <a href="./Ketchup%20Chips.md" class="wikilink" title="Ketchup Chips">Ketchup Chips</a>.
- During the event, eating 25 Ketchup Chips and 25 Maple Syrup, awarded the "Canadian Feast <a href="./Badges.md" class="wikilink" title="Badge">Badge</a>".
- Maple Syrup is labeled as "Grade A" and has "Product of/Produit du Canada" on the back.
