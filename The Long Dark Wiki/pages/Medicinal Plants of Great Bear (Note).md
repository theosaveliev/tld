**Medicinal Plants of Great Bear** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item cut from the Redux version of <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1">Episode 1</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode. It is unknown if this note was intended to be found inside of a pharmacy, as there is currently no pharmacy in <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a>. Regardless, it was replaced by the book <a href="./Medicinal%20Plants%20of%20Great%20Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Medicinal Plants of Great Bear">Medicinal Plants of Great Bear</a> and is considered <a href="./Cut%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20Content.md" class="wikilink" title="Cut/Discontinued Content">Cut/Discontinued Content</a>.

## Transcript

<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">

The following text is transcribed from **Medicinal Plants of Great Bear**

</div>

<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

ANNOUNCEMENT:\
This came up the last time that Mainland doctor dropped in to see us, and now we're ready to kick it off. To add to our shrinking pharmacy here in town, we'll be keeping an inventory of local plants that might supplement some of our traditional meds. So if you're out in the woods, or even just around town, things to keep an eye out for are the following:\
\
(\*)Reishi Mushrooms, to brew into an antibiotic tea.\
(\*)Old Man's Beard lichen, for its natural antiseptic properties. You can bind cuts, scrapes, and wounds with a dressing made from the stuff.\
(\*)Rose hips, which we can process into an anti-inflammatory, pain-killing tea.

Stuart has volunteered to maintain the inventory and record the items as we collect them, as well as check for quality. This is especially important with the mushrooms. We don't want to make anyone even sicker, or worse.\
We know times are tough, but some of us have been through this sort of thing before. We can make it.

</div>
