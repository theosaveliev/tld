**Medicinal Plants of Great Bear** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1">Episode 1</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode to start the <a href="./The%20Basics%20of%20Survival.md" class="wikilink" title="The Basics of Survival">The Basics of Survival</a> side mission.

During the course of the mission, the survivor finds the book *Medicinal Plants of Great Bear* from the <a href="./Milton%20Post%20Office.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton Post Office">Milton Post Office</a> in <a href="./Milton.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton">Milton</a>, and are tasked to find, harvest, and prepare some medicinal flora native to <a href="./Great%20Bear%20Island.md" class="wikilink" title="Great Bear Island">Great Bear Island</a>.
