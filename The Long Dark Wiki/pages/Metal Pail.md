# Metal Pail

> {{Infobox/Item
> |image= MetalPail.jpg
> |wintermute=yes}}A '''Metal Pail''' is an interactable object that can be harvested. A [[Hacksaw]] can be used to harvest 1 [[Scrap Metal]] after 15 minutes of sawing. They are often found near homes and industrial areas.
> 
> They sometimes have items hidden inside, such as [[Lantern Fuel]], [[Cardboard Matches]], [[Firestriker]], or food.
