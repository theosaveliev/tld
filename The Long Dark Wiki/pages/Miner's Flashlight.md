> |renewable=No (unique item)}}'''Miner's Flashlight''' is a variant of the [[Flashlight]] in ''The Long Dark'', added by the ''[[Tales from the Far Territory]]'' expansion.
> 
> ==Overview==
> The Miner's Flashlight is a yellow, heavy duty electrical torch that has a larger, longer lasting battery than the regular Flashlight, but at the expense of being 0.25 kg heavier. While a 100% charged Miner's Flashlight lasts for up to 1,5 hours of continuous use (50% more than the regular Flashlight), the high beam mode lasts the same 25 seconds.
> 
> Unlike other item variants, multiple Miner's Flashlights may spawn (on Pilgrim, Voyageur and Stalker) making it the second variant which is not unique, the other one being the Sport Bow.
> 
> Miner's Flashlight was added in [[Tales from the Far Territory]] update Part 3: "Frontier Comforts".
> 
> ==Location==
> The Miner's Flashlight spawns a single time per world in various states of repair. The flashlight's location may differ between saves, but it is found within: 
> 
> === Far Range Branch Line ===
> It can be found in 3 locations within the [[Far Range Branch Line]]:
> 
> *Inside the boxcars located just after [[Steely Chasm]] and near [[Remnant Cave]], behind a [[Metal Container]]
> *Inside the [[Train Shuttle]] on [[Surfeit Trestle]]
> *On a shelf in the south train tunnel office. 
> 
> === Zone of Contamination ===
> It is also located in the [[Zone of Contamination]], inside the [[Concentrator]] in a control room overlooking 2 large vats. It can be reached by walking further up the facility, jumping into a hopper, and then following the pipes to bypass a broken catwalk. This location will always spawn a Miner's Flashlight (except on Interloper). 
> 
> It is possible to have 2 versions of this item spawn on one playthrough.
