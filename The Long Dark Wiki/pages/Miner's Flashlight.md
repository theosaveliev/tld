<p><strong>Miner's Flashlight</strong> is a variant of the <a href="./Flashlight.md" class="wikilink" title="Flashlight">Flashlight</a> in <em>The Long Dark</em>, added by the <em><a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a></em> expansion.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>The Miner's Flashlight is a yellow, heavy duty electrical torch that has a larger, longer lasting battery than the regular Flashlight, but at the expense of being 0.25 kg heavier. While a 100% charged Miner's Flashlight lasts for up to 1,5 hours of continuous use (50% more than the regular Flashlight), the high beam mode lasts the same 25 seconds.</p>
<p>Unlike other item variants, multiple Miner's Flashlights may spawn (on Pilgrim, Voyageur and Stalker) making it the second variant which is not unique, the other one being the Sport Bow.</p>
<p>Miner's Flashlight was added in <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> update Part 3: "Frontier Comforts".</p>
<h2 id="Location">Location</h2>
<p>The Miner's Flashlight spawns a single time per world in various states of repair. The flashlight's location may differ between saves, but it is found within:</p>
<h3 id="Far_Range_Branch_Line">Far Range Branch Line</h3>
<p>It can be found in 3 locations within the <a href="./Far%20Range%20Branch%20Line.md" class="wikilink" title="Far Range Branch Line">Far Range Branch Line</a>:</p>
<ul>
<li>Inside the boxcars located just after <a href="./Steely%20Chasm.md" class="wikilink" title="Steely Chasm">Steely Chasm</a> and near <a href="./Remnant%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Remnant Cave">Remnant Cave</a>, behind a <a href="./Metal%20Container.md" class="wikilink" title="Metal Container">Metal Container</a></li>
<li>Inside the <a href="./Train%20Shuttle.md" class="wikilink" title="Train Shuttle">Train Shuttle</a> on <a href="./Surfeit%20Trestle.md" class="wikilink" title="Surfeit Trestle">Surfeit Trestle</a></li>
<li>On a shelf in the south train tunnel office.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="Zone_of_Contamination">Zone of Contamination</h3>
<p>It is also located in the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>, inside the <a href="./Concentrator.md" class="wikilink" title="Concentrator">Concentrator</a> in a control room overlooking 2 large vats. It can be reached by walking further up the facility, jumping into a hopper, and then following the pipes to bypass a broken catwalk. This location will always spawn a Miner's Flashlight (except on Interloper).</p>
<p>It is possible to have 2 versions of this item spawn on one playthrough.</p>
