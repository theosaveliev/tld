**Miner's Footpath** is a location in the [Zone of Contamination](./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md). It is a stone's throw away from the most southwestern entrance to the [Langston Mine](./Langston%20Mine.md). The entrance of the mine here offers a [Jerry Can](./Jerry%20Can.md) to top off [Storm Lanterns](./Storm%20Lantern.md). A small pond with Rabbits is nearby for an easy source of food. To the south is [Smoker's Cave](./Smoker%27s%20Cave.md) and [Desperate Elevation](./Desperate%20Elevation.md).
