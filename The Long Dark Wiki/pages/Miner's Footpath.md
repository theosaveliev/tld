<p><strong>Miner's Footpath</strong> is a location in the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>. It is a stone's throw away from the most southwestern entrance to the <a href="./Langston%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Langston Mine">Langston Mine</a>. The entrance of the mine here offers a <a href="./Jerry%20Can.md" class="wikilink" title="Jerry Can">Jerry Can</a> to top off <a href="./Storm%20Lantern.md" class="wikilink" title="Storm Lanterns">Storm Lanterns</a>. A small pond with Rabbits is nearby for an easy source of food. To the south is <a href="./Smoker%27s%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Smoker&#39;s Cave">Smoker's Cave</a> and <a href="./Desperate%20Elevation.md" class="wikilink" title="Desperate Elevation">Desperate Elevation</a>.</p>
