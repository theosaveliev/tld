# Miner's Footpath

> {{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Miner's Footpath.jpg|region=Zone of Contamination|description=A large field close to the southwestern opening to the [[Langston Mine]]|map=Miner's Footpath Location.png}}
> 
> '''Miner's Footpath''' is a location in the [[Zone of Contamination]]. It is a stone's throw away from the most southwestern entrance to the [[Langston Mine]]. The entrance of the mine here offers a [[Jerry Can]] to top off [[Storm Lantern|Storm Lanterns]]. A small pond with Rabbits is nearby for an easy source of food. To the south is [[Smoker's Cave]] and [[Desperate Elevation]].
