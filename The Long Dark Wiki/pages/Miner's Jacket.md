The **Miner's Jacket** is a unique <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> jacket found in various locations across the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>. It most often spawns somewhere inside the <a href="./Langston%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Langston Mine">Langston Mine</a>, <a href="./Concentrator.md" class="wikilink" title="Concentrator">Concentrator</a>, <a href="./Conveyor%20Room.md" class="wikilink" title="Conveyor Room">Conveyor Room</a>, <a href="./Headframe%20Building.md" class="wikilink" title="Headframe Building">Headframe Building</a>, or <a href="./Idle%20Camp.md" class="wikilink" title="Idle Camp">Idle Camp</a>.
