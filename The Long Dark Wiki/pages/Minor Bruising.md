**Properties**

**Category:** [Afflictions](./Afflictions.md)

**Caused By:** Predator [Struggle](./Struggle.md)

**Effect:** Negative

**Type:** Injury

**Affliction Duration:** Instant Treatment

**Treatment**

**Recovery Time:** Affliction instantly heals once acquired

**Minor Bruising** is an [Affliction](./Afflictions.md) that may be inflicted or caused during a [Struggle](./Struggle.md) or from falls.

Minor Bruising damages [Condition](./Condition.md) by 5%, and makes any future losses of condition slightly more damaging than it normally would. Bruising cannot be treated, and will disappear after the fall or struggle is resolved.
