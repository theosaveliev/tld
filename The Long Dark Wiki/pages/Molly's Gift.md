**Molly's Gift** is a side mission in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3">Episode 3</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="story">story</a>. In this mission, Astrid retrieves <a href="./Marine%20Flare.md" class="wikilink" title="Marine Flares">Marine Flares</a> from a car in <a href="./Thomson%27s%20Crossing.md" class="wikilink" title="Thompson&#39;s Crossing">Thompson's Crossing</a>.

## Tasks

- Find and search the car trunk Molly mentioned.
- Clear out Grey Wolves from the pond.
- Clear out Grey Wolves from the forest.
- Find more Marine Flares.
- Experiment with Marine Flares.

## Walkthrough

When leaving Community Hall for the first time, <a href="./Molly.md" class="wikilink" title="Molly">Molly</a> calls Astrid on the party line and tells her that something useful is in the trunk of the blue car near the swingset. The car is found on the other end of the main street in Thompson's Crossing. Inside the trunk are several <a href="./Marine%20Flare.md" class="wikilink" title="Marine Flares">Marine Flares</a>, which may be used to ward off <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="timberwolves">timberwolves</a>.
