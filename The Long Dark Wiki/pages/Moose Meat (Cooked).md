**Moose Meat (Cooked)** is a cooked [food](./Food%20And%20Drink.md) item in _The Long Dark_.

<span id="overview"></span><span id="Overview"></span>

## Overview

Cooked Moose Meat is obtained by using a lit [Fire](./Fire.md) and improves the [Cooking](./Cooking.md) skill. Cooking increases its condition by 50%, makes it decay 4 times slower than raw meat, and adds a reduced chance of [Food Poisoning](./Food%20Poisoning.md) over raw meat.

Using a [Cooking Pot](./Cooking%20Pot.md) reduces cooking time by 25%. Cooked food on an active fire for too long will burn it, destroying the item and damaging a Cooking Pot if used.
