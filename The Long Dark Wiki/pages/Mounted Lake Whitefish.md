The **Mounted Lake Whitefish** is a [Decorations](./Decorations.md) item that can be [Crafted](./Crafting.md) as part of the [Safehouse Customization](./Safehouse%20Customization.md) feature.

## Overview

A taxidermized [Lake Whitefish](./Lake%20Whitefish%20%28Raw%29.md) with a makeshift mount, a Mounted Lake Whitefish can be crafted at a [Furniture Workbench](./Furniture%20Workbench.md), but requires [Woodworking Tools](./Woodworking%20Tools.md).

Once crafted, the item is automatically placed into the player's inventory. It can only be placed on walls when in Customization Mode. It can later be picked up and placed back into the player's inventory or destroyed (yielding no resources).
