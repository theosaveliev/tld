**Naturalist Notebook 1** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> found on a shelf in the coal tower at <a href="./Vacant%20Depot.md" class="wikilink" title="Vacant Depot">Vacant Depot</a>.

The note refers to the behavior of the local <a href="./Cougar.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougars">Cougars</a> on <a href="./Great%20Bear%20Island.md" class="wikilink" title="Great Bear Island">Great Bear Island</a>.

## Transcript

<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">

The following text is transcribed from **Naturalist Notebook 1**

</div>

<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

I made multiple sightings this morning. And I can hear them stalking me.

They have very distinct territories on the island. This was expected, but even for a wild cat they take this behaviour to an extreme. Which worries me.

I believe it's possible to live at peace with them - and my work here will show that. But I need to track their movements and note the boundaries of their hunting grounds. And somehow survive.

</div>
