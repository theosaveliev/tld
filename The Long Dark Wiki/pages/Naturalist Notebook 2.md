**Naturalist Notebook 2** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> found in the <a href="./Fishing%20Hut.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Hut">Fishing Hut</a> at <a href="./Ambler%27s%20Lake.md" class="wikilink" title="Ambler&#39;s Lake">Ambler's Lake</a> in <a href="./Sundered%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Sundered Pass">Sundered Pass</a>.

The note refers to the behavior of the local <a href="./Cougar.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougars">Cougars</a> on <a href="./Great%20Bear%20Island.md" class="wikilink" title="Great Bear Island">Great Bear Island</a>.
