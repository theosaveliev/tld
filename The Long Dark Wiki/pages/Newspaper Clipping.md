**Newspaper Clipping** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3">Episode 3</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode as a part of the <a href="./Church%20Artifact%20%28mission%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Church Artifact">Church Artifact</a> side mission.

During the course of the mission, <a href="./Astrid%20Greenwood.md" class="wikilink" title="Astrid Greenwood">Astrid Greenwood</a> finds the note *Newspaper Clipping* on the floor at the <a href="./Draft%20Dodger%27s%20Cabin.md" class="wikilink" title="Draft Dodger&#39;s Cabin">Draft Dodger's Cabin</a>.
