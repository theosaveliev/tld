**No-Half Measures** is a <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, then Silence</a> of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This is a side mission achievement achieved after completing the side mission <a href="./A%20Riddle%20Among%20the%20Shadows.md" class="wikilink" title="A Riddle Among the Shadows">A Riddle Among the Shadows</a>. Opening the locked <a href="./Forest%20Talkers.md" class="wikilink" title="Forest Talkers">Forest Talkers</a> cache in the <a href="./Last%20Prospect.md" class="wikilink" title="Last Prospect">Last Prospect</a> entrance will allow you to find the note <a href="./Forest%20Talker%20Memo.md" class="wikilink" title="Forest Talker Memo">Forest Talker Memo</a>, and unlocks the achievement.

## Trivia

The achievement gets its name from some text in the <a href="./Forest%20Talker%20Memo.md" class="wikilink" title="Forest Talker Memo">Forest Talker Memo</a> note: "The time for half-measures is over."
