# No Half\-Measures

**No Half-Measures**

[No Half Measures icon](../assets/No_Half-Measures_icon.png)

**Description:** Uncover the latest Forest Talker Plans.

**No-Half Measures** is a [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in [Episode 4: Fury, then Silence](./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md) of _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

This is a side mission achievement achieved after completing the side mission [A Riddle Among the Shadows](./A%20Riddle%20Among%20the%20Shadows.md). Opening the locked [Forest Talkers](./Forest%20Talkers.md) cache in the [Last Prospect](./Last%20Prospect.md) entrance will allow you to find the note [Forest Talker Memo](./Forest%20Talker%20Memo.md), and unlocks the achievement.

## Trivia

The achievement gets its name from some text in the [Forest Talker Memo](./Forest%20Talker%20Memo.md) note: "The time for half-measures is over."
