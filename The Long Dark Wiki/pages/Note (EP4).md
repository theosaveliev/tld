**Note** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) note found in [Episode 4](./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story. It is found on a wooden shelf in the room opened by the [Blackrock Gate Key](./Blackrock%20Gate%20Key.md), where the first [Noisemaker](./Noisemaker.md) is found.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Note**_

Re: Donated food packs

We're going to need some volunteers to dig into a few of these rations that got dropped on us.

We'll be in the barracks tonight with the cook to open them up. If they're any good we might just keep them for ourselves.

Show up at 7 if you're interested.

-- Howie
