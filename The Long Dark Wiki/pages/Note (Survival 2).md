**Note** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectibles">Collectibles</a> found in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> at <a href="./Blackrock.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock">Blackrock</a>.
