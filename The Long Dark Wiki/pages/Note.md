**Note** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectibles">Collectibles</a> found in <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 5">Episode 5</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story. It is found inside the <a href="./Fukkatsu%20Maru.md" class="wikilink" title="Fukkatsu Maru">Fukkatsu Maru</a> during the main mission <a href="./A%20Cry%20For%20Help.md" class="wikilink" title="A Cry For Help">A Cry For Help</a>. It is found in lowest deck's engine room, under the corpse of the hanged man.
