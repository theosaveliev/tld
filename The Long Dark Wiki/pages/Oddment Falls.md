**Oddment Falls** is an unmarked location found within the [Far Range Branch Line](./Far%20Range%20Branch%20Line.md) region.

It is located at the southwest corner of the map, not far from [Surfeit Trestle](./Surfeit%20Trestle.md) and the concrete walkway leading into the south tunnel of the map in the lower pond area. The falls offer no loot or shelter, and [Wolves](./Wolf.md) tend to patrol the area.
