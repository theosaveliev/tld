<p><strong>Oddment Falls</strong> is an unmarked location found within the <a href="./Far%20Range%20Branch%20Line.md" class="wikilink" title="Far Range Branch Line">Far Range Branch Line</a> region.</p>
<p>It is located at the southwest corner of the map, not far from <a href="./Surfeit%20Trestle.md" class="wikilink" title="Surfeit Trestle">Surfeit Trestle</a> and the concrete walkway leading into the south tunnel of the map in the lower pond area. The falls offer no loot or shelter, and <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolves">Wolves</a> tend to patrol the area.</p>
