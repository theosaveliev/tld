# Oddment Falls

> {{Infobox/Location|title=Oddment Falls|region=Far Range Branch Line|description=A set of waterfalls near Surfeit Trestle|image=Oddment Falls.jpg|location=|map=Oddment Falls Location.png}}
> 
> '''Oddment Falls''' is an unmarked location found within the [[Far Range Branch Line]] region.
> 
> It is located at the southwest corner of the map, not far from [[Surfeit Trestle]] and the concrete walkway leading into the south tunnel of the map in the lower pond area. The falls offer no loot or shelter, and [[Wolf|Wolves]] tend to patrol the area.
