**Off Season Fun** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode. It is found in one of the locked cabins at the <a href="./Lake%20Cabins.md" class="wikilink" title="Lake Cabins">Lake Cabins</a>.
