**Office Memo** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> note found in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story. It is found in the weapon locker in the room by the <a href="./Blackrock%20Gate%20Key.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Gate Key">Blackrock Gate Key</a>, where the first <a href="./Noisemaker.md" class="wikilink" title="Noisemaker">Noisemaker</a> is found.
