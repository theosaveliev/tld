# Ogre's Teardrop

> {{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Ogre's Teardrop.jpg|region=Sundered Pass|shelter=no|description=A large frozen waterfall below the valley|map=Ogre's Teardrop Location.png}}'''Ogre's Teardrop''' is a frozen waterfall located in [[Sundered Pass]]. It is located deep below the valley and is a short walk from [[Juniper Runoff]] and under [[Perilous Crossing]].
> ==Overview==
> There is a small frozen pond in front of the waterfall, along with a few [[Cat Tail]] plants. There is no other noteworthy loot nearby. A [[Green Maple Sapling]] can be found near the pond. The pond is not deep enough for [[Ice Fishing]].
> 
> A cave above in the mountains passes behind the waterfall. The cave has no warmth bonus, making [[Sleep|Sleeping]] or resting here without an active [[Campfire]] very dangerous and cold. A [[Frozen Corpse]] can often be found inside. A buried [[Supply Cache]] has a chance of spawning outside the cave near the crossroads, and can be uncovered when the [[Handheld Shortwave Radio]] is powered.
> 
> == Shortcut ==
> A shortcut from the mountain above can be taken to reach this location by [[Mountain Goating]] down a nearby cliff face.<gallery>
