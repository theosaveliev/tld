**Ogre's Teardrop** is a frozen waterfall located in [Sundered Pass](./Sundered%20Pass.md). It is located deep below the valley and is a short walk from [Juniper Runoff](./Juniper%20Runoff.md) and under [Perilous Crossing](./Perilous%20Crossing.md).

## Overview

There is a small frozen pond in front of the waterfall, along with a few [Cat Tail](./Cat%20Tail.md) plants. There is no other noteworthy loot nearby. A [Green Maple Sapling](./Green%20Maple%20Sapling.md) can be found near the pond. The pond is not deep enough for [Ice Fishing](./Ice%20Fishing.md).

A cave above in the mountains passes behind the waterfall. The cave has no warmth bonus, making [Sleeping](./Sleep.md) or resting here without an active [Campfire](./Campfire.md) very dangerous and cold. A [Frozen Corpse](./Frozen%20Corpse.md) can often be found inside. A buried [Supply Cache](./Supply%20Cache.md) has a chance of spawning outside the cave near the crossroads, and can be uncovered when the [Handheld Shortwave Radio](./Handheld%20Shortwave%20Radio.md) is powered.

## Shortcut

A shortcut from the mountain above can be taken to reach this location by [Mountain Goating](./Mountain%20Goating.md) down a nearby cliff face.
