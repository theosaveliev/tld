<td><p>Has no shelter|} <strong>Ogre's Teardrop</strong> is a frozen waterfall located in <a href="./Sundered%20Pass.md" class="wikilink" title="Sundered Pass">Sundered Pass</a>. It is located deep below the valley and is a short walk from <a href="./Juniper%20Runoff.md" class="wikilink" title="Juniper Runoff">Juniper Runoff</a> and under <a href="./Perilous%20Crossing.md" class="wikilink" title="Perilous Crossing">Perilous Crossing</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>There is a small frozen pond in front of the waterfall, along with a few <a href="./Cat%20Tail.md" class="wikilink" title="Cat Tail">Cat Tail</a> plants. There is no other noteworthy loot nearby. A <a href="./Green%20Maple%20Sapling.md" class="wikilink" title="Green Maple Sapling">Green Maple Sapling</a> can be found near the pond. The pond is not deep enough for <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a>.</p>
<p>A cave above in the mountains passes behind the waterfall. The cave has no warmth bonus, making <a href="./Sleep.md" class="wikilink" title="Sleeping">Sleeping</a> or resting here without an active <a href="./Campfire.md" class="wikilink" title="Campfire">Campfire</a> very dangerous and cold. A <a href="./Frozen%20Corpse.md" class="wikilink" title="Frozen Corpse">Frozen Corpse</a> can often be found inside. A buried <a href="./Supply%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Supply Cache">Supply Cache</a> has a chance of spawning outside the cave near the crossroads, and can be uncovered when the <a href="./Handheld%20Shortwave%20Radio.md" class="wikilink" title="Handheld Shortwave Radio">Handheld Shortwave Radio</a> is powered.</p>
<h2 id="Shortcut">Shortcut</h2>
<p>A shortcut from the mountain above can be taken to reach this location by <a href="./Mountain%20Goating.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountain Goating">Mountain Goating</a> down a nearby cliff face.</p>
