**Old Substation Work Memo** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> note found in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story. It is found on a wooden bench near the door inside the <a href="./Old%20Substation.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Substation">Old Substation</a>.
