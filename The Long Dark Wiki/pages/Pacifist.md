**Pacifist** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after surviving the first twenty-five days without killing an animal in a single <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> game mode. It is most easily done on Pilgrim.

Capturing or killing an animal with the <a href="./Hunting%20Rifle.md" class="wikilink" title="hunting rifle">hunting rifle</a>, <a href="./Survival%20Bow.md" class="wikilink" title="survival bow">survival bow</a>, <a href="./Snare.md" class="wikilink" title="snare">snare</a>, or <a href="./Fishing%20Tackle.md" class="wikilink" title="fishing tackle">fishing tackle</a> will nullify the achievement. Therefore, it is highly recommended that the player seeks sustenance by scavenging meat from deceased animals killed by <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="wolves">wolves</a> and ransacking abandoned buildings.

This achievement must be made in the first twenty-five days of the play through. If the player kills an animal within the first twenty-five days of surviving it will nullify the achievement for that entire play through.
