**Pacifist** is an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after surviving the first twenty-five days without killing an animal in a single [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) game mode. It is most easily done on Pilgrim.

Capturing or killing an animal with the [hunting rifle](./Hunting%20Rifle.md), [survival bow](./Survival%20Bow.md), [snare](./Snare.md), or [fishing tackle](./Fishing%20Tackle.md) will nullify the achievement. Therefore, it is highly recommended that the player seeks sustenance by scavenging meat from deceased animals killed by [wolves](./Wolf.md) and ransacking abandoned buildings.

This achievement must be made in the first twenty-five days of the play through. If the player kills an animal within the first twenty-five days of surviving it will nullify the achievement for that entire play through.
