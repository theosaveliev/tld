A **Paddle** is a rare harvestable item found inside the <a href="./Orca%20Gas%20Station.md" class="wikilink" title="Orca Gas Station">Orca Gas Station</a> in the <a href="./Mountain%20Town.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountain Town">Mountain Town</a> region.

- Three can be found inside the store next to the register counter and they require a <a href="./Hatchet.md" class="wikilink" title="Hatchet">Hatchet</a> to harvest.
- They can be found over the doorways inside some Cabins in <a href="./Coastal%20Highway.md" class="wikilink" title="Coastal Highway">Coastal Highway</a>.
- As of the <a href="./Beachcombing.md" class="wikilink" title="Beachcombing">Beachcombing</a> update, they can sometimes be found washed ashore in rowboats.
