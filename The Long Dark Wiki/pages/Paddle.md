**General**

**Item Type:** Item

**Wintermute:** No

**Statistics**

**Harvest:** 2 × [Reclaimed Wood](./Reclaimed%20Wood.md)

A **Paddle** is a rare harvestable item found inside the [Orca Gas Station](./Orca%20Gas%20Station.md) in the [Mountain Town](./Mountain%20Town.md) region.

- Three can be found inside the store next to the register counter and they require a [Hatchet](./Hatchet.md) to harvest.
- They can be found over the doorways inside some Cabins in [Coastal Highway](./Coastal%20Highway.md).
- As of the [Beachcombing](./Beachcombing.md) update, they can sometimes be found washed ashore in rowboats.
