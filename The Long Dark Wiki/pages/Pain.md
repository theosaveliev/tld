## Overview

Pain is a negative affliction introduced in the [Steadfast Ranger](https://hinterlandforums.com/forums/topic/22877-the-long-dark-survival-mode-updated-to-v148-steadfast-ranger/) update. It can be caused by a <a href="./Sprained%20Wrist.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Wrist">Sprained Wrist</a>, <a href="./Sprained%20Ankle.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Ankle">Sprained Ankle</a>, <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggle">Struggle</a>, or a fall.

While in effect, Pain causes the player's vision to blur slightly around the edges, and will also distort sounds. Pain can be cured immediately with two <a href="./Painkillers.md" class="wikilink" title="Painkillers">Painkillers</a> or one <a href="./Rose%20Hip%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Rose Hip Tea">Rose Hip Tea</a>. Left untreated, Pain will simply heal on its own after four hours.

Because its only negative impact on actual gameplay is an inability to read <a href="./Research%20Book.md" class="wikilink" title="Research Books">Research Books</a>, treating Pain with <a href="./First%20Aid.md" class="wikilink" title="First Aid">First Aid</a> can be a potentially wasteful decision; Painkillers and Rose Hip Tea can be used to greater effect by treating <a href="./Burns.md" class="wikilink" title="Burns">Burns</a> or <a href="./Broken%20Ribs.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Ribs">Broken Ribs</a>.
