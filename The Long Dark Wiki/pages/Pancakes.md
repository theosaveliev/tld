<h2 id="Analysis">Analysis</h2>
<p>Prepared pancakes are generally not a very useful food to bring along when travelling, as they tend to decay rather quickly. However, their dry ingredients only weigh around 0.225 kg; including the water brings the total weight of ingredients up to 0.375 kg. This brings their total energy density up to 2,222 kcal/kg, or 1,300 kcal/kg if the water required is included. If the survivor has level 5 <a href="./Cooking.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking">Cooking</a>, this energy density can be improved to 2,777 kcal/kg, or 1,625 kcal/kg including water. Overall, they make an adequate food source when travelling.</p>
