**Paradise Lost** is a [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in [Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle](./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md) of _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

This is an unmissable story achievement achieved after completing the mission [The Crash](./The%20Crash.md). Climb your way out of the ravine you crashed in and walk towards [Mackenzie's Crashed Plane](./Mackenzie%27s%20Crashed%20Plane.md), then make your way to the Town of Milton. Go into the [Milton House (Grey Mother's)](./Milton%20House%20%28Grey%20Mother%27s%29.md) and step inside.
