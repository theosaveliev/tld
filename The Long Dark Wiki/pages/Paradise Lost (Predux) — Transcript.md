This is the transcript for Chapter 4 [Paradise Lost (Predux)](./Paradise%20Lost%20%28Predux%29.md) for Predux [Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle](./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md).

## Find the climbing area on the outskirts of Milton.

_After retrieving [Lilith Barker's](./Lilith%20Barker.md) climbing equipment, Mackenzie then heads to [Milton Park](./Milton%20Park.md) by following [Lily's Map](./Lily%27s%20Map.md). He ties the [Mountaineering Rope](./Mountaineering%20Rope.md) to a rock, but before he leaves Milton behind, Mackenzie says:_

Mackenzie: "I have a bad feeling that I'm never going to see this place again."

_Mackenzie eventually uses the rope climb and begins climbing down, but the rope begins to fail two-thirds of the way down the climb and then snaps._

Mackenzie: "What the hell...I guess that rope was older than it looked."

## Find the route that leads through the mountains.

_With the rope broken and no way back up to Milton, Mackenzie travels further into the mountains. Upon traveling a bit further away from the climbing spot, Mackenzie finds another rope to climb up into the mountains. After passing a waterfall and frozen river, he sees the road on the other side of the collapsed tunnel, and reminisces about his Crashed Plane._

Mackenzie: "That plane's taken me to a lot of places. Sorry to leave you here, old girl. But right now I gotta find Astrid."

_He automatically enters the cave and passes through the mountains, leaving Milton behind._

_Upon making it to the other side, he sees a man overwhelmed by a [bear](./Old%20Bear.md)._

[Jeremiah](./Jeremiah.md): "Shoot the damn Bear!!"

_The pilot picks up a hunting rifle, then aims at the bear before the screen turns black. A gunshot is heard, and the credits roll._
