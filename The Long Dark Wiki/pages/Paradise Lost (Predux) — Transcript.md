This is the transcript for Chapter 4 <a href="./Paradise%20Lost%20%28Predux%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Paradise Lost (Predux)">Paradise Lost (Predux)</a> for Predux <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle">Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle</a>.

## Find the climbing area on the outskirts of Milton.

*After retrieving <a href="./Lilith%20Barker.md" class="wikilink" title="Lilith Barker&#39;s">Lilith Barker's</a> climbing equipment, Mackenzie then heads to <a href="./Milton%20Park.md" class="wikilink" title="Milton Park">Milton Park</a> by following <a href="./Lily%27s%20Map.md" class="wikilink" title="Lily&#39;s Map">Lily's Map</a>. He ties the <a href="./Mountaineering%20Rope.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountaineering Rope">Mountaineering Rope</a> to a rock, but before he leaves Milton behind, Mackenzie says:*

Mackenzie: "I have a bad feeling that I'm never going to see this place again."

*Mackenzie eventually uses the rope climb and begins climbing down, but the rope begins to fail two-thirds of the way down the climb and then snaps.*

Mackenzie: "What the hell...I guess that rope was older than it looked."

## Find the route that leads through the mountains.

*With the rope broken and no way back up to Milton, Mackenzie travels further into the mountains. Upon traveling a bit further away from the climbing spot, Mackenzie finds another rope to climb up into the mountains. After passing a waterfall and frozen river, he sees the road on the other side of the collapsed tunnel, and reminisces about his Crashed Plane.*

Mackenzie: "That plane's taken me to a lot of places. Sorry to leave you here, old girl. But right now I gotta find Astrid."

*He automatically enters the cave and passes through the mountains, leaving Milton behind.*

*Upon making it to the other side, he sees a man overwhelmed by a <a href="./Old%20Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="bear">bear</a>.*

<a href="./Jeremiah.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah">Jeremiah</a>: "Shoot the damn Bear!!"

*The pilot picks up a hunting rifle, then aims at the bear before the screen turns black. A gunshot is heard, and the credits roll.*
