**Patterned Wool Mittens (Brown)** is the name given to 3 unique variations of [Wool Mittens](./Wool%20Mittens.md) that can be acquired from the [Trader](./Trader.md)**,** are a type of [Clothing](./Clothing.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Overview

Wool mittens have the best warmth-to-weight ratio of any pair of gloves and are easy to maintain, however they offer little protection against animal attacks.
