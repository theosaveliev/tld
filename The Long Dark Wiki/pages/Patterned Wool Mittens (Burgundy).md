**Patterned Wool Mittens (Burgundy)** is the name given to 3 unique variations of <a href="./Wool%20Mittens.md" class="wikilink" title="Wool Mittens">Wool Mittens</a> that can be acquired from the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a>**,** are a type of <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Overview

Wool mittens have the best warmth-to-weight ratio of any pair of gloves and are easy to maintain, however they offer little protection against animal attacks.
