<p><strong>Patterned Wool Socks (Plaid)</strong> is the name given to 3 unique variations of <a href="./Wool%20Socks.md" class="wikilink" title="Wool Socks">Wool Socks</a> that can be acquired from the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a><strong>,</strong> are a type of <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> in <em><a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a></em>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Wool Socks are mid-tier socks. They providing double the warmth and weight of <a href="./Sports%20Socks.md" class="wikilink" title="Sports Socks">Sports Socks</a> and decay slower than them, but decay faster than <a href="./Climbing%20Socks.md" class="wikilink" title="Climbing Socks">Climbing Socks</a> and have inferior stats.</p>
<p>Wool Socks are the best available socks in <a href="./Interloper.md" class="wikilink" title="Interloper">Interloper</a>/ <a href="./Misery.md" class="wikilink" title="Misery">Misery</a> mode.</p>
