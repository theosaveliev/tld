> |renewable=No ([[Rose Hip]])}}'''Pemmican Bar''' is a [[Food And Drink]] item in ''[[The Long Dark]]''. It is learned after acquiring its unique [[Recipe Cards|Recipe Card]].
> ==Overview==
> It is only acquired when trading with the [[Trader]] for the Recipe Card of the same name.
> 
> Cooking the ingredients generates four (4) bars, each weighing 0.0625kg (stacking up to 0.25kg for all 4).
> 
> *Increased carry weight ([[Unburdened]]) (+3kg for 3 hours)
> *Increased maximum stamina ([[Nourished]]) (+15% for 3 hours)
> *Positive [[Fatigue]] increase (15%)
> Pemmican Bars do not produce [[Scent]], and are the lightest food item in the game, as well as the most calorically dense food item. This makes then an excellent food for traveling and maximizing carry weight.
>

> == Trivia ==
> 
> * Real world Pemmican is quite famous for lasting a very long time (from 6 months to several years). It is often used in very poor survival situations, and is often typically associated with the diets of various Native American tribes or being used by American Voyager settlers.
> * Pemmican is typically composed of dried meat pounded into a coarse powder or mash, then mixed with an equal amount of melted fat and cooked. Occasionally, berries are added to the mix (Rose Hips are used in game).
