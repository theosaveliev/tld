**Pensive Pond** is a small recreational pond in the hills of [Pleasant Valley](./Pleasant%20Valley.md).

## Overview

Picnickers can enjoy canoeing and, when sunny, sit under the gazebo. A [hut](./Fishing%20Hut.md) with a [fishing hole](./Ice%20Fishing.md) and [pot-belly stove](./Pot%20Belly%20Stove.md) sits at the center of the pond. Importantly, the fishing hut also has a door, making it a decent temporary shelter. For players with sufficiently-warm [clothing](./Clothing.md) and a [bedroll](./Bedroll.md), the hut's relatively easy access to animals and firewood means it could serve as a base for multi-day [hunting](./Hunting.md) & fishing "retreats" to fight [cabin fever](./Cabin%20Fever.md).

Be careful of the surrounding cliffs and prowling predators in low-visibility conditions. There's a chance of the [revolver](./Revolver.md) spawning inside the fishing hut.

A [Cougar](./Cougar.md) spawns just east of here, which can pose a danger to players carrying large amounts of freshly-caught fish back to their main base. However, brave players may choose to try hunting the Cougar, using the fishing hut for protection.

## Location

Pensive Pond can be found easily from [Pleasant Valley Farmstead](./Pleasant%20Valley%20Farmstead.md). Standing at the round silo on the farmstead, turn to face [Heartbreak Bridge](./Heartbreak%20Bridge.md). Cliffs can be seen in the background, on top of which Pensive Pond can be found. Following [Lower Falls](./Lower%20Falls.md) and climbing up carefully, some shelter, fishing or a picnic can be had.
