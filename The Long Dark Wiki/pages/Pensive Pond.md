<td><p>Provides some shelter|} <strong>Pensive Pond</strong> is a small recreational pond in the hills of <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley">Pleasant Valley</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Picnickers can enjoy canoeing and, when sunny, sit under the gazebo. A <a href="./Fishing%20Hut.md" class="wikilink" title="hut">hut</a> with a <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="fishing hole">fishing hole</a> and <a href="./Pot%20Belly%20Stove.md" class="wikilink" title="pot-belly stove">pot-belly stove</a> sits at the center of the pond. Importantly, the fishing hut also has a door, making it a decent temporary shelter. For players with sufficiently-warm <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="clothing">clothing</a> and a <a href="./Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="bedroll">bedroll</a>, the hut's relatively easy access to animals and firewood means it could serve as a base for multi-day <a href="./Hunting.md" class="wikilink" title="hunting">hunting</a> &amp; fishing "retreats" to fight <a href="./Cabin%20Fever.md" class="wikilink" title="cabin fever">cabin fever</a>.</p>
<p>Be careful of the surrounding cliffs and prowling predators in low-visibility conditions. There's a chance of the <a href="./Revolver.md" class="wikilink" title="revolver">revolver</a> spawning inside the fishing hut.</p>
<p>A <a href="./Cougar.md" class="wikilink" title="Cougar">Cougar</a> spawns just east of here, which can pose a danger to players carrying large amounts of freshly-caught fish back to their main base. However, brave players may choose to try hunting the Cougar, using the fishing hut for protection.</p>
<h2 id="Location">Location</h2>
<p>Pensive Pond can be found easily from <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley%20Farmstead.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley Farmstead">Pleasant Valley Farmstead</a>. Standing at the round silo on the farmstead, turn to face <a href="./Heartbreak%20Bridge.md" class="wikilink" title="Heartbreak Bridge">Heartbreak Bridge</a>. Cliffs can be seen in the background, on top of which Pensive Pond can be found. Following <a href="./Lower%20Falls.md" class="wikilink" title="Lower Falls">Lower Falls</a> and climbing up carefully, some shelter, fishing or a picnic can be had.</p>
