> |map=Pensive Pond Location.png}}'''Pensive Pond''' is a small recreational pond in the hills of [[Pleasant Valley]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> Picnickers can enjoy canoeing and, when sunny, sit under the gazebo. A [[Fishing Hut|hut]] with a [[Ice Fishing|fishing hole]] and [[Pot Belly Stove|pot-belly stove]] sits at the center of the pond. Importantly, the fishing hut also has a door, making it a decent temporary shelter.  For players with sufficiently-warm [[clothing]] and a [[bedroll]], the hut's relatively easy access to animals and firewood means it could serve as a base for multi-day [[hunting]] & fishing "retreats" to fight [[Cabin Fever|cabin fever]].
> 
> Be careful of the surrounding cliffs and prowling predators in low-visibility conditions. There's a chance of the [[revolver]] spawning inside the fishing hut.
> 
> A [[Cougar]] spawns just east of here, which can pose a danger to players carrying large amounts of freshly-caught fish back to their main base. However, brave players may choose to try hunting the Cougar, using the fishing hut for protection.
> 
> == Location ==
> Pensive Pond can be found easily from [[Pleasant Valley Farmstead]]. Standing at the round silo on the farmstead, turn to face [[Heartbreak Bridge]]. Cliffs can be seen in the background, on top of which Pensive Pond can be found. Following [[Lower Falls]] and climbing up carefully, some shelter, fishing or a picnic can be had.
