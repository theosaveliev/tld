<p><strong>Pickles</strong> is a rare <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Food And Drink">Food And Drink</a> item in <em>The Long Dark</em>, added by the <em><a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a></em> expansion.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>This item is only found only when trading with the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a>. It has a chance to appear in any trade and does not require the player to raise Trust with the Trader.</p>
<p>Pickles cost the following to acquire from the Trader:</p>
<ul>
<li>4.00 kg Any Raw Fish Meat</li>
<li>2.00 L <img src="../assets/Water_%28Potable%29_icon.png" loading="lazy" width="29" height="29" alt="Water_(Potable)_icon.png" /> <a href="./Water%20%28Potable%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Water (Potable)">Water (Potable)</a></li>
</ul>
