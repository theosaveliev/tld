**Piece Of Paper** is a [Collectibles](./Collectibles.md) found in [Episode 5](./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story. It is found inside the [Fukkatsu Maru's](./Fukkatsu%20Maru.md) Cargo Hold during the main mission [A Cry For Help](./A%20Cry%20For%20Help.md). It is found in the cargo hold, on the ground below the first tied up crewmate.
