**Piece of Paper** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectibles">Collectibles</a> found in <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 5">Episode 5</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story. It is found inside the Train Maintenance Shed on the 2nd floor, in an office to the right when first entering from above in the mission <a href="./To%20SAR%20And%20Back.md" class="wikilink" title="To SAR And Back">To SAR And Back</a>.

## Transcript

<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">

The following text is transcribed from **Piece of Paper**

</div>

<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

Dear Mia,

I've been thinkin' we should see about finally letting "Goose" have a rest.

Figure we can park her right at the end of line, nearest P. Mills.

We could bring folks out, let the kids climb around the engine, "blow the whistle." Hehe. That kind of thing.

There's so little to bring anyone up this far north, much less from the Mainland.

Might stir up some trouble in town - no one likes a good idea these days. Much less the fellow who thought it up. But a bit of history might be just the trick.

What do ya think?

K.
