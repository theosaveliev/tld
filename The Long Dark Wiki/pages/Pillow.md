A **Pillow** is a small interactable object that can be harvested with a [Hunting Knife](./Hunting%20Knife.md) for 2 [Cloth](./Cloth.md) after 9 minutes. They are often found in many Trailers across the island alongside [Old Bedrolls](./Old%20Bedroll.md).

| Cloth | Time | Calories | Condition of Tool |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | 9 minutes | 27 | -2% Hunting Knife |
| 2 | 11 minutes | 34 | -3% Improvised Knife |
