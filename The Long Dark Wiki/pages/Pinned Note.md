## Transcript

<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">

The following text is transcribed from **Pinned Note**

</div>

<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

Dear community,

The current events have prevented us from getting our usual deliveries. We have decided to ration whatever supplies we have left until we have more clarity on a date for deliveries to resume as normal.

We'll be selling fresh produce while it lasts.

Please note that batteries are out of stock, and we're out of gas as well.

If you have any surplus of homemade canned fruits and veggies, dry fish and meat, preserved eggs, fresh game meat or fresh, or any properly frozen food you'd like to contribute to the Co-Op, please contact Josie or Camino.

The Co-Op team will put together food packages for members of our community most in need, and in particular families who have recently lost a loved one.

Thank you for your help, patience, and resilience.
