**Plane Crash Injuries** is a cut [Affliction](./Afflictions.md) encountered in the mission [Survival Of The Fittest](./Survival%20Of%20The%20Fittest.md) of [Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle](./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md) in the Predux [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) storyline. It is considered [Cut/Discontinued Content](./Cut%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20Content.md) but can still be experienced by using the [Time Capsule](./Time%20Capsule.md) to play versions V1.15, V1.30, or V1.37 of Steam versions of Wintermute.

## Overview

It was acquired immediately after waking up in the ravine after the plane crash, and would persist for 5 Days. It prevents [Will Mackenzie](./Will%20Mackenzie.md) from Sprinting, holding [Stones](./Stone.md), or from using the [Rock Climb](./Rock%20Climb.md) to leave the ravine on Day 4 (Causing him to fall and [Sprain his Ankle](./Sprained%20Ankle.md)). It finally heals on Day 5, and serves as a way to slow the player down in order to immerse them in the world as part of the tutorial.

In the Redux, Mackenzie instead wakes up with [Blood Loss](./Blood%20Loss.md), a Sprained Wrist, a Sprained Ankle, and 2 [Pain](./Pain.md) from the Sprains, with the cause listed as originating from Plane Crash Injuries.

## Trivia

- This Affliction uses the same icon as [Sprained Wrist](./Sprained%20Wrist.md).
- Plane Crash Injuries was retconned out of Episode 1 with the removal of the mission [Survival Of The Fittest](./Survival%20Of%20The%20Fittest.md). Mackenzie now only stays in the ravine for 2 days in the Redux Episode 1 mission [The Crash](./The%20Crash.md) (though Mackenzie will still fail to climb out on day 1 if you attempt to leave early).
  - A nod to this cut Affliction also exists in [Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md), as Astrid was passed out for 5 days (rather than Mackenzie) and wakes up with [Pain](./Pain.md), a [Sprained Wrist](./Sprained%20Wrist.md), and a [Sprained Ankle](./Sprained%20Ankle.md), with the cause being Plane Crash Injuries.
