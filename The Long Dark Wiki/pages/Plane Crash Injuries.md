**Plane Crash Injuries** is a cut <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> encountered in the mission <a href="./Survival%20Of%20The%20Fittest.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Of The Fittest">Survival Of The Fittest</a> of <a href="./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle">Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle</a> in the Predux <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> storyline. It is considered <a href="./Cut%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20Content.md" class="wikilink" title="Cut/Discontinued Content">Cut/Discontinued Content</a> but can still be experienced by using the <a href="./Time%20Capsule.md" class="wikilink" title="Time Capsule">Time Capsule</a> to play versions V1.15, V1.30, or V1.37 of Steam versions of Wintermute.

## Overview

It was acquired immediately after waking up in the ravine after the plane crash, and would persist for 5 Days. It prevents <a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will Mackenzie">Will Mackenzie</a> from Sprinting, holding <a href="./Stone.md" class="wikilink" title="Stones">Stones</a>, or from using the <a href="./Rock%20Climb.md" class="wikilink" title="Rock Climb">Rock Climb</a> to leave the ravine on Day 4 (Causing him to fall and <a href="./Sprained%20Ankle.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprain his Ankle">Sprain his Ankle</a>). It finally heals on Day 5, and serves as a way to slow the player down in order to immerse them in the world as part of the tutorial.

In the Redux, Mackenzie instead wakes up with <a href="./Blood%20Loss.md" class="wikilink" title="Blood Loss">Blood Loss</a>, a Sprained Wrist, a Sprained Ankle, and 2 <a href="./Pain.md" class="wikilink" title="Pain">Pain</a> from the Sprains, with the cause listed as originating from Plane Crash Injuries.

## Trivia

- This Affliction uses the same icon as <a href="./Sprained%20Wrist.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Wrist">Sprained Wrist</a>.
- Plane Crash Injuries was retconned out of Episode 1 with the removal of the mission <a href="./Survival%20Of%20The%20Fittest.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Of The Fittest">Survival Of The Fittest</a>. Mackenzie now only stays in the ravine for 2 days in the Redux Episode 1 mission <a href="./The%20Crash.md" class="wikilink" title="The Crash">The Crash</a> (though Mackenzie will still fail to climb out on day 1 if you attempt to leave early).
  - A nod to this cut Affliction also exists in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a>, as Astrid was passed out for 5 days (rather than Mackenzie) and wakes up with <a href="./Pain.md" class="wikilink" title="Pain">Pain</a>, a <a href="./Sprained%20Wrist.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Wrist">Sprained Wrist</a>, and a <a href="./Sprained%20Ankle.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Ankle">Sprained Ankle</a>, with the cause being Plane Crash Injuries.
