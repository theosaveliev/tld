> </gallery>|map=Platelayer's Shortcut Location.png}}'''Platelayer's Shortcut''' is a moderately complex system of indoor caves in the [[Far Range Branch Line]] area. It contains [[Coal]] and [[Stick|sticks]] as well as a few dead [[Deer]] and possibly human corpses. It is used to navigate the rockfall blockage located at [[Foundered Track]].
> 
> The lower cave entrance is found below [[Surfeit Trestle]]. The upper entrance is located behind the [[Foundered Track]] area near the main railroad.
