**Platelayer's Shortcut** is a moderately complex system of indoor caves in the [Far Range Branch Line](./Far%20Range%20Branch%20Line.md) area. It contains [Coal](./Coal.md) and [sticks](./Stick.md) as well as a few dead [Deer](./Deer.md) and possibly human corpses. It is used to navigate the rockfall blockage located at [Foundered Track](./Foundered%20Track.md).
