## Overview

The church is a small worship building, with many pews and [Books](./Book.md) inside. Other loot includes [Cloth (Object)](./Cloth%20%28Object%29.md), [Water (Potable)](./Water%20%28Potable%29.md), and the occasionally small food stuffs.

A [Mementos](./Mementos.md) can be found in the region leading to the piano. The [Loose Boards](./Loose%20Boards.md) under the piano has a chance of containing multiple [Emergency Stim](./Emergency%20Stim.md), along with other medical supplies.
