# Pleasant Valley Church

> {{Infobox/Location|image=Pleasant Valley Church.jpg|region=Pleasant Valley|location=At [[Thomson's Crossing]].|description=A local community church|type=Church|bed=No|shelter=yes}}The '''Pleasant Valley Church''' is a church in [[Thomson's Crossing]] in [[Pleasant Valley]].
> ==Overview==
> The church is a small worship building, with many pews and [[Book|Books]] inside. Other loot includes [[Cloth (Object)]], [[Water (Potable)]], and the occasionally small food stuffs. 
> 
> A [[Mementos]] can be found in the region leading to the piano. The [[Loose Boards]] under the piano has a chance of containing multiple [[Emergency Stim]], along with other medical supplies. 
> ==Wintermute==
> In Wintermute, the church is the start of the [[Church Artifact (mission)]], requiring Astrid to find and return the long stolen [[Church Artifact]].
