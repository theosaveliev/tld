<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>The church is a small worship building, with many pews and <a href="./Book.md" class="wikilink" title="Books">Books</a> inside. Other loot includes <a href="./Cloth%20%28Object%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Cloth (Object)">Cloth (Object)</a>, <a href="./Water%20%28Potable%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Water (Potable)">Water (Potable)</a>, and the occasionally small food stuffs.</p>
<p>A <a href="./Mementos.md" class="wikilink" title="Mementos">Mementos</a> can be found in the region leading to the piano. The <a href="./Loose%20Boards.md" class="wikilink" title="Loose Boards">Loose Boards</a> under the piano has a chance of containing multiple <a href="./Emergency%20Stim.md" class="wikilink" title="Emergency Stim">Emergency Stim</a>, along with other medical supplies.</p>
