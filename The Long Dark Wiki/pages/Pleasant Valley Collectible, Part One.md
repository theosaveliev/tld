**Pleasant Valley Collectible, Part One** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3">Episode 3</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode as a part of the <a href="./Forest%20Talkers%20%28side%20mission%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Forest Talkers">Forest Talkers</a> side mission.

During the course of the mission, <a href="./Astrid%20Greenwood.md" class="wikilink" title="Astrid Greenwood">Astrid Greenwood</a> finds the note *Pleasant Valley Collectible, Part One* from on the wall of the <a href="./Thomson%27s%20Crossing.md" class="wikilink" title="rural store at the crossroads">rural store at the crossroads</a>.
