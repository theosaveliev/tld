**Pleasant Valley Collectible, Part Three** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 3](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode as a part of the [Forest Talkers](./Forest%20Talkers%20%28side%20mission%29.md) side mission.

During the course of the mission, [Astrid Greenwood](./Astrid%20Greenwood.md) finds the note _Pleasant Valley Collectible, Part Three_ from in the control room for the radio tower on [Signal Hill](./Signal%20Hill.md) which you go to [later in the story](./Aftermath.md).
