> |map=Poacher's Camp Location.png}}'''Poacher's Camp''' is one of the few marked locations within [[Forlorn Muskeg]].
> 
> ==Overview==
> Poacher's Camp is nestled inside a railway car, situated partway down the track that bisects Forlorn Muskeg. The car is surrounded by broken trees and other railway cars, and the entire assemblage is surrounded by portions of weak ice and [[Cat Tail]].
> 
> Inside the car is a [[Fire Barrel]] and a few containers, which may hold useful items. The player may find [[Rifle Ammunition]], [[Revolver Ammunition]], [[Coal]], [[Scrap Metal|scrap metal]], [[antibiotics]], a [[Storm Lantern]], and a [[bedroll]].
> 
> The car proves useful in protecting against the wind and elements it gives a 2°C warmth bonus from being inside, and doubles as a good sleeping point due to its fire barrel; this location can be useful if the player wants to achieve [[Beneath a Starry Sky]]. However, due to frequent blizzards in Forlorn Muskeg, it's not recommended to use the place as a base for more than one or two nights.
> 
> Wolves and bears will not follow you inside, even if they are mid-charge.
> 
> It's a perfect place to hunt the [[bear]] nearby.
> 
> The location is not considered indoors. Saplings and hides will '''not''' cure if left inside the train car.
