**Pond** is a location in [Anna Island -- Track Section 4](./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%204.md).

## Overview

A small track of train track runs a frozen pond on the way to the western [Railyard (Track Section 4)](./Railyard%20%28Track%20Section%204%29.md) and a [Repair Depot](./Repair%20Depot.md). The lake is filled with [Cat Tail](./Cat%20Tail.md) plants and other natural plants and fungus, which may be worth stopping the train if you need to forage for supplies. To the east is the [Canyon](./Canyon.md) that eventually leads to [Anna Island -- Track Section 6](./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%206.md) or further east to the other Railyard in the region and later to [Anna Island -- Track Section 5](./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%205.md).
