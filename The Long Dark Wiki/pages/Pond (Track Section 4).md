<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>A small track of train track runs a frozen pond on the way to the western <a href="./Railyard%20%28Track%20Section%204%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Railyard (Track Section 4)">Railyard (Track Section 4)</a> and a <a href="./Repair%20Depot.md" class="wikilink" title="Repair Depot">Repair Depot</a>. The lake is filled with <a href="./Cat%20Tail.md" class="wikilink" title="Cat Tail">Cat Tail</a> plants and other natural plants and fungus, which may be worth stopping the train if you need to forage for supplies. To the east is the <a href="./Canyon.md" class="wikilink" title="Canyon">Canyon</a> that eventually leads to <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%206.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 6">Anna Island -- Track Section 6</a> or further east to the other Railyard in the region and later to <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%205.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 5">Anna Island -- Track Section 5</a>.</p>
