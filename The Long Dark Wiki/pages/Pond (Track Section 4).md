# Pond \(Track Section 4\)

> {{IronRoad}}{{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Pond (Track Section 4).jpg|imagecaption=|region=Anna Island -- Track Section 4|location=The middle of the region, closer to the western Railway|sublocations=|description=|type=|bed=|firebox=|forge=|workbench=|shelter=|missions=|characters=|map=}}'''Pond''' is a location in [[Anna Island -- Track Section 4]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> A small track of train track runs a frozen pond on the way to the western [[Railyard (Track Section 4)]] and a [[Repair Depot]]. The lake is filled with [[Cat Tail]] plants and other natural plants and fungus, which may be worth stopping the train if you need to forage for supplies. To the east is the [[Canyon]] that eventually leads to [[Anna Island -- Track Section 6]] or further east to the other Railyard in the region and later to [[Anna Island -- Track Section 5]].
