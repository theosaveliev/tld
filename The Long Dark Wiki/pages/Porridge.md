<p><strong>Porridge</strong> is a food item made with a <a href="./Cooking%20Pot.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Pot">Cooking Pot</a>. It can be made at Level 1 cooking and takes 20 minutes to prepare.</p>
<p>For Interloper players, Porridge can be one of the most useful foods, as it has a decently high caloric value and provides the <a href="./Warming%20Up.md" class="wikilink" title="Warming Up">Warming Up</a> bonus. Furthermore, <a href="./Running%20River%20Oats.md" class="wikilink" title="Running River Oats">Running River Oats</a> are relatively easy to obtain from kitchens.</p>
