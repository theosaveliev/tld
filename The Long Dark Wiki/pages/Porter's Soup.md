<p><strong>Porter's Soup</strong> is a <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Food And Drink">Food And Drink</a> item in <em><a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a></em>. It is learned after acquiring its unique <a href="./Recipe%20Cards.md" class="wikilink" title="Recipe Card">Recipe Card</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>It is only acquired when trading with the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li>+10% Maximum <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> (<a href="./Nourished.md" class="wikilink" title="Nourished">Nourished</a>) for 3 hours</li>
<li>Max Carry Weight (<a href="./Unburdened.md" class="wikilink" title="Unburdened">Unburdened</a>)</li>
<li>Negative <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a> increase (-15%)</li>
</ul>
