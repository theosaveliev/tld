The **Pot Belly Stove** is an immovable <a href="./Fire.md#Contained_fires" class="wikilink" title="contained fire">contained fire</a>. The pot belly stove functions identically to other contained fires and player-made <a href="./Campfire.md" class="wikilink" title="Campfire">Campfire</a>. The pot belly stove provides no advantage over other contained fires, but it does allow protection against wind, unlike the player-made campfire. It has one <a href="./Cooking.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking">Cooking</a> slot.

The pot belly stove can be re-lit indefinitely, though it requires fresh starter, tinder, and fuel each time.

## Locations

Pot belly stoves have fixed locations that do not change between games. A pot belly stove is located in every <a href="./Fishing%20Hut.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Hut">Fishing Hut</a> as well as in some other structures.
