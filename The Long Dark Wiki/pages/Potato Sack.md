A **Potato Sack** is an interactable storage container in *The Long Dark*.

## Overview

They are often found in many homes and typically yield 5 <a href="./Potato.md" class="wikilink" title="Potatoes">Potatoes</a> when searched. The brand on the sack says Perfect Potatoes, and are 10 lbs sacks meant for cold storage.
