# Precarious Causeway

> {{Infobox/Region|connections=[[Desolation Point]]|difficulty=Beginner}}{{Mods}}
> {{Quote2|A transition region that bridges the gap between 'Lower Great Bear' and 'Eastern Great Bear'. This causeway saw plenty of use before the collapse and the vehicles left behind make that apparent.|TLDEV Region Description}}
> 
> '''Precarious Causeway''' is the 1st [[The Long Development|Modded Region]] added to the game, as part of The Long Development's unofficial region mods. The region is accessed from [[Desolation Point]] via the now mostly fixed [[Broken Bridge (Desolation Point location)|Broken Bridge]]. It connects to both [[Rocky Thoroughfare]] to the east and [[Shattered Marsh]] to the west (via the [[Cave (Transition Zone)|Transition Zone Cave]] [[Luminous Cavern]]).
