**Prepared Acorns (Large Portion)** are a material in The Long Dark.

## Overview

Prepared Acorns (Large Portion) can be created after shelling <a href="./Acorns.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorns">Acorns</a> into <a href="./Prepared%20Acorns.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepared Acorns">Prepared Acorns</a> and then grouping 4 of them into a Large Portion via the Crafting menu. The Large Portion can then be cooked into <a href="./Acorns%20%28Cooked%20-%20Large%20Portion%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorns (Cooked - Large Portion)">Acorns (Cooked - Large Portion)</a>, saving fuel and cooking times in the process compared to individually cooking 4 Prepared Acorns.

## Trivia

- Curiously, 1 Prepared Acorns (Large Portion) weighs 0.05 kg more than 4 Prepared Acorns, despite 4 Prepared Acorns only weighing 0.20 kg.
- 1 Prepared Acorns (Large Portion) can be found at the top of the elevator shaft in the <a href="./Headframe%20Building.md" class="wikilink" title="Headframe Building">Headframe Building</a>, on a <a href="./Barrel.md" class="wikilink" title="Barrel">Barrel</a>.
