## Overview

**Prepared Acorns** are the shelled version of regular <a href="./Acorns.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorns">Acorns</a>. They can be cooked into <a href="./Acorns%20%28Cooked%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorns (Cooked)">Acorns (Cooked)</a>, which can then be either eaten or turned into <a href="./Acorn%20Grounds.md" class="wikilink" title="Acorn Grounds">Acorn Grounds</a> (after cooking).

Preparing Acorns can be a time and resource-intensive process, taking 10 minutes to shell each batch (removing the cap and its hard shell, based on the graphical representation). It costs 0.25 liters of <a href="./Water%20%28Potable%29.md" class="wikilink" title="water">water</a> per acorn you wish to cook, or 0.5 liters to cook 4 at once, which is the benefit of making the "large portion" variants.
