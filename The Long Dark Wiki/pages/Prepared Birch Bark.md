**Prepared Birch Bark** is a natural medical and food item in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Overview

Prepared Birch Bark is an ingredient for cooking <a href="./Birch%20Bark%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Birch Bark Tea">Birch Bark Tea</a>, a food and medical item and is crafted from 2 <a href="./Birch%20Bark.md" class="wikilink" title="Birch Bark">Birch Bark</a>. Crafting takes 5 minutes and doesn't require a tool or light.

To cook Birch Bark Tea, one Prepared Birch Bark and 0.25 liters of <a href="./Water%20%28Potable%29.md" class="wikilink" title="potable water">potable water</a> must be cooked for 10 minutes at a lit <a href="./Fire.md" class="wikilink" title="Fire">Fire</a> with a <a href="./Cooking%20Pot.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Pot">Cooking Pot</a> or <a href="./Recycled%20Can.md" class="wikilink" title="Recycled Can">Recycled Can</a>. Consuming Birch Bark Tea restores 100 Calories, 30% Hydration and grants the benefit "<a href="./Restore%20Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Restore Condition">Restore Condition</a>" and potentially "<a href="./Warming%20Up.md" class="wikilink" title="Warming Up">Warming Up</a>" if the beverage is also hot. Cooking Birch Bark Tea increases <a href="./Skills.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Skill">Cooking Skill</a>.

Birch Bark can can be found in the wilderness near lone birch trees or abundantly at birch forests, typically in single pieces after <a href="./Weather.md" class="wikilink" title="High Winds or Blizzards">High Winds or Blizzards</a>. Raw Birch Bark cannot be consumed directly and must first be crafted into Birch Bark Tea.

The Benefit "Restore Condition" heals 5% Condition (2.5% per hour, for 2 hours), regardless if the player is awake/asleep, or if <a href="./Needs.md" class="wikilink" title="Needs">Needs</a> are not met. The Benefit does not stack however, and drinking multiple cups of Birch Bark Tea within 5 hours of one another is a waste of resources.

Prepared Birch Bark (0.05kg) has half the weight of it's materials (2 Birch Bark, 0.10kg), Neither Birch Bark or Birch Bark Tea <a href="./Decay.md" class="wikilink" title="decays">decays</a>.

## Tips

- Birch Forests are present near the <a href="./Lonely%20Homestead.md" class="wikilink" title="Lonely Homestead">Lonely Homestead</a> (<a href="./Pleasant%20Valley.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley">Pleasant Valley</a>), western <a href="./The%20Ravine.md" class="wikilink" title="The Ravine">The Ravine</a>, and north-western <a href="./Bleak%20Inlet.md" class="wikilink" title="Bleak Inlet">Bleak Inlet</a>. Lone birch trees can be found sporadically in all regions.
- Birch Bark respawns after <a href="./Weather.md" class="wikilink" title="High Winds or Blizzards">High Winds or Blizzards</a>.
- Birch Bark may be found via <a href="./Beachcombing.md" class="wikilink" title="Beachcombing">Beachcombing</a>.
- Birch Bark, Prepared Birch Bark and Birch Bark Tea never <a href="./Decay.md" class="wikilink" title="decay">decay</a>.

## Trivia

- It is possible to survive exclusively on Birch Bark Tea, especially on easier difficulties or by using the starvation strategy (only eating just before sleep).
- Birch Bark Tea is disabled during the <a href="./Challenge.md" class="wikilink" title="Challenge">Challenge</a>: <a href="./As%20the%20Dead%20Sleep.md" class="wikilink" title="As the Dead Sleep">As the Dead Sleep</a>, but Birch Bark may still be collected and used as Tinder.
- <a href="./Cup%20of%20Coffee.md" class="wikilink" title="Cup of Coffee">Cup of Coffee</a> is the only item which can restore all 4 <a href="./Needs.md" class="wikilink" title="Needs">Needs</a> (if consumed while hot), but Birch Bark Tea functions similarly with its Benefit "Restore Condition", since this heals Condition (2.5% hourly) faster than Fatigue damages it (1% hourly), so it is possible to survive indefinitely without Sleep by drinking 1 cup of Birch Bark Tea every 5 hours (albeit at 0% Fatigue, moving slowly and unable to climb/sprint).
