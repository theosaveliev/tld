**Prepared Burdock** is the prepared version of a <a href="./Burdock%20Root.md" class="wikilink" title="Burdock Root">Burdock Root</a> that is ready to be cooked or eaten raw as is. It was introduced in the free update portion of the Part 3: Frontier Comforts update for the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> expansion.
