> ==Overview==
> '''Prepper's Pie''' is a unique recipe with a powerful Condition-restoring effect similar to [[Dockworker's Pie]]. It requires a combination of natural and artificial ingredients, and is learned from a recipe card that spawns randomly in one of nine [[Prepper Cache]] bunkers. While it is marginally less calorie-efficient than its ingredients (total of 1200 kcal vs the pie's 1125 kcal), it has powerful effects--namely a large amount of [[Condition]] restoration. This allows it to form a similar function to the rare [[Emergency Stim]]. 
> 
> Although Prepper's Pie has a base caloric value of 900, it requires level 5 [[Cooking]] to craft, so its calorie count will always be 25% greater (1125 kcal).
> 
> Most of the ingredients for Prepper's Pie are relatively common. However, Burdock Root is somewhat rare (1-4 per [[Region]]), and it often appears in remote or hidden areas. 
> 
> '''Note''': [[Prepared Burdock]] and [[Prepared Reishi Mushrooms]] cannot be used in Prepper's Pie, instead requiring their raw forms.
> 
> ==Effects==
> * +15% [[Stamina]] (instant)
> * +15% Maximum Stamina (for 3 hours)
> * +25% [[Condition]] (immediate healing effect) 
> * [[Cabin Fever]] outside time increase (minimal)
> * [[Burdened]] -3kg Carry Weight (for 1 hour)
