<p><strong>Prepper's Pie</strong> is a food item in <em>The Long Dark</em>, it is learned from a unique <a href="./Recipe%20Cards.md" class="wikilink" title="Recipe Card">Recipe Card</a> and is made with a <a href="./Cooking%20Skillet.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Skillet">Cooking Skillet</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p><strong>Prepper's Pie</strong> is a unique recipe with a powerful Condition-restoring effect similar to <a href="./Dockworker%27s%20Pie.md" class="wikilink" title="Dockworker&#39;s Pie">Dockworker's Pie</a>. It requires a combination of natural and artificial ingredients, and is learned from a recipe card that spawns randomly in one of nine <a href="./Prepper%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper Cache">Prepper Cache</a> bunkers. While it is marginally less calorie-efficient than its ingredients (total of 1200 kcal vs the pie's 1125 kcal), it has powerful effects--namely a large amount of <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> restoration. This allows it to form a similar function to the rare <a href="./Emergency%20Stim.md" class="wikilink" title="Emergency Stim">Emergency Stim</a>.</p>
<p>Although Prepper's Pie has a base caloric value of 900, it requires level 5 <a href="./Cooking.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking">Cooking</a> to craft, so its calorie count will always be 25% greater (1125 kcal).</p>
<p>Most of the ingredients for Prepper's Pie are relatively common. However, Burdock Root is somewhat rare (1-4 per <a href="./Region.md" class="wikilink" title="Region">Region</a>), and it often appears in remote or hidden areas.</p>
<p><strong>Note</strong>: <a href="./Prepared%20Burdock.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepared Burdock">Prepared Burdock</a> and <a href="./Prepared%20Reishi%20Mushrooms.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepared Reishi Mushrooms">Prepared Reishi Mushrooms</a> cannot be used in Prepper's Pie, instead requiring their raw forms.</p>
<h2 id="Effects">Effects</h2>
<ul>
<li>+15% <a href="./Stamina.md" class="wikilink" title="Stamina">Stamina</a> (instant)</li>
<li>+15% Maximum Stamina (for 3 hours)</li>
<li>+25% <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> (immediate healing effect)</li>
<li><a href="./Cabin%20Fever.md" class="wikilink" title="Cabin Fever">Cabin Fever</a> outside time increase (minimal)</li>
<li><a href="./Burdened.md" class="wikilink" title="Burdened">Burdened</a> -3kg Carry Weight (for 1 hour)</li>
</ul>

<h3 id="Location">Location</h3>
<p>The recipe can be found inside a "Stockpile" <a href="./Prepper%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper Cache">Prepper Cache</a>, the one with a kitchen counter inside. It is also possible to find the recipe inside a <a href="./Cardboard%20Box.md" class="wikilink" title="Cardboard Box">Cardboard Box</a> inside an Abandoned Prepper Cache on <a href="./Interloper.md" class="wikilink" title="Interloper">Interloper</a>/<a href="./Misery.md" class="wikilink" title="Misery">Misery</a>, as all Caches are Abandoned by default on these modes.</p>
