## Trivia

- Originally, only 12 inmates were on the list. After Episode 1's Redux launch however, Hobbs was added to the list resulting in 13 prisoners. After Episode 4 was released, the list was further amended to have Vachon and Heller stealthily added to the list, making the bus list consist of 15 prisoners.
- LECLERC, Y. is mentioned by <a href="./Vachon.md" class="wikilink" title="Vachon">Vachon</a> at the start of <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence</a>, killed with an <a href="./Molly.md" class="wikilink" title="arrow through the chest">arrow through the chest</a>.
