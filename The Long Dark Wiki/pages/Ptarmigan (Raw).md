## Overview

Ptarmigan (Raw) is harvested from a [Ptarmigan Carcass](./Ptarmigan%20Carcass.md), which contain between 0.8-1.5 Kg of raw meat. Consuming raw meat carries a high chance of contracting [Food Poisoning](./Food%20Poisoning.md). Many Recipes use raw Ptarmigan meat as an ingredient.

- At 0% condition food disappears if inside of a container, but not outside of containers or in the player's inventory.
- Meat decays slower outdoors than indoors. It decays even slower when cooked.
