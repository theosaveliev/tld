> '''Ptarmigan (Raw)''' is a raw [[Food And Drink|food]] item in ''The Long Dark''.
> 
> ==Overview==
> Ptarmigan (Raw) is harvested from a [[Ptarmigan Carcass]], which contain between 0.8-1.5 Kg of raw meat. Consuming raw meat carries a high chance of contracting [[Food Poisoning]]. Many Recipes use raw Ptarmigan meat as an ingredient.
> 
> * At 0% condition food disappears if inside of a container, but not outside of containers or in the player's inventory.
> * Meat decays slower outdoors than indoors. It decays even slower when cooked.
