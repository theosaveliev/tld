<p><strong>Ptarmigan (Raw)</strong> is a raw <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in <em>The Long Dark</em>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Ptarmigan (Raw) is harvested from a <a href="./Ptarmigan%20Carcass.md" class="wikilink" title="Ptarmigan Carcass">Ptarmigan Carcass</a>, which contain between 0.8-1.5 Kg of raw meat. Consuming raw meat carries a high chance of contracting <a href="./Food%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Food Poisoning">Food Poisoning</a>. Many Recipes use raw Ptarmigan meat as an ingredient.</p>
<ul>
<li>At 0% condition food disappears if inside of a container, but not outside of containers or in the player's inventory.</li>
<li>Meat decays slower outdoors than indoors. It decays even slower when cooked.</li>
</ul>
