<td><p>|}<strong>Ptarmigan Down</strong> is a Material resource that can be harvested from the bodies of slain <a href="./Ptarmigan.md" class="wikilink" title="Ptarmigans">Ptarmigans</a>. It was released in the paid portion of the Part 3: Frontier Comforts update of the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> expansion.</p>
<p>Its primary use is for crafting the clothing accessory <a href="./Improvised%20Insulation.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Insulation">Improvised Insulation</a>, and for improving an ordinary <a href="./Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Bedroll">Bedroll</a> into the warmer <a href="./Improvised%20Down%20Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Down Bedroll">Improvised Down Bedroll</a>. Down bundles are also used to repair both previously mentioned items.</p>
