> |renewable=Yes}}{{TFTFT}}'''Ptarmigan Down''' is a Material resource that can be harvested from the bodies of slain [[Ptarmigan|Ptarmigans]]. It was released in the paid portion of the Part 3: Frontier Comforts update of the [[Tales from the Far Territory]] expansion.
> 
> Its primary use is for crafting the clothing accessory [[Improvised Insulation]], and for improving an ordinary [[Bedroll]] into the warmer [[Improvised Down Bedroll]]. Down bundles are also used to repair both previously mentioned items.
