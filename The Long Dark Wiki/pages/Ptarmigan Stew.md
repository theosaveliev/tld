> |renewable=Yes ([[Trader]])}}'''Ptarmigan Stew''' is a food item in ''The Long Dark'' and is made with a [[Cooking Pot]].
> ==Overview==
> Like other stews, it increases [[Fatigue|Fatigue,]] however also bestows [[Improved Rest]] for 3 hours. This makes it quite useful for restoring condition by allowing for a longer period of sleep. It is also calorie-efficient for the ingredients required, effectively doubling their calorie value. Regardless, this bonus is offset by a fairly long cooking time.
> ==Effects==
> *3 hours of Improved Rest (+1% [[Condition]] restored per hour of sleep, or after 18 hours)
> *-15% [[Fatigue]] (instantly)
