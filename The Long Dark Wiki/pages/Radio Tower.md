> |map=Radio Tower Location.png}}The '''Radio Tower''' is a radio tower and location overlooking [[Milton]] in the [[Mountain Town]] region.
> 
> ==Overview==
> It can be reached by heading up the slope nearby [[Grey Mother]]'s wood shed at the back of [[Milton House (Grey Mother's)]]. When the player finds the [[Polaroids|polaroid]] of this place, it serves as a Vista location in which using a piece of charcoal will reveal a large part of the map. A [[Transmitters|Transmitter]] can be found near the tower. 
> 
> At the base of the tower is a lootable supply crate, and potentially a [[Frozen Corpse]].
> 
> There is a small [[Cave (Mountain Town location)|cave]] nearby containing a glowing blue rock - the location of a side-mission supply cache in [[Milton Supply Caches]]. The cave can include a [[Research Book]] found on the ground inside. 
> 
> ==Trivia==
> *The altitude of the Radio Tower has been estimated as being 510 meters above sea level.
