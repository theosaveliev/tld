The **Radio Tower** is a radio tower and location overlooking [Milton](./Milton.md) in the [Mountain Town](./Mountain%20Town.md) region.

## Overview

It can be reached by heading up the slope nearby [Grey Mother](./Grey%20Mother.md)'s wood shed at the back of [Milton House (Grey Mother's)](./Milton%20House%20%28Grey%20Mother%27s%29.md). When the player finds the [polaroid](./Polaroids.md) of this place, it serves as a Vista location in which using a piece of charcoal will reveal a large part of the map. A [Transmitter](./Transmitters.md) can be found near the tower.

At the base of the tower is a lootable supply crate, and potentially a [Frozen Corpse](./Frozen%20Corpse.md).

There is a small [cave](./Cave%20%28Mountain%20Town%20location%29.md) nearby containing a glowing blue rock - the location of a side-mission supply cache in [Milton Supply Caches](./Milton%20Supply%20Caches.md). The cave can include a [Research Book](./Research%20Book.md) found on the ground inside.

## Trivia

- The altitude of the Radio Tower has been estimated as being 510 meters above sea level.
