# Railyard \(Track Section 4\)

> {{IronRoad}}{{Infobox/Location|title=|image=<gallery>
> Railyard (Track Section 4 East).jpg|Eastern Railway
> Railyard (Track Section 4 West).jpg|Western Railway
> </gallery>|imagecaption=|region=Anna Island -- Track Section 4|location=2 railway depots to the east and west of the map|sublocations=|description=|type=|bed=|firebox=|forge=|workbench=|shelter=yes|missions=|characters=|map=}}'''Railyard''' is a set of 2 locations in [[Anna Island -- Track Section 4]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> 
> === Railyard East ===
> A mostly empty set of land with some tracks that lead into a smashed in tunnel leading to [[Anna Island -- Track Section 6]]. Further east leads to [[Anna Island -- Track Section 5]].
> 
> === Railyard West ===
> To the west of the map lies the first [[Repair Depot|Repair Depots]] the player will find after acquiring the [[Prototype Train]]. This Railyard has a [[Water Tower]] and [[Oil Tank]] for refueling the train.
> 
> Heading further West will take the player to [[Anna Island -- Track Section 3]]. An Aurora gate and a bridge block access to the next island over, and must be opened during the [[Aurora]].{{Anna Island -- Track Section 4}}
