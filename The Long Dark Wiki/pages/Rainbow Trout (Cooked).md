**Rainbow Trout (Cooked)** is a [food](./Food%20And%20Drink.md) item in *The Long Dark*.

## Overview

Rainbow Trout are small saltwater fish found commonly under the ice off the coast. They can be caught by [Ice Fishing](./Ice%20Fishing.md) using [Fishing Tackle](./Fishing%20Tackle.md). Rainbow Trout can be between 0.5 and 2.3 kg raw.

At 0% condition food disappears if inside of a container, but not outside of containers or in the player's inventory.

Cooking trout takes 10 minutes and is obtained by using a lit [Fire](./Fire.md). Cooking increases it's condition by 50%, decays 4 times slower than raw meat, has a reduced chance of [Food Poisoning](./Afflictions.md) over raw meat, and decreases its weigh by 35%. Cooked trout's energy density is 385 calories-per-kilogram. Cooking fish also yields a small amount of [Lantern Fuel](./Lantern%20Fuel.md).

Cooking trout improves the [Cooking Skill](./Skills.md#Cooking). Using a [Cooking Pot](./Cooking%20Pot.md) reduces cooking time by 25%. Cooked food on an active fire for too long will burn (twice its 'Ready' cooking time), destroying the item and damaging a Cooking Pot if used.
