# Raven Crossing

> {{Infobox/Location|image = SS-BI-RavenCrossing01.jpg
> |description = a short highway bridge |region = Bleak Inlet|type = outdoors |map=Raven Crossing Location.png}}
> '''Raven Crossing''' is a short bridge, presumably crossing the Raven Creek, located a short walk west of the [[Landslide (Bleak Inlet)|Landslide]], and roughly halfway between the [[Cabin]] and the [[Cannery Worker Residences]]. 
> 
> A [[Frozen Corpse|frozen corpse]] and [[hatchet]] may spawn nearby, along with a couple of containers. [[Rabbit|Rabbits]] may also be found in the surrounding area. A [[Backpack]] is found on the side of the bridge closest to the Landslide.
