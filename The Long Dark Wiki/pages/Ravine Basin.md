The **Ravine Basin** is a location found in [The Ravine](./The%20Ravine.md) transition zone. The location is found in the center of the region, and can only be reached via a [Rope Climbing](./Rope%20Climbing.md), which always requires a [Mountaineering Rope](./Mountaineering%20Rope.md) (often found in one of the region's caves). The Basin is one way to reach [Bleak Inlet](./Bleak%20Inlet.md), and the only way to reach Bleak Inlet's upper mountain area.

## Overview

The basin is only reachable by climbing down the Rope Climbing section. At the bottom of the Rope Climb is a crashed [Train Shuttle](./Train%20Shuttle.md), often containing a [Research Book](./Research%20Book.md), a [Distress Pistol](./Distress%20Pistol.md) box with [Flare Shells](./Flare%20Shell.md), and other supplies.

Further down stream, a [Warm Cave](./Warm%20Cave.md) is found near a small frozen pond that contains a [Tuffet](./Bed.md) bed, also a tree limb inside. A ravaged [Deer Carcass](./Deer%20Carcass.md) is always found near the cave entrance. A [Rabbit](./Rabbit.md) grove and several [Cat Tail](./Cat%20Tail.md) plants are located around the cave. [Sticks](./Stick.md) and [Cedar Limbs](./Cedar%20Limb.md) can be used for fuel.
