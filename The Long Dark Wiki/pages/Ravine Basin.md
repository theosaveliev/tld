<td><p>Has no shelter|} The <strong>Ravine Basin</strong> is a location found in <a href="./The%20Ravine.md" class="wikilink" title="The Ravine">The Ravine</a> transition zone. The location is found in the center of the region, and can only be reached via a <a href="./Rope%20Climbing.md" class="wikilink" title="Rope Climbing">Rope Climbing</a>, which always requires a <a href="./Mountaineering%20Rope.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountaineering Rope">Mountaineering Rope</a> (often found in one of the region's caves). The Basin is one way to reach <a href="./Bleak%20Inlet.md" class="wikilink" title="Bleak Inlet">Bleak Inlet</a>, and the only way to reach Bleak Inlet's upper mountain area.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>The basin is only reachable by climbing down the Rope Climbing section. At the bottom of the Rope Climb is a crashed <a href="./Train%20Shuttle.md" class="wikilink" title="Train Shuttle">Train Shuttle</a>, often containing a <a href="./Research%20Book.md" class="wikilink" title="Research Book">Research Book</a>, a <a href="./Distress%20Pistol.md" class="wikilink" title="Distress Pistol">Distress Pistol</a> box with <a href="./Flare%20Shell.md" class="wikilink" title="Flare Shells">Flare Shells</a>, and other supplies.</p>
<p>Further down stream, a <a href="./Warm%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Warm Cave">Warm Cave</a> is found near a small frozen pond that contains a <a href="./Bed.md" class="wikilink" title="Tuffet">Tuffet</a> bed, also a tree limb inside. A ravaged <a href="./Deer%20Carcass.md" class="wikilink" title="Deer Carcass">Deer Carcass</a> is always found near the cave entrance. A <a href="./Rabbit.md" class="wikilink" title="Rabbit">Rabbit</a> grove and several <a href="./Cat%20Tail.md" class="wikilink" title="Cat Tail">Cat Tail</a> plants are located around the cave. <a href="./Stick.md" class="wikilink" title="Sticks">Sticks</a> and <a href="./Cedar%20Limb.md" class="wikilink" title="Cedar Limbs">Cedar Limbs</a> can be used for fuel.</p>
<h2 id="Kill_Barriers">Kill Barriers</h2>
<p><strong>CAUTION!</strong> Great care must be taken to never climb down the slopes of the basin walls to reach the bottom (<a href="./Mountain%20Goating.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountain Goating">Mountain Goating</a>), as the area is filled with invisible kill barriers leftover from a time when the basin never had a transition to Bleak Inlet, in order to keep players from being stuck and slowly dying inside the basin. This should be considered an <a href="./Environmental%20Hazard.md" class="wikilink" title="Environmental Hazard">Environmental Hazard</a>.
