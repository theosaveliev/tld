# Ravine's End Waterfall

> {{Infobox/Location|image = SS-BI-Ravine'sEndWaterfall.jpg
> |region = Bleak Inlet|type = outdoors |map=Ravine's End Waterfall Location.png}}
> 
> The '''Ravine's End Waterfall''' is one of two named waterfalls within [[Bleak Inlet]], the other being [[Lower Raven Falls]]. It consists of two sets of double waterfalls, and is located a short walk from the cave connecting Bleak Inlet to [[Forlorn Muskeg]]. [[Bunker Alpha]] is not far from the falls.
> 
> == Overview ==
> [[Cat Tail|Cat tails]] are common on the ice in front of the falls. [[Rose Hip]], mushrooms, [[Green Birch Sapling]]s and [[Green Maple Sapling]]s may all be found in the nearby area.
> 
> Surveyingthis location (e.g. for the [[Faithful Cartographer]] achievement) cannot be accomplished from the mountain where [[Pensive Lookout]] and [[Echo One Radio Tower]] are, but rather lower western Bleak Inlet, following the river from Frozen Delta until it reaches the base of the mountain.
