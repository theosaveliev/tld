## Overview

[Cat tails](./Cat%20Tail.md) are common on the ice in front of the falls. [Rose Hip](./Rose%20Hip.md), mushrooms, [Green Birch Saplings](./Green%20Birch%20Sapling.md) and [Green Maple Saplings](./Green%20Maple%20Sapling.md) may all be found in the nearby area.

Surveyingthis location (e.g. for the [Faithful Cartographer](./Faithful%20Cartographer.md) achievement) cannot be accomplished from the mountain where [Pensive Lookout](./Pensive%20Lookout.md) and [Echo One Radio Tower](./Echo%20One%20Radio%20Tower.md) are, but rather lower western Bleak Inlet, following the river from Frozen Delta until it reaches the base of the mountain.
