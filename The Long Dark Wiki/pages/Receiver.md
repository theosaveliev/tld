A **Receiver** is a stationary object across various buildings in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a> and <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence</a> in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>. They occasionally ring for the player, allowing them to receive a phone call as a type of cutscene. They do not appear in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a>.

## Episode 3

<a href="./Molly.md" class="wikilink" title="Molly">Molly</a> frequently calls <a href="./Astrid%20Greenwood.md" class="wikilink" title="Astrid Greenwood">Astrid Greenwood</a> throughout the episode, with Astrid eventually asking how Molly knows where to call to. Molly reveals that all phone lines in Pleasant Valley are on a party line, and that she merely needs to wait for Astrid to pick up the phone.

Molly calls Astrid several times via phone Receivers in:

- <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley%20Farmstead.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley Farmstead">Pleasant Valley Farmstead</a>
- <a href="./Barn%20%28Molly%27s%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Barn (Molly&#39;s)">Barn (Molly's)</a>
- <a href="./Community%20Hall.md" class="wikilink" title="Community Hall">Community Hall</a>
- A burned down house near the <a href="./Crash%20Site%20%28Pleasant%20Valley%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)">Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)</a>
- <a href="./Signal%20Hill.md" class="wikilink" title="Signal Hill">Signal Hill</a>

## Episode 4

<a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will Mackenzie">Will Mackenzie</a> is occasionally called by <a href="./Jace.md" class="wikilink" title="Jace">Jace</a> over the phone as he performs tasks for <a href="./Mathis.md" class="wikilink" title="Mathis">Mathis</a> and his gang of <a href="./Convicts.md" class="wikilink" title="Convicts">Convicts</a>, with Jace feeding Mackenzie information on how to sabotage Mathis' plans. The phones in Blackrock appear to be on a party line much like the ones in Pleasant Valley. Several Receivers can be found in:

- The Infirmary ward of <a href="./Blackrock%20Prison.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Prison">Blackrock Prison</a>
- <a href="./Power%20Plant.md" class="wikilink" title="Power Plant">Power Plant</a>
- <a href="./Last%20Prospect.md" class="wikilink" title="Last Prospect">Last Prospect</a>
- The Warden's Office at Blackrock Prison
