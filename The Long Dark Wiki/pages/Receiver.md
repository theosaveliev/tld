# Receiver

**Categories:** Interactable Objects · **Source:** [Fandom](https://thelongdark.fandom.com/wiki/Receiver)

**Receiver**

[Receiver](../assets/Receiver.jpg)

**Release:** 1.60

**Story item:** Yes

A **Receiver** is a stationary object across various buildings in [Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) and [Episode 4: Fury, Then Silence](./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md) in [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md). They occasionally ring for the player, allowing them to receive a phone call as a type of cutscene. They do not appear in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md).

## Episode 3

[Molly](./Molly.md) frequently calls [Astrid Greenwood](./Astrid%20Greenwood.md) throughout the episode, with Astrid eventually asking how Molly knows where to call to. Molly reveals that all phone lines in Pleasant Valley are on a party line, and that she merely needs to wait for Astrid to pick up the phone.

Molly calls Astrid several times via phone Receivers in:

- [Pleasant Valley Farmstead](./Pleasant%20Valley%20Farmstead.md)
- [Barn (Molly's)](./Barn%20%28Molly%27s%29.md)
- [Community Hall](./Community%20Hall.md)
- A burned down house near the [Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)](./Crash%20Site%20%28Pleasant%20Valley%29.md)
- [Signal Hill](./Signal%20Hill.md)

## Episode 4

[Will Mackenzie](./Will%20Mackenzie.md) is occasionally called by [Jace](./Jace.md) over the phone as he performs tasks for [Mathis](./Mathis.md) and his gang of [Convicts](./Convicts.md), with Jace feeding Mackenzie information on how to sabotage Mathis' plans. The phones in Blackrock appear to be on a party line much like the ones in Pleasant Valley. Several Receivers can be found in:

- The Infirmary ward of [Blackrock Prison](./Blackrock%20Prison.md)
- [Power Plant](./Power%20Plant.md)
- [Last Prospect](./Last%20Prospect.md)
- The Warden's Office at Blackrock Prison
