**Reclaimed Wood** is a fuel and crafting material in *The Long Dark*.

## Overview

Reclaimed Wood is a relatively common resource harvested from furniture, or found near settlements, especially destroyed buildings. Reclaimed Wood may be used as fuel for <a href="./Campfire.md" class="wikilink" title="campfires">campfires</a>, and crafting <a href="./Snare.md" class="wikilink" title="snare">snares</a>. Using Reclaimed Wood to start a campfire decreases the probability of it lighting by 5%.

Reclaimed wood has a burning duration of 30 minutes.

## Tips

- Reclaimed wood can be found inside and near buildings, fishing huts, or by harvesting crates, shelves, tables, chairs and etc. with a <a href="./Heavy%20Hammer.md" class="wikilink" title="Heavy Hammer">Heavy Hammer</a>, <a href="./Improvised%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Knife">Improvised Knife</a>, or <a href="./Improvised%20Hatchet.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Hatchet">Improvised Hatchet</a>. Small wooden items may be broken down with bare hands, expending more calories and taking more time however.
- Reclaimed Wood and <a href="./Cedar%20Firewood.md" class="wikilink" title="Cedar Firewood">Cedar Firewood</a> are efficient sources of fuel per weight, at 120 min/kg. Since pieces of Reclaimed Wood are smaller than Cedar Firewood, they give more control of a fire's duration, allowing more efficient fuel without waste.
- A single piece of Reclaimed Wood will heat a canned food or drink without it burning, as the fire will extinguish 10-20 minutes after the food or drink is done.
