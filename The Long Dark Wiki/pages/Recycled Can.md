A **Recycled Can** is a tool that players used primarily for melting snow, boiling water, and cooking <a href="./Cooking.md#Canned" class="wikilink" title="canned goods">canned goods</a> and <a href="./Cooking.md#Drinks" class="wikilink" title="drinks">drinks</a>.

## Overview

The player will always start the game with one recycled can in the inventory, even on Interloper. Additional Recycled Cans are obtained by the player after consuming a <a href="./Cooking.md#Canned" class="wikilink" title="canned good">canned good</a>. However, if the player smashes open a can, they will not receive a Recycled Can once the food is consumed. Additionally, Recycled Cans occasionally spawn in houses with kitchens or in various other locations.

A Recycled Can will decrease the amount of time required for canned food to cook by **50%**, compared to the time needed to cook indirectly near the <a href="./Fire.md" class="wikilink" title="Fire">Fire</a>. This time can be decreased by an additional **20%** if the player uses a <a href="./Cooking%20Pot.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Pot">Cooking Pot</a> instead of a Recycled Can. A <a href="./Cooking%20Skillet.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Skillet">Cooking Skillet</a> can decrease cooking times even more than a Recycled can as well.

A Recycled Can can melt or boil up to 0.5 liters (0.13 gallons) of snow or water at a time. A Cooking Pot has four times the capacity (2L / 0.52 gallons) and melts/boils **20%** faster.

A Recycled Can will degrade by **10%** if its contents are burned or boiled dry. The Recycled Can will not degrade in any other circumstances.

## Trivia

- All <a href="./Fire.md" class="wikilink" title="Fire">Fires</a> have the same cooking, boiling, and melting times for food and drink items used with a Recycled Can. The cooking speed is not dependent on the temperature of the fire.
- Using a Recycled Can will not decrease the amount of time required to cook canned food, compared to cooking the food in its own can.
- You can only use two Recycled Cans, Cooking Pots/Skillets at a time on most cooking surfaces, but stoves have six slots.
- On Interloper, the Recycled Can is the only item you start with, besides some basic clothing.
- Recycled Cans can be used for shooting practice, as they will be knocked back from the impact of a bullet/arrow.
