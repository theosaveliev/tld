**Red Irish Lord (Raw)** is a raw [food](./Food%20And%20Drink.md) item in _The Long Dark_. Red Irish Lord have a minimum raw weight of 1.5 kg and a maximum raw weight of 3.1 kg.

Red Irish Lord can be harvested to obtain [Fishing Bait](./Fishing%20Bait.md), yielding 3 units per kilogram harvested.
