**Restore Condition** is a positive <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> acquired after drinking <a href="./Birch%20Bark%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Birch Bark Tea">Birch Bark Tea</a>. This Affliction remains in place for up to 2 hours per tea consumed.

## Causes

Restore Condition is received when consuming any of the following items:

|  | Food and Drink |
|----|----|
| **Template:iconlink plain** | <a href="./Birch%20Bark%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Birch Bark Tea">Birch Bark Tea</a> |

## Effects

This affliction causes the following effects:

|  | Effect | Magnitude | Duration |
|----|----|----|----|
| **Template:iconlink plain** | <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> restoration | 2.5% per hour | 2 hours[^1] |
