**Rheumatic Joints** is an <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> found within *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*. It is one of 6 unique Afflictions that can be encountered only within Misery Mode experience.

Rheumatic Joints is a permanent Affliction unique to <a href="./Misery.md" class="wikilink" title="Misery">Misery</a> which occurs from Stages 2-4 of Misery. It reduces the survivor's speed while <a href="./Stamina.md" class="wikilink" title="sprinting">sprinting</a>, the length of time available to aim the <a href="./Survival%20Bow.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Bow">Survival Bow</a>, and increases the chance of getting a <a href="./Sprained%20Wrist.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Wrist">Sprained Wrist</a> or <a href="./Sprained%20Ankle.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Ankle">Sprained Ankle</a> when on uneven terrain[^1]. It also causes minor Condition damage when incurring a <a href="./Sprained%20Ankle.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Ankle">Sprained Ankle</a> or <a href="./Sprained%20Wrist.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Wrist">Sprained Wrist</a>, making <a href="./Mountain%20Goating.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountain Goating">Mountain Goating</a> and slopes a danger to the player once <a href="./Broken%20Body.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Body">Broken Body</a> sets in.

## Effects

- -40% <a href="./Stamina.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprint">Sprint</a> speed
- -1s <a href="./Survival%20Bow.md" class="wikilink" title="Aiming">Aiming</a> time
- Chance to get Sprains increased from 15% (base chance) to 40%
- 2% <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> damage upon receiving a Sprained Wrist or Sprained Ankle (Do note that the damage is doubled, i.e. 4%, when you also have Broken Body.)
