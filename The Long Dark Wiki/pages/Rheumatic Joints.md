## Overview

> “__A painful inflammation has taken hold.  
   
 You cannot Sprint as quickly, nor Aim a weapon for long before having to lower it. Sprains are more likely.__”
>
> — In-game Description

Rheumatic Joints is a permanent Affliction unique to [Misery](./Misery.md) which occurs from Stages 2-4 of Misery. It reduces the survivor's speed while [sprinting](./Stamina.md), the length of time available to aim the [Survival Bow](./Survival%20Bow.md), and increases the chance of getting a [Sprained Wrist](./Sprained%20Wrist.md) or [Sprained Ankle](./Sprained%20Ankle.md) when on uneven terrain<span id="fnref1"></span>[¹](#fn1). It also causes minor Condition damage when incurring a [Sprained Ankle](./Sprained%20Ankle.md) or [Sprained Wrist](./Sprained%20Wrist.md), making [Mountain Goating](./Mountain%20Goating.md) and slopes a danger to the player once [Broken Body](./Broken%20Body.md) sets in.

<span id="effects"></span><span id="Effects"></span>

## Effects

- -40% [Sprint](./Stamina.md) speed
- -1s [Aiming](./Survival%20Bow.md) time
- Chance to get Sprains increased from 15% (base chance) to 40%
- 2% [Condition](./Condition.md) damage upon receiving a Sprained Wrist or Sprained Ankle (Do note that the damage is doubled, i.e. 4%, when you also have Broken Body.)
