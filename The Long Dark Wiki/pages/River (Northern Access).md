# River \(Northern Access\)

> {{Infobox/Location|title=|image=River Northern Access.jpg|region=Mystery Lake|description=A small river between Carter Dam and Mystery Lake.|map=River (Northern Access) Location.png}}The '''River (Northern Access)''' is an unmarked location in [[Mystery Lake (region)|Mystery Lake]]. It is located not far from [[Train Bridge]].
> 
> The river connects [[Mystery Lake (location)|Mystery Lake]] to the [[Carter Hydro Dam]] and runs under the [[Train Bridge]]. Along the river  many [[Cat Tail]] Plants and [[Stone|Stones]] can be found. [[Alan's Cave]] can be easily accessed by following the river. Many [[Deer]] and occasionally a [[Moose]] spawn in the area. A small birch tree forest is nearby, providing [[Birch Bark]].{{Mystery Lake}}
