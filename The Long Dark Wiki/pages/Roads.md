[Great Bear Island](./Great%20Bear%20Island.md) features a number of prominent **roads** which used to connect the [regions](./Region.md) and their various locations prior to the earthquakes and the [First Flare](./First%20Flare.md). When visibility is poor, following roads can make navigation much easier.

<span id="major_roads"></span><span id="Major_roads"></span><span id="major-roads"></span>

## Major roads

These roads travel great distances through multiple regions of Great Bear Island, and are paved. There are three major roads, two of which are interconnected (Blackrock and Inland Highways).

<span id="blackrock_road"></span><span id="Blackrock_Road"></span><span id="blackrock-road"></span>

### Blackrock Road

This road starts at [Mirthless Fork](./Mirthless%20Fork.md) in [Keeper's Pass South](./Keeper%27s%20Pass%20South.md) and goes over a long bridge. Soon thereafter, it's blocked by a rockfall, forcing the player to go through an ice cave to enter [Keeper's Pass North](./Keeper%27s%20Pass%20North.md). After going through a ravine and crossing over [Builder's Bridge](./Builder%27s%20Bridge.md), players can re-join the road as it follows a cliff above a river. Once in [Blackrock](./Blackrock.md) the road is blocked again by a semi trailer at the [Blocked Bridge](./Blocked%20Bridge.md), forcing players to make another detour. After returning to the road, players will come to an intersection from which they may travel west or east.

<span id="western_section"></span><span id="Western_section"></span><span id="western-section"></span>

#### Western section

Heading westward, the road passes by the [Old Substation](./Old%20Substation.md), before coming to [Narrow Bridge](./Narrow%20Bridge.md), where players will have to carefully walk on the beams to avoid falling into the river below. A landslide blocks the road at [Bear's Bend](./Bear%27s%20Bend.md), from which it continues northward to reach [Grand Meadow](./Grand%20Meadow.md) and finally the [Power Plant](./Power%20Plant.md).

<span id="eastern_section"></span><span id="Eastern_section"></span><span id="eastern-section"></span>

#### Eastern section

East of the intersection, players will pass the ruins of the [Jailer's Residence](./Jailer%27s%20Residence.md), before coming to another intersection. Going northward will take players straight to [Blackrock Prison](./Blackrock%20Prison.md) after passing one [vehicle](./Vehicle.md). Continuing eastward, the road crosses a wooden bridge and passes by [Cook's Farm](./Cook%27s%20Farm.md) and [Foreman's Clearcut](./Foreman%27s%20Clearcut.md), and, after a short walk, the transition cave to [Timberwolf Mountain](./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md).

Beyond the cave, this section of the road passes a small pond and the [Bricklayer's Retreat](./Bricklayer%27s%20Retreat.md), before turning generally northward and passing by [Wedge Cave](./Wedge%20Cave.md) and [No Road Cave](./No%20Road%20Cave.md). The road ends at the [Mule Bridge](./Mule%20Bridge.md), which is broken in Survival mode.

<span id="great_bear_highway"></span><span id="Great_Bear_Highway"></span><span id="great-bear-highway"></span>

### Great Bear Highway

This road starts at the [Coastal Highway Terminus](./Coastal%20Highway%20Terminus.md), a fallen bridge in [Bleak Inlet](./Bleak%20Inlet.md). The road goes past the [Trailers west of the Cannery](./Trailers%20west%20of%20the%20Cannery.md) and the [Cannery itself](./Last%20Resort%20Cannery.md), before crossing over the [Long Bridge](./Long%20Bridge.md). An intersection leads up to the [Coast Mountain Road](./Coast%20Mountain%20Road.md) near the [Cannery Worker Residences](./Cannery%20Worker%20Residences.md), followed by a bridge at [Raven Crossing](./Raven%20Crossing.md). A [landslide](./Landslide%20%28Bleak%20Inlet%29.md) blocks the road as it heads to [Coastal Highway](./Coastal%20Highway.md) itself.

In the Coastal Highway region, the same obstruction at the western end of the region is named the [Rockfall](./Rockfall.md). The road runs eastward skirting the coast, passing the [Fishing Camp](./Fishing%20Camp.md), and the [Log Sort](./Log%20Sort.md). A minor road to the left leads up the hill towards the [Trans-Island Railway Line](./Trans-Island%20Railway%20Line.md). Then it passes the lower entrance to the [Cinder Hills Coal Mine](./Cinder%20Hills%20Coal%20Mine.md), before arriving at the [Coastal Townsite](./Coastal%20Townsite.md). The road passes by the [Waterfront Cottages](./Waterfront%20Cottages.md) before being blocked again at the [Commuter's Lament](./Commuter%27s%20Lament.md), where it enters the [Old Island Connector](./Crumbling%20Highway.md). In this region, the road passes by the [Abandoned Harris Home](./Abandoned%20Harris%20Home.md) before being blocked at the eastern end of the region.

The road's section in [Desolation Point](./Desolation%20Point.md) runs below [Katie's Secluded Corner](./Katie%27s%20Secluded%20Corner.md) and goes past the [Lonely Lighthouse](./Lonely%20Lighthouse.md) and [Hibernia Processing](./Hibernia%20Processing.md), ending at [Broken Bridge](./Broken%20Bridge%20%28Desolation%20Point%20location%29.md).

<span id="inland_highway"></span><span id="Inland_Highway"></span><span id="inland-highway"></span>

### Inland Highway

This road starts near the [Spruce Falls Bridge](./Spruce%20Falls%20Bridge.md) in [Mountain Town](./Mountain%20Town.md) and passes by the [Hushed River Valley](./Hushed%20River%20Valley.md) [ connection cave](./Cave%20%28Transition%20Zone%29.md) and [St. Christopher's Church](./St.%20Christopher%27s%20Church.md) to reach [Milton](./Town%20of%20Milton.md). It then passes by the [Orca Gas Station](./Orca%20Gas%20Station.md) and comes to a collapsed tunnel where a [prison bus crashed](./Crashed%20Prison%20Transport%20Bus.md). Beyond this tunnel, the road enters [Keeper's Pass South](./Keeper%27s%20Pass%20South.md), where a road to [Blackrock](./Blackrock.md) branches off at [Mirthless Fork](./Mirthless%20Fork.md).

The road then crosses a bridge and enters [Pleasant Valley](./Pleasant%20Valley.md) at [Long Curve](./Long%20Curve.md). It passes near [Pleasant Valley Farmstead](./Pleasant%20Valley%20Farmstead.md), crossing over the [Heartbreak Bridge](./Heartbreak%20Bridge.md) and the [Contemplation Bridge](./Contemplation%20Bridge.md) to enter the small town of [Thomson's Crossing](./Thomson%27s%20Crossing.md). The road continues over a bridge. An intersection leads to the Mining Road and the Point of Disagreement Road. This road ends at a landslide aptly named [End of the Road](./End%20of%20the%20Road.md).

## Regional roads

These roads generally travel shorter distances. Some of the more notable ones are listed below.

<span id="coast_mountain_road"></span><span id="Coast_Mountain_Road"></span><span id="coast-mountain-road"></span>

### [Coast Mountain Road](./Coast%20Mountain%20Road.md)

A short road that used to lead from the [Cannery Worker Residences](./Cannery%20Worker%20Residences.md) to the [Echo One Radio Tower](./Echo%20One%20Radio%20Tower.md) in [Bleak Inlet](./Bleak%20Inlet.md). It was broken in half by an earthquake, requiring a long rope climb near [Pensive Lookout](./Pensive%20Lookout.md) to get between the two sections.

<span id="farmstead_roads"></span><span id="Farmstead_Roads"></span><span id="farmstead-roads"></span>

### Farmstead Roads

These roads are in Pleasant Valley, and are generally level, travelling though fields and woodland. They connect the [Pleasant Valley Outbuildings](./Pleasant%20Valley%20Outbuildings.md) to the [farmhouse](./Pleasant%20Valley%20Farmstead.md), [Three Strikes Farmstead](./Three%20Strikes%20Farmstead.md), and the Inland Highway. Several wooden bridges may be found crossing the rivers in the region.

<span id="forsaken_airfield_road"></span><span id="Forsaken_Airfield_Road"></span><span id="forsaken-airfield-road"></span>

### Forsaken Airfield Road

Leads from the [Vacant Depot](./Vacant%20Depot.md) to the [Main Hangar](./Main%20Hangar.md). The road first climbs up a short hill, then enters a narrow canyon. After some time it breaks out of the canyon, affording a view of the airport at [Whealy's Turn](./Whealy%27s%20Turn.md). It then re-enters the canyon before emerging into more open country at the [Junker's Paddock](./Junker%27s%20Paddock.md). Soon thereafter, the road branches in two, with one branch leading to the airport and another going through the flat, featureless landscape of [Forsaken Airfield](./Forsaken%20Airfield.md) to eventually climb a hill leading to [Mindful Cabin](./Mindful%20Cabin.md).

<span id="mining_road"></span><span id="Mining_Road"></span><span id="mining-road"></span>

### Mining Road

This road in [Pleasant Valley](./Pleasant%20Valley.md) connects the Inland Highway to the [Cinder Hills Coal Mine](./Cinder%20Hills%20Coal%20Mine.md). It initially leads through some forest before climbing up steeply to the mine entrance.

<span id="langston_mine_road"></span><span id="Langston_Mine_Road"></span><span id="langston-mine-road"></span>

### Langston Mine Road

Starting at [Vacant Depot](./Vacant%20Depot.md), this road parallels the [Trans-Island Railway Line](./Trans-Island%20Railway%20Line.md), passing by some derailed railcars, a locomotive, and a [Chemical Hazard](./Chemical%20Hazard.md). It then passes through [Flagman's Gap](./Flagman%27s%20Gap.md) and heads near [Idle Camp](./Idle%20Camp.md), where a minor road branches off. This small road splits in two, with one branch leading to [Boss's Settlement](./Boss%27s%20Settlement.md), the other [Harrowed Residence](./Harrowed%20Residence.md). The main road passes through a couple more Chemical Hazards before arriving at the [Langston Mine](./Langston%20Mine.md) itself. From there, it continues southwards around the perimeter of the open pit, passing by the [Pump House](./Pump%20House.md) and [Overladen Pond](./Overladen%20Pond.md), before going through one more Chemical Hazard and ending at a trailer.

<span id="misty_falls_road"></span><span id="Misty_Falls_Road"></span><span id="misty-falls-road"></span>

### Misty Falls Road

Starts shortly after the Mining Road in [Pleasant Valley](./Pleasant%20Valley.md) and is paved up to the [Derelict Cabins](./Derelict%20Cabins.md), at which point the road splits in two. The low branch gradually goes downhill towards the [Draft Dodger's Cabin](./Draft%20Dodger%27s%20Cabin.md) through the forest, crossing over a bridge along the way. The high branch slowly goes uphill, crosses another wooden bridge before coming to the [Point of Disagreement](./Point%20of%20Disagreement.md). From there, it makes a right, and players will see a broken-down cabin and the rope to [Timberwolf Mountain](./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md). The road ends at the [Misty Falls Picnic Area](./Misty%20Falls%20Picnic%20Area.md), and players may wish to rest-up in the [nearby cave](./Misty%20Falls%20Cave.md).

<span id="signal_hill_road"></span><span id="Signal_Hill_Road"></span><span id="signal-hill-road"></span>

### Signal Hill Road

A road that starts roughly halfway between [Contemplation Bridge](./Contemplation%20Bridge.md) and [Thomson's Crossing](./Thomson%27s%20Crossing.md), passing mainly through woodlands and generally slowly uphill. Notable landmarks include the [Picnic Spot](./Picnic%20Spot.md) and [Pensive Pond](./Pensive%20Pond.md) before coming to [Signal Hill](./Signal%20Hill.md) itself.

<span id="weather_station_road"></span><span id="Weather_Station_Road"></span><span id="weather-station-road"></span>

### Weather Station Road

This road starts at [Vacant Depot](./Vacant%20Depot.md) and is blocked by a landslide, forcing players to go around. It then continues along the edge of the mountain over [Baneful Bridge](./Baneful%20Bridge.md), passing [Lotte's Rest](./Lotte%27s%20Rest.md). From there, the road continues to hug the mountain, crossing over a river at [Cascading Gap](./Cascading%20Gap.md) before arriving into the alpine at [Placid Notch](./Placid%20Notch.md). At the [Timberwolf](./Timberwolf.md)-infested [Old Settlement](./Old%20Settlement.md), the road splits in half: one branch continues uphill past [Ambler's Lake](./Ambler%27s%20Lake.md) and [Final Refuge](./Final%20Refuge.md), ending at an avalance called [Unyielding Inundation](./Unyielding%20Inundation.md). The other branch crosses over a broken bridge before going quickly uphill, ending at the [Weather Station](./Weather%20Station.md).
