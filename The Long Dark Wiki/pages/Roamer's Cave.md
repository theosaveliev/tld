# Roamer's Cave

> {{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Roamer's Cave.jpg|region=Sundered Pass|shelter=partial|type=cave|description=A cave in the mountains with a burned out trailer and scaffolding leading higher into the mountains nearby|map=Roamer's Cave Location.png}}'''Roamer's Cave''' is a location in [[Sundered Pass]]. It is located east of [[Ambler's Lake]], and can be reached by climbing a very long [[Rope Climbing]] section up the mountain. Reaching this location without an [[Emergency Stim]], [[Crampons]], or [[Cup of Coffee]] is not advised, as there are no ledges to rest on when making the very long climb.
> 
> == Overview ==
> Aside from the Cave itself, there is a burnt out trailer along with a shed and some wooden scaffolding leading up into the mountain. [[Rabbit|Rabbits]] and [[Ptarmigan]] can be found in the field of the plateau in front of the cave.
> 
> Initially, the area here is a dead end. A passage to the [[High Peak Plateau]] can only be opened up while progressing through the [[Last Horizon]] Tale.{{Sundered Pass}}
