**Rockfish (Raw)** is a raw <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in *The Long Dark*. Rockfish have a minimum raw weight of 1.8 kg and a maximum raw weight of 5.0 kg.

Rockfish can be harvested to obtain <a href="./Fishing%20Bait.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Bait">Fishing Bait</a>, yielding 3 units per kilogram harvested.
