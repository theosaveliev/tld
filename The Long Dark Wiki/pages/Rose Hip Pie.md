<h2 id="Analysis">Analysis</h2>
<p>Owing to the abundance of Rose Hips in the wilderness and the food's fatigue-restoring properties, these pies can make an excellent travelling food. The ingredients are fairly heavy, weighing in at 0.43 kg, but the total caloric value of the pies is 675 kcal, making for an energy density of 1,570 kcal/kg. With level 5 cooking, this can be boosted to 1,962 kcal/kg. Overall, these pies are much more versatile than their <a href="./Peach%20Pie.md" class="wikilink" title="peach equivalent">peach equivalent</a>.</p>
