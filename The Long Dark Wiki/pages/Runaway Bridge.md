> }}'''Runaway Bridge''' is a fixed bridge in [[Ash Canyon]], an anomaly compared to the various swinging bridges in the region.
> 
> ==Overview==
> A path leading to a rock that you can deploy a [[Mountaineering Rope]] on for easy access to the [[Climber's Cave]]. There are some [[Wolf|wolves]] on the plateau to the right after crossing the bridge, but there is no apparent way to climb up to them, nor can they seem to find a way to reach you.
> 
> Before crossing the bridge, there is a hole in the rocks to the left that can be crawled through. This leads to a path up a narrow fallen tree. At the end of this path is a nook containing a tuffet [[bed]] and a potential [[Curator's Rifle]] spawn.
