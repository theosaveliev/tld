**Rüdiger's Diary 3** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectibles">Collectibles</a> found in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Last%20Horizon.md" class="wikilink" title="Last Horizon">Last Horizon</a> <a href="./Tales.md" class="wikilink" title="Tale">Tale</a>. It is found in a hidden room in <a href="./Bunker%20Omega.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Omega">Bunker Omega</a> near the end of the Tale.

## Transcript

<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">

The following text is transcribed from **Rüdiger's Diary 3**

</div>

<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

Just waiting now. Trapped in squalor, brought low by my ambition.

Still, I believed I was right.

I \*am\* right.

If we are to move forward as a species, we will need better models for understanding our planet and its potential for \*creative destruction.\*

Eras when it wipes the slate clean and starts again.

I wanted to profit from this knowledge. Probabilistically my chances were good. A dice roll, yes. But the numbers suggest a favorable one.

Based on my current condition, I overestimated my chances.

</div>
