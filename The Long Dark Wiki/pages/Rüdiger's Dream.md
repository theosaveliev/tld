**Rüdiger's Dream** is an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in *[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)*.

## Guide

This achievement is easiest to unlock when playing on [Pilgrim](./Pilgrim.md). The player must complete all 3 [Tales](./Tales.md) in a single [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) game:

- [Signal Void](./Signal%20Void.md)
- [Buried Echoes](./Buried%20Echoes.md)
- [Last Horizon](./Last%20Horizon.md)

Completing [Sutherland's Tale](./Sutherland%27s%20Tale.md) is not needed to unlock this achievement.**Lua execution error in Module:Navbox function navbox**
