**Rüdiger's Watch** is a [Collectibles](./Collectibles.md) found in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) as part of the [Last Horizon](./Last%20Horizon.md) [Tale](./Tales.md). It is found in a hidden room in [Bunker Omega](./Bunker%20Omega.md) near the end of the Tale.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Rüdiger's Watch**_

An expensive-looking gold wristwatch. The face of the watch was shattered by a great force.

## Trivia

- [Dr. Rüdiger](./Dr.%20R%C3%BCdiger.md) was apparently attacked by a [Cougar](./Cougar.md) before [The Foreman](./The%20Foreman.md) found him, explaining why the watch is damaged. "I could see he'd already had a bad time of it out there. It must have been a cougar."

- [Foreman's Technical Log 4](./Foreman%27s%20Technical%20Log%204.md)

- The initials "A.R." are found on the back of the watch.
