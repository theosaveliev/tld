<td><p>Has an interior|} <strong>Scaffolder's Hideout</strong> is a cave not far from <a href="./Boss%27s%20Settlement.md" class="wikilink" title="Boss&#39;s Settlement">Boss's Settlement</a> in the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>, with a wooden bridge separating the two locations.</p>
<p>The cave has a campfire and 3 beds, one of which may be occupied by a <a href="./Corpse.md" class="wikilink" title="Corpse">Corpse</a>, as well as an extinguished <a href="./Campfire.md" class="wikilink" title="Campfire">Campfire</a>. The cave is also a source of <a href="./Coal.md" class="wikilink" title="Coal">Coal</a>. Near the wooden bridge often contains a <a href="./Simple%20Arrow.md" class="wikilink" title="Simple Arrow">Simple Arrow</a>, <a href="./Broken%20Arrow.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Arrow">Broken Arrow</a>, and <a href="./Rabbit%20Carcass.md" class="wikilink" title="Rabbit Carcass">Rabbit Carcass</a> near the trees.</p>
