# Scaffolder's Hideout

> {{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Scaffolder's Hideout.jpg|region=Zone of Contamination|description=A cave not far from [[Boss's Settlement]]|bed=no|shelter=yes|map=Scaffolder's Hideout Location.png}}'''Scaffolder's Hideout''' is a cave not far from [[Boss's Settlement]] in the [[Zone of Contamination]], with a wooden bridge separating the two locations.
> 
> The cave has a campfire and 3 beds, one of which may be occupied by a [[Corpse]], as well as an extinguished [[Campfire]]. The cave is also a source of [[Coal]]. Near the wooden bridge often contains a [[Simple Arrow]], [[Broken Arrow]], and [[Rabbit Carcass]] near the trees.
> 
> A small teddy bear can be found buried in some rocks at the rear of the cave.
