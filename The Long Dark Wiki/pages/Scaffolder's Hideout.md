**Scaffolder's Hideout** is a cave not far from [Boss's Settlement](./Boss%27s%20Settlement.md) in the [Zone of Contamination](./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md), with a wooden bridge separating the two locations.

The cave has a campfire and 3 beds, one of which may be occupied by a [Corpse](./Corpse.md), as well as an extinguished [Campfire](./Campfire.md). The cave is also a source of [Coal](./Coal.md). Near the wooden bridge often contains a [Simple Arrow](./Simple%20Arrow.md), [Broken Arrow](./Broken%20Arrow.md), and [Rabbit Carcass](./Rabbit%20Carcass.md) near the trees.

A small teddy bear can be found buried in some rocks at the rear of the cave. ![The Teddy Bear](../assets/Teddy_Bear_Scaffolder%27s_Hideout.jpg)_The Teddy Bear_
