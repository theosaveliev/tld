**Search Party** is a hidden <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>* <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things">Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things</a> of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This is a hidden achievement granted after completing the main story mission <a href="./To%20Those%20Who%20Are%20Missing.md" class="wikilink" title="To Those Who Are Missing">To Those Who Are Missing</a>. The objectives for the mission are to locate three missing persons in <a href="./Perseverance%20Wilds.md" class="wikilink" title="Perseverance Wilds">Perseverance Wilds</a>, and to get the achievement this task must be done within 40 minutes.

The achievement timer begins the moment the player enters Perseverance Wilds (the timer does not start immediately upon beginning the mission inside <a href="./Perseverance%20Mills.md" class="wikilink" title="Perseverance Mills">Perseverance Mills</a>)
