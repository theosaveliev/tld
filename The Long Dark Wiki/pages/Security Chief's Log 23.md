**Security Chief's Log 23** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectibles">Collectibles</a> found in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Signal%20Void.md" class="wikilink" title="Signal Void">Signal Void</a> <a href="./Tales.md" class="wikilink" title="Tale">Tale</a>. It is found inside one of the bunkers as the tale progresses.

## Transcript

<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">

The following text is transcribed from **Security Chief's Log 23**

</div>

<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

We ran power to Rüdiger's machine for the first time yesterday. It was nothing like what they predicted, even at half-energy.

I still have no idea what the endgame for all of this will be. The whole project. And I’m seeing now that few of the men building it do either. It’s all over their faces. All this time underground isn’t helping their morale, but I’m confident our fragile truce will hold until we’re finished.

We worked hard to make it this far after what happened to Team 3.

But afterwards? Impossible to say.

</div>
