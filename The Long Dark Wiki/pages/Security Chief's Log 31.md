**Security Chief's Log 31** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectibles">Collectibles</a> found in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Buried%20Echoes.md" class="wikilink" title="Buried Echoes">Buried Echoes</a> <a href="./Tales.md" class="wikilink" title="Tale">Tale</a>. It is found at the crashed Helicopter near <a href="./Old%20Corral.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Corral">Old Corral</a> along with the <a href="./Foreman%27s%20Key.md" class="wikilink" title="Foreman&#39;s Key">Foreman's Key</a>.

## Transcript

<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">

The following text is transcribed from **Security Chief's Log 31**

</div>

<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">

If this message has reached you, it means they made it out, and to the Mainland.

What a mess, old friend. I sent them your way after we managed to get a chopper flight-worthy.

By now they shared my old call sign with you, from the Alaska days. I hope that, and this note from my logbook, convinces you. Do you recognize the book? Do you remember it?

It's gone bad over here, and strange beyond everything. But it's on me now. And there are others still missing down below.

Take care, and keep an eye on the horizon. You never know.

-- Vertasky

</div>
