> The '''Security Chief's Rifle Holster''' is a type of accessory in ''The Long Dark''. It is part of the ''[[Tales from the Far Territory]]'' DLC.
> 
> ==Overview==
> The Security Chief's Rifle Holster is a [[Clothing]] item that when worn in either accessory slot, reduces the weight of an equipped [[Hunting Rifle]], or one of its variants, by 50%:
> 
> *[[Hunting Rifle]]
> **Unloaded 4.00 kg = (2.00kg)
> **Fully Loaded 4.20 kg = (2.10kg)
> *[[Barb's Rifle]]
> **Unloaded 4.50 kg = (2.25kg)
> **Fully Loaded 4.70 kg = (2.35kg)
> * [[Bunker Rifle]]
> **Unloaded 1.50 kg = (0.75kg)
> **Fully Loaded 1.70 kg = (0.85kg)
> * [[Curator's Rifle]]
> **Unloaded 4.00 kg = (2.00kg)
> **Fully Loaded 4.20 kg = (2.10kg)
> *[[Vaughn's Rifle]]
> **Unloaded 3.00 kg = (1.50kg)
> **Fully Loaded 3.20 kg = (1.60kg)
> 
> The holster provides no other bonuses, such as [[Warmth]] or [[Clothing#Windproof|Windproofing]]. Like other clothing, the holster is vulnerable to damage from [[Struggle]] and [[Weather|blizzards]], though it is sturdy and relatively easy to repair.
> 
> == Location ==
> The holster is found in the kitchen of [[Bunker Omega]], having belonged to the Chief Security Officer overseeing the Rüdiger's Project. It is the ultimate prize for completing the [[Tales|Tale]] [[Signal Void]].
>
