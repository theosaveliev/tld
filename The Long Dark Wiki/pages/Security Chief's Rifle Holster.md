Its fine construction reduces the weight of your primary Rifle.**”<br>The **Security Chief's Rifle Holster** is a type of accessory in _The Long Dark_. It is part of the _[Tales from the Far Territory](./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md)_ DLC.

## Overview

The Security Chief's Rifle Holster is a [Clothing](./Clothing.md) item that when worn in either accessory slot, reduces the weight of an equipped [Hunting Rifle](./Hunting%20Rifle.md), or one of its variants, by 50%:

- [Hunting Rifle](./Hunting%20Rifle.md)
  - Unloaded 4.00 kg = (2.00kg)
  - Fully Loaded 4.20 kg = (2.10kg)
- [Barb's Rifle](./Barb%27s%20Rifle.md)
  - Unloaded 4.50 kg = (2.25kg)
  - Fully Loaded 4.70 kg = (2.35kg)
- [Bunker Rifle](./Bunker%20Rifle.md)
  - Unloaded 1.50 kg = (0.75kg)
  - Fully Loaded 1.70 kg = (0.85kg)
- [Curator's Rifle](./Curator%27s%20Rifle.md)
  - Unloaded 4.00 kg = (2.00kg)
  - Fully Loaded 4.20 kg = (2.10kg)
- [Vaughn's Rifle](./Vaughn%27s%20Rifle.md)
  - Unloaded 3.00 kg = (1.50kg)
  - Fully Loaded 3.20 kg = (1.60kg)

The holster provides no other bonuses, such as [Warmth](./Warmth.md) or [Windproofing](./Clothing.md#Windproof). Like other clothing, the holster is vulnerable to damage from [Struggle](./Struggle.md) and [blizzards](./Weather.md), though it is sturdy and relatively easy to repair.

## Location

The holster is found in the kitchen of [Bunker Omega](./Bunker%20Omega.md), having belonged to the Chief Security Officer overseeing the Rüdiger's Project. It is the ultimate prize for completing the [Tale](./Tales.md) [Signal Void](./Signal%20Void.md).

Security Chief's Rifle Holster is not available on [Interloper](./Interloper.md).
