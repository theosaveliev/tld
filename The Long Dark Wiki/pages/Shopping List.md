**Shopping List** is a [Collectibles](./Collectibles.md) found in [Episode 5](./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story. It is found during the main mission [High Energy](./High%20Energy.md), inside the [Glove Box](./Glove%20Box.md) of the single [Truck](./Truck.md) parked outside the [Suzuki Radio Telescope](./Suzuki%20Radio%20Telescope.md).

The code 1859 is a reference to the [Carrington Event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrington_Event) that occurred in the same year, a reference to Atwood's work and her research leading up to the [First Flare](./First%20Flare.md).
