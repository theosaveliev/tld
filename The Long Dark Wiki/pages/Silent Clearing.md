## Overview

Silent Clearing is located to the northeast of [Coastal Townsite](./Coastal%20Townsite.md).

[Green Birch Saplings](./Green%20Birch%20Sapling.md), [Old Man's Beard Lichen](./Old%20Man%27s%20Beard%20Lichen.md), [Burdock](./Burdock.md), and some [Reishi Mushrooms](./Reishi%20Mushroom.md) can be harvested at Silent Clearing.

At one end a [Frozen Corpse](./Frozen%20Corpse.md) may spawn along with a [Distress Pistol](./Distress%20Pistol.md) and several [Flare Shells](./Flare%20Shell.md). [Deer](./Deer.md) are commonly found in the clearing.

To the north, there is [Mountaineering Rope](./Mountaineering%20Rope.md) attached to a higher part of the Highway. Use caution when attempting to climb. It consumes a lot of fatigue to scale the cliff.
