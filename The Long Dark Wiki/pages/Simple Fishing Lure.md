**Simple Fishing Lure** is a basic Fishing Lure introduced in the free update portion of the Part 3: Frontier Comforts update for the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> expansion.

## Overview

Fishing Lures are accessories added to a <a href="./Fishing%20Tackle.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Tackle">Fishing Tackle</a> to increase the user's chances to catch fish while <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a> (including some of the more rare fish).

Can be created at a <a href="./Work%20Bench.md" class="wikilink" title="Work Bench">Work Bench</a>, and takes 30 minutes and 1 <a href="./Scrap%20Metal.md" class="wikilink" title="Scrap Metal">Scrap Metal</a> to create.

It's nothing more than a shiny piece of metal, meant to catch the eye of any fish that pass by, though it does a poor job imitating anything a fish should want to eat compared to the 3 other <a href="./Fishing%20Lure%20%28disambiguation%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Lures">Fishing Lures</a>.

If a Fishing Tackle is equipped with a Simple Fishing Lure and snaps when fishing, the Lure will be lost and must be crafted again.
