**Simple Fishing Lure** is a basic Fishing Lure introduced in the free update portion of the Part 3: Frontier Comforts update for the [Tales from the Far Territory](./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md) expansion.

## Overview

Fishing Lures are accessories added to a [Fishing Tackle](./Fishing%20Tackle.md) to increase the user's chances to catch fish while [Ice Fishing](./Ice%20Fishing.md) (including some of the more rare fish).

Can be created at a [Work Bench](./Work%20Bench.md), and takes 30 minutes and 1 [Scrap Metal](./Scrap%20Metal.md) to create.

It's nothing more than a shiny piece of metal, meant to catch the eye of any fish that pass by, though it does a poor job imitating anything a fish should want to eat compared to the 3 other [Fishing Lures](./Fishing%20Lure%20%28disambiguation%29.md).

If a Fishing Tackle is equipped with a Simple Fishing Lure and snaps when fishing, the Lure will be lost and must be crafted again.
