**Simple Tools** are a Tool item used for crafting simple items and repairing equipment.

## Overview

Simple tools weigh 1.0 kilogram. Simple tools can repair an item 25% faster than without any tools. They also increase the chance of a successful repair by 10%.

Simple tools also reduce the time required to craft [Simple Arrows](./Simple%20Arrow.md) by 25%, even if the tools are not required for the crafting itself.

The item always comes in 100% condition if found. Use degrades the condition of the tools. Ruined tools at 0% condition can no longer be used or repaired. The item can be repaired using itself, It take 2 hours to repair tools and the success rate depends on your [repair survival skill](./Repair.md).
