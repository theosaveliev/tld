<p><strong>Smoker's Cave</strong> is an unmarked cave in the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>The cave provides a warmth bonus, and is a good source of <a href="./Coal.md" class="wikilink" title="Coal">Coal</a>. There is no other loot present. The cave is home to a <a href="./Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear">Bear</a>, caution is advised when exploring the cave. <a href="./Poisoned%20Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Poisoned Wolves">Poisoned Wolves</a> often patrol the area.</p>
<p><a href="./Miner%27s%20Footpath.md" class="wikilink" title="Miner&#39;s Footpath">Miner's Footpath</a> is to the north, while <a href="./Desperate%20Elevation.md" class="wikilink" title="Desperate Elevation">Desperate Elevation</a> is to the south. <a href="./Craggy%20Passage.md" class="wikilink" title="Craggy Passage">Craggy Passage</a> is even farther to the south.</p>
