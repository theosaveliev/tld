# Smoker's Cave

> {{Infobox/Location|title=|image=Smoker's Cave.jpg|shelter = partial|map=Smoker's Cave Location.png|region=Zone of Contamination}}
> 
> '''Smoker's Cave''' is an unmarked cave in the [[Zone of Contamination]].
> 
> ==Overview==
> The cave provides a warmth bonus, and is a good source of [[Coal]]. There is no other loot present. The cave is home to a [[Bear]], caution is advised when exploring the cave. [[Poisoned Wolf|Poisoned Wolves]] often patrol the area.
> 
> [[Miner's Footpath]] is to the north, while [[Desperate Elevation]] is to the south. [[Craggy Passage]] is even farther to the south.
>
