# Song of the Earth

**Song of the Earth** is a hidden [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in *[Wintermute](./Wintermute.md)* [Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things](./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md) of *[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)*.

## Guide

This is an unmissable hidden achievement granted after completing the main story mission [High Energy](./High%20Energy.md). After reactivating all 3 Geothermal Stations in the [Suzuki Geothermal Region](./Suzuki%20Geothermal%20Region.md) the achievement will unlock.
