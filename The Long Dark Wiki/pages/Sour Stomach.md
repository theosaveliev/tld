Sour Stomach is a permanent Affliction unique to <a href="./Misery.md" class="wikilink" title="Misery Mode">Misery Mode</a> which occurs from Stage 2-4 of Misery. It permanently reduces the maximum calories the survivor can store in their body. Sour Stomach also disables both of the benefits from Cooking Skill level 5: you now have a chance of <a href="./Food%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Food Poisoning">Food Poisoning</a> when <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="eating cooked or artificial food">eating cooked or artificial food</a>, as well as <a href="./Risk%20of%20Intestinal%20Parasites.md" class="wikilink" title="Risk of Intestinal Parasites">Risk of Intestinal Parasites</a> when eating cooked predator meat again.

## Effects

- Maximum <a href="./Needs.md" class="wikilink" title="Calories Stored">Calories Stored</a> reduced by 1000 (1500 maximum)
- Disables <a href="./Cooking.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Skill">Cooking Skill</a> level 5 perks "immunity to <a href="./Food%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Food Poisoning">Food Poisoning</a>" and "immunity to <a href="./Intestinal%20Parasites.md" class="wikilink" title="Intestinal Parasites">Intestinal Parasites</a>" when eating non-raw food
- All Food Poisoning chances of food items are doubled. Safe food items with 0% Food Poisoning risk are still safe, though.
- The rate at which the Risk of Intestinal Parasites meter is filled after consuming predator meat is increased by 1.5. One piece gives 1% risk, two pieces 6%, three pieces 13% and so on.

## Strategies

An effective way of combating Food Poisoning and Parasites Risk once this Affliction is acquired is to consume <a href="./Cured%20Meat.md" class="wikilink" title="Cured Meat">Cured Meat</a> and <a href="./Cured%20Fish.md" class="wikilink" title="Cured Fish">Cured Fish</a> from a <a href="./Meat%20Curing%20Box.md" class="wikilink" title="Meat Curing Box">Meat Curing Box</a>. Meat consumed from Meat Curing Boxes does not decay and has no Risk of Intestinal Parasites, though the box will need to be topped off with large amounts of <a href="./Salt.md" class="wikilink" title="Salt">Salt</a> to function. Cured Meat and Cured Fish have no Food Poisoning risk if the meat is cured in high (above 75%) condition.
