A **Sprain Risk** is caused by a survivor's <a href="./Encumbrance.md" class="wikilink" title="Encumbrance">Encumbrance</a> being over carry weight, and can result in potential sprains.

## Risk

|  |  |  |
|----|----|----|
| “ | <span style="font-style:italic">You are Exhausted or Encumbered, and at a higher risk for a Sprain when traversing sloped ground.</span> | ” |

Carrying too much weight beyond carrying capacity or becoming too tired to carry your heavy load will warn the player that they are at risk of acquiring a sprain. Walking off road onto uneven ground (icon shown in the photo) may have a chance of a <a href="./Sprained%20Wrist.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Wrist">Sprained Wrist</a> or <a href="./Sprained%20Ankle.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Ankle">Sprained Ankle</a> ooccurring.

The exact mechanics that result in a sprain are simple: if the player walks across uneven terrain for more than 10 seconds (and the steep slopes icon doesn't disappear), the player will aquire a sprain. <a href="./Mountain%20Goating.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountain Goating">Mountain Goating</a> is not advised when the player has a Sprain Risk, as they will almost certainly sprain themselves when going down sharp terrain.

Consider either resting or lightening your load in order to eliminate the risk, or consume a <a href="./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Go! Energy Drink">Go!
