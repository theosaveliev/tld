A **Sprained Ankle** can be caused by a fall, being exhausted or encumbered while walking on uneven or steep terrain, or from an [animal attack](./Struggle.md).

## Risk

> “**You are Exhausted or Encumbered, and at a higher risk for a Sprain when traversing sloped ground.**”

Carrying too much weight beyond carrying capacity or becoming too tired to carry your heavy load will warn the player that they are at [risk](./Sprain%20Risk.md) of acquiring a sprain. Walking off road onto uneven ground (icon shown in the photo) may have a chance of a [Sprained Wrist](./Sprained%20Wrist.md) or Ankle occurring. Consider either resting or lightening your load in order to eliminate the risk, or consume a [Go! Energy Drink](./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md) or [Emergency Stim](./Emergency%20Stim.md).

## Affliction

> “**You have twisted your ankle and are now suffering a sprain. Your mobility will be reduced until you treat it, or it heals over time.**”
>
> — In-game description

If a player fails to heed a sprain risk or is attacked by hostile wildlife, they can develop a sprain. Acquiring a sprain will always give the [Pain](./Pain.md) Affliction at the same time. While at least one Sprained Ankle affects the player, sprinting is not possible and the player will walk with a limp, with the screen shaking with each step. 2 Sprained Ankles causes the player to slow down even more and for the screen to shake even further.

- A Sprained Ankle is treated by using a [Bandage](./Bandage.md) or sleeping for 4 cumulative hours. It will not pass on its own.
- The associating Pain Affliction can be treated independently with [Painkillers](./Painkillers.md) or [Rose Hip Tea](./Rose%20Hip%20Tea.md); rest will contribute to resolving all sprains and Pain.
- [Rope Climbing](./Rope%20Climbing.md) is not possible with either a Sprained Ankle or Wrist.
- It is possible to sprain both ankles at once, either in the same Struggle event or from continuing to traverse sloped surfaces while over encumbered.

## Trivia

- Equipping a [Revolver](./Revolver.md) will reduce the frequency of screen shakes.
