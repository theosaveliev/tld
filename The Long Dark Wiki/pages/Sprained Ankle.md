A **Sprained Ankle** can be caused by a fall, being exhausted or encumbered while walking on uneven or steep terrain, or from an <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="animal attack">animal attack</a>.

## Risk

|  |  |  |
|----|----|----|
| “ | <span style="font-style:italic">You are Exhausted or Encumbered, and at a higher risk for a Sprain when traversing sloped ground.</span> | ” |

Carrying too much weight beyond carrying capacity or becoming too tired to carry your heavy load will warn the player that they are at <a href="./Sprain%20Risk.md" class="wikilink" title="risk">risk</a> of acquiring a sprain. Walking off road onto uneven ground (icon shown in the photo) may have a chance of a <a href="./Sprained%20Wrist.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Wrist">Sprained Wrist</a> or Ankle occurring. Consider either resting or lightening your load in order to eliminate the risk, or consume a <a href="./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Go! Energy Drink">Go! Energy Drink</a> or <a href="./Emergency%20Stim.md" class="wikilink" title="Emergency Stim">Emergency Stim</a>.

If a player fails to heed a sprain risk or is attacked by hostile wildlife, they can develop a sprain. Acquiring a sprain will always give the <a href="./Pain.md" class="wikilink" title="Pain">Pain</a> Affliction at the same time. While at least one Sprained Ankle affects the player, sprinting is not possible and the player will walk with a limp, with the screen shaking with each step. 2 Sprained Ankles causes the player to slow down even more and for the screen to shake even further.

- A Sprained Ankle is treated by using a <a href="./Bandage.md" class="wikilink" title="Bandage">Bandage</a> or sleeping for 4 cumulative hours. It will not pass on its own.
- The associating Pain Affliction can be treated independently with <a href="./Painkillers.md" class="wikilink" title="Painkillers">Painkillers</a> or <a href="./Rose%20Hip%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Rose Hip Tea">Rose Hip Tea</a>; rest will contribute to resolving all sprains and Pain.
- <a href="./Rope%20Climbing.md" class="wikilink" title="Rope Climbing">Rope Climbing</a> is not possible with either a Sprained Ankle or Wrist.
- It is possible to sprain both ankles at once, either in the same Struggle event or from continuing to traverse sloped surfaces while over encumbered.

## Trivia

- Equipping a <a href="./Revolver.md" class="wikilink" title="Revolver">Revolver</a> will reduce the frequency of screen shakes.
