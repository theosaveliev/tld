A **Sprained Wrist** can be caused by a fall, being exhausted or encumbered while walking on uneven or steep terrain, or from an <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="animal attack">animal attack</a>.

## Risk

|  |  |  |
|----|----|----|
| “ | <span style="font-style:italic">You are Exhausted or Encumbered, and at a higher risk for a Sprain when traversing sloped ground.</span> | ” |

Carrying too much weight beyond carrying capacity or becoming too tired to carry your heavy load will warn the player that they are at <a href="./Sprain%20Risk.md" class="wikilink" title="risk">risk</a> of acquiring a sprain. Walking off road onto uneven ground (icon shown in the photo) may have a chance of a <a href="./Sprained%20Ankle.md" class="wikilink" title="Sprained Ankle">Sprained Ankle</a> or Wrist occurring. Consider either resting or lightening your load in order to eliminate the risk, or consume a <a href="./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Go! Energy Drink">Go! Energy Drink</a> or <a href="./Emergency%20Stim.md" class="wikilink" title="Emergency Stim">Emergency Stim</a>.

If a player fails to heed a Sprain Risk or is attacked by hostile wildlife, they can develop a sprain. Acquiring a sprain will always give the <a href="./Pain.md" class="wikilink" title="Pain">Pain</a> Affliction at the same time.

While at least 1 Sprained Wrist affects the player:

- Equipping a <a href="./Bear%20Spear.md" class="wikilink" title="Bear Spear">Bear Spear</a> or any variant of the <a href="./Hunting%20Rifle.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Rifle">Hunting Rifle</a> or <a href="./Survival%20Bow.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Bow">Survival Bow</a> is not possible since these are considered two-handed weapons.
- All <a href="./Revolver.md" class="wikilink" title="Revolver">Revolver</a> variants, <a href="./Distress%20Pistol.md" class="wikilink" title="Distress Pistol">Distress Pistol</a>, and <a href="./Stone.md" class="wikilink" title="Stone">Stones</a> are considered one-handed weapons and can be used with 1 Sprained Wrist.

With 2 Sprained Wrists, all Weapons can no longer be used, but lighting tools such as <a href="./Flare.md" class="wikilink" title="Flares">Flares</a>, <a href="./Marine%20Flare.md" class="wikilink" title="Marine Flares">Marine Flares</a>, <a href="./Storm%20Lantern.md" class="wikilink" title="Storm Lanterns">Storm Lanterns</a>, <a href="./Torch.md" class="wikilink" title="Torches">Torches</a>, and <a href="./Flashlight.md" class="wikilink" title="Flashlights">Flashlights</a> can.

- A Sprained Wrist is treated by using a <a href="./Bandage.md" class="wikilink" title="Bandage">Bandage</a> or sleeping for 4 cumulative hours. It will not pass on its own.
- The associating Pain Affliction can be treated independently with <a href="./Painkillers.md" class="wikilink" title="Painkillers">Painkillers</a> or <a href="./Rose%20Hip%20Tea.md" class="wikilink" title="Rose Hip Tea">Rose Hip Tea</a>; rest will contribute to resolving all sprains and Pain.
- <a href="./Rope%20Climbing.md" class="wikilink" title="Rope Climbing">Rope Climbing</a> is not possible with either a Sprained Wrist or Ankle.
- It is possible to sprain both wrists at once, either in the same Struggle event or from continuing to traverse sloped surfaces while over encumbered.
