A **Sprained Wrist** can be caused by a fall, being exhausted or encumbered while walking on uneven or steep terrain, or from an [animal attack](./Struggle.md).

## Risk

> “**You are Exhausted or Encumbered, and at a higher risk for a Sprain when traversing sloped ground.**”

Carrying too much weight beyond carrying capacity or becoming too tired to carry your heavy load will warn the player that they are at [risk](./Sprain%20Risk.md) of acquiring a sprain. Walking off road onto uneven ground (icon shown in the photo) may have a chance of a [Sprained Ankle](./Sprained%20Ankle.md) or Wrist occurring. Consider either resting or lightening your load in order to eliminate the risk, or consume a [Go! Energy Drink](./Go!%20Energy%20Drink.md) or [Emergency Stim](./Emergency%20Stim.md).

## Affliction

> “**You have sprained your wrist. You will not be able to hold weapons until you treat it, or it heals over time.**”
>
> — In-game description

If a player fails to heed a Sprain Risk or is attacked by hostile wildlife, they can develop a sprain. Acquiring a sprain will always give the [Pain](./Pain.md) Affliction at the same time.

While at least 1 Sprained Wrist affects the player:

- Equipping a [Bear Spear](./Bear%20Spear.md) or any variant of the [Hunting Rifle](./Hunting%20Rifle.md) or [Survival Bow](./Survival%20Bow.md) is not possible since these are considered two-handed weapons.
- All [Revolver](./Revolver.md) variants, [Distress Pistol](./Distress%20Pistol.md), and [Stones](./Stone.md) are considered one-handed weapons and can be used with 1 Sprained Wrist.

With 2 Sprained Wrists, all Weapons can no longer be used, but lighting tools such as [Flares](./Flare.md), [Marine Flares](./Marine%20Flare.md), [Storm Lanterns](./Storm%20Lantern.md), [Torches](./Torch.md), and [Flashlights](./Flashlight.md) can.

- A Sprained Wrist is treated by using a [Bandage](./Bandage.md) or sleeping for 4 cumulative hours. It will not pass on its own.
- The associating Pain Affliction can be treated independently with [Painkillers](./Painkillers.md) or [Rose Hip Tea](./Rose%20Hip%20Tea.md); rest will contribute to resolving all sprains and Pain.
- [Rope Climbing](./Rope%20Climbing.md) is not possible with either a Sprained Wrist or Ankle.
- It is possible to sprain both wrists at once, either in the same Struggle event or from continuing to traverse sloped surfaces while over encumbered.
