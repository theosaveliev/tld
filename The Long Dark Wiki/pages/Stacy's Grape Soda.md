**Stacy's Grape Soda** is a packaged <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="food">food</a> item in <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark"><em>The Long Dark</em></a>.

## Overview

It can be consumed to restore 250 <a href="./Hunger.md" class="wikilink" title="calories">calories</a> as well as 30% <a href="./Thirst.md" class="wikilink" title="hydration">hydration</a>, the equivalent of 0.20 litres of <a href="./Water%20%28Potable%29.md" class="wikilink" title="water">water</a>. They are found uncommonly inside all types of structures, <a href="./Vehicle.md" class="wikilink" title="Vehicles">Vehicles</a>, and on <a href="./Frozen%20Corpse.md" class="wikilink" title="frozen corpses">frozen corpses</a>.

All three types of soda share the same calories and hydration. Stacy's Grape Soda never causes <a href="./Food%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Food Poisoning">Food Poisoning</a>.

## Trivia

- "Stacy's Grape Soda" was named after [Stacyplays](https://www.youtube.com/user/stacyplays) (aka Stacy Hinojosa), a YouTuber who ran a "let's play" series during The Long Dark's alpha/beta (2014-2016). In her videos she mentions how grape soda is her favorite type of drink. Her photograph is also present hanging inside of some houses in Coastal Highway.
- The back of a Grape Soda lists "Ingredients: Carbonated Water, High Fructose Corn Syrup, Natural Flavours, Blue 1, Red 40."
