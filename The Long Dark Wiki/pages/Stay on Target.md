**Stay on Target** is a <a href="./Research%20Book.md" class="wikilink" title="Research Book">Research Book</a> used to improve the <a href="./Archery.md" class="wikilink" title="Archery">Archery</a> skill. It can also be used as Fuel when starting a <a href="./Fire.md" class="wikilink" title="Fire">Fire</a>.

## Research

*Stay on Target* can be researched for 5 hours that can be broken into multiple 1-hour minimum research sessions. 10 <a href="./Archery.md#Skill_levels" class="wikilink" title="Archery skill points">Archery skill points</a> are awarded on completing the research. By selecting the Settled Mind <a href="./Feats.md" class="wikilink" title="Feat">Feat</a>, research time will be reduced by 20%, putting it at 4 hours.

Research can only be performed under the following conditions:

- There is light present to read
- <a href="./Warmth.md" class="wikilink" title="Warmth">Warmth</a>, <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a>, <a href="./Thirst.md" class="wikilink" title="Thirst">Thirst</a>, and <a href="./Hunger.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunger">Hunger</a> exceed 0%
- No negative <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Afflictions">Afflictions</a>

If the player's fatigue, thirst or hunger meters go down to zero or if the environment becomes dark during a research session, the session will be terminated instantly. The hour of research that was in progress will not be completed and any time that had already been spent will be wasted. For a multiple-hour research session, only the incomplete hour will be lost, all the previous hours will still count towards completion of the book.

#### Desolation Point

- <a href="./Hibernia%20Processing.md" class="wikilink" title="Hibernia Processing">Hibernia Processing</a>

#### Hushed River Valley

- <a href="./Lonely%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Lonely Cave">Lonely Cave</a>

#### Mystery Lake

- <a href="./Lake%20Overlook.md" class="wikilink" title="Lake Overlook">Lake Overlook</a> Cave

#### Pleasant Valley

- Inside the Barn at <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley%20Outbuildings.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley Outbuildings">Pleasant Valley Outbuildings</a>
- <a href="./High%20Blind.md" class="wikilink" title="High Blind">High Blind</a> at <a href="./Hilltop%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Hilltop Cave">Hilltop Cave</a>

#### Winding River

- Outside the broken window entrance to the <a href="./Carter%20Hydro%20Dam.md" class="wikilink" title="Carter Hydro Dam">Carter Hydro Dam</a>, often near a backpack and a <a href="./Deer%20Carcass.md" class="wikilink" title="Deer Carcass">Deer Carcass</a>

#### Zone of Contamination

- Makeshift cafeteria/mess hall inside the <a href="./Langston%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Langston Mine">Langston Mine</a>, near the corpse where the duplicate <a href="./Sport%20Bow.md" class="wikilink" title="Sport Bow">Sport Bow</a> can be found

*Stay on Target* will also spawn inside the Hunting variant of the <a href="./Prepper%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper Cache">Prepper Cache</a> as well as inside <a href="./Cargo%20Container.md" class="wikilink" title="Cargo Containers">Cargo Containers</a> found in <a href="./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolf Mountain">Timberwolf Mountain</a>.

## Trivia

- Attempting to burn a copy of *Stay on Target* when it has not been fully read will give a confirmation warning before it can be burned.
- Once a copy of *Stay on Target* (or any research book) is fully researched, it will no longer appear in the Tools section of the Backpack.
