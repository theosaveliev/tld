<p><strong>Steely Chasm</strong> is a steel-beam bridge over a rapidly flowing river in the <a href="./Far%20Range%20Branch%20Line.md" class="wikilink" title="Far Range Branch Line">Far Range Branch Line</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Unlike many rivers in the game, this river is animated to look somewhat turbulent and violent. A rabbit grove can be found across it, as well as some low-quality shelter in the form of a number of railway carts as well as a cave. In order to exit the Far Range Branch region into Transfer Pass, one only needs to find the tunnel connecting to it, always following the tracks.</p>
<p>Invisible barriers prevent the player from falling into the river below, an uncommon feature near most cliffs. This prevents the player from falling to their death.</p>
