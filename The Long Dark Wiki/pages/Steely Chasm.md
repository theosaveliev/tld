# Steely Chasm

> {{Infobox/Location|title=Steely Chasm|region=Far Range Branch Line|description=A steel trestle bridging a river|shelter=no|image=Steely chasm.png|map=Steely Chasm Location.png}}
> 
> '''Steely Chasm''' is a steel-beam bridge over a rapidly flowing river in the [[Far Range Branch Line]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> Unlike many rivers in the game, this river is animated to look somewhat turbulent and violent. A rabbit grove can be found across it, as well as some low-quality shelter in the form of a number of railway carts as well as a cave. In order to exit the Far Range Branch region into Transfer Pass, one only needs to find the tunnel connecting to it, always following the tracks.
> 
> Invisible barriers prevent the player from falling into the river below, an uncommon feature near most cliffs. This prevents the player from falling to their death.
