> '''Sudden Washout''' is a cave in [[Forsaken Airfield]].
> 
> ==Overview==
> The cave is located to the western most section of the map, south of [[Mindful Cabin]], and directly west of [[Final Approach]] in the mountains (past another [[Warm Cave]]).
> 
> The cave itself is very easy to miss, as its entrance is located in a depression, likely created by heavy rainfall -- hence the name. The cave is not far from a [[Rope Climbing]] section that leads down to the main plain. A few [[Rose Hip]] bushes may be found growing near the entrance, and [[Coal]] can be found inside, along with assorted low-tier loot.
