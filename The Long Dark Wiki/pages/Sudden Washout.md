Provides some shelter<br>**Sudden Washout** is a cave in [Forsaken Airfield](./Forsaken%20Airfield.md).

## Overview

The cave is located to the western most section of the map, south of [Mindful Cabin](./Mindful%20Cabin.md), and directly west of [Final Approach](./Final%20Approach.md) in the mountains (past another [Warm Cave](./Warm%20Cave.md)).

The cave itself is very easy to miss, as its entrance is located in a depression, likely created by heavy rainfall -- hence the name. The cave is not far from a [Rope Climbing](./Rope%20Climbing.md) section that leads down to the main plain. A few [Rose Hip](./Rose%20Hip.md) bushes may be found growing near the entrance, and [Coal](./Coal.md) can be found inside, along with assorted low-tier loot.
