<p><strong>Sudden Washout</strong> is a cave in <a href="./Forsaken%20Airfield.md" class="wikilink" title="Forsaken Airfield">Forsaken Airfield</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>The cave is located to the western most section of the map, south of <a href="./Mindful%20Cabin.md" class="wikilink" title="Mindful Cabin">Mindful Cabin</a>, and directly west of <a href="./Final%20Approach.md" class="wikilink" title="Final Approach">Final Approach</a> in the mountains (past another <a href="./Warm%20Cave.md" class="wikilink" title="Warm Cave">Warm Cave</a>).</p>
<p>The cave itself is very easy to miss, as its entrance is located in a depression, likely created by heavy rainfall -- hence the name. The cave is not far from a <a href="./Rope%20Climbing.md" class="wikilink" title="Rope Climbing">Rope Climbing</a> section that leads down to the main plain. A few <a href="./Rose%20Hip.md" class="wikilink" title="Rose Hip">Rose Hip</a> bushes may be found growing near the entrance, and <a href="./Coal.md" class="wikilink" title="Coal">Coal</a> can be found inside, along with assorted low-tier loot.</p>
