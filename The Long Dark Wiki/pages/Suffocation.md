**Suffocation** is a very short but highly lethal <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> that occurs when the player character is exposed to toxic airborne gases. It is a rare but incredibly dangerous Affliction to encounter in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a>, being present only within the <a href="./Last%20Prospect.md" class="wikilink" title="Last Prospect">Last Prospect</a> mine and the <a href="./Langston%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Langston Mine">Langston Mine</a>.

It is also present in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>, specifically <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, then Silence</a>, where it can be encountered in three separate locations. Several areas of the Last Prospect mine, <a href="./Donner.md" class="wikilink" title="one smoke filled section">one smoke filled section</a> of <a href="./Blackrock%20Prison.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Prison">Blackrock Prison</a>, and the destroyed <a href="./Steam%20Tunnels.md" class="wikilink" title="Steam Tunnels">Steam Tunnels</a> near the end of the episode.

## Overview

|  |  |  |
|----|----|----|

Unlike most other Afflictions, Suffocation Risk does not build up over time, instead being replaced with a timer in the bottom left corner of the screen that counts down in real seconds as long as the player character remains within a toxic cloud's area of effect. The player's vision will also begin to blur as the player character begins coughing and tearing up from breathing in the gas, along with a noticeable loss of hearing.

A <a href="./Respirator.md" class="wikilink" title="Respirator">Respirator</a> with sufficient <a href="./Canister.md" class="wikilink" title="Canisters">Canisters</a> may also be equipped to prevent Suffocation, but once the Condition of air Canisters degrades, the player will begin to suffocate again if they run out of replacements.

The maximum amount of time in the timer changes depending on difficulty level selected in Survival Mode or Wintermute. This timer ranges from 30-50 seconds (up to 60 seconds in Wintermute), and will appear even when in the <a href="./Survival%20Menu.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Menu">Survival Menu</a> to remind the player how much time is left.

## Affliction

Once the timer reaches 0, <u>**the player will die instantly**</u>, regardless of how much <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> they have left (similar to falling from an extreme height). Reaching an area with clean air will reset the countdown and slowly restore the player character's vision and hearing. Forcing clean air into the gas clouds using nearby machinery, such as in the Last Prospect mines in Episode 4 with a <a href="./Valve%20Socket%20Handle.md" class="wikilink" title="Valve Socket Handle">Valve Socket Handle</a> (Wintermute Only), will also reset the countdown.

Time-accelerated activities while within a suffocation cloud , such as passing time, sleeping, or crafting, will instantly kill the player.

In Survival Mode, the only way to continue your game after acquiring Suffocation is to <a href="./Cheat%20Death.md" class="wikilink" title="Cheat Death">Cheat Death</a>, but not without suffering the consequences...

## Notes

- Suffocation was introduced in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4">Episode 4</a> of the Wintermute story mode campaign. It was later added to Survival Mode in the Perilous Constraint update. Further more, it was reintroduced in the Langston Mine in the Buried Echoes update in the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a>.
- It is impossible to burn toxic mineshaft gas up with any fire source, such as <a href="./Campfire.md" class="wikilink" title="campfires">campfires</a>, <a href="./Noisemaker.md" class="wikilink" title="noisemakers">noisemakers</a>, <a href="./Torch.md" class="wikilink" title="torches">torches</a>, <a href="./Wood%20Matches.md" class="wikilink" title="matches">matches</a>, or gunfire, as the gas is not flammable.
