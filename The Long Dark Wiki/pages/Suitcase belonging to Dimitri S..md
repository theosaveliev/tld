**Suitcase belonging to Dimitri S.** is a unique brown <a href="./Suitcase.md" class="wikilink" title="Suitcase">Suitcase</a> only found in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a> as part of the <a href="./Fallen%20Star.md" class="wikilink" title="Fallen Star">Fallen Star</a> mission. It placed next to Gwen, a <a href="./Crash%20Survivor.md" class="wikilink" title="Crash Survivor">Crash Survivor</a>, at the <a href="./Crash%20Site%20%28Pleasant%20Valley%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)">Crash Site (Pleasant Valley)</a>. <a href="./Astrid%20Greenwood.md" class="wikilink" title="Astrid Greenwood">Astrid Greenwood</a> ventures to the crash site to find <a href="./Insulin.md" class="wikilink" title="Insulin">Insulin</a> to cure Dimitri's <a href="./Hyperglycemic%20Risk.md" class="wikilink" title="Hyperglycemic Risk">Hyperglycemic Risk</a>.

## Loot

The suitcase contains the Insulin needed to heal Dimitri.
