Its edge is serrated, allowing it to do more damage in a [Struggle](./Struggle.md) while also allowing it to perform [Carcass Harvesting](./Carcass%20Harvesting.md) faster than a normal knife. The tradeoff is that it is a little bit heavier than a normal Hunting Knife, but an added bonus of this knife is its increased durability, making it a must have for long term survival games. / Knives have number of uses: harvesting gut, hide and meat from [animal](./Wildlife.md) carcasses, as well as [Cloth](./Cloth.md) and [Tinder Plugs](./Tinder%20Plug.md). It may also serve as a makeshift [Can Opener](./Can%20Opener.md) to avoid spillage, or to break through iced-over fishing holes for [Ice Fishing](./Ice%20Fishing.md), and as a self-defense weapon during wolf attacks. / It is not obtainable in [Interloper](./Interloper.md). / ## Location / It can appear as a potential trade when trading with the [Trader](./Trader.md), Sutherland, after completing Special Request #3. It is possible for the Trader to offer multiple Survival Knives across trades. / ## Differences from the Hunting Knife / The Survival Knife is by far the best Knife in the game, but the Cougar Claw Knife still bests it in some categories. / - More damage than the [Hunting Knife](./Hunting%20Knife.md) in a [Struggle](./Struggle.md) (damage values are in-between a Hunting Knife and [Cougar Claw Knife](./Cougar%20Claw%20Knife.md)). / - It has the fastest frozen meat harvesting speed of any knife. / - Other harvesting speeds are the same as the hunting knife. / - / - Twice as much durability. / - 0.25 Kg heavier (same as the [Improvised Knife](./Improvised%20Knife.md)). / - Has the same crafting times as Hunting Knife. / ## Harvesting / Harvesting the knife gives 1 [Scrap Metal](./Scrap%20Metal.md) after 45 minutes. / ## Repairing / The knife can be repaired using a [Whetstone](./Whetstone.md) and the [Sharpening](./Sharpening.md) skill, restoring 3% condition to the tool and using 5% condition on the Whetstone.
