**Survival School** was the third mission of the Predux <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story. It has since been replaced after the V1.47 Wintermute Redux version of the game, but is still playable to those that download versions V1.15, V1.30, or V1.37 of Steam version of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>* by using the <a href="./Time%20Capsule.md" class="wikilink" title="Time Capsule">Time Capsule</a>.

Due to its overwhelmingly negative impact on breaking the pace of the game (especially for players already familiar with Ice Fishing, Hunting, Mending, and Foraging/players whom have already played Episode 2 before) and being able to do only one task at a time, this mission has since been replaced by the optional side missions <a href="./Survival%20School.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival School">Survival School</a> and <a href="./The%20Basics%20of%20Survival.md" class="wikilink" title="The Basics of Survival">The Basics of Survival</a>. The main mission that filled this spot of the story was instead replaced by <a href="./Signal%20to%20Noise.md" class="wikilink" title="Signal to Noise">Signal to Noise</a>.

## Objectives

- Learn to craft Fishing Tackle, then catch fish from Mystery Lake.
  - Get to the Camp Office and find the fishing gear you need.
  - \[0/5.00 KG of fish\] Place fish meat in the container at Jeremiah's Cabin.
- Learn to find, track, shoot, and butcher a Deer.
  - Find the hunting grounds Jeremiah mentioned.
  - \[0/10.00 KG\] Place the harvested deer meat in the container at Jeremiah's Cabin.
- Learn to mend and maintain clothing. It'll save your life some day.
  - Find the Lookout tower, where Jeremiah left some skins drying.
  - Repair Jeremiah's coat.
  - Return the mended coat to Jeremiah.
- Learn to identify and harvest some of the edible, medicinal flora native to Great Bear Island. (Place in the container at Jeremiah's Cabin)
  - \[0/8\] Reishi Mushrooms
  - \[0/8\] Old Man's Beard
  - \[0/8\] Rosehip
  - \[0/4\] Cattail Stalks
- Return to Jeremiah.

## Walkthrough

Any of the 4 mission tasks can be taken and completed in any order desired, but they must all be finished in order to get to the next mission <a href="./Jeremiah%27s%20Folly.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s Folly">Jeremiah's Folly</a>. Only one School of Survival can be active at one time.

### Fishing

Selecting this option will task Mackenzie to head to the <a href="./Camp%20Office.md" class="wikilink" title="Camp Office">Camp Office</a> to craft a <a href="./Fishing%20Tackle.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Tackle">Fishing Tackle</a> and catch fish from the <a href="./Ice%20Fishing%20Huts.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing Huts">Ice Fishing Huts</a> at <a href="./Mystery%20Lake%20%28location%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Mystery Lake">Mystery Lake</a>. Mackenzie realizes that this is more of a chore for Jeremiah to put fish in his belly than to teach him anything, but Jeremiah points out that with his wounds he won't be able to hunt or trap for the rest of the season, so this helps them both out.

The Fishing Tackle blueprint will unlock, so head to the Camp Office and craft a tackle. Then start <a href="./Ice%20Fishing.md" class="wikilink" title="Ice Fishing">Ice Fishing</a> when you walk out to the lake and into a Fishing Hut. Break the ice in one of the huts and gather the specified amount of fish in terms of weight, then return to the Homestead and place them inside of the <a href="./Supply%20Bin.md" class="wikilink" title="Supply Bin">Supply Bin</a> next to the door.

### Hunting

Jeremiah will instruct Mackenzie to head to a clearing over the hill to the <a href="./Unnamed%20Pond.md" class="wikilink" title="Unnamed Pond">Unnamed Pond</a> and to kill a stag. His orders the pilot to shoot and kill a Deer, then bring its meat back to the cabin. If Mackenzie gets a hit but doesn't immediately kill the beast, Jeremiah's advice is to track the blood in the snow as the Deer bleeds out, then harvest the carcass. Jeremiah will then hand you 5 <a href="./Rifle%20Ammunition.md" class="wikilink" title="Rifle Ammunition">Rifle Ammunition</a> and send you on your way. Be aware that Jeremiah will NOT give you more ammo if you run out.

Clear as much from your inventory as you can, as you will need the extra storage space soon. Hunt during the morning or day to maximize your visual scanning of the area. Head forward over the hill the moment you step out of Trapper's Homestead until you find the Unnamed Pond, then crouch, get close, take aim, and kill a Deer. Harvest all the <a href="./Venison%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Venison (Raw)">Venison (Raw)</a> from its body and return to the cabin, then place the meat in the Supply Bin. You may need to kill multiple deer to accomplish this task.

### Mending

Jeremiah will explain to Mackenzie the importance of wearing good clothing in the Winter. Windproof and Wetproof clothing on the outer layers, while wearing warm clothing on the inside. Eventually, Jeremiah will give Mackenzie his old park, and will ask him to head to the nearby <a href="./Lookout%20%28Mystery%20Lake%20location%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Forestry Lookout">Forestry Lookout</a> to retrieve some skins Jeremiah left up there as sewing supplies to fix the "parka" (The Parka in question is his damaged <a href="./Bearskin%20Coat.md" class="wikilink" title="Bearskin Coat">Bearskin Coat</a> from the Old Bear's attack, it weighs 5.00 kg, and is found in the Clothing section of the Backpack). Simply make your way there, grab the <a href="./Cured%20Black%20Bear%20Hide.md" class="wikilink" title="Cured Black Bear Hide">Cured Black Bear Hide</a>, use a <a href="./Sewing%20Kit.md" class="wikilink" title="Sewing Kit">Sewing Kit</a> or <a href="./Fishing%20Tackle.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Tackle">Fishing Tackle</a> to repair <a href="./Jeremiah%27s%20Bearskin%20Coat.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s Bearskin Coat">Jeremiah's Bearskin Coat</a>, then return to the Trapper's Homestead. FYI, you can't wear his coat at any point after receiving it, nor can you harvest it (however you can drop it, but don't lose it). After repairing the coat, talk to Jeremiah and select the dialogue option Story to give it back to him, finishing the task.

### Foraging

Jeremiah will briefly explain much of the flora of Great Bear Island, mentioning berries, nuts, and so forth, but then skipping to what mainly grows in the Winter. He will focus on 4 main types of flora; <a href="./Cat%20Tail.md" class="wikilink" title="Cat Tail">Cat Tail</a>, <a href="./Rose%20Hip.md" class="wikilink" title="Rose Hip">Rose Hip</a>, <a href="./Reishi%20Mushroom.md" class="wikilink" title="Reishi Mushroom">Reishi Mushroom</a>, and <a href="./Old%20Man%27s%20Beard%20Lichen.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Man&#39;s Beard Lichen">Old Man's Beard Lichen</a>. You will then be given a list of these items, how many to find, and you will need to place them inside Jeremiah's Supply Bin outside the Homestead. Many ingredients can be found in the rabbit grove east of the door to the Homestead when stepping outside.

When it comes to finding the items needed:

- Old Man's Beard Lichen can be found hanging from trees and is very easy to spot.
- Reishi Mushrooms grow on tree stumps.
- Cat Tails grow near fresh water streams. Check the <a href="./Unnamed%20Pond.md" class="wikilink" title="Unnamed Pond">Unnamed Pond</a>, <a href="./Frozen%20Creek.md" class="wikilink" title="Frozen Creek">Frozen Creek</a>, or <a href="./Mystery%20Lake%20%28location%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Mystery Lake (location)">Mystery Lake (location)</a>.
- Rose Hips are red berry looking plants on small rose bushes.

After completing all 4 Schools, return to Jeremiah and a cutscene begins where Mackenzie thanks Jeremiah for the training. After going through all that toil, Jeremiah tosses Mackenzie <a href="./Jeremiah%27s%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="his knife">his knife</a> as a graduation gift, saying he has a feeling that the pilot might need it, and the next mission starts.

## Differences from Redux Episode 2

Several plot points and locations have been replaced/altered since the Redux re-release of Episode 2:

### Plot Points and Gameplay Changes

- This entire mission was cut from the game due to very poor reception, and was turned into a two optional side mission in Redux, with all the tutorials being recycled into the side missions <a href="./The%20Basics%20of%20Survival.md" class="wikilink" title="The Basics of Survival">The Basics of Survival</a> and <a href="./Survival%20School.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival School">Survival School</a>.
  - Fishing was recycled into <a href="./The%20Frozen%20Angler%20%28Survival%20School%29.md" class="wikilink" title="The Frozen Angler">The Frozen Angler</a> in Episode 2's Survival School.
  - Hunting was recycled into <a href="./Frontier%20Shooting%20Guide%20%28Survival%20School%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Frontier Shooting Guide">Frontier Shooting Guide</a> and <a href="./Advanced%20Guns%20Guns%20Guns!%20%28Survival%20School%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Advanced Guns Guns Guns!">Advanced Guns Guns Guns!</a> in Episode 2's Survival School.
  - Mending was recycled into <a href="./A%20Sewing%20Primer%20%28The%20Basics%20of%20Survival%29.md" class="wikilink" title="A Sewing Primer">A Sewing Primer</a> in Episode 1's The Basics of Survival.
  - Harvestable Plants was recycled into <a href="./Medicinal%20Plants%20of%20Great%20Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Medicinal Plants of Great Bear">Medicinal Plants of Great Bear</a> in Episode 1's The Basics of Survival.
  - <a href="./Jeremiah%27s%20Bearskin%20Coat.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s Bearskin Coat">Jeremiah's Bearskin Coat</a> was cut from the game.
- Jeremiah mentions not having anything electronic in his cabin that could be affected by the Aurora, feigning ignorance that the Aurora isn't as strong as Mackenzie makes it out to be. It would later be revealed that he has a shortwave radio hidden in the floorboards of his cabin that he uses to speak with his mysterious correspondent. This was changed in Redux, as the shortwave radio is now a central part of the plot, as Mackenzie is required to find multiple parts to fix it on many occasions (<a href="./Radio%20Parts.md" class="wikilink" title="Radio Parts">Radio Parts</a>, <a href="./Transponder%20Parts.md" class="wikilink" title="Transponder Parts">Transponder Parts</a>).
  - This section of Episode 2's story was also replaced by <a href="./Signal%20to%20Noise.md" class="wikilink" title="Signal to Noise">Signal to Noise</a>.
- Jeremiah rewards Mackenzie with his unique Hunting Knife, <a href="./Jeremiah%27s%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s Knife">Jeremiah's Knife</a>, after graduating from his Survival School. It serves as a reskinned <a href="./Hunting%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Knife">Hunting Knife</a>, with identical stats. This item was cut from the game, and regular Hunting Knives can be found more often throughout Episode 2 in the Redux.
