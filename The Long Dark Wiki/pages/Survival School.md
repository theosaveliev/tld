**Survival School**is a side mission in [Episode 2](./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md) of the [story](./Wintermute.md).

## Overview

The mission can be started by finding any of the three quest-related books.

Finding each book will reveal another task to complete. All three tasks must be revealed and completed in order to complete the mission.

## Objectives

- Learn to craft [Fishing Tackle](./Fishing%20Tackle.md), and catch fish from Mystery Lake (_[The Frozen Angler](./The%20Frozen%20Angler.md)_)
- Preserve nature's balance in nearby areas by reducing the [wolf](./Wolf.md) population (_[Advanced: Guns Guns Guns!](./Advanced%20Guns%20Guns%20Guns!%20%28Survival%20School%29.md)_)
  - Cull a wolf in the [Mystery Lake](./Mystery%20Lake%20%28disambiguation%29.md) region.
  - Cull a wolf in the [Forlorn Muskeg](./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md) region.
  - Cull a wolf in the [Broken Railroad](./Broken%20Railroad.md) region.
- Learn to [hunt](./Hunting.md) and kill a [deer](./Deer.md) (_[Frontier Shooting Guide](./Frontier%20Shooting%20Guide%20%28Survival%20School%29.md)_)
- Work through the survival school lessons
  - Once the other three tasks for this mission are completed, this task will automatically be completed as well and the mission will succeed.

## Location of the books

- _Frontier Shooting Guide_ can be found in the hunter's blind near [Unnamed Pond](./Unnamed%20Pond.md) in [Mystery Lake](./Mystery%20Lake%20%28disambiguation%29.md).
- _Advanced: Guns Guns Guns!_ can be found in the [Poacher's Camp](./Poacher%27s%20Camp.md) in [Forlorn Muskeg](./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md).
- _The Frozen Angler_ can be found in the [Camp Office](./Camp%20Office.md) on [Mystery Lake](./Mystery%20Lake%20%28disambiguation%29.md).

## Rewards

- [Deerskin Boots](./Deerskin%20Boots.md) and [Deerskin Pants](./Deerskin%20Pants.md) blueprints, awarded after completing the "Hunt a stag" sub-objective.
- [Wolfskin Coat](./Wolfskin%20Coat.md) blueprint, awarded after completing the "Preserve nature's balance" sub-objective.
