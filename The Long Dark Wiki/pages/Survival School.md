**Survival School **is a side mission in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="story">story</a>.

## Overview

The mission can be started by finding any of the three quest-related books.

Finding each book will reveal another task to complete. All three tasks must be revealed and completed in order to complete the mission. 

## Objectives

- Learn to craft <a href="./Fishing%20Tackle.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Tackle">Fishing Tackle</a>, and catch fish from Mystery Lake (*<a href="./The%20Frozen%20Angler.md" class="wikilink" title="The Frozen Angler">The Frozen Angler</a>*)
- Preserve nature's balance in nearby areas by reducing the <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="wolf">wolf</a> population (*<a href="./Advanced%20Guns%20Guns%20Guns!%20%28Survival%20School%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Advanced: Guns Guns Guns!">Advanced: Guns Guns Guns!</a>*)
  - Cull a wolf in the <a href="./Mystery%20Lake%20%28disambiguation%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Mystery Lake">Mystery Lake</a> region.
  - Cull a wolf in the <a href="./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md" class="wikilink" title="Forlorn Muskeg">Forlorn Muskeg</a> region.
  - Cull a wolf in the <a href="./Broken%20Railroad.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Railroad">Broken Railroad</a> region.
- Learn to <a href="./Hunting.md" class="wikilink" title="hunt">hunt</a> and kill a <a href="./Deer.md" class="wikilink" title="deer">deer</a> (*<a href="./Frontier%20Shooting%20Guide%20%28Survival%20School%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Frontier Shooting Guide">Frontier Shooting Guide</a>*)
- Work through the survival school lessons
  - Once the other three tasks for this mission are completed, this task will automatically be completed as well and the mission will succeed.

## Location of the books

- *Frontier Shooting Guide* can be found in the hunter's blind near <a href="./Unnamed%20Pond.md" class="wikilink" title="Unnamed Pond">Unnamed Pond</a> in <a href="./Mystery%20Lake%20%28disambiguation%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Mystery Lake">Mystery Lake</a>.
- *Advanced: Guns Guns Guns!* can be found in the <a href="./Poacher%27s%20Camp.md" class="wikilink" title="Poacher&#39;s Camp">Poacher's Camp</a> in <a href="./Forlorn%20Muskeg.md" class="wikilink" title="Forlorn Muskeg">Forlorn Muskeg</a>.
- *The Frozen Angler* can be found in the <a href="./Camp%20Office.md" class="wikilink" title="Camp Office">Camp Office</a> on <a href="./Mystery%20Lake%20%28disambiguation%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Mystery Lake">Mystery Lake</a>.

## Rewards

- <a href="./Deerskin%20Boots.md" class="wikilink" title="Deerskin Boots">Deerskin Boots</a> and <a href="./Deerskin%20Pants.md" class="wikilink" title="Deerskin Pants">Deerskin Pants</a> blueprints, awarded after completing the "Hunt a stag" sub-objective.
- <a href="./Wolfskin%20Coat.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolfskin Coat">Wolfskin Coat</a> blueprint, awarded after completing the "Preserve nature's balance" sub-objective.
