The **T-Shirt (CM)** is the name given to this unique variation of the [T-Shirt](./T-Shirt.md) that can be acquired from the [Trader](./Trader.md), and is a type of [Clothing](./Clothing.md) item in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

<span id="overview"></span><span id="Overview"></span>

## Overview

The T-shirt is the worst inner torso clothing in the game. This is more rare than the regular [T-Shirt](./T-Shirt.md), due to how it is acquired from the Trader. It offers a small amount of warmth, and nothing else.
