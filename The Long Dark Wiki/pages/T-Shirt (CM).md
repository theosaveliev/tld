The **T-Shirt (CM)** is the name given to this unique variation of the <a href="./T-Shirt.md" class="wikilink" title="T-Shirt">T-Shirt</a> that can be acquired from the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a>, and is a type of <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> item in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Overview

The T-shirt is the worst inner torso clothing in the game. This is more rare than the regular <a href="./T-Shirt.md" class="wikilink" title="T-Shirt">T-Shirt</a>, due to how it is acquired from the Trader. It offers a small amount of warmth, and nothing else.
