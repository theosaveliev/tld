<td><p>|}<strong>Tales</strong> are narrative-based objectives in the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> DLC for <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a>.</p>
<p>They are not to be confused with the short, self-contained <a href="./Challenge.md" class="wikilink" title="Challenge">Challenge</a> scenarios that can be selected in the Survival menu.</p>
<p>Triggering a Tale within Survival Mode will cause a new submenu in the <a href="./Survival%20Menu.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Menu">Survival Menu</a> to appear (flag shaped tab). Similar to the mission objectives menu in <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>, this menu keeps track of all current tasks related to a Tale.</p>
<p>During the Course of the update, a total of 3 Tales were added to the game. A bonus 4th Tale was added in Part 6, but has its own method to track objectives.</p>
<p>As of update V2.27, all Tales become active and can be completed out of order once the <a href="./Handheld%20Shortwave%20Radio.md" class="wikilink" title="Handheld Shortwave Radio">Handheld Shortwave Radio</a> is acquired in a playthrough, as long that Tale has been previously completed on another playthrough and its associated Badge is acquired. This requires each Tale to be completed in the original order at least once first, with the exception of <a href="./Sutherland%27s%20Tale.md" class="wikilink" title="Sutherland&#39;s Tale">Sutherland's Tale</a>, which does not require the Handheld Shortwave Radio to be acquired, and can be started as soon as sufficient Trust is earned with <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Sutherland">Sutherland</a>.</p>
