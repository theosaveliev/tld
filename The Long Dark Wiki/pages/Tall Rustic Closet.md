**Tall Rustic Closet** is a <a href="./Decorations.md" class="wikilink" title="Decorations">Decorations</a> that can be <a href="./Crafting.md" class="wikilink" title="Crafted">Crafted</a> as part of the <a href="./Safehouse%20Customization.md" class="wikilink" title="Safehouse Customization">Safehouse Customization</a> feature.

## Overview

A makeshift wooden closet, a Tall Rustic Closet can be crafted at a <a href="./Furniture%20Workbench.md" class="wikilink" title="Furniture Workbench">Furniture Workbench</a>, but requires <a href="./Woodworking%20Tools.md" class="wikilink" title="Woodworking Tools">Woodworking Tools</a>.

The closet has various containers for storage:

- 1 large cabinet with 25.00 kg of capacity
- 1 medium cabinet with 20.00 kg of capacity
- 3 small drawers with 6.00 kg of capacity
- For a total of 63.00 kg of storage

Progress for Crafting this item will be stored in the Furniture Workbench's container inventory, rather than the player's inventory. Once crafted, the item can be automatically placed in the Safehouse. Canceling this prompt will force the item back into the Furniture Workbench's container inventory. The closet can only be placed on a wide, clear space on the floor or a flat surface when in Customization Mode.

Despite having a Backpack carry weight value, the closet cannot be picked up and placed back into the player's inventory once crafted, and so it cannot be carried from one base to another. The only option is to move or destroy it (destroying the closet will yield no resources, so consider the material cost/time investment before creating this item).
