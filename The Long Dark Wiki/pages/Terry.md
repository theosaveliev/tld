**Terry** a minor character in <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things">Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things</a> of <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> in <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark"><em>The Long Dark</em></a>. He appears in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> and <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a> as the "**Radio Voice Man**."

## Appearance

Terry wears a green jacket, glasses.

## History

A resident of <a href="./Perseverance%20Mills.md" class="wikilink" title="Perseverance Mills">Perseverance Mills</a>, Terry operates his <a href="./Terry%27s%20Radio%20Shack.md" class="wikilink" title="own radio hut.">own radio hut.</a>

He is a man of a conspiratorial/distrusting of government types nature, with his motto being "Trust <u>No</u> One." Terry also enjoys cannabis blunts.

Terry is first heard on a radio broadcast as <a href="./Will%20Mackenzie.md" class="wikilink" title="Will Mackenzie">Will Mackenzie</a> attempts to contact Perseverance Mills when <a href="./Jeremiah.md" class="wikilink" title="Jeremiah&#39;s">Jeremiah's</a> shortwave radio is powered by the <a href="./Aurora.md" class="wikilink" title="Aurora">Aurora</a>, in order to locate <a href="./Astrid%20Greenwood.md" class="wikilink" title="Astrid Greenwood">Astrid Greenwood</a>. He intercepts a distress call from the Mills, with a man on the radio mentioning needing help and a quarantine. Before Mackenzie can ask what the problem is or if Astrid is at the Mills, the shortwave radio shorts out and contact is lost.
