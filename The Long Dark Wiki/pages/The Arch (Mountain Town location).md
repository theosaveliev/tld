**The Arch** is a location in [Milton](./Milton.md) in the [Mountain Town](./Mountain%20Town.md) region. It is similar in name and appearance to [The Arch](./The%20Arch%20%28Old%20Island%20Connector%20location%29.md) in [Crumbling Highway](./Crumbling%20Highway.md).

## Overview

It leads from behind the [Bank Manager's House](./Bank%20Manager%27s%20House.md) to the [Wood Lot](./Wood%20Lot.md) beside the [Orca Gas Station](./Orca%20Gas%20Station.md). It is directly below the [Shortwave Tower](./Radio%20Tower.md). If you are planning to travel through, plan ahead and be ready to defend yourself from the wolves that stalk the Wood Lot. [Old Man's Beard Lichen](./Old%20Man%27s%20Beard%20Lichen.md), [Rose Hip](./Rose%20Hip.md), [Reishi Mushroom](./Reishi%20Mushroom.md), and even a [Deer Carcass](./Deer%20Carcass.md) can be found near The Arch and close to the Wood Lot.
