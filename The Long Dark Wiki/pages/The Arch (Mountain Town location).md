> '''The Arch''' is a location in [[Milton]] in the [[Mountain Town]] region. It is similar in name and appearance to [[The Arch]] in [[Crumbling Highway]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> It leads from behind the [[Bank Manager's House]] to the [[Wood Lot]] beside the [[Orca Gas Station]]. It is directly below the [[Shortwave Tower (Mountain Town location)|Shortwave Tower]]. If you are planning to travel through, plan ahead and be ready to defend yourself from the wolves that stalk the Wood Lot. [[Old Man's Beard Lichen]], [[Rose Hip]], [[Reishi Mushroom]], and even a [[Deer Carcass]] can be found near The Arch and close to the Wood Lot.
