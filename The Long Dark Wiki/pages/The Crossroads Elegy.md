**The Crossroads Elegy** is a [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in [Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) of _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

This achievement is an unmissable story achievement reaching the final doorway at the end of the [Cinder Hills Coal Mine](./Cinder%20Hills%20Coal%20Mine.md) that leads to [Coastal Highway](./Coastal%20Highway.md) Mission [Aftermath](./Aftermath.md). Simply interact with the door an the ending cutscene plays as you complete the Episode, along with earning the achviement.
