**The Crossroads Elegy** is a <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy">Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy</a> of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is an unmissable story achievement reaching the final doorway at the end of the <a href="./Cinder%20Hills%20Coal%20Mine.md" class="wikilink" title="Cinder Hills Coal Mine">Cinder Hills Coal Mine</a> that leads to <a href="./Coastal%20Highway.md" class="wikilink" title="Coastal Highway">Coastal Highway</a> Mission <a href="./Aftermath.md" class="wikilink" title="Aftermath">Aftermath</a>. Simply interact with the door an the ending cutscene plays as you complete the Episode, along with earning the achviement.
