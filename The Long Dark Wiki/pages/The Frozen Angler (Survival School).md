**The Frozen Angler** is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> item found in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2">Episode 2</a> of the <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> story mode and starts the ice fishing portion of the <a href="./Survival%20School.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival School">Survival School</a> side mission.

During the course of the mission, Mackenzie finds the book in a chair in <a href="./Camp%20Office.md" class="wikilink" title="Camp Office">Camp Office</a> and is required to craft a fishing tackle and then fish in the nearby Mystery Lake.
