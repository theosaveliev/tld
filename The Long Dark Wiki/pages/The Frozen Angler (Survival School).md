**The Frozen Angler** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) item found in [Episode 2](./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md) of the [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) story mode and starts the ice fishing portion of the [Survival School](./Survival%20School.md) side mission.

During the course of the mission, Mackenzie finds the book in a chair in [Camp Office](./Camp%20Office.md) and is required to craft a fishing tackle and then fish in the nearby Mystery Lake.
