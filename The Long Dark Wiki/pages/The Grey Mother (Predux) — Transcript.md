## There's a Firewood Bin outside Grey Mother's house. Stock it with fuel you find around town -- Sticks, Firewood, broken down furniture, or Coal if you can find any.

CHAPTER THREE

THE GREY MOTHER

_Mackenzie sets out to find fuel for Grey Mother's fire. Eventually he finds 24 hours worth of fuel and puts it in Grey Mother's firewood bin, prompting another tutorial message_

Containers
> __Search Everything!
> Search all the containers you find, as you never know when one might hold life-saving supplies! You can also store things in Containers, to be retrieved later.__
>
>
> — What I Know description

_Mackenzie walks inside and triggers a cutscene_

### 3Rd Person Cutscene

_Grey Mother claps slowly as Mackenzie walks in and stands in front of a chair that was not there before. Grey Mother laughs in amusement_

Grey Mother: "So, the Outsider returns! You're already doing better than the others. You've found the fuel, but did you find any answers?"

Mackenzie: "All I've found are wolves, bad weather, and an empty town."

Grey Mother: "It's just as I told you. They're all gone."

_The cutscene ends as the player stands over Grey Mother. The message "GREY MOTHER NOW TRUSTS YOU MORE NOW. (+5)" appears. Talking to her triggers another 3rd person cutscene_

Mackenzie: "What really happened here? Seems like people where here recently. Where did they go? Why didn't they just call for help or...just drive out?"

Grey Mother: "The power went out. Who knows the reason. People started to get cold, hungry. They panicked. Started to talk crazy. Others walked here from the highway...some, belonged here. Others, were Outsiders like you."

Mackenzie: "Did anyone see a woman? Brown hair, would have had a tan jacket...scarf with...birds on it. Possibly injured."

Grey Mother: "There was an Outsider woman. I heard her pass through. She wasn't alone. There were angry voices, and...but the storm was so loud."

Mackenzie: "Voices?"

Grey Mother: "I lost the voices and I couldn't get them back. Nevermind. You did well with the fuel. Now, do the same with food. Or I won't last the winter."

_A dialogue compass appears as the cutscene ends_

- HELP COMING?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Surely...someone will come looking. Someone will send help. Search and rescue? The military? The government?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "To Milton? No."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "You don't think help is coming?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "We needed help ten years ago. During the Collapse. When you Mainlanders were sitting pretty, and the others raped our Island. Took what they wanted. We needed help ten years ago, and it didn't come then. Why would it come now?"

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "That was a long time ago. Things are different now."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Ha! Not for us. Not for Great Bear. And not for Grey Mother."

- OTHERS?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Is there anyone else left in town?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "After the lights went out...a blizzard blew in. People got scared. Were afraid of being trapped. Or...more trapped. They panicked. Some left to see what they could discover from the outside."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "And...what did they find out?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "They didn't come back."

- MAINLANDER?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "You keep calling me Mainlander. Why?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "You're an outsider. Mainlander. You're one of them. You don't belong here."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Right...but I mean...you sound angry about it."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Years ago, when the Collapse happened...we lost everything. The young people didn't understand how dependent we'd become. Some people may have accepted dependence on the Mainland. But I don't want the scraps from your table."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Guess I don't know that much...about the history."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "I'm not surprised. You Mainlanders have always been focused on your own story."

- PANTRY?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "So...I hate to ask, but...food."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "The town...it's abandoned. They've left me to starve. I'll need food to survive this."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Survive until...what?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "I'll worry about that. You worry about finding me food to keep me alive."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "But...what if people come back? Won't I be taking what they need to survive?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "You mean...stealing? Maybe so. But it's fair. [They stole from me all those years ago](./Lilith%20Barker.md) and now it's their turn."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "What did they steal from you?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Nevermind that! Find me food. Ransack the gas station on the edge of town. There's always food there. Owner has a "special supplier" from the Mainland. You're a Mainlander so stealing should come naturally. Or...if you're feeling guilty...bring me only what Nature has to offer. It'll be more work but maybe you'll sleep better at night."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I'll...see what I can do."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Take the Prybar from the shed, if you haven't already. You might need it to get into some of the more...stubborn...places."

- ANGRY VOICES?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Can you tell me more about the angry voices you heard?'

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "It was...windy. The blizzard was already waking up. But I heard yelling. A scream. Later, I heard from the gossips that someone had passed through the town in the night. More than one person. A woman, and some men. One of the men was found..."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Found?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "They found him dead the next morning."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "And the woman? The one whose voice you heard?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "I'm not sure. The voices were...thin. Far. The blizzard was waking up. Soon there was only wind and...darkness."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "The scream...could it have been the woman?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "I don't know. I don't remember. It's all cloudy now."

- SUPPLIES?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "A town like Milton - - where do supplies come from?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "The Mainland, of course. Where else?'

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I don't know. Local stuff. Farms? Producers on the Island?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Those days are in the past. We became dependent on the Mainland just like you wanted us to. Then, when things Collapsed...you cut us off. Until you found things you could take, things you could sell to...the others. The ones in the South. Some tried farming, but..."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Times were hard for everyone back then. Still are. You aren't the only ones to have suffered. In the cities..."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Bah! The cities. You got what you deserved. You'll get no sympathy from Grey Mother."

- OUTSIDERS?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "You said there were others. Other...outsiders?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Crawled in after the night with the lights. I heard them. Murmurs in town. I still had ears in town, the gossips. They were scared. Some were from the highways. Said there were others. Already frozen. Already...dead."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "They stayed here in town?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "School house. The old schoolhouse. Still had a coal stove. They slept in the school house...waiting."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Waiting for..."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Mercy. They found it."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I don't understand..."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "You'll understand when you find it."

## Grey Mother will starve unless you stock her pantry with food. Scavenge what you can from the environment around you. Some food sources are easier to find than others.

_This objective is a very quiet one like the firewood objective. Once the player gathers enough food, they must speak to Grey Mother. There are 4 different responses depending on whether or not the player breaks into the [Orca Gas Station](./Orca%20Gas%20Station.md) (even if they do not steal anything). Grey Mother will somehow know the weight of your honesty depending on how you get her food_

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "So, tell me Mainlander. Did you do what I asked? Did you ransack the gas station? Did you clean them out?"

### Stole from the Gas Station

- NO, I DIDN'T (LIE)

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "No, I couldn't do it. I thought about what would happen if people came back and...couldn't bring myself to steal from them."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "I may be blind but I can still see the lies on you, Outsider. If you'll lie to me about that, what else will you lie about?"

- YES, I DID (TRUTH)

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I did. I feel bad about taking other people's stuff but right now you need it more, and I can't waste any more time - - I need to find my friend."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Might makes right, huh, Mainlander? You might just have what it takes to survive this long Winter after all."

### Did not steal from the Gas Station

- NO, I DIDN'T (TRUTH)

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I know you're angry about people abandoning you here, but if people come back and they found all their food found its way into your pantry...they'll be even less likely to help you. And I have a feeling that if someone comes back, you're going to need all the help you can get to make it through the Winter. So no...I didn't steal the gas station's supplies. But I found enough food to help you make it through the next few weeks, and I'll be back to check on you when I can."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Well...I...guess I misjudged you, Mainlander. Over the years...I have learned that most people disappoint you, given the chance. You give me some hope."

- YES, I DID (LIE)

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I did like you asked me. I took whatever I could find. If the situation is as bad as you said, it's everyone for themselves."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Your words are harsh but there's no heart behind them. You're a kinder soul than I would have believed, Mainlander."

### 3Rd Person Cutscene

Mackenzie: "So...you're all set for food and fuel. I'd like to hear what you know about my friend now."

_Grey Mother "Looks" over her rifle_

Grey Mother: "Friend...yeah, she passed through. Some people heard her, in the storm."

_Mackenzie shakes his head_

Mackenzie: "You already said that. Where did she go?"

_Grey Mother sets her rifle on the left side of her chair_

Grey Mother: "She passed through town. too many closed doors, broken houses. Nowhere to run, to hide."

Mackenzie: "Why didn't anyone help [her](./Astrid%20Greenwood.md)?

Grey Mother: "Yes, why didn't they? They should have! They should have found [her](./Lilith%20Barker.md) before it was too late."

## Investigate the tunnels leading out of Milton. Grey Mother wants to know what happened there.

_Mackenzie travels to the tunnel, and a cutscene plays as Mackenzie finds a [Crashed Prison Transport Bus](./Crashed%20Prison%20Transport%20Bus.md) with a dead convict on the ground. He spots a piece of Astrid's scarf and rips it out of the back right window. He then uses his parka to rub the frost off the back door window and peers inside. The camera changes to show the inside, with a message written in blood on the floor: PERSERVERANCE_

_Mackenzie then makes his way back to Grey Mother to report what he has found, triggering another 3rd person cutscene_

Grey Mother: "Did you find it?"

_Mackenzie takes a seat_

Mackenzie: "It was there, like you said."

Grey Mother: "And your friend?"

Mackenzie: "No sign of her. Tunnel's blocked. Looks like maybe...an avalanche. But I think she got through before that happened."

_Grey Mother does not take this news well_

Grey Mother: "If the tunnel is blocked, that's the end for Grey Mother."

Mackenzie: "So the tunnel is the only way through the mountains?"

Grey Mother: "No, not the only way through. But...my only way through."

_The scene changes to show the Fireplace_

Grey Mother: "Grey Mother is tired now. Let her rest for a while..."

_The cutscene ends as the player stands over Grey Mother. The message "GREY MOTHER NOW TRUSTS YOU MORE NOW. (+10)" appears._

## Grey Mother seems deep in thought. Return to her once she's rested a while.

_Talking to Grey Mother and selecting STORY gives this response_

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Leave me to rest, outsider."

_After sleeping or exploring for several hours, Mackenzie returns to Grey Mother, triggering another 3rd person cutscene_

Mackenzie: "I can't be here any more. My friend is out there and she needs my help. Please...tell me how to find her, and I'll do whatever I can to help you get out of here."

Grey Mother: "There's only one thing left to be done. You need to put [her](./Lilith%20Barker.md) to rest."

Mackenzie: "Put her to...I'm not giving up yet!"

Grey Mother: "On the other side of town, there's a farmhouse. You'll find a key to a safety deposit box somewhere in there. Go to the bank, and bring me the box that matches the key. Then you'll understand what [happened to her](./Lilith%20Barker.md)."

Mackenzie: "Wait...farm? Bank? What's this have to do with my friend?"

Grey Mother: "[They left her](./Lilith%20Barker.md), didn't they? She needed help and [they let her go](./Martin%20Barker.md). This is how we make it right."

Will Mackenzie: "I don't understand..."

Grey Mother: "In the bank. You'll find the answers. Now go."

_A dialogue compass appears as the cutscene ends_

- SICK?

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "You seem...distracted. Tired. Are you ok?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "I'm old. Some times the mind wanders, Outsider. Travels to other places. Other times. I think about [her](./Lilith%20Barker.md). Cold and alone."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Are you talking about my friend?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "They should have helped [her](./Lilith%20Barker.md)."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "You have a sense of her passing there?"

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I think she left a sign for me. A word: "PERSERVERANCE"."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I don't know. Maybe...she wanted me to know she wouldn't give up?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Or reminding you not to."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "It has to mean something more."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Why? Because you matter so much to her?"

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "No. Because she wrote in her own blood."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "There is a town. Far to the North. Perseverance Mills. Maybe she was showing you the way."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "[Perseverance Mills](./Perseverance%20Mills.md). How do I get there?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "There is no way now. By car, perhaps. On foot? No path that Grey Mother knows of."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "You mentioned a farm?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Yes...on the other side of town. The old Barker Farm."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Barker?"

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Yes. A liar and a [thief](./Grey%20Mother%27s%20Pearls.md), mostly."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Right."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "You'll see."

Grey Mother **(Text)**: "Bah. Bank from the Mainland. Never belonged here."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "Usually banks are pretty secure. Not sure I'll be able to get in."

Mackenzie **(Text)**: "I'll see what I can find."

## Find Lily's grave marker and pay service to her memory.

_Mackenzie travels to the [Graveyard](./Graveyard.md) near the church and a cutscene plays. Mackenzie stands before a tombstone with Grey Mother's Pearls, then kneels down on one knee and rubs the face iff to clean it. He then places the pearls on top of the tombstone. The camera pans around to show the name on the tombstone, Lilith Barker._

_Mackenzie returns to Grey Mother and another cutscene plays as he walks over to her_

Mackenzie: "Lily was your...daughter?"

Grey Mother: "You have a good heart, Mackenzie. You see things others would miss."

Mackenzie: "But the name - - Lily Barker...related to the farmer? You said they were liars and cheats."

_Mackenzie takes a seat, as the camera pans over Grey Mother rubbing her blanket over her legs_

Grey Mother: "The years have made me angry. Lily was my daughter...our daughter. I am also a Barker. The Farmer was my husband."

Mackenzie: "You lived...apart."

Grey Mother: "Yes. Our Lily died climbing."

_Mackenzie looks up at the photo above the fireplace of Lily, as the camera zooms in on it_

Grey Mother: "She wanted to summit all the peaks on Great Bear. She was young, but already very talented. One day she wanted to head out on her own. I said no, but he said yes. "Let her go, she needs to face it alone." So she faced it alone. And fell. I never forgave him."

Mackenzie: "And the pearls?"
