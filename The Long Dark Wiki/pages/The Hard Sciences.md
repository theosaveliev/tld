# The Hard Sciences

**The Hard Sciences**

[The Hard Sciences icon](../assets/The_Hard_Sciences_icon.png)

**Description:** Reach the Suzuki Radio Telescope and deliver Jeremiah's "Wintermute" message to Dr. Atwood.

**The Hard Sciences** is a hidden [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[Wintermute](./Wintermute.md)_ [Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things](./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md) of _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

<span id="guide"></span><span id="Guide"></span>

## Guide

This is an unmissable hidden achievement granted after completing the main story mission [The Cold, Broken Road](./The%20Cold%2C%20Broken%20Road.md). After passing through the mine in [Blackrock Foothills](./Blackrock%20Foothills.md) and arriving at the [Suzuki Radio Telescope](./Suzuki%20Radio%20Telescope.md), the player will walk inside and finally speak to [Atwood](./Atwood.md) in her office, delivering the message "Wintermute" and unlocking the achievement after the cutscene ends.
