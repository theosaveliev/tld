**The Hard Sciences** is a hidden <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a>* <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things">Episode 5: The Light At The End Of All Things</a> of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This is an unmissable hidden achievement granted after completing the main story mission <a href="./The%20Cold%2C%20Broken%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="The Cold, Broken Road">The Cold, Broken Road</a>. After passing through the mine in <a href="./Blackrock%20Foothills.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Foothills">Blackrock Foothills</a> and arriving at the <a href="./Suzuki%20Radio%20Telescope.md" class="wikilink" title="Suzuki Radio Telescope">Suzuki Radio Telescope</a>, the player will walk inside and finally speak to <a href="./Atwood.md" class="wikilink" title="Atwood">Atwood</a> in her office, delivering the message "Wintermute" and unlocking the achievement after the cutscene ends.
