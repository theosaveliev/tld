**The Hunted, Part Three** is the final installment of "The Hunted" series of <a href="./Challenge.md" class="wikilink" title="Challenges">Challenges</a>. It was formally to be released in the future as part of a free update for the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> expansion, but the release is currently to be announced.

Completing the challenge will award the player with a unique <a href="./Badges.md" class="wikilink" title="Badge">Badge</a> of the same name.

## Overview

The Hunted, Part 3 continues the story of a survivor escaping the wrath of the <a href="./Old%20Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Bear">Old Bear</a>. This time, the survivor is tasked with ultimately ending the life of the bear, lest they themselves meet their end.
