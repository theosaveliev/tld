**The Hunted, Part Three** is the final installment of "The Hunted" series of [Challenges](./Challenge.md). It was formally to be released in the future as part of a free update for the [Tales from the Far Territory](./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md) expansion, but the release is currently to be announced.

Completing the challenge will award the player with a unique [Badge](./Badges.md) of the same name.

## Overview

The Hunted, Part 3 continues the story of a survivor escaping the wrath of the [Old Bear](./Old%20Bear.md). This time, the survivor is tasked with ultimately ending the life of the bear, lest they themselves meet their end.
