**The Will to Live** is an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in *[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)*.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after surviving for five hundred whole days in a single [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) game. It is most easily done on Pilgrim.
