**The Will to Live** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after surviving for five hundred whole days in a single <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> game. It is most easily done on Pilgrim.

## Game history

This achievement was introduced on 1 April 2015 in <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Changelog.md#0.212" class="wikilink" title="version 0.212">version 0.212</a>.**Lua execution error in Module:Navbox function navbox**
