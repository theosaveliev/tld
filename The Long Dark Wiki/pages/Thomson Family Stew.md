<p><strong>Thomson Family Stew</strong> is a food item in <em>The Long Dark</em>, it is learned from a unique <a href="./Recipe%20Cards.md" class="wikilink" title="Recipe Card">Recipe Card</a> and is made with a <a href="./Cooking%20Pot.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Pot">Cooking Pot</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Calories-wise, Thomson Family Stew is inefficient, with at least a level 4 cooking skill (the minimum to create it), the ingredients separately provide over 2100 kcal, almost double the calories of the finished stew. The weight of Thomson Family Stew (0.35kg) is far more efficient than that of its ingredients (1.5 kg). Cooked at level 5, its energy density is over 3200 kcal per kilogram, one of the densest in the game. This makes Thomson Family Stew an efficient food for travel or exploring.</p>
<p>Additionally Thomson Family Stew has both the highest Vitamin C density and amount per-item, making it highly effective at combating <a href="./Scurvy.md" class="wikilink" title="Scurvy">Scurvy</a>. Note: Thomson Family Stew contains 80 units of Vitamin C, while consuming the ingredients directly provides a total of 141 units (Canned Corn 52 units, Carrot 9 units (x4), Cooked Potato 24 (x2), Reishi Tea, made from 2 Mushrooms: 5 units).</p>
<p>Canned Corn is one of the rarest foods in the game and is thus a very hard ingredient to find for this Recipe. It is mainly found in:</p>
<ul>
<li>The 4 <a href="./Signal%20Void.md" class="wikilink" title="Signal Void">Signal Void</a> bunkers
<ul>
<li><a href="./Bunker%20Alpha.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Alpha">Bunker Alpha</a> (2 cans)</li>
<li><a href="./Bunker%20Beta.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Beta">Bunker Beta</a> (3 cans)</li>
<li><a href="./Bunker%20Gamma.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Gamma">Bunker Gamma</a> (2 cans)</li>
<li><a href="./Bunker%20Omega.md" class="wikilink" title="Bunker Omega">Bunker Omega</a> (5 cans)</li>
</ul></li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li>The top floor of the <a href="./Concentrator.md" class="wikilink" title="Concentrator">Concentrator</a> of the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a> (3 cans)</li>
<li>Occasionally in <a href="./Supply%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Supply Cache">Supply Caches</a></li>
</ul>
<p><a href="./Canned%20Corn.md" class="wikilink" title="Canned Corn">Canned Corn</a> can also be obtained from the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a>.</p>
