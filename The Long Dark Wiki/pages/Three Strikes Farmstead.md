> '''Three Strikes Farmstead''' is a collection of several farm buildings in [[Pleasant Valley]], located about 5 minutes walk northwest of the [[Pleasant Valley Farmstead]].
> == Overview ==
> Three Strikes Farmstead is a location in Pleasant Valley. It is in the northwest of the region.
> 
> It is also known as Three Strikes Homestead. The name may refer to three strikes in baseball, which is an out--possibly referencing the homesteaders' bad luck or simply the three broken-down structures on the edge of property. It may also refer to the three carcasses -- wolf, doe, and stag -- that can be found in the nearby area in Survival Mode.
> 
> [[Reishi Mushroom|Reishi mushrooms]] and two frozen deer carcasses can be found near the broken-down buildings. Several rabbits, along with a wolf carcass, can also be found in the field nearby.
> 
> A [[Fire Barrel|fire barrel]] and possibly a [[Hunting Rifle|hunting rifle]] can be found in the main barn. Other loot may include a first aid kit and a human corpse.
> 
> == Wintermute ==
> In [[Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy|Episode 3]], the player must visit the site to complete the [[Ghost Stag]] as part of the [[Tall Tales]] side quest. Some optional story-relevant materials may also be found in the barn along with some relatively valuable loot.
