<p><strong>Three Strikes Farmstead</strong> is a collection of several farm buildings in <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley">Pleasant Valley</a>, located about 5 minutes walk northwest of the <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley%20Farmstead.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley Farmstead">Pleasant Valley Farmstead</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Three Strikes Farmstead is a location in Pleasant Valley. It is in the northwest of the region.</p>
<p>It is also known as Three Strikes Homestead. The name may refer to three strikes in baseball, which is an out--possibly referencing the homesteaders' bad luck or simply the three broken-down structures on the edge of property. It may also refer to the three carcasses -- wolf, doe, and stag -- that can be found in the nearby area in Survival Mode.</p>
<p><a href="./Reishi%20Mushroom.md" class="wikilink" title="Reishi mushrooms">Reishi mushrooms</a> and two frozen deer carcasses can be found near the broken-down buildings. Several rabbits, along with a wolf carcass, can also be found in the field nearby.</p>
<p>A <a href="./Fire%20Barrel.md" class="wikilink" title="fire barrel">fire barrel</a> and possibly a <a href="./Hunting%20Rifle.md" class="wikilink" title="hunting rifle">hunting rifle</a> can be found in the main barn. Other loot may include a first aid kit and a human corpse.</p>
<h2 id="Wintermute">Wintermute</h2>
<p>In <a href="./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 3">Episode 3</a>, the player must visit the site to complete the <a href="./Ghost%20Stag.md" class="wikilink" title="Ghost Stag">Ghost Stag</a> as part of the <a href="./Tall%20Tales.md" class="wikilink" title="Tall Tales">Tall Tales</a> side quest. Some optional story-relevant materials may also be found in the barn along with some relatively valuable loot.</p>
