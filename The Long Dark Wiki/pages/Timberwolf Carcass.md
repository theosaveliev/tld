> ==Drops==
> A Wolf Carcass may be harvested for 4.00-7.00 kg of [[Wolf Meat (Raw)|raw meat]], 1 [[Fresh Wolf Pelt]] and 2 guts. Wolf hides can be cured to craft [[Wolfskin Coat]]. On Stalker and Interloper modes, wolf meat carries the risk of [[Intestinal Parasites]].
