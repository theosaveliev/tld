<td><p>Yes|} A <strong>Timberwolf Carcass</strong> is a harvestable "object" found after <a href="./Hunting.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting">Hunting</a> a <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolf">Timberwolf</a>. <a href="./Crow.md" class="wikilink" title="Crows">Crows</a> will fly around a Timberwolf Carcass and always drop 3 <a href="./Crow%20Feather.md" class="wikilink" title="Crow Feathers">Crow Feathers</a> around the carcass at a time.</p>
<h2 id="Drops">Drops</h2>
<p>A Wolf Carcass may be harvested for 4.00-7.00 kg of <a href="./Wolf%20Meat%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="raw meat">raw meat</a>, 1 <a href="./Fresh%20Wolf%20Pelt.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Wolf Pelt">Fresh Wolf Pelt</a> and 2 guts. Wolf hides can be cured to craft <a href="./Wolfskin%20Coat.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolfskin Coat">Wolfskin Coat</a>. On Stalker and Interloper modes, wolf meat carries the risk of <a href="./Intestinal%20Parasites.md" class="wikilink" title="Intestinal Parasites">Intestinal Parasites</a>.</p>
