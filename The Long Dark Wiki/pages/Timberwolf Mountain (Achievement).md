**Timberwolf Mountain** is an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after reaching the [Summit](./Summit.md) on top of [Timberwolf Mountain](./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md) in a [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) game. Although this achievement is most easily done on Pilgrim, it can be done within one in-game day, regardless of difficulty, by selecting Timberwolf Mountain as the starting region. It can also be done in the [Challenge](./Challenge.md) [Hopeless Rescue](./Hopeless%20Rescue.md), as it is required to climb to the top of the mountain for a [Distress Pistol](./Distress%20Pistol.md).

## Game history

This achievement was introduced on 1 April 2015 in [version 0.212](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Early%20Access%20Changelog.md#0-212).
