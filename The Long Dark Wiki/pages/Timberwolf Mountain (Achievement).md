**Timberwolf Mountain** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after reaching the <a href="./Summit.md" class="wikilink" title="Summit">Summit</a> on top of <a href="./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolf Mountain">Timberwolf Mountain</a> in a <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> game. Although this achievement is most easily done on Pilgrim, it can be done within one in-game day, regardless of difficulty, by selecting Timberwolf Mountain as the starting region. It can also be done in the <a href="./Challenge.md" class="wikilink" title="Challenge">Challenge</a> <a href="./Hopeless%20Rescue.md" class="wikilink" title="Hopeless Rescue">Hopeless Rescue</a>, as it is required to climb to the top of the mountain for a <a href="./Distress%20Pistol.md" class="wikilink" title="Distress Pistol">Distress Pistol</a>.

## Game history

This achievement was introduced on 1 April 2015 in <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Changelog.md#0.212" class="wikilink" title="version 0.212">version 0.212</a>.
