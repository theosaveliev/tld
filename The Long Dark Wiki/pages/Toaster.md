A **Toaster** is a harvestable item found in the homes of many kitchen countertops. It requires a <a href="./Hacksaw.md" class="wikilink" title="Hacksaw">Hacksaw</a> to harvest and yields 1 <a href="./Scrap%20Metal.md" class="wikilink" title="Scrap Metal">Scrap Metal</a> after 15 minutes of sawing.

| Object  | Scrap Metal | Time       | Calories | Condition (of Hacksaw) |
|---------|-------------|------------|----------|------------------------|
| Toaster | 1           | 15 minutes | 45       | -5%                    |
