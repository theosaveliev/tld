A **Toaster** is a harvestable item found in the homes of many kitchen countertops. It requires a [Hacksaw](./Hacksaw.md) to harvest and yields 1 [Scrap Metal](./Scrap%20Metal.md) after 15 minutes of sawing.

| Object | Scrap Metal | Time | Calories | Condition (of Hacksaw) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Toaster | 1 | 15 minutes | 45 | -5% |
