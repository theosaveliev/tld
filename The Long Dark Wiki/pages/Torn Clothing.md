**Torn Clothing** is a minor <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> that can be encountered in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*. Torn Clothing is the only Affliction that is indirectly linked to the player's overall wellbeing, affecting their Clothing rather than their body or mind.

## Overview

Torn Clothing is an Affliction that is often paired with <a href="./Minor%20Bruising.md" class="wikilink" title="Bruising">Bruising</a> and <a href="./Laceration.md" class="wikilink" title="Lacerations">Lacerations</a>, and is mainly acquired through Falls and <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggles">Struggles</a> with hostile wildlife. When acquired, it results in an audible fabric tearing sound, followed by multiple pieces of the player's outermost layer of <a href="./Clothing.md" class="wikilink" title="Clothing">Clothing</a> losing a percentage of its overall condition value. The same percentage is applied to every piece of affected clothing. The clothing affected depends on the affliction source.

### Falls

Falling from a significant height will result in the player's outermost legs and feet clothing being torn from the weight of the impact. It is advised to practice <a href="./Mountain%20Goating.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountain Goating">Mountain Goating</a> in order to avoid being injured while attempting to scale down mountain or hill sides with large drops.

### Hostile Wildlife Encounters

Encounters with hostile predator <a href="./Wildlife.md" class="wikilink" title="Wildlife">Wildlife</a> has a chance of having every piece of the player's outermost clothing being damaged during a <a href="./Struggle.md" class="wikilink" title="Struggle">Struggle</a>, alongside other various injuries. Being attacked by <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolves">Wolves</a> almost always has a chance in the player's clothing being torn the longer a Struggle goes on. <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolves">Timberwolves</a> resort to bite-and-run tactics to wear down the player, but can also initiate a Struggle if the player stands still long enough. <a href="./Bear.md" class="wikilink" title="Bears">Bears</a> will always damage the player's clothing when attacked.
