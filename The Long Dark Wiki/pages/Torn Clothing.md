**Torn Clothing** is a minor [Affliction](./Afflictions.md) that can be encountered in *[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)*. Torn Clothing is the only Affliction that is indirectly linked to the player's overall wellbeing, affecting their Clothing rather than their body or mind.

## Overview

Torn Clothing is an Affliction that is often paired with [Bruising](./Minor%20Bruising.md) and [Lacerations](./Laceration.md), and is mainly acquired through Falls and [Struggles](./Struggle.md) with hostile wildlife. When acquired, it results in an audible fabric tearing sound, followed by multiple pieces of the player's outermost layer of [Clothing](./Clothing.md) losing a percentage of its overall condition value. The same percentage is applied to every piece of affected clothing. The clothing affected depends on the affliction source.

### Falls

Falling from a significant height will result in the player's outermost legs and feet clothing being torn from the weight of the impact. It is advised to practice [Mountain Goating](./Mountain%20Goating.md) in order to avoid being injured while attempting to scale down mountain or hill sides with large drops.

### Hostile Wildlife Encounters

Encounters with hostile predator [Wildlife](./Wildlife.md) has a chance of having every piece of the player's outermost clothing being damaged during a [Struggle](./Struggle.md), alongside other various injuries. Being attacked by [Wolves](./Wolf.md) almost always has a chance in the player's clothing being torn the longer a Struggle goes on. [Timberwolves](./Timberwolf.md) resort to bite-and-run tactics to wear down the player, but can also initiate a Struggle if the player stands still long enough. [Bears](./Bear.md) will always damage the player's clothing when attacked.
