**Tough as Nails** was an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_. At the release of [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) (v1.00), it was removed from the game and no longer displays in Steam, nor is it included in the console ports of the game.
