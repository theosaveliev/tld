A **Towel** is an interactable item that can be harvested for <a href="./Cloth.md" class="wikilink" title="Cloth">Cloth</a>. They are often found near sinks and bathrooms inside homes. It takes 10 minutes to harvest by hand for 2 Cloth, or 6 minutes for 2 Cloth with a <a href="./Hunting%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Knife">Hunting Knife</a>.

| Cloth | Time       | Calories | Condition of Tool    |
|-------|------------|----------|----------------------|
| 2     | 10 minutes | 31       | -0% by Hand          |
| 2     | 6 minutes  | 18       | -2% Hunting Knife    |
| 2     | 8 minutes  | 23       | -3% Improvised Knife |
