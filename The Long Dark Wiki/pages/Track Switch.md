# Track Switch

<div>

**Track Switches** are stationary interactable objects in <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark"><em>The Long Dark</em></a>, that are only found on <a href="./Cauldron%20Island.md" class="wikilink" title="Cauldron Island">Cauldron Island</a>, and <a href="./Episode%205%20-%20The%20Light%20At%20The%20End%20Of%20All%20Things.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute Episode 5">Wintermute Episode 5</a>.

## Overview

**Track Switches** are levers found near junctions on useable railway tracks. They allow the direction of the <a href="./Steam%20Train.md" class="wikilink" title="Steam Train">Steam Train</a> and the <a href="./Prototype%20Train.md" class="wikilink" title="Prototype Train">Prototype Train</a> to be changed.

On top of the switch is a long pole with a yellow and red arrow. This arrow points in the direction the switch is set, allowing the Survivor to see which way the train will head from some distance. When the switch is thrown, the arrow will rotate to indicate the new direction, and the tracks on the switch will also move to direct the train in the new direction.

A good way to tell which way the train will move at a glance is to pay attention to the lever itself instead of the arrows. Rather than straining your eyes on the small arrows, see which side the lever itself is on. If it is on the left, the train will move left and vice versa for the right.

Other track switches exist on the <a href="./Trans-Island%20Railway%20Line.md" class="wikilink" title="Trans-Island Railway Line">Trans-Island Railway Line</a>, such as near <a href="./Indolent%20Hoppers.md" class="wikilink" title="Indolent Hoppers">Indolent Hoppers</a> and <a href="./The%20Ravine.md" class="wikilink" title="The Ravine">The Ravine</a>, but these can't be interacted with and are purely decorative.
