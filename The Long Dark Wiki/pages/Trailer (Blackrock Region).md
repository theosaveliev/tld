# Trailer \(Blackrock Region\)

<div>

The Trailer is a location found in the western area of <a href="./Blackrock.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock">Blackrock</a>, on the western bank of the flowing river on the road to the <a href="./Power%20Plant.md" class="wikilink" title="Power Plant">Power Plant</a>.

## Overview

This trailer is the only guaranteed indoors location in this area; the only other ones are the Power Plant and <a href="./Old%20Substation.md" class="wikilink" title="Old Substation">Old Substation</a>, with the trailer being right in the middle of them. It allows for shelter if the survivor is surprised by the timber wolf pack from the <a href="./Grand%20Meadow.md" class="wikilink" title="Grand Meadow">Grand Meadow</a> and is unable to escape Northwards. The trailer features several containers as well as a bed and <a href="./Scrap%20Metal.md" class="wikilink" title="Scrap Metal">Scrap Metal</a> scavenging opportunities. There are two pieces of <a href="./Cedar%20Firewood.md" class="wikilink" title="Cedar Firewood">Cedar Firewood</a> spawning at the nearest power pole, along with a wolf/deer carcass spawning nearby.

## Notable loot

- The trailer contains 2 lockers, one of which is usually locked (requires pry bar).
- 2 revolver rounds usually spawn near/under the lockers.
- Small amount of miscellaneous food and first aid supplies in containers, as well as a few pieces of <a href="./Reclaimed%20Wood.md" class="wikilink" title="Reclaimed Wood">Reclaimed Wood</a>.
