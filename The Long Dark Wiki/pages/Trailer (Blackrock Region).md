# Trailer \(Blackrock Region\)

The Trailer is a location found in the western area of [Blackrock](./Blackrock.md), on the western bank of the flowing river on the road to the [Power Plant](./Power%20Plant.md).

## Overview

This trailer is the only guaranteed indoors location in this area; the only other ones are the Power Plant and [Old Substation](./Old%20Substation.md), with the trailer being right in the middle of them. It allows for shelter if the survivor is surprised by the timber wolf pack from the [Grand Meadow](./Grand%20Meadow.md) and is unable to escape Northwards. The trailer features several containers as well as a bed and [Scrap Metal](./Scrap%20Metal.md) scavenging opportunities. There are two pieces of [Cedar Firewood](./Cedar%20Firewood.md) spawning at the nearest power pole, along with a wolf/deer carcass spawning nearby.

## Notable loot

- The trailer contains 2 lockers, one of which is usually locked (requires pry bar).
- 2 revolver rounds usually spawn near/under the lockers.
- Small amount of miscellaneous food and first aid supplies in containers, as well as a few pieces of [Reclaimed Wood](./Reclaimed%20Wood.md).
