<td><p>Has no shelter|} <strong>Turbulent Pasture</strong> is a set of small frozen ponds in the <a href="./Zone%20of%20Contamination.md" class="wikilink" title="Zone of Contamination">Zone of Contamination</a> northeast of <a href="./Indolent%20Hoppers.md" class="wikilink" title="Indolent Hoppers">Indolent Hoppers</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>It has a few <a href="./Cat%20Tail.md" class="wikilink" title="Cat Tails">Cat Tails</a> to scavenge in a moderate Chemical Hazard zone. 5 <a href="./Green%20Maple%20Sapling.md" class="wikilink" title="Maple Saplings">Maple Saplings</a> can be found on a nearby hill, perfect for crafting a <a href="./Travois.md" class="wikilink" title="Travois">Travois</a> and going further up the hill leads to a wooden bridge and ends at a wooden structure.</p>
<p>Heading east one can find the <a href="./Forgotten%20Exploration.md" class="wikilink" title="Forgotten Exploration">Forgotten Exploration</a>.</p>
