## Overview

It has a few [Cat Tails](./Cat%20Tail.md) to scavenge in a moderate Chemical Hazard zone. 5 [Maple Saplings](./Green%20Maple%20Sapling.md) can be found on a nearby hill, perfect for crafting a [Travois](./Travois.md) and going further up the hill leads to a wooden bridge and ends at a wooden structure.

Heading east one can find the [Forgotten Exploration](./Forgotten%20Exploration.md).
