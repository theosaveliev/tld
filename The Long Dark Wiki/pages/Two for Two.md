# Two for Two

**Two for Two**

| Property | Details |

**Two for Two** was an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in *[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)*. At the release of [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) (v1.00), it was removed from the game and no longer displays in Steam, nor is it included in the console ports of the game.

It was replaced by the [The First of Many](./The%20First%20of%20Many.md) achievement.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after surviving for two days in a single [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) game. It is most easily done on Pilgrim.**Lua execution error in Module:Navbox function navbox**
