# Two for Two

**Categories:** Achievements · **Source:** [Fandom](https://thelongdark.fandom.com/wiki/Two_for_Two)

**Two for Two**

[Two for Two](../assets/Two_for_Two.jpg)

**Description:** Survive for 2 days in a single Survival Mode game.

**Two for Two** was an [achievement](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_. At the release of [Wintermute](./Wintermute.md) (v1.00), it was removed from the game and no longer displays in Steam, nor is it included in the console ports of the game.

It was replaced by the [The First of Many](./The%20First%20of%20Many.md) achievement.
