 / **Unburdened** is a unique Benefit [Affliction](./Afflictions.md) in _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_ only encountered in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) when consuming certain [Cooking](./Cooking.md) Recipes. It was added in the Part 3 update to the [Tales from the Far Territory](./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md).

## Overview

> “**Your pack feels lighter because of something you ate.**”

Unburdened is acquired from consuming certain recipes that effects a survivor's [Encumbrance](./Encumbrance.md) by temporarily increasing their maximum total Carry Weight by 3kg for 3 hours. This effect stacks with any other increases to Carry Weight ([Moose-Hide Satchel](./Moose-Hide%20Satchel.md), [Technical Backpack](./Technical%20Backpack.md), [Well Fed](./Well%20Fed.md)).

The following foods when consumed grant the Unburdened Affliction as a side effect:

- ![](../assets/Coastal_Fishcakes_icon.png) [Coastal Fishcakes](./Coastal%20Fishcakes.md)
- ![](../assets/Rabbit_Pie_icon.png) [Rabbit Pie](./Rabbit%20Pie.md)
- ![](../assets/Venison_Pie_icon.png) [Venison Pie](./Venison%20Pie.md)
- ![](../assets/Pemmican_Bar_icon.png) [Pemmican Bar](./Pemmican%20Bar.md)
- ![](../assets/Porter%27s_Soup_icon.png) [Porter's Soup](./Porter%27s%20Soup.md)

The effect of this Affliction does not stack, and each application will not override the previous Affliction. Partially eating the previously mentioned foods only adds a partial amount of Unburdened benefit duration.
