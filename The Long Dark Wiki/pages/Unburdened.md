# Unburdened

> {{Infobox/Affliction|image=Unburdened icon.png|category=[[Afflictions]]|caused by=Consume [[Food And Drink]]|+/- effect=Positive|type (Benefit/Injury/Disease)=Benefit|benefit duration=3 Hours|release=21 June 2023|update=2.17|description=Your pack feels lighter because of something you ate.}}{{TFTFT}}
> 
> '''Unburdened''' is a unique Benefit [[Afflictions|Affliction]] in ''[[The Long Dark]]'' only encountered in [[Survival Mode]] when consuming certain [[Cooking]] Recipes. It was added in the Part 3 update to the [[Tales from the Far Territory]].
> ==Overview==
> {{Quote2|Your pack feels lighter because of something you ate.}}
> Unburdened is acquired from consuming certain recipes that effects a survivor's [[Encumbrance]] by temporarily increasing their maximum total Carry Weight by 3kg for 3 hours. This effect stacks with any other increases to Carry Weight ([[Moose-Hide Satchel]], [[Technical Backpack]], [[Well Fed]]).
> 
> The following foods when consumed grant the Unburdened Affliction as a side effect:
> 
> * {{Iconlink|Coastal Fishcakes}}
> * {{Iconlink|Rabbit Pie}}
> * {{Iconlink|Venison Pie}}
> * {{Iconlink|Pemmican Bar}}
> * {{Iconlink|Porter's Soup}}
> The effect of this Affliction does not stack, and each application will not override the previous Affliction. Partially eating the previously mentioned foods only adds a partial amount of Unburdened benefit duration.
