# Urn

**Categories:** Interactable Objects · **Source:** [Fandom](https://thelongdark.fandom.com/wiki/Urn)

**Urn**

[Urn](../assets/Urn.jpg)

**Release:** 1.00

**Container**

**Capacity:** 0.25 kg(0.55 lbs)

The **Urn** was a cut container found during the Predux version of [Episode 1: Do Not Go Gentle](./Episode%201%20-%20Do%20Not%20Go%20Gentle.md). It was found in the [Paradise Meadows Farm](./Paradise%20Meadows%20Farm.md) farmhouse bedroom, and contained [Bank Deposit Box Key 15](./Bank%20Deposit%20Box%20Key%2015.md), needed to finish the mission [The Grey Mother (Predux)](./The%20Grey%20Mother%20%28Predux%29.md).

It has since been removed from the game after the V1.47 Wintermute Redux version of Episode 1 was released, but can still be experienced by downloading versions V1.15, V1.30, or V1.37 of Steam version of _[The Long Dark](./The%20Long%20Dark.md)_ by using the [Time Capsule](./Time%20Capsule.md).

It now considered [Cut/Discontinued Content](./Cut%20%E2%80%94%20Discontinued%20Content.md). A spiritual successor to the Urn would appear years later in [Episode 3: Crossroads Elegy](./Episode%203%20-%20Crossroads%20Elegy.md) as the [Vase](./Vase.md).
