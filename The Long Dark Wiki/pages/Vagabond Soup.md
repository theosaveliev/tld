> |renewable=Yes}}'''Vagabond Soup''' is a [[Food And Drink]] item in ''[[The Long Dark]]''. It is learned after acquiring its unique [[Recipe Cards|Recipe Card]].
> 
> ==Overview==
> Recipe Card is found by trading with the [[Trader]].
> 
> * +5% Maximum Condition ([[Nourished]]) for 3 hours
> * Negative [[Fatigue]] increase (-15%)
> Compared to [[Rabbit Stew]], Vagabond Soup improves the caloric value of 0.5kg of rabbit meat by 200 calories less, but does not require flour and has half the cook time. 
> 
> At Cooking 5, the calorie value of Vagabond Soup is the same as its components. Its healing properties are slightly worse (by 1%) than that of Rabbit Stew, except on Interloper, in which case it heals 2% more Condition due to Improved Rest's bonus being reduced.
