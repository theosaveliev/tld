<p><strong>Vagabond Soup</strong> is a <a href="./Food%20And%20Drink.md" class="wikilink" title="Food And Drink">Food And Drink</a> item in <em><a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a></em>. It is learned after acquiring its unique <a href="./Recipe%20Cards.md" class="wikilink" title="Recipe Card">Recipe Card</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Recipe Card is found by trading with the <a href="./Trader.md" class="wikilink" title="Trader">Trader</a>.</p>
<ul>
<li>+5% Maximum Condition (<a href="./Nourished.md" class="wikilink" title="Nourished">Nourished</a>) for 3 hours</li>
<li>Negative <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a> increase (-15%)</li>
</ul>
<p>Compared to <a href="./Rabbit%20Stew.md" class="wikilink" title="Rabbit Stew">Rabbit Stew</a>, Vagabond Soup improves the caloric value of 0.5kg of rabbit meat by 200 calories less, but does not require flour and has half the cook time.</p>
<p>At Cooking 5, the calorie value of Vagabond Soup is the same as its components. Its healing properties are slightly worse (by 1%) than that of Rabbit Stew, except on Interloper, in which case it heals 2% more Condition due to Improved Rest's bonus being reduced.</p>
