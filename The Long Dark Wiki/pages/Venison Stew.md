## Overview

Like other stews, it increases [Fatigue,](./Fatigue.md) however also bestows [Improved Rest](./Improved%20Rest.md) for 3 hours. This makes it quite useful for restoring condition by allowing for a longer period of sleep. It is also calorie-efficient for the ingredients required, albeit less so than other varieties of stew. Regardless, this bonus is offset by a fairly long cooking time.

## Effects

- 3 hours of Improved Rest (+1% [Condition](./Condition.md) restored per hour of sleep, or after 18 hours)
- -15% [Fatigue](./Fatigue.md) (instantly)
