<p><strong>Venison Stew</strong> is a food item in <em>The Long Dark</em> and is made with a <a href="./Cooking%20Pot.md" class="wikilink" title="Cooking Pot">Cooking Pot</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>Like other stews, it increases <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue,">Fatigue,</a> however also bestows <a href="./Improved%20Rest.md" class="wikilink" title="Improved Rest">Improved Rest</a> for 3 hours. This makes it quite useful for restoring condition by allowing for a longer period of sleep. It is also calorie-efficient for the ingredients required, albeit less so than other varieties of stew. Regardless, this bonus is offset by a fairly long cooking time.</p>
<h2 id="Effects">Effects</h2>
<ul>
<li>3 hours of Improved Rest (+1% <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> restored per hour of sleep, or after 18 hours)</li>
<li>-15% <a href="./Fatigue.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue">Fatigue</a> (instantly)</li>
</ul>
