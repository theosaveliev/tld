A **Warm Cave** is a common type of outdoor cave that has no transition screen but provides a <a href="./Warmth.md" class="wikilink" title="Warmth">Warmth</a> bonus. Such caves are found in every region except <a href="./Coastal%20Highway.md" class="wikilink" title="Coastal Highway">Coastal Highway</a>, <a href="./Keeper%27s%20Pass%20North.md" class="wikilink" title="Keeper&#39;s Pass North">Keeper's Pass North</a>, and <a href="./Keeper%27s%20Pass%20South.md" class="wikilink" title="Keeper&#39;s Pass South">Keeper's Pass South</a>.

<a href="./Cave%20%28Transition%20Zone%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Cave (Transition Zone)">Cave (Transition Zone)</a> are not considered Warm Caves, as these are indoor spaces. Certain Bear Caves (such as the ones in Coastal Highway ) do not have interiors, and also do not count.

## Overview

Warm Caves are divided into two layers, a cold front area and warm back area.

### Front Sheltered Zone

The front of the cave simply has the same air temperature as outside the cave, however it is completely sheltered from the wind regardless of wind direction, and a <a href="./Magnifying%20Lens.md" class="wikilink" title="Magnifying Lens">Magnifying Lens</a> can be used within it.

### Back Warm Zone

The back of the cave is warmer like indoor spaces, but the back of the cave does not have a fixed temperature. Instead there is a warmth bonus which is generally sufficient to allow sleeping without a fire, at least if the player has sufficiently warm clothing and <a href="./Bedroll.md" class="wikilink" title="Bedroll">Bedroll</a>.

- The player walks slower as they transition to the back of the cave, and immediately receives a Warmth bonus.
- It is possible to <a href="./Curing.md" class="wikilink" title="cure">cure</a> hides, guts, and saplings in the back of such caves.
- Does not progress <a href="./Cabin%20Fever.md" class="wikilink" title="Cabin Fever">Cabin Fever</a>.
- A <a href="./Campfire.md" class="wikilink" title="Campfire">Campfire</a> may be lit on the floor, in the front or back of the cave.
- A Magnifying Lens may not be used, although it is possible to light a fire within the back zone while standing in the front zone.
- The light level for the purpose of performing tasks is the same as outside (<a href="./Mending.md" class="wikilink" title="Mending">Mending</a> and Repairing is not possible during a Blizzard/Heavy Fog <a href="./Weather.md" class="wikilink" title="Weather">Weather</a> at night).

## Special "Warm Caves"

There are also a few exterior buildings that give a warmth bonus despite not having a transition to an indoor space:

- The outdoor room with the bed at the <a href="./Maintenance%20Yard.md" class="wikilink" title="Maintenance Yard">Maintenance Yard</a>
- Break room at <a href="./Last%20Resort%20Cannery.md" class="wikilink" title="Last Resort Cannery">Last Resort Cannery</a>
- Room outside the <a href="./Workshop.md" class="wikilink" title="Workshop">Workshop</a>
- <a href="./Stone%20Church.md" class="wikilink" title="Stone Church">Stone Church</a>

The following areas behave like Warm Caves, though these indoor areas progress Cabin Fever.

- <a href="./Mountaineer%27s%20Hut.md" class="wikilink" title="Mountaineer&#39;s Hut">Mountaineer's Hut</a>
- <a href="./Forgotten%20Exploration.md" class="wikilink" title="Forgotten Exploration">Forgotten Exploration</a>
- The front porch of the <a href="./Pleasant%20Valley%20Farmstead.md" class="wikilink" title="Pleasant Valley Farmstead">Pleasant Valley Farmstead</a>
- The maintenance hallway in the south tunnel in <a href="./Far%20Range%20Branch%20Line.md" class="wikilink" title="Far Range Branch Line">Far Range Branch Line</a>
