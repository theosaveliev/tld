## Guide

This achievement is obtained after harvesting ten entire [deer](./Deer.md) carcasses in a single [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) game. It is most easily done on Pilgrim, but will also be eventually acquired through normal gameplay, especially on harder difficulties where manufactured food is rarer. A [Hunting Knife](./Hunting%20Knife.md) or [Improvised Knife](./Improvised%20Knife.md) is highly recommended.
