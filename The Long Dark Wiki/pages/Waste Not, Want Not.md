**Waste Not, Want Not** is an <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This achievement is obtained after harvesting ten entire <a href="./Deer.md" class="wikilink" title="deer">deer</a> carcasses in a single <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> game. It is most easily done on Pilgrim, but will also be eventually acquired through normal gameplay, especially on harder difficulties where manufactured food is rarer. A <a href="./Hunting%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Hunting Knife">Hunting Knife</a> or <a href="./Improvised%20Knife.md" class="wikilink" title="Improvised Knife">Improvised Knife</a> is highly recommended.

An entire carcass includes **all** of the <a href="./Venison%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="meat">meat</a>, hide and <a href="./Fresh%20Gut.md" class="wikilink" title="gut">gut</a>.
