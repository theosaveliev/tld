> }}<nowiki> </nowiki>'''Watchful Falls''' is a landmark far side of [[Monolith Lake]], with the [[Lake Cave]] just behind it, in [[Hushed River Valley]].
> 
> == Overview ==
> There is a patch of [[Scrub Brush]] and a shallow cave immediately beside the waterfall. The cave may contain a ravaged [[Wolf Carcass]], a corpse, and some supplies, including a whetstone and a skill book.
> 
> A [[Moose]] may spawn on Monolith Lake near Watchful Falls, and there is an area with [[rabbit]]s nearby. The semi-secure cave can serve as a good base to hunt game and harvest carcasses.
