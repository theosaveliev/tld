## Overview

A **Water Tower** initially contains 6,000 liters of [Unsafe Water](./Water%20%28Unsafe%29.md), and is accompanied by two yellow signs labelled "WATER". When the Survivor pulls the train up close to the tower, a small sign under the signpost will flip a black panel to green a green one, letting them know that the [train](./Prototype%20Train.md) is in position to be filled (fuel and water caps must be in the middle of the signs to trigger this).

The player can then exit the train and walk over to the yellow lever on the side of the tower to start dispensing water. If the sign panel is not green, then the option to place water into the train directly will not appear.

![The sign panel displaying that the train is not in position for refueling.](../assets/Refueling_Panel.jpg)

![The panel flipped to green, signaling that refueling is possible.](../assets/Refueling_Panel_Green.jpg)

Water is transferred into the train at a rate of 5.00 liters per minute (20.00 min cap at 5 minutes per increment), meaning that a fully empty locomotive can be refilled with 600.00 L of water in 2 hours. Make sure to turn the train Engine off while refueling, as this can burn already stored water and not fully refuel the train.

Water can also be transferred into the Survivor's backpack, 5.00 liters at a time. This may be useful for thirsty railroaders who want to [purify the water](./Water%20Purification%20Tablets.md), or boil it on the [train's](./Prototype%20Train.md) cooking slot while driving.

- Excess unsafe water can be stored on the train's [Cupboard](./Cupboard.md) for later use (either for boiling or for the train).
- Keep in mind that passing time while refueling the train will put the Survivor at risk from the elements, so make sure the weather is warm.
  - Passing time and canceling the refueling process part way will only deposit a partial amount of water into the train up until the moment it is canceled. (needs verification).
- Water Towers refuel over time, so that the player will never run out of water for the train.
  - If the water level in the tower falls below 4,900 liters, it will immediately refill, allowing the tower to serve as an infinite source of Unsafe Water.
