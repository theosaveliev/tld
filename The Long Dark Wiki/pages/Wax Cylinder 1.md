It is found in [Desolation Point](./Desolation%20Point.md), on the [Fulmar Autopilot Boat](./Fulmar%20Autopilot%20Boat.md) right next to the Play Me! note and [Wax Cylinder Player](./Wax%20Cylinder%20Player.md).

The Cylinder is narrated by Alice McLeod.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 1**_

Whoever finds this message. Please, Do not turn away.

I know how strange this all must seem to you.

I found something. An island far from where you're standing. There's a deep mystery here. I've only just scratched the surface.

And I can't leave.

I've seen enough to know that solving it will help us all survive what's coming. If you've found this message and you want to live, you'll have to trust me.

You'll have to find me.

I'm leaving this message using the only recording technology that still seems to work, hoping against all hope that someone finds it before something happens to me.

This boat is equipped with a kind of auto-navigation I've managed to improvise. I have no idea if it'll work.

But if you found this recording, it must have brought the boat to you.

And if it brought the boat to you, it can bring you back to me.

Load up on supplies. Get the boiler going, and engage the throttle. With any luck, the navigation system should take over.

It'll follow a route I've set for it, to bring you here.

Theoretically, it should even let you travel back to Great Bear if you need to.

There's not much time, and what I have to share could change your world forever. So please, don't give up on me.

Please hurry.

Come find me, on Cauldron Island.
