<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 10</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%204.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 4">Anna Island -- Track Section 4</a> inside the <a href="./Repair%20Depot.md" class="wikilink" title="Repair Depot">Repair Depot</a> on a shelf on the 2nd floor.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Chief Engineer Best.</p>
<h2 id="Transcript">Transcript</h2>
<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">
<p>The following text is transcribed from <strong>Wax Cylinder 10</strong></p>
</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>These islands are a gift. And perfect for our work.</p>
<p>It's likely we'd not be able to replicate these exact conditions outside the context of these very specific islands, something I've tried to impress on McLeod time and time again.</p>
<p>But the caldera's volcanic origins have created an excellent test bed for my ideas.</p>
<p>And we'll use the island's warmth to our advantage.</p>
<p>Properly purposed, we can use the natural heat to keep our fuel and water infrastructure functional during the harsh winter. We might avoid them freezing altogether.</p>
<p>McLeod doesn't particularly care about the geothermal aspects of these islands. He doesn't appreciate what they will mean about extending the season for our research.</p>
<p>For security purposes and to protect our research from prying eyes, we've exiled ourselves to these godforsaken northern islands.</p>
<p>Fine.</p>
<p>But I'll be damned if we don't benefit from this warmth to create some comfort for ourselves along the way.</p>
</div>
