## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 10**_

These islands are a gift. And perfect for our work.

It's likely we'd not be able to replicate these exact conditions outside the context of these very specific islands, something I've tried to impress on McLeod time and time again.

But the caldera's volcanic origins have created an excellent test bed for my ideas.

And we'll use the island's warmth to our advantage.

Properly purposed, we can use the natural heat to keep our fuel and water infrastructure functional during the harsh winter. We might avoid them freezing altogether.

McLeod doesn't particularly care about the geothermal aspects of these islands. He doesn't appreciate what they will mean about extending the season for our research.

For security purposes and to protect our research from prying eyes, we've exiled ourselves to these godforsaken northern islands.

Fine.

But I'll be damned if we don't benefit from this warmth to create some comfort for ourselves along the way.
