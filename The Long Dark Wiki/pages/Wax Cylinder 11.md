**Wax Cylinder 11** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) as part of the [Iron Road](./Iron%20Road.md) expansion. It is found in [Port Remnant](./Port%20Remnant.md) at [Broken Shoreline](./Broken%20Shoreline.md), the sunken ship just closest to the Lighthouse.

The Cylinder is narrated by Elsa.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 11**_

Erik.

My dearest love.

I'm scared.

I've managed to hide dozens of cylinders, all over the islands.

It's taken me weeks. I even risked enlisting help from some of my students, to make sure the cylinders were spread as far apart and in as many places as possible. I convinced them it was a secret game.

The poor innocents, they have no idea what's going on. And I don't have the heart to tell them.

But McLeod's men are getting suspicious.

I think I'm being followed. I need to get away.

I have to find a way off the island. I need to find a way back to you.

Yesterday, I tried to get on the boat at Port Remnant but was turned back.

I've heard others say McLeod's closed off all access to Great Bear and to the Mainland. He hasn't left the islands in months.

Am I trapped here?

Erik. How will we find each other?

Erik?

Is this the end for me? For us?
