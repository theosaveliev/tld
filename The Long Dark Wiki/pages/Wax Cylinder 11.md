<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 11</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Port%20Remnant.md" class="wikilink" title="Port Remnant">Port Remnant</a> at <a href="./Broken%20Shoreline.md" class="wikilink" title="Broken Shoreline">Broken Shoreline</a>, the sunken ship just closest to the Lighthouse.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Elsa.</p>

</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>Erik.</p>
<p>My dearest love.</p>
<p>I'm scared.</p>
<p>I've managed to hide dozens of cylinders, all over the islands.</p>
<p>It's taken me weeks. I even risked enlisting help from some of my students, to make sure the cylinders were spread as far apart and in as many places as possible. I convinced them it was a secret game.</p>
<p>The poor innocents, they have no idea what's going on. And I don't have the heart to tell them.</p>
<p>But McLeod's men are getting suspicious.</p>
<p>I think I'm being followed. I need to get away.</p>
<p>I have to find a way off the island. I need to find a way back to you.</p>
<p>Yesterday, I tried to get on the boat at Port Remnant but was turned back.</p>
<p>I've heard others say McLeod's closed off all access to Great Bear and to the Mainland. He hasn't left the islands in months.</p>
<p>Am I trapped here?</p>
<p>Erik. How will we find each other?</p>

<p>Is this the end for me? For us?</p>
