<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 12</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%202.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 2">Anna Island -- Track Section 2</a> inside the <a href="./Repair%20Depot.md" class="wikilink" title="Repair Depot">Repair Depot</a> on an old workstation.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Chief Engineer Best.</p>
<h2 id="Transcript">Transcript</h2>
<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">
<p>The following text is transcribed from <strong>Wax Cylinder 12</strong></p>
</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>Chief Engineer's Log.</p>
<p>Best here.</p>
<p>I feel like I haven't been warm since I arrived on this damn rock. Even in summer the pale sun's warmth doesn't penetrate the cold.</p>
<p>Does the wind ever stop blowing here? Its voice is always in my ears. It's maddening.</p>
<p>In any case, a small update on the portable heater project.</p>
<p>It's all fine and good to build up a railway that runs on whale oil, but if we truly want this energy source to flourish we need to find some novel applications.</p>
<p>I've been tinkering with something more modest, but useful considering the climate we find ourselves in.</p>
<p>I've contrived a portable heater made from lantern parts and some other bits and pieces I was able to machine. It has been designed to consume Whale Oil as fuel, using our soon-to-be patented mix and injection system.</p>
<p>It's a miniaturized version of, essentially, part of the mechanism that makes the train engine run so well.</p>
<p>Working on this has been an enjoyable distraction from...other matters.</p>
<p>This is just a test version but it already shows great promise.</p>
<p>It puts out enough heat to warm up a small room and the oil in the reservoir will burn for hours, provided you keep the temperature fairly low.</p>
<p>Increase the consumption rate, and you can probably boil water on it if you're patient.</p>
<p>I could see such a tool being useful as a replacement for coal stoves and wood fireplaces, in locations where those fuel supplies are not that available.</p>
<p>There may be applications for emergency use as well. My mind goes to, for example, that group of workers that were trapped in the blizzard a few months ago and all came back with frostbite. This heater might have prevented permanent damage.</p>
<p>I'm going to pass this unit on to St.Germain to get some of his boys to test it in the field, and if the results are suitable I'll expand the trial to a second iteration of the design.</p>
<p>I already have some ideas for how to improve it, not to mention other applications for this fuel source.</p>
<p>Of course, its usefulness is partly hindered by the lack of widespread access to whale oil supplies, but that's something we'll have to solve at some point regardless.</p>
<p>If we expand the testing program, we can probably rely on fuel supply caches until we think of something better.</p>
<p>Eventually, some kind of household-to-household fuel replenishing program could probably work. On a large scale, like getting milk delivered to your home.</p>
<p>The mind reels at the possibilities if we can really make this viable!</p>
<p>This was meant to be a short report. As usual, I've droned on too long.</p>
<p>I will say I miss the long talks McLeod and I once had when we were closer. There aren't many others on this island who share an engineer's love of tinkering, and I'm left to speak mainly to myself.</p>
<p>Not everyone here is equal to our ambitions, or the ones we once had at any rate, and I do miss sparring with another great mind.</p>
<p>I should scratch that part out. It's too personal.</p>
</div>
