**Wax Cylinder 13** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) as part of the [Iron Road](./Iron%20Road.md) expansion. It is found in [Anna Island --Track Section 3](./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%203.md) inside the [Cove (Track Section 3)](./Cove%20%28Track%20Section%203%29.md) cabin on a shelf.

The Cylinder is narrated by Reverend Angus Hornswallow.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 13**_

Sometimes the landscape here reminds me of home. Northern Scotland...rugged and beautiful.

But there's a silence here that feels...older even than my homeland. It's unsettling.

This morning I stood at a point overlooking the bay.

Below, I could see the workers at one of the many whaling stations that run along the interior of the caldera.

As they carved up one of the great beasts, it occurred to me that McLeod's work here, everything he has wrought, serves to remind us that God has put Nature here to serve Man.

Some of my blessed congregation whisper about the vast number of whales the company is processing here every week.

And truly, the scale is staggering.

But, in service to mankind's ambition, and under the eyes of God, whom I represent here in this most remote of locales...

Is this not the truest symbol of his love for us? That he would put this here for our benefit?

To serve us, as we serve him?
