<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 14</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%201.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 1">Anna Island -- Track Section 1</a> at the <a href="./Huts.md" class="wikilink" title="Huts">Huts</a> cabin in the locker.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Elsa.</p>
<h2 id="Transcript">Transcript</h2>
<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">
<p>The following text is transcribed from <strong>Wax Cylinder 14</strong></p>
</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>Erik, I feel alone here.</p>
<p>Yes, I have my students, and they are sweethearts.</p>
<p>But apart from the Reverend, I have not made many friends here.</p>
<p>The Reverend -- he insists I use his Christian name, Angus, but I find it uncomfortable as this is not how things are done back home in Norway.</p>
<p>He has been generous with his time, checking in on me.</p>
<p>Last week he left me one of McLeod's cylinder players, and some music to listen to. He said he felt it would help me remain in a "pastoral" mood.</p>
<p>And indeed, it has helped.</p>
<p>But I find myself thinking more and more about you.</p>
<p>I know you are suffering, and if not for the money I make here I would see no reason for us to be half a world apart.</p>
<p>I heard from your mother that she had you out at the seaside, and that the air did you good.</p>
<p>She said your lungs are still hurting. Why did you tell me otherwise in your last letter?</p>
<p>My plan is, every time I feel lonely for home, to record a message to you and tell you something beautiful I have seen.</p>
<p>And I will record it in English, so that you are forced to practice! This way, when you are better, we can travel abroad together.</p>
<p>I love Norway, but if there is one thing this experience has taught me, it's that there is a whole big world out there for us to discover.</p>
<p>We cannot just hide at home, where we feel safe.</p>
<p>Now for a beautiful thing.</p>
<p>This morning I watched the sun rise over the rocky landscape. As the warmth hit the water, a mist began to rise, and the light made the mist glow like candlelight.</p>
<p>It reminded me of our small church back home, and the way the candles flicker over your face, whenever you play the organ there.</p>
<p>I miss home. I miss you, Erik.</p>
<p>I will send money with this message.</p>
<p>All my love.</p>
<p>Your Elsa.</p>
</div>
