<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 15</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%202.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 2">Anna Island -- Track Section 2</a> inside the <a href="./Repair%20Depot.md" class="wikilink" title="Repair Depot">Repair Depot</a> on a small drawer in between the bunk beds.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Chief Engineer Best.</p>
<h2 id="Transcript">Transcript</h2>
<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">
<p>The following text is transcribed from <strong>Wax Cylinder 15</strong></p>
</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>Best here.</p>
<p>I'm testing this new cylinder field recorder, the origins of which I've chronicled elsewhere.</p>
<p>I hope the recording is clear against the din of the workers behind me.</p>
<p>St. Germain, this is a maintenance note for your team.</p>
<p>As we discussed, I've collected the recordings from our site measurements and compiled them in one large grid.</p>
<p>They tell a worrisome tale about the relative stability of the islands in this caldera.</p>
<p>I'm seeing non-trivial shifts and heaves along some of the track sections.</p>
<p>At the moment I'm standing with a crew along...</p>
<p>...section twelve.</p>
<p>They're having to re-seat the track here after a near derailment.</p>
<p>McLeod is driving the men harder and harder, with unsustainable quotas. Someone is bound to make a mistake, and that mistake will cost lives.</p>
<p>Until we know more, I suggest we double the track monitoring crews and increase the frequency of your team's sweeps.</p>
<p>The trains are coming through at greater and greater speeds and I'm concerned things will end in catastrophe if not for our constant vigilance.</p>
</div>
