## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 15**_

Best here.

I'm testing this new cylinder field recorder, the origins of which I've chronicled elsewhere.

I hope the recording is clear against the din of the workers behind me.

St. Germain, this is a maintenance note for your team.

As we discussed, I've collected the recordings from our site measurements and compiled them in one large grid.

They tell a worrisome tale about the relative stability of the islands in this caldera.

I'm seeing non-trivial shifts and heaves along some of the track sections.

At the moment I'm standing with a crew along...

...section twelve.

They're having to re-seat the track here after a near derailment.

McLeod is driving the men harder and harder, with unsustainable quotas. Someone is bound to make a mistake, and that mistake will cost lives.

Until we know more, I suggest we double the track monitoring crews and increase the frequency of your team's sweeps.

The trains are coming through at greater and greater speeds and I'm concerned things will end in catastrophe if not for our constant vigilance.
