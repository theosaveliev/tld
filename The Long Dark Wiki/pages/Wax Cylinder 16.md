<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 16</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Port%20Remnant.md" class="wikilink" title="Port Remnant">Port Remnant</a> inside the <a href="./Obstructed%20Transit.md" class="wikilink" title="Obstructed Transit">Obstructed Transit</a> Warehouse.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Chief Engineer Best.</p>
<h2 id="Transcript">Transcript</h2>
<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">
<p>The following text is transcribed from <strong>Wax Cylinder 16</strong></p>
</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>This is the Chief Engineer.</p>
<p>Another update regarding a small innovation from my workshop.</p>
<p>This one actually came from one of St. Germain's boys.</p>
<p>He's a railworker...Murphy I believe is his name.</p>
<p>Some huge, rough bear of a man, former fisherman from Halifax if I recall what the men told me.</p>
<p>St. Germain reports that Murphy has quite the voice, and no end of maritime shanties to share with the crews. Even when he's sober!</p>
<p>St. Germain says it's been good for morale. I can imagine.</p>
<p>Seems like Murphy's no small tinkerer himself. He took apart one of McLeod's cylinder players and made a kind of rugged handheld version.</p>
<p>Suitable for taking onto the boats, or even to the killing beaches, they say.</p>
<p>Apparently, he records and plays the shanties to try to bring some joy to their work.</p>
<p>I can't imagine the contrast myself.</p>
<p>In any case, there may be some promise to the notion of a portable wax cylinder recorder. I'll have to investigate more closely, and see if we can improve on Murphy's design.</p>
<p>Might be a superior medium for recording technical logs, as this climate is not friendly to paper.</p>
</div>
