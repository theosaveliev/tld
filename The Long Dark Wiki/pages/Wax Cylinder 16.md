The Cylinder is narrated by Chief Engineer Best.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 16**_

This is the Chief Engineer.

Another update regarding a small innovation from my workshop.

This one actually came from one of St. Germain's boys.

He's a railworker...Murphy I believe is his name.

Some huge, rough bear of a man, former fisherman from Halifax if I recall what the men told me.

St. Germain reports that Murphy has quite the voice, and no end of maritime shanties to share with the crews. Even when he's sober!

St. Germain says it's been good for morale. I can imagine.

Seems like Murphy's no small tinkerer himself. He took apart one of McLeod's cylinder players and made a kind of rugged handheld version.

Suitable for taking onto the boats, or even to the killing beaches, they say.

Apparently, he records and plays the shanties to try to bring some joy to their work.

I can't imagine the contrast myself.

In any case, there may be some promise to the notion of a portable wax cylinder recorder. I'll have to investigate more closely, and see if we can improve on Murphy's design.

Might be a superior medium for recording technical logs, as this climate is not friendly to paper.
