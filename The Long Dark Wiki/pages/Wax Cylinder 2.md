**Wax Cylinder 2** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) as part of the [Iron Road](./Iron%20Road.md) expansion. It is found in [Port Remnant](./Port%20Remnant.md) in the ruined lighthouse. Picking the Wax Cylinder up also picks up a key to the lighthouse door, but the key is not added to the player's inventory.

The Cylinder is narrated by Alice McLeod.
