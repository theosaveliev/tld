# Wax Cylinder 2

> {{IronRoad}}{{Infobox item|name=|image=Wax Cylinder 2 icon.png|release=Iron Road Part 1|update=2.58|story=|type=Collectibles|equipable=No|stackable=No|weight=0.00|actions=Examine|actions_radial=|actions_ground=Take It, Read Text, Leave It|examine=An audio recording made of wax.|key=}}'''Wax Cylinder 2''' is a [[Collectibles|Collectible]] in [[Survival Mode]] as part of the [[Iron Road]] expansion. It is found in [[Port Remnant]] in the ruined lighthouse. Picking the Wax Cylinder up also picks up a key to the lighthouse door, but the key is not added to the player's inventory.
> 
> The Cylinder is narrated by Alice McLeod.
