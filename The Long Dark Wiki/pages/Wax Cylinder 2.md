<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 2</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Port%20Remnant.md" class="wikilink" title="Port Remnant">Port Remnant</a> in the ruined lighthouse. Picking the Wax Cylinder up also picks up a key to the lighthouse door, but the key is not added to the player's inventory.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Alice McLeod.</p>
