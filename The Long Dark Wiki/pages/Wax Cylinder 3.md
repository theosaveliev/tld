## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 3**_

During my time on the island, I've recovered many of these wax cylinders. They were left behind by people who haven't been here for many, many years.

They're fragments of the story of what happened here, the history my great-grandfather tried to erase. The one he wants the world to forget.

Keep your eyes open. I'm sure there are many more cylinders that I haven't found. That I probably won't find.

I've done my best to make sense of them. But the cylinders are scattered, and I only have a partial record.

Slowly, with what I have already uncovered, I've been building a picture of what happened here so many years ago.

You need to hear these voices. Let their words fill the silent landscape around you. Let them help bring your mind to things that happened nearly a hundred years ago.

Let them be a weak antidote to this endless howling wind.

I hear these voices and I feel connected to a heritage I almost never knew.

It was my mother who first told me...

...but that's a story for later.

I've left two cylinders in the church for you.

Listen to these voices from the past. Let them flow over you.

And you will begin to understand why I had to come here.
