<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 3</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Port%20Remnant.md" class="wikilink" title="Port Remnant">Port Remnant</a> inside the <a href="./Desecrated%20Church.md" class="wikilink" title="Desecrated Church">Desecrated Church</a>.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Alice McLeod.</p>
<h2 id="Transcript">Transcript</h2>
<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">
<p>The following text is transcribed from <strong>Wax Cylinder 3</strong></p>
</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>During my time on the island, I've recovered many of these wax cylinders. They were left behind by people who haven't been here for many, many years.</p>
<p>They're fragments of the story of what happened here, the history my great-grandfather tried to erase. The one he wants the world to forget.</p>
<p>Keep your eyes open. I'm sure there are many more cylinders that I haven't found. That I probably won't find.</p>
<p>I've done my best to make sense of them. But the cylinders are scattered, and I only have a partial record.</p>
<p>Slowly, with what I have already uncovered, I've been building a picture of what happened here so many years ago.</p>
<p>You need to hear these voices. Let their words fill the silent landscape around you. Let them help bring your mind to things that happened nearly a hundred years ago.</p>
<p>Let them be a weak antidote to this endless howling wind.</p>
<p>I hear these voices and I feel connected to a heritage I almost never knew.</p>
<p>It was my mother who first told me...</p>
<p>...but that's a story for later.</p>
<p>I've left two cylinders in the church for you.</p>
<p>Listen to these voices from the past. Let them flow over you.</p>
<p>And you will begin to understand why I had to come here.</p>
</div>
