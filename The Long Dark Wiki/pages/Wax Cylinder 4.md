<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 4</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Port%20Remnant.md" class="wikilink" title="Port Remnant">Port Remnant</a> inside the <a href="./Desecrated%20Church.md" class="wikilink" title="Desecrated Church">Desecrated Church</a>.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Reverend Angus Hornswallow.</p>
<h2 id="Transcript">Transcript</h2>
<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">
<p>The following text is transcribed from <strong>Wax Cylinder 4</strong></p>
</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>From the desk of Reverend Angus Hornswallow.</p>
<p>Presbyterian diocese, Cauldron Island.</p>
<p>February the second, the year of our lord, 19XX.</p>
<p>Begin recording.</p>
<p>Will God forgive them?</p>
<p>It is said that he will.</p>
<p>I know that I should also forgive, but the rage grows inside me when I see what they are doing.</p>
<p>The desecration is...unfathomable.</p>
<p>I was sent to minister to their souls. To bring some hope to their hearts, and spread the good word across this wind-blasted isle.</p>
<p>But what I have seen would shake the faith of even the most devout.</p>
<p>I fear I do not have it in my heart to forgive them.</p>
<p>And this fact brings my whole life, my calling, into question.</p>
<p>I am not strong enough to find the charity required to overlook their sins.</p>
<p>Damn them. Damn McLeod and his insatiable ego.</p>
<p>Damn them all to hell for what they have done.</p>
</div>
