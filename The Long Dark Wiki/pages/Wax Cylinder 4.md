**Wax Cylinder 4** is a [Collectible](./Collectibles.md) in [Survival Mode](./Survival%20Mode.md) as part of the [Iron Road](./Iron%20Road.md) expansion. It is found in [Port Remnant](./Port%20Remnant.md) inside the [Desecrated Church](./Desecrated%20Church.md).

The Cylinder is narrated by Reverend Angus Hornswallow.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 4**_

From the desk of Reverend Angus Hornswallow.

Presbyterian diocese, Cauldron Island.

February the second, the year of our lord, 19XX.

Begin recording.

Will God forgive them?

It is said that he will.

I know that I should also forgive, but the rage grows inside me when I see what they are doing.

The desecration is...unfathomable.

I was sent to minister to their souls. To bring some hope to their hearts, and spread the good word across this wind-blasted isle.

But what I have seen would shake the faith of even the most devout.

I fear I do not have it in my heart to forgive them.

And this fact brings my whole life, my calling, into question.

I am not strong enough to find the charity required to overlook their sins.

Damn them. Damn McLeod and his insatiable ego.

Damn them all to hell for what they have done.
