## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 5**_

Erik? It has been months since I've been able to get any mail off the island.

This is Elsa.

I hope you can hear me.

I've managed to get my hands on one of these cylinder contraptions the chief engineer was always going on about.

I'm trying to leave some record, some memory, of what I have seen here on these islands.

I do it for the children. The innocents, who do not deserve to live through what is happening here.

Meanwhile, he's trying to destroy the truth.

I've heard that he's planning to break everything. Collecting all the records, then smashing or melting them down.

So nobody will ever know what he has started here.

But I'm small. Insignificant. Nobody notices me.

I will use that to my advantage.

I will gather all the records that I can, and hide them. So McLeod cannot ever find them.

Perhaps in the future, they will again be found.

And the truth can be known.

I love you, Erik. I will hide this cylinder if I can't find anyone to take it off the island.

But know that I think of you, always.
