<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 6</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Port%20Remnant.md" class="wikilink" title="Port Remnant">Port Remnant</a> at the <a href="./Whale%20Bone%20Shrine.md" class="wikilink" title="Whale Bone Shrine">Whale Bone Shrine</a> close to the <a href="./Desecrated%20Church.md" class="wikilink" title="Desecrated Church">Desecrated Church</a>, right next to the <a href="./Map%20%28Train%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Map (Train)">Map (Train)</a>.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Alice McLeod.</p>
