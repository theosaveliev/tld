<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 7</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%206.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 6">Anna Island -- Track Section 6</a> in <a href="./Train%20Shelter.md" class="wikilink" title="Train Shelter">Train Shelter</a>, in the cabin beside the train.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Alice McLeod.</p>
<h2 id="Transcript">Transcript</h2>
<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">
<p>The following text is transcribed from <strong>Wax Cylinder 7</strong></p>
</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>I don't know who I'm talking to here. But whoever you are, whoever finds this...</p>
<p>You made it!</p>
<p>And can you believe it? Can you believe your eyes?</p>
<p>It took me months to bring this machine back to life. That was before the aurora.</p>
<p>My great-grandfather's greatest invention. This is the Prototype.</p>
<p>In his journal he described it as, "an angel rendered in the finest steel".</p>
<p>It's hard to imagine now, but in its day this thing was absolutely state of the art.</p>
<p>And listen...you're going to need it. You have a long way to go, and you won't make it on foot. Not with what you know.</p>
<p>This is a steam engine, and it's not too hard to use.</p>
<p>The main thing you need to know is that it runs on Whale Oil.</p>
<p>Yeah...and that's important.</p>
<p>The whole island, all the infrastructure, my great-grandfather's entire empire here, was built around whaling.</p>
<p>And this Prototype was at the heart of his entire plan.</p>
<p>For now, all you need to know is, you'll need this train if you want to find me.</p>
<p>You can't let the train run out of fuel, or water for the boiler.</p>
<p>Keep your eye open for refuelling stations. I've marked some on the map in the cabin.</p>
<p>I wasn't able to explore all the tracks, so there may be others I didn't find.</p>
<p>Besides that...think of the train as a safe place. Like a shelter.</p>
<p>There are things coming, things you'll encounter on your journey, that only the train can help you with.</p>
<p>When you see them, you'll understand.</p>
<p>For now...take the train. Handle it carefully. It's a century old and fragile, and it's your only hope of getting to me.</p>
<p>Good luck.</p>
</div>
