The Cylinder is narrated by Alice McLeod.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 7**_

I don't know who I'm talking to here. But whoever you are, whoever finds this...

You made it!

And can you believe it? Can you believe your eyes?

It took me months to bring this machine back to life. That was before the aurora.

My great-grandfather's greatest invention. This is the Prototype.

In his journal he described it as, "an angel rendered in the finest steel".

It's hard to imagine now, but in its day this thing was absolutely state of the art.

And listen...you're going to need it. You have a long way to go, and you won't make it on foot. Not with what you know.

This is a steam engine, and it's not too hard to use.

The main thing you need to know is that it runs on Whale Oil.

Yeah...and that's important.

The whole island, all the infrastructure, my great-grandfather's entire empire here, was built around whaling.

And this Prototype was at the heart of his entire plan.

For now, all you need to know is, you'll need this train if you want to find me.

You can't let the train run out of fuel, or water for the boiler.

Keep your eye open for refuelling stations. I've marked some on the map in the cabin.

I wasn't able to explore all the tracks, so there may be others I didn't find.

Besides that...think of the train as a safe place. Like a shelter.

There are things coming, things you'll encounter on your journey, that only the train can help you with.

When you see them, you'll understand.

For now...take the train. Handle it carefully. It's a century old and fragile, and it's your only hope of getting to me.

Good luck.
