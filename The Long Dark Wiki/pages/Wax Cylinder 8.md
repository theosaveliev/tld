The Cylinder is narrated by Chief Engineer Best.

## Transcript

_The following text is transcribed from **Wax Cylinder 8**_

This is Best. Chief Engineer of this...madhouse.

They'll never know. They'll never know what we made here.

Damn McLeod and all the years I wasted on him!

We created something special here. An entire world of whale-oil powered technology.

The promise of endless fuel, so much more accessible than pumping oil from the ground. And so efficient!

And it would have been ours. We were a decade ahead of any potential competitors. We would have been at the heart of a whole new world of energy.

Even the infrastructure. Tracks. Refuelling stations. Maintenance sheds. Fuel Caches.

Built to last a hundred years, or more.

Even this island's bouts of heartburn, violent as they were, couldn't break what we have built.

And McLeod is going to throw it all away.

I'll be damned if I'll let it happen.

I'll find a way to save as much as I can.
