<p><strong>Wax Cylinder 8</strong> is a <a href="./Collectibles.md" class="wikilink" title="Collectible">Collectible</a> in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> as part of the <a href="./Iron%20Road.md" class="wikilink" title="Iron Road">Iron Road</a> expansion. It is found in <a href="./Anna%20Island%20--%20Track%20Section%206.md" class="wikilink" title="Anna Island -- Track Section 6">Anna Island -- Track Section 6</a> at the <a href="./Train%20Shelter.md" class="wikilink" title="Train Shelter">Train Shelter</a> on table in the shelter.</p>
<p>The Cylinder is narrated by Chief Engineer Best.</p>
<h2 id="Transcript">Transcript</h2>
<div style="margin: 0.8em 1.6em;font-style: italic;">
<p>The following text is transcribed from <strong>Wax Cylinder 8</strong></p>
</div>
<div style="background: #2D3035; border: 1px solid #2D3035; border-radius: 0 0 120px 120px / 0 0 8px 8px; box-shadow: 0 5px 7px -7px rgb(0 0 0 / 40%); max-width: 90ch; overflow: hidden; padding: 2em; margin-bottom: 1em;">
<p>This is Best. Chief Engineer of this...madhouse.</p>
<p>They'll never know. They'll never know what we made here.</p>
<p>Damn McLeod and all the years I wasted on him!</p>
<p>We created something special here. An entire world of whale-oil powered technology.</p>
<p>The promise of endless fuel, so much more accessible than pumping oil from the ground. And so efficient!</p>
<p>And it would have been ours. We were a decade ahead of any potential competitors. We would have been at the heart of a whole new world of energy.</p>
<p>Even the infrastructure. Tracks. Refuelling stations. Maintenance sheds. Fuel Caches.</p>
<p>Built to last a hundred years, or more.</p>
<p>Even this island's bouts of heartburn, violent as they were, couldn't break what we have built.</p>
<p>And McLeod is going to throw it all away.</p>
<p>I'll be damned if I'll let it happen.</p>
<p>I'll find a way to save as much as I can.</p>
