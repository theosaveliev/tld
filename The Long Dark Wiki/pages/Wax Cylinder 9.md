# Wax Cylinder 9

> {{IronRoad}}{{Infobox item|name=|image=Wax Cylinder 2 icon.png|release=Iron Road Part 1|update=2.58|story=|type=Collectibles|equipable=No|stackable=No|weight=0.00|actions=Examine|actions_radial=|actions_ground=Take It, Read Text, Leave It|examine=An audio recording made of wax.|key=}}'''Wax Cylinder 9''' is a [[Collectibles|Collectible]] in [[Survival Mode]] as part of the [[Iron Road]] expansion. It is found in [[Anna Island -- Track Section 4]] at the aurora gate down the railroad from the [[Repair Depot]].
> 
> The Cylinder is narrated by Alice McLeod.
>
