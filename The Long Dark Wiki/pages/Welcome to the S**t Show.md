**Welcome to the S\*\*t Show** is a <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in <a href="./Episode%204%20-%20Fury%2C%20Then%20Silence.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 4: Fury, then Silence">Episode 4: Fury, then Silence</a> of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This is an unmissable story achievement achieved after completing the mission <a href="./Blackrock%20Blues.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Blues">Blackrock Blues</a>. Find the <a href="./Blackrock%20Infirmary%20Medication.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Infirmary Medication">Blackrock Infirmary Medication</a> and bring it back to <a href="./Franklin.md" class="wikilink" title="Franklin">Franklin</a> at the <a href="./Blackrock%20Prison.md" class="wikilink" title="Blackrock Prison">Blackrock Prison</a>.

## Trivia

The achievement's name is a quote from <a href="./Franklin.md" class="wikilink" title="Franklin">Franklin</a>: "There you are! Well...welcome to the shitshow."
