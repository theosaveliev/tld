**Well Fed** is a positive <a href="./Afflictions.md" class="wikilink" title="Affliction">Affliction</a> gained by avoiding starvation.   

## Overview

Well Fed activates when the player has managed to avoid starvation (<a href="./Hunger.md" class="wikilink" title="calorie store count">calorie store count</a> at 0) for 72 hours (three full days). When this is achieved, the player character will get a 5% buff to maximum <a href="./Condition.md" class="wikilink" title="Condition">Condition</a> (capping it at 105%) and an increased <a href="./Encumbrance.md" class="wikilink" title="carrying capacity">carrying capacity</a> of 5.00 kg (11.00 lbs.)

The carrying capacity effects stack with wearing a <a href="./Moose-Hide%20Satchel.md" class="wikilink" title="Moose-Hide Satchel">Moose-Hide Satchel</a> and <a href="./Technical%20Backpack.md" class="wikilink" title="Technical Backpack">Technical Backpack</a>, meaning that having all 3 present adds 15.00 kg (33.00 lbs) to your 30.00 kg <a href="./Encumbrance.md" class="wikilink" title="Encumbrance">Encumbrance</a> capacity. Max Carry Weight can be increased further by acquiring the <a href="./Unburdened.md" class="wikilink" title="Unburdened">Unburdened</a> affliction, temporarily increasing max Carry Weight by 3.00 kg for 3 hours, thus raising the maximum Carry Weight possible to 48.00 kg.

### Losing Well Fed

Reaching Starvation at any point (reaching 0 Calorie Store) or gaining the <a href="./Chemical%20Poisoning.md" class="wikilink" title="Chemical Poisoning">Chemical Poisoning</a> Affliction will remove Well Fed and its effects.

If Well Fed is not already active, then achieving 0 calorie store/Chemical Poisoning will reset the 72-hour timer required to activate Well Fed. This mechanic (and <a href="./Scurvy.md" class="wikilink" title="Scurvy">Scurvy</a>) is designed to counteract the benefits of <a href="./Hibernation.md" class="wikilink" title="Hibernation">Hibernation</a>.

## Trivia

This affliction was added in the <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Changelog.md#1.41" class="wikilink" title="2018 December Update">2018 December Update</a> and is the fourth positive affliction added to the game at the time (the other three being <a href="./Warming%20Up.md" class="wikilink" title="Warming Up">Warming Up</a>, <a href="./Improved%20Rest.md" class="wikilink" title="Improved Rest">Improved Rest</a>, and <a href="./Fatigue%20Reduced.md" class="wikilink" title="Fatigue Reduced">Fatigue Reduced</a>) being the only one not requiring the consumption of specific food items to activate as well as the only one not having a set effect duration.
