**Well Fed** is a positive [Affliction](./Afflictions.md) gained by avoiding starvation.

## Overview

Well Fed activates when the player has managed to avoid starvation ([calorie store count](./Hunger.md) at 0) for 72 hours (three full days). When this is achieved, the player character will get a 5% buff to maximum [Condition](./Condition.md) (capping it at 105%) and an increased [carrying capacity](./Encumbrance.md) of 5.00 kg (11.00 lbs.)

The carrying capacity effects stack with wearing a [Moose-Hide Satchel](./Moose-Hide%20Satchel.md) and [Technical Backpack](./Technical%20Backpack.md), meaning that having all 3 present adds 15.00 kg (33.00 lbs) to your 30.00 kg [Encumbrance](./Encumbrance.md) capacity. Max Carry Weight can be increased further by acquiring the [Unburdened](./Unburdened.md) affliction, temporarily increasing max Carry Weight by 3.00 kg for 3 hours, thus raising the maximum Carry Weight possible to 48.00 kg.

### Losing Well Fed

Reaching Starvation at any point (reaching 0 Calorie Store) or gaining the [Chemical Poisoning](./Chemical%20Poisoning.md) Affliction will remove Well Fed and its effects.

If Well Fed is not already active, then achieving 0 calorie store/Chemical Poisoning will reset the 72-hour timer required to activate Well Fed. This mechanic (and [Scurvy](./Scurvy.md)) is designed to counteract the benefits of [Hibernation](./Hibernation.md).

## Trivia

This affliction was added in the [2018 December Update](./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Changelog.md#1.41) and is the fourth positive affliction added to the game at the time (the other three being [Warming Up](./Warming%20Up.md), [Improved Rest](./Improved%20Rest.md), and [Fatigue Reduced](./Fatigue%20Reduced.md)) being the only one not requiring the consumption of specific food items to activate as well as the only one not having a set effect duration.
