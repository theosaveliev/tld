> 
> There are two locations with the same name: one on the Summit and the other [[Wing]] found roughly between [[Echo Peak West]] and [[Forest Cave (Timberwolf Mountain location)|Forest Cave]].
> ==Overview==
> The location of this wing is on the Summit, just between a piece of landing gear and the [[Tail Section]]. A [[Warm Cave]] is found nearby and can be used for shelter.
> 
> It must be surveyed in order to unlock the [[Faithful Cartographer]] achievement.
