**Wing (Summit)** is a part of a wrecked plane that crashed on the [Summit](./Summit.md) of [Timberwolf Mountain](./Timberwolf%20Mountain.md).

There are two locations with the same name: one on the Summit and the other [Wing](./Wing.md) found roughly between [Echo Peak West](./Echo%20Peak%20West.md) and [Forest Cave](./Forest%20Cave%20%28Timberwolf%20Mountain%20location%29.md).

## Overview

The location of this wing is on the Summit, just between a piece of landing gear and the [Tail Section](./Tail%20Section.md). A [Warm Cave](./Warm%20Cave.md) is found nearby and can be used for shelter.

It must be surveyed in order to unlock the [Faithful Cartographer](./Faithful%20Cartographer.md) achievement.
