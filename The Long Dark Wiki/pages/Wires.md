<p><strong>Wires</strong> are an item found in <a href="./Survival%20Mode.md" class="wikilink" title="Survival Mode">Survival Mode</a> in <em><a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a></em>. They were introduced in Part 2 of the <a href="./Tales%20from%20the%20Far%20Territory.md" class="wikilink" title="Tales from the Far Territory">Tales from the Far Territory</a> addon. They can be found in most industrial areas in the game world.</p>
<h2 id="Uses">Uses</h2>
<ul>
<li>2 are used to repair <a href="./Transmitters.md" class="wikilink" title="Transmitters">Transmitters</a> across Great Bear Island.</li>
<li>1 bundle can be used at a <a href="./Work%20Bench.md" class="wikilink" title="Work Bench">Work Bench</a> to make a <a href="./Fishing%20Lure%20%28Wire%20Bundle%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Fishing Lure (Wire Bundle)">Fishing Lure (Wire Bundle)</a>.</li>
<li>1 bundle is used to destroy the <a href="./Prepper%27s%20Abandoned%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper&#39;s Abandoned Cache">Prepper's Abandoned Cache</a> at the end of <a href="./Sutherland%27s%20Tale.md" class="wikilink" title="Sutherland&#39;s Tale">Sutherland's Tale</a>.</li>
</ul>
