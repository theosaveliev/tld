> == Uses ==
> *2 are used to repair [[Transmitter|Transmitters]] across Great Bear Island.
> *1 bundle can be used at a [[Work Bench]] to make a [[Fishing Lure (Wire Bundle)]].
> * 1 bundle is used to destroy the [[Prepper's Abandoned Cache]] at the end of [[Sutherland's Tale]].
