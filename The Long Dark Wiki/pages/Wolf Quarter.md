## Overview

A **Wolf Quarter** is a section harvested from a <a href="./Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf">Wolf</a> or <a href="./Timberwolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Timberwolf">Timberwolf</a>. A <a href="./Poisoned%20Wolf.md" class="wikilink" title="Poisoned Wolf">Poisoned Wolf</a> will not allow a Wolf Quarter to be harvested, as they have no meat on their bodies.

You can only obtain wolf quarters by *quartering* a wolf carcass. To quarter an animal, you have to go into the harvest menu, then select the quartering action. For a wolf, this will always take 1 hour. You can only quarter a wolf if its less than 50% frozen.

After quartering, the carcass will despawn, leaving behind 2-3 quarters, as well as its <a href="./Fresh%20Gut.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Gut">Fresh Gut</a> and <a href="./Fresh%20Wolf%20Pelt.md" class="wikilink" title="Fresh Wolf Pelt">Fresh Wolf Pelt</a>. It will also leave behind the wolves legs and blood, but these cannot be picked up, and it will soon despawn. As quartering only takes 1 hour for wolves, and gives you all necessary parts, it makes transport and harvesting easier, especially if a blizzard is coming or if it will be night soon.

Unlike directly harvesting a fresh carcass' meat, you will still need to harvest a quarter for its meat. 50% of the quarters total weight will be turned into usable <a href="./Wolf%20Meat%20%28Raw%29.md" class="wikilink" title="Wolf Meat (Raw)">Wolf Meat (Raw)</a>. Any remaining raw meat left inside the bag will be lost if the Quarter's Condition reaches 0%, with bags spoiling within 1 day of harvesting.

## Trivia

Timberwolf Quarter bags are slightly larger than regular Wolf Quarters.
