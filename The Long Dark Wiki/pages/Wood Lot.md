> |map=Wood Lot Location.png}}'''Wood Lot''' is the name of three locations either side of [[Milton]] in the [[Mountain Town]] region. 
> 
> ==Overview==
> All three locations are former forests which are now just tree stumps and piles of felled trees. [[Wolf|Wolves]] can also be found regularly in all locations, along with [[Deer]], mushrooms, firewood, and other natural items. A [[Deer Carcass]] often spawns in the Wood Lot nearest the [[Orca Gas Station]] and the [[Crashed Prison Transport Bus]].
> 
> == Locations ==
> 
> * The first is between [[St Christopher's Church]] and [[Milton]] bridge
> * The second and third are adjacent to the [[Orca Gas Station]] and the [[Crashed Prison Transport Bus]].
> 
> == Faithful Cartographer ==
> All 3 Wood Lots must be surveyed in order to make progress towards the [[Faithful Cartographer]] achievement.{{Mountain Town}}
