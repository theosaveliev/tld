# Wyatt's Skeleton

> {{TFTFT}}{{Infobox item
> |name=|image=Wyatt's Skeleton icon.png|release=2 December 2024|update=2.33|story=No|type=Tools|equipable=No|stackable=No|weight=0.00|decay-inside=|decay-outside=|examine=Human remains. Some of the bones appear burnt.|key=}}'''Wyatt's Skeleton''' is an item found during [[Sutherland's Tale]].
> ==Overview==
> It is found in the [[Trade Drop Box]] after starting the 5th Special Request, and must be returned back to the [[Prepper's Abandoned Cache]] with a [[Wire Bundle]], then destroying the cache to advance Sutherland's questline.
> 
> It cannot be dropped or placed in a container, so it cannot be lost.
