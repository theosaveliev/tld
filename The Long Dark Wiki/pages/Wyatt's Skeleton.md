<p><strong>Wyatt's Skeleton</strong> is an item found during <a href="./Sutherland%27s%20Tale.md" class="wikilink" title="Sutherland&#39;s Tale">Sutherland's Tale</a>.</p>
<h2 id="Overview">Overview</h2>
<p>It is found in the <a href="./Trade%20Drop%20Box.md" class="wikilink" title="Trade Drop Box">Trade Drop Box</a> after starting the 5th Special Request, and must be returned back to the <a href="./Prepper%27s%20Abandoned%20Cache.md" class="wikilink" title="Prepper&#39;s Abandoned Cache">Prepper's Abandoned Cache</a> with a <a href="./Wires.md" class="wikilink" title="Wire Bundle">Wire Bundle</a>, then destroying the cache to advance Sutherland's questline.</p>
<p>It cannot be dropped or placed in a container, so it cannot be lost.</p>
