**You'll Be With Her Soon** is a <a href="./Wintermute.md" class="wikilink" title="Wintermute">Wintermute</a> <a href="./The%20Long%20Dark%20%E2%80%94%20Achievements.md" class="wikilink" title="achievement">achievement</a> in <a href="./Episode%202%20-%20Luminance%20Fugue.md" class="wikilink" title="Episode 2: Luminance Fugue">Episode 2: Luminance Fugue</a> of *<a href="./The%20Long%20Dark.md" class="wikilink" title="The Long Dark">The Long Dark</a>*.

## Guide

This is an unmissable story achievement achieved after completing the mission <a href="./Renewed%20Hope.md" class="wikilink" title="Renewed Hope">Renewed Hope</a>. Walk into the final room in the <a href="./Carter%20Hydro%20Dam.md" class="wikilink" title="Carter Hydro Dam">Carter Hydro Dam</a> and the ending cutscene plays. Upon completing Episode 2, the achievement is unlocked.

## Trivia

The achievement's name is a reference to the quote from <a href="./Mathis.md" class="wikilink" title="Mathis">Mathis</a>: "Well, don't worry, pilot. You'll be with her soon."
